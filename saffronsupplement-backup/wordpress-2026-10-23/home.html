FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about saffron supplements, dosage, safety, and effectiveness.

What is saffron extract and how is it different from raw saffron?
+
Saffron extract is a concentrated, standardized form of the saffron spice (Crocus sativus), processed to preserve and concentrate its key bioactive compounds: crocin, crocetin, and safranal. A high-quality 30mg extract capsule can contain as many active molecules as several grams of raw saffron threads, with the advantage of verified potency and consistent dosing. Raw saffron varies enormously in quality and active content.
What is the recommended daily saffron supplement dosage?
+
The most widely studied and recommended dose for standardized saffron extract is 28–30 mg per day, typically split into two 14–15mg doses (morning and evening) or taken as a single dose. This mirrors the dosing used in the majority of peer-reviewed clinical trials. Higher doses are not necessarily more effective and may cause side effects. Always follow product label instructions and consult a healthcare provider.
How long before I notice results from saffron supplements?
+
Most clinical studies observe meaningful improvements in mood and sleep quality within 2–4 weeks of consistent daily use. Some individuals notice subtle shifts in mood or appetite within the first week. Cognitive and eye health benefits typically require 6–12 weeks of continuous supplementation. As with all nutraceuticals, results vary based on individual biology, lifestyle, and supplement quality.
Are saffron supplements safe? Are there any side effects?
+
At clinically studied doses (28–30mg/day), saffron extract has an excellent safety profile in healthy adults. Mild side effects reported in some individuals include dry mouth, slight nausea, or headaches, typically resolving within days. Saffron should not be taken in high doses during pregnancy. Saffron may interact with certain medications (particularly antidepressants and blood pressure medications) — always consult your physician if you take prescription drugs.
What should I look for when buying a saffron supplement?
+
Look for: (1) A standardized extract — ideally Satiereal® or one standardized by HPLC to specific crocin/safranal percentages. (2) Third-party testing from independent labs like NSF, USP, or Eurofins. (3) Transparent labeling with exact extract dose (not "proprietary blend"). (4) Reputable sourcing (Iranian, Spanish, or Kashmiri saffron). (5) No unnecessary fillers, artificial colors, or underdosed formulas with inflated mg claims from raw powder.
Can saffron supplements help with anxiety and depression?
+
Multiple randomized controlled trials have shown saffron extract (28–30mg/day) to be significantly more effective than placebo for mild-to-moderate depression, and comparable to low doses of some antidepressant medications. It appears to work through multiple mechanisms, including serotonin reuptake inhibition, NMDA receptor modulation, and anti-inflammatory pathways. However, saffron supplements are not a replacement for prescribed medications — always work with a qualified healthcare provider for mental health conditions.
