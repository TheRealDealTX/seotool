How the estimate works

Each space starts from a typical number of hands-on hours for one organizer working through an average-sized room with a moderate amount of stuff. A walk-in closet, for example, usually takes longer than a linen closet, and a garage takes longer than either. The calculator then adjusts for clutter level, overall home size and the number of organizers on site.

The result shows total organizer hours (the labor involved) and hours on site (how long your home is busy). Two organizers working for four hours is eight organizer hours but only four hours of your day.

What makes projects faster or slower

  • Decision speed. The biggest variable is how quickly keep-or-let-go decisions get made. Our decision helper can help you practice.
  • Pre-sorting. Gathering like items together before your session can save time — but please don't stress-clean first.
  • Product installation. Closet systems, drawer inserts and shelving add setup time.
  • Donation logistics. Hauling donations or consignment pieces adds a short trip at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Is this estimate a quote?

No. It's a planning tool. We quote every project individually after a consultation because each home, and each person's pace, is different.

Why does clutter level matter so much?

Most organizing time is spent sorting and deciding, not arranging. A heavily filled space simply has more decisions to make.

Does it save my answers?

Yes, your selections are stored only in your own browser so you can come back to them later.
