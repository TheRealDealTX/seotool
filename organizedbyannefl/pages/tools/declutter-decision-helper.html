Keep it or let it go? Ask the decision helper

Hold the item in your hand, answer a few quick questions and get a clear, guilt-free verdict. Repeat for the next item — momentum is everything.

Why a decision tree helps

Decluttering is rarely hard because of the stuff. It's hard because of the decisions — dozens of them, back to back, each with a little emotion attached. A short, consistent set of questions takes the guesswork out and helps you move faster without second-guessing.

Tips for a faster session

  • Set up four bags or boxes before you start: keep, donate, sell and recycle/toss.
  • Work one small category at a time, like mugs or T-shirts, rather than a whole room.
  • Set a timer for 20 minutes and stop while you still have energy.
  • Take donations to the car the same day so they actually leave.

Want to build the habit? Try our 30-day declutter challenge or download ideas from our room-by-room decluttering checklist. If it all feels like too much, our decluttering services are judgment-free.

Frequently asked questions

What if I still can't decide?

Put the item in a dated 'maybe' box. If you haven't opened the box in six months, donate it without looking inside.

What about sentimental items?

Keep the pieces that tell the story best and give them a place of honor. A photo can preserve the memory of the rest.

Where can I donate in Fort Lauderdale?

Many local thrift stores, shelters and charities accept household goods. Call ahead for what they currently need, or let us handle the drop-off during a session.
