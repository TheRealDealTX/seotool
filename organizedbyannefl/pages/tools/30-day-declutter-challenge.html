The 30-day declutter challenge

One small task a day, about 15 minutes each. Check off days as you go — your progress saves in this browser, so you can come back anytime.

Your progress

Pick a day and start small — fifteen minutes counts.

Do the days in any order. Skipped one? No problem — just pick up where you are.

How to do the challenge

Every task is designed to take about fifteen minutes. Small wins build momentum, and momentum is what turns decluttering from a dreaded weekend project into an easy habit. You don't need to go in order, and you don't need to be perfect.

  • Keep a donation bag in your car. Drop items in as you go and make one trip at the end of each week.
  • Use the decision helper when you get stuck: our keep-or-let-go tool keeps things moving.
  • Celebrate milestones. Days 10, 20 and 30 deserve a little reward.

After the 30 days

Once the backlog is gone, maintenance is simple: a ten-minute reset each evening and a quick monthly edit of hot spots like the junk drawer. If you'd like a pro to tackle the bigger rooms, explore our home organizing services.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to finish in exactly 30 days?

Not at all. Go at your own pace — the checklist saves your progress so you can take breaks.

Is my progress private?

Yes. Your checkmarks are saved only in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Can I print it?

Yes. Use the Print checklist button to print the list and stick it on the fridge.
