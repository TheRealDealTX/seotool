How we work

Consult

We walk the space with you, in person or by video, and listen to what's not working.

Plan

A custom plan using what you already own, plus a curated product list and layout ideas.

Sort & edit

We sort, categorize and edit together — judgment-free, at a pace that feels right.

Style

Containers, labels and a touch of decor so the space is as beautiful as it is functional.

Maintain

Simple routines and a reset guide so the system keeps working long after we leave.

Two ways to work together

Do-it-all or do-it-together

Some clients want to hand over the keys and come home to a finished space. Others want a clear plan and a guide by their side. Both are welcome.

  • Full-service organizing. In-home consultation, a curated plan, hands-on sorting, product sourcing, installation, labels and donation drop-off.
  • Plan & guide. We review areas of opportunity and concern, build your organizational plan around existing systems, and coach you as you implement it.
  • Virtual consultation. A one-hour video session with help taking measurements, a curated product list and a mock diagram of your space.

Frequently asked questions

How much do your organizing services cost?

Every project is quoted individually after a consultation, because scope, team size and products vary widely. Our guide to professional organizer costs explains the factors involved.

How long does a typical project take?

A single closet or pantry is often finished in one session, while a whole home may take several days spread across a few weeks. Try our time estimator for a ballpark.

Do I have to be home while you organize?

For editing decisions we'll need you nearby, at least at first. Once decisions are made, many clients run errands or work while we set up the space.

Do you take donations away?

Yes. We bag, label and drop off donations to local charities, and can point you toward consignment for higher-value pieces.

Ready for a calmer home?

Tell us about the space that's stressing you out. We'll reply within one business day with next steps for an in-home or virtual consultation.
