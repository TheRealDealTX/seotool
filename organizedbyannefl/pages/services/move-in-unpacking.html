Move-In & Unpacking

Move-In and Unpacking Services in Fort Lauderdale

From pre-move purge to the last unpacked box, we set up your new home so it feels settled and organized from day one.

Book a consultation Estimate your project

Moving is exciting, but the weeks of boxes that follow can take the joy out of a new home. Our unpacking services in Fort Lauderdale help you skip that stage. We can step in before the move to help you purge, plan where everything will live and pack with purpose, then unpack and set up your new home so it feels settled almost immediately. You focus on the move itself; we focus on getting your belongings into the right places.

Who this service is for

  • Families relocating to South Florida who want to settle in fast
  • Professionals who cannot spare weekends to unpack
  • Downsizers moving into a condo or smaller home
  • Seasonal residents opening a new winter home
  • Anyone who would rather never see another box cutter

Before the move: purge and plan

The best time to declutter is before you pay to move something. We help you sort through each room, decide what is worth bringing and arrange donation drop-offs before moving day. We also review the floor plan of your new home and map out where furniture and categories of belongings will go, so boxes can be labeled by destination room rather than by the room they came from. If you need help with the letting-go stage, our decluttering and downsizing service goes deeper.

After the move: unpack and set up

  1. Priorities first. We start with the spaces you need right away: the kitchen, bathrooms and bedrooms, so you can cook, shower and sleep on the first night.
  2. Unpack by zone. Each room is unpacked and organized into logical zones, not just emptied onto shelves.
  3. Contain and label. We create a container plan and add labels where they help, especially in pantries, closets and shared storage.
  4. Clear the clutter. We break down boxes, gather packing paper and remove the debris so your home looks finished.

Curious how long your unpack might take? Try the organizing time estimator for a quick estimate.

Pro tip

Pack an "open first" box for every room with essentials like sheets, towels, a few dishes, phone chargers, toilet paper and basic tools. Label it clearly and keep it on the truck last so it comes off first.

Condo and high-rise moves

Moving into a Fort Lauderdale high-rise comes with extra logistics. Many buildings require you to reserve the service elevator for a specific time window, provide a certificate of insurance from your movers and follow rules about move days and hours. Some limit how long trucks can stay in the loading area. We recommend contacting your building management early and sharing the details with your movers. Once you are in, we help you make the most of compact condo closets and kitchens with vertical storage and smart furniture choices.

Design touches we like

A new home is a fresh start, so we like to set it up with intention: matching hangers in closets from the beginning, drawer dividers before drawers fill up, a drop zone by the door for keys and mail, and a labeled shelf in the garage or utility closet for hurricane supplies. These small choices keep the house from drifting back into chaos.

Fort Lauderdale considerations

Summer moves in South Florida are hot and humid, and afternoon storms are common, so we plan for protected staging areas and avoid leaving cardboard in garages where it can absorb moisture. Hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30, so if you are moving during those months, we help you set up a storm kit early; our hurricane season organization guide has a checklist. We work across Fort Lauderdale neighborhoods like Coral Ridge, Flagler Village and Victoria Park, as well as Plantation, Davie, Weston and other nearby cities.

What to expect on the day

Our team arrives after the movers have placed furniture and boxes. We confirm the room plan with you, then work steadily through the priority spaces. You will be asked about preferences as we go, like which cabinet should hold everyday dishes, but you will not need to direct every box. By the end, the unpacked rooms are ready to live in and the empty boxes are gone. Want help beyond the unpack? We can continue with whole-home organizing as you settle in.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pack and move my belongings?

We are organizers, not movers. We help you purge, plan and pack with purpose, and we coordinate with your moving company so unpacking goes smoothly.

Can you start unpacking the same day the movers leave?

Yes, and that is often the best time. Starting right away keeps boxes from settling in as part of the decor.

What do I need to know about moving into a condo?

Contact building management early about service elevator reservations, move-day hours and any insurance certificates your movers need to provide.

Will you take away the empty boxes?

We break down boxes and gather packing materials so your home looks finished, and we can help arrange their removal or recycling.
