Virtual Organizing

Virtual Organizing Consultation by Video

A one-hour video session with measuring help, a curated product list and a mock diagram, available wherever you live.

Book a consultation Estimate your project

Not every project needs an organizer in the room. Our virtual organizing consultation brings the same design-minded planning we use in Fort Lauderdale homes to you by video, wherever you live. In a one-hour session, we review the space together, help you take measurements, and then send a curated product list and a mock diagram of the space so you can complete the project on your own schedule. It is a practical, budget-friendly way to get a professional plan and the confidence to carry it out.

Who this service is for

  • Do-it-yourselfers who want an expert plan before buying products
  • Clients outside South Florida, or seasonal residents who are up north
  • Anyone tackling one space, like a closet, pantry or home office
  • Busy schedules that make an in-home visit hard to arrange
  • Past clients who want help with a new room

How the session works

  1. Book and share. After you reach out, we schedule your session and may ask for a few photos of the space ahead of time.
  2. Video walkthrough. During the hour, you show us the space on your phone or tablet. We review areas of concern, ask how you use the room and talk through your goals and budget.
  3. Measure together. We guide you through taking the measurements that matter, such as shelf depth, rod height, cabinet openings and door clearances.
  4. Plan and recommend. We talk through layout ideas and categories, and suggest what to edit before anything new is purchased.
  5. Receive your plan. Afterward, you receive a curated product list matched to your measurements and a mock diagram showing where everything will go.
Pro tip

Have a tape measure, a notepad and a charged phone ready, and clear a little floor space so you can move the camera around easily. Natural light helps us see the space clearly.

What you receive

The curated product list focuses on items that fit your exact space and style, from drawer dividers and shelf risers to hangers, bins and modular components. We aim for pieces that are easy to find and work with what you already own, so you are not buying more than you need. The mock diagram is a simple visual layout of the organized space, showing zones, container placement and labeling ideas, so you can follow it step by step as you work.

Spaces that work well virtually

Almost any single space is a good candidate, especially closets, pantries, home offices, bathrooms, laundry rooms and kids' rooms. Closets are a popular choice; our closet rod calculator pairs nicely with a video session to plan hanging space. For pantries, our pantry organization ideas give you a head start on what to look for.

Design touches we bring

Even over video, we keep a touch of design at the center. We think about color, sightlines and how a space feels when you open the door, not just how much it holds. Expect suggestions like consistent container styles, matching hangers, labels that suit your decor and small finishing touches that make the result feel polished.

Fort Lauderdale and beyond

For local clients, a virtual session is a great first step before booking in-home help, or a way to plan a condo project before seasonal residents return. If you live in the area, we consider local factors like humidity, hurricane season preparedness and compact high-rise storage in our recommendations. If you are elsewhere, we tailor the plan to your climate and home. When you want hands-on help, our in-home organizing covers Fort Lauderdale and our wider service area.

What to expect after the call

You will have a clear plan, a shopping list and a diagram to follow. Most clients start by editing the space, then purchase products and set up the new system over a weekend. If questions come up along the way, reach out by email and we can point you in the right direction. Many clients find that one well-organized room inspires the next.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need for a virtual organizing consultation?

A phone, tablet or laptop with a camera, a stable internet connection and a tape measure. Photos sent ahead of time are helpful but optional.

Can you help if I live outside Florida?

Yes. Virtual sessions are available anywhere, and we tailor product suggestions and storage advice to your home and climate.

Do I have to buy the products on the list?

No. The list is a recommendation, and we prioritize using what you already own wherever possible.

Can a virtual session lead to in-home help?

Absolutely. If you are in our service area, the plan from your video session can become the starting point for hands-on organizing.

What's included

  • One-hour video session
  • Review of your areas of concern
  • Guidance taking accurate measurements
  • Curated product list for your space
  • Mock diagram of the organized layout
  • Step-by-step plan to do it yourself
