Questions, answered

What does a professional organizer do?

We help you edit what you own, design a system that fits your routines, and set up containers and labels so everything has a home. We work side by side with you or take the lead, whichever you prefer.

Do I need to declutter before you arrive?

No. Please don't clean or tidy first — seeing the space as it really is helps us design a system that works for the way you actually live.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Fort Lauderdale and work throughout Broward County, including Wilton Manors, Oakland Park, Plantation, Weston, Coral Springs, Hollywood and Pompano Beach. Virtual consultations are available anywhere.

Will you make me throw things away?

Never. Every keep-or-let-go decision is yours. We ask helpful questions, offer options like donating or selling, and handle the drop-offs if you'd like.

Do you shop for organizing products?

Yes. We start with what you already have, then share a curated product list or shop for you from local stores so everything fits your space and style.

How do I get started?

Send us a note through the contact form or email info@organizedbyannefl.com. We'll set up a consultation to talk through your space, goals and timeline.
