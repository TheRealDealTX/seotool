About the appraisal service

A closer look at the property, the scope, and the numbers.

When a property claim involves a disagreement over the amount of loss, simply comparing two bottom-line totals may not explain why the estimates are different. Alabama Insurance Appraiser focuses on the details behind those numbers.

A meaningful valuation review may require careful attention to damaged materials, dimensions, repair sequencing, labor, unit pricing, access, matching considerations, estimate line items, and other information that affects the cost of returning the property to its pre-loss condition.

The goal is to organize those differences into a clear appraisal position supported by property-specific documentation. As an Alabama Insurance Appraiser service, the focus remains on the amount of loss and the appraisal framework stated in the insurance policy.

The appraisal process

How insurance appraisal commonly progresses.

Every policy can be different. However, when an appraisal clause applies and appraisal is properly invoked, the process often follows a similar sequence.

01

Review the policy language

The appraisal provision should be reviewed first so the assignment follows the procedure stated in the applicable insurance policy.

02

Identify the disputed amount

Estimates, photographs, measurements, repair scopes, and other claim records are reviewed to locate the valuation differences.

03

Appraisers evaluate the loss

Each selected appraiser develops and supports a position concerning the amount of the property loss.

04

Address remaining differences

If the appraisers cannot agree, the appraisal clause may provide a procedure for submitting unresolved valuation differences to an umpire.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about insurance appraisal in Alabama.

What does an Alabama Insurance Appraiser do?

An insurance appraiser evaluates the amount of a property loss when an appraisal process is available under the applicable insurance policy. The appraiser may review damage, measurements, repair scope, estimates, pricing, and supporting documentation.

When can insurance appraisal be useful?

Insurance appraisal is commonly considered when the insurer and policyholder disagree about the amount of a property loss. The exact appraisal rights and procedures depend on the insurance policy.

Does appraisal determine insurance coverage?

Appraisal generally addresses valuation or the amount of loss rather than resolving every coverage issue. Coverage questions remain subject to the policy and the facts of the claim.

What claim documents should be available for review?

Helpful materials can include the policy appraisal provision, carrier and contractor estimates, photographs, measurements, inspection reports, invoices, engineering documents, and relevant claim correspondence.

Do you handle residential and commercial property claims?

The site is positioned for appraisal and estimating assignments involving residential, commercial, multi-family, church, retail, office, and other property types throughout Alabama.

What estimating platforms can be used?

Services include detailed estimating workflows using commonly used platforms such as Xactimate and Symbility, along with property documentation tools such as Matterport when appropriate.

Do you serve areas outside Birmingham?

Yes. Alabama Insurance Appraiser is designed for statewide service, including Huntsville, Montgomery, Mobile, Tuscaloosa, Hoover, Auburn, Dothan, Decatur, Florence, Gadsden, and surrounding communities.
