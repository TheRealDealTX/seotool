Core appraisal service

Independent evaluation of the amount of property loss

When estimates differ, a useful appraisal review looks beyond the final totals and identifies the scope, quantity, pricing, and repair-method differences behind them.

The appraisal assignment can include review of the damaged property, measurements, photographs, competing estimates, repair sequencing, material considerations, labor, access, and other information that affects the cost of repair.

  • Review of the applicable appraisal provision
  • Inspection and property-specific loss documentation
  • Comparison of competing repair scopes and estimates
  • Supported position concerning the amount of loss

Residential and commercial

Services adapted to the property and the loss

Residential property

Homes, condominiums, townhomes, and rental properties

Commercial property

Retail, office, multi-family, church, and institutional buildings

Commercial assignments may require additional attention to building systems, access, occupancy, sequencing, specialized materials, and larger repair scopes.

Services FAQ

Questions about appraisal, estimating, and documentation services

What is included in a property insurance appraisal service?

The scope depends on the assignment and policy, but it may include document review, property inspection, measurements, damage evaluation, estimate comparison, pricing review, and development of a supported position concerning the amount of loss.

Can you prepare both Xactimate and Symbility estimates?

Yes. Detailed line-item estimating can be prepared using Xactimate or Symbility when the selected platform is appropriate for the assignment.

Is Matterport required for every property claim?

No. Matterport is one available documentation tool. Whether it is useful depends on the property, loss conditions, required measurements, and goals of the assignment.

Do you work on both residential and commercial losses?

Yes. Services can support homes, condominiums, rental properties, retail buildings, offices, multi-family properties, churches, and other commercial or institutional buildings.

What documents should I provide?

Helpful records may include the applicable appraisal provision, carrier and contractor estimates, photographs, measurements, engineering reports, invoices, repair proposals, and relevant claim correspondence.

Does an appraisal decide insurance coverage?

Appraisal generally focuses on valuation or the amount of loss. Coverage questions remain subject to the insurance policy, claim facts, and applicable law.

Are services available throughout Alabama?

Yes. The service is positioned for assignments statewide, including Birmingham, Huntsville, Montgomery, Mobile, Tuscaloosa, Auburn, Dothan, and surrounding communities.
