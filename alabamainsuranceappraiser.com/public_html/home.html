Why property owners call us

An Alabama Insurance Appraiser who looks behind the bottom line

Two estimates can be $30,000 apart and still agree on most of the house. The difference usually hides in a few dozen line items.

Comparing two totals tells you almost nothing. As an independent Alabama Insurance Appraiser, we break each estimate down to the measurements, materials, quantities, labor, access, repair method and code items behind it, then build a supported amount-of-loss position you can actually defend.

That focus stays on the amount of loss and on the appraisal procedure your policy sets out — not on reopening the whole claim.

Storm season in Alabama

Storm damage may look obvious. The cost to repair it rarely is.

Alabama sees spring tornado outbreaks across Dixie Alley, large hail in the Tennessee Valley and the Birmingham metro, and hurricane-force wind on the Gulf Coast. The damage is easy to photograph; the repair scope behind it is not.

Roof systems, flashing, ventilation, gutters, siding, windows, interior water intrusion, code upgrades, matching, access and repair sequencing all change the number. An Alabama Insurance Appraiser reviews the complete scope, not just the headline damage.

The appraisal process

How insurance appraisal usually unfolds in Alabama

Every policy is different, but when an appraisal clause applies and is properly invoked, most claims follow a similar path.

Alabama courts treat appraisal as a contract process that is separate from arbitration, which is why the exact wording of your policy matters so much. An experienced Alabama Insurance Appraiser starts there — with the appraisal provision itself.

Read the full process guide
  1. 01

    Review the policy language

    Confirm the appraisal provision, the written-demand requirement, deadlines and how an umpire is chosen.

  2. 02

    Identify the disputed amount

    Line up the estimates, photos, measurements and correspondence to pin down exactly where the numbers differ.

  3. 03

    Appraisers evaluate the loss

    Each side's appraiser inspects, measures and builds a supported position on the amount of loss.

  4. 04

    Resolve remaining differences

    Items the appraisers can't agree on go to the umpire under the policy's procedure, and an award is signed.

All FAQsClaim glossary
What does an Alabama Insurance Appraiser do?

An Alabama Insurance Appraiser evaluates the amount of a property loss when the policyholder and the insurance company disagree on what it will cost to repair or replace the damage. The work covers the inspection, measurements, repair scope, line-item pricing, competing estimates and the documentation that supports a final number under the policy's appraisal clause.

When should I consider insurance appraisal?

Appraisal is worth considering when the carrier has accepted that a loss happened but the two sides are still apart on how much it is worth — after estimates, supplements and re-inspections have been exchanged. The exact right to appraisal, the deadlines and the procedure come from your policy, so read the appraisal provision first.

Does appraisal decide whether my claim is covered?

Generally, no. Appraisal resolves the value of the loss, not every coverage question. If the carrier has denied that the policy covers the damage at all, that is usually a coverage issue that may call for legal advice rather than appraisal alone.

How much does an insurance appraiser cost?

Fees depend on the size and complexity of the loss and are agreed in writing before work begins. Under most appraisal clauses each side pays its own appraiser and the two sides split the umpire's fee. Ask any appraiser how the fee is calculated before you sign.

Do you handle commercial and multi-family claims?

Yes. Our Alabama Insurance Appraiser services cover single-family homes and condos as well as office, retail, apartment, church, warehouse, industrial and institutional property — claims where the estimate can run to hundreds of line items.

Which estimating platforms do you use?

We write and review estimates in Xactimate and Symbility, the two platforms most carriers use, and capture properties with Matterport 3D scanning when a digital record of rooms, measurements and damage will help.

Do you work outside Birmingham?

Yes — statewide. We take assignments in Huntsville, Montgomery, Mobile, Tuscaloosa, Hoover, Auburn, Dothan, Decatur, Florence, Gadsden and the smaller towns and rural counties in between, from the Tennessee Valley to the Gulf Coast.

How long does insurance appraisal take?

A well-documented residential claim can resolve in a few weeks once both appraisers are named; large commercial losses take longer. Inspection scheduling, the number of disputed items and whether an umpire is needed drive the timeline.
