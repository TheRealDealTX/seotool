Insurance Claim & Appraisal Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the terms you'll see on estimates, policies and appraisal paperwork.

ACDFHMOPRSUWX
Actual cash value (ACV)
Replacement cost minus depreciation. Many policies pay ACV first and the rest after repairs.
Additional living expenses (ALE)
Coverage D: reasonable extra costs of living elsewhere while a home is uninhabitable after a covered loss.
Amount of loss
The dollar value of a covered loss. This is what appraisal decides.
Appraisal award
The written decision setting the amount of loss, usually signed by two of the three appraisal panel members.
Appraisal clause
The policy provision describing how a disagreement over the amount of loss can be resolved by appraisers and an umpire.
Coverage A
Dwelling coverage — the limit for the house itself. Percentage deductibles are calculated on it.
Declarations page
The summary page of your policy listing coverages, limits, deductibles and the insured property.
Depreciation
Reduction in value for age and wear. Recoverable depreciation is paid after repairs on replacement cost policies.
Disinterested appraiser
An appraiser with no financial stake in the outcome beyond a reasonable fee. Many policies require one.
FORTIFIED roof
A roof built to IBHS FORTIFIED standards for wind resistance; often eligible for premium discounts in Alabama.
Hurricane / named-storm deductible
A deductible stated as a percentage of Coverage A that applies when the policy's storm trigger is met.
Matching
Whether repaired or replaced materials reasonably match the undamaged materials around them.
Matterport
3D capture technology that creates a measurable digital model of a property.
Mitigation
Steps taken to prevent further damage after a loss, such as tarping or water extraction.
Ordinance or law coverage
Coverage for added costs of meeting current building codes during repairs, subject to limits.
Overhead and profit (O&P)
A general contractor's markup, commonly 10% and 10%, for coordinating multiple trades.
Proof of loss
A sworn statement of the amount claimed, which some policies require within a set time.
Replacement cost value (RCV)
The cost to repair or replace damaged property with like kind and quality, without deducting depreciation.
Roofing square
100 square feet of roof surface — the unit used in roofing estimates.
Scope of loss
The list of damaged items and the repairs needed to restore them.
Supplement
An additional request for payment for items missed or underpaid in the original estimate.
Symbility
Claims estimating software used by a number of insurers and adjusting firms.
Umpire
A neutral third person who resolves items the two appraisers can't agree on.
Waste factor
Extra material added to an estimate to account for cutting and fitting, especially on roofs.
Xactimate
The estimating software most widely used by insurers, adjusters and contractors.
