How percentage deductibles work in Alabama

Many Alabama homeowner policies, especially in Mobile and Baldwin counties, carry a separate hurricane or named-storm deductible stated as a percentage of Coverage A. On a $350,000 dwelling limit, a 2% deductible is $7,000 — even if the damage is only $9,000. Some policies apply a percentage deductible to all wind and hail losses.

When the deductible applies depends on how the policy defines the triggering event — for example, a storm named by the National Hurricane Center, or a hurricane watch or warning issued for any part of Alabama. Read your policy's definition carefully.

Ways Alabama homeowners lower wind risk

Alabama law requires insurers to offer premium discounts for homes built or retrofitted to IBHS FORTIFIED standards, and the state's Strengthen Alabama Homes program has offered grants toward FORTIFIED roofs. Ask your agent how a FORTIFIED roof would affect your premium and deductible options.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does the deductible apply per storm or per year?

Policies differ. Some apply the hurricane deductible per event, others once per hurricane season. Check your policy.

Does appraisal change my deductible?

No. Appraisal sets the amount of loss; the deductible is then applied under the policy.
