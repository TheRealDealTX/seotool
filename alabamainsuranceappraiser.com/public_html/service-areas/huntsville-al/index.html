Insurance Appraiser in Huntsville, AL

Independent property claim appraisal and estimating for homeowners and businesses in Huntsville and across Madison County.

Call (844) 537-1427Send claim details

Huntsville and Madison County are growing faster than almost anywhere in the Southeast, and the Tennessee Valley's hail and tornado season makes for a steady stream of property claims. A single spring storm can leave entire subdivisions waiting on roof claims.

Alabama Insurance Appraiser works with Huntsville homeowners, commercial owners, property managers, contractors and attorneys when a claim comes down to a disagreement over the amount of loss. We inspect, measure and estimate the damage, compare it with the carrier's numbers and build a documented appraisal position.

Property risks we see in Huntsville

Hail

The Tennessee Valley is one of Alabama's most active hail areas, and large-hail days can damage thousands of roofs in an afternoon.

Tornadoes

Supercells crossing North Alabama in spring and late fall bring tornado and straight-line wind damage.

New construction

Rapid growth means many newer homes with architectural shingles, vinyl siding and open floor plans.

Commercial & tech

Research Park, the Redstone corridor and new retail and multi-family developments.

What makes Huntsville claims different

Newer Huntsville subdivisions often share the same builder-grade shingle line and siding color. After a hailstorm, that helps establish damage patterns — but it also creates matching disputes when a product has been discontinued. Open floor plans also make continuous-flooring and paint-scope questions common on interior claims.

Check the policy first

Your appraisal rights, deadlines and procedures come from your policy, not from where you live. Read the appraisal provision before you send a written demand.

Huntsville insurance appraisal FAQ

Do you cover Madison, Harvest and Hampton Cove?

Yes, along with the rest of Madison County and nearby Limestone and Marshall county communities.

Is hail damage on a newer roof still worth disputing?

Yes. Newer roofs carry less depreciation, so a properly scoped replacement can be worth significantly more than a repair estimate.
