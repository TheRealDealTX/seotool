Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information bcpaperdesigns.com ("we", "us") handles when you use this website.

Information you give us

If you email us, including through the contact form (which opens your own email app), we receive your name, email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and keep it no longer than needed for that conversation.

Information stored in your browser

Our tools and checklists save your inputs, such as a party date, guest counts or ticked items, in your browser's local storage so they are there when you return. This data stays on your device; it is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Cookies and analytics

We do not set advertising cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the font files, under Google's own privacy policy. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in future, this page will be updated first.

Children

This site is written for adults planning parties. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any email correspondence by writing to info@bcpaperdesigns.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
