About BC Paper Designs

BC Paper Designs is a paper party studio for baby showers and kids' birthday parties. We design themed decorations, share how to make them, and build free tools that take the guesswork out of planning.

Where we started

The studio began in 2017 making complete party sets: welcome banners, door signs, cake toppers, centerpieces, cupcake toppers, favor tags, place cards, invitations and thank-you cards. Pieces were printed on a photo-quality printer on 110 lb heavyweight cardstock, then cut by hand and assembled with hot glue, skewers, toothpicks and clear string, so they arrived ready to hang. Any set could carry any message.

Pink elephants, construction trucks, monsters, reptiles, whales, woodland animals and safari prints were some of the themes parents asked for again and again. Those themes live on here as free guides.

What you will find here

  • 31 party theme guides with palettes, decor lists, DIY projects and checklists
  • Five free tools: a printable banner maker, food calculator, planning timeline, theme quiz and bingo generator
  • Planning guides on themes, games, printing and quantities

How we write our guides

Everything is practical: real sizes, quantities and timings, colors given as hex codes so you can match paper, balloons and frosting. When we mention a popular character theme, we design around generic animals and shapes; we are not affiliated with any brand or rights holder.

Questions, corrections or a party you would like help with? Write to info@bcpaperdesigns.com.
