How to print and hang your banner

  1. Type your message. Short messages ("OH BABY", "HAPPY 1ST", a first name) read best from across a room.
  2. Pick colors that match your theme. Every theme guide has a "Make the banner" button that loads its palette here.
  3. Load heavy cardstock (65-110 lb, depending on your printer) and print at actual size with background graphics on.
  4. Cut along the dashed line. Scissors are fine; a paper trimmer makes the straight edges faster.
  5. Punch two holes near the top corners, about 3/4 inch in, and thread twine or ribbon through the front of each flag.
  6. Hang it with removable hooks and slide the flags so the spacing is even, about 1 inch apart.

How long will my banner be?

Each flag prints about 7.4 inches wide. With 1 inch between flags, a 10-letter message plus one blank flag between words runs roughly 8 feet. For a long wall, add a garland of paper hearts or extra blank flags at each end.

Tip: Printing "HAPPY BIRTHDAY" uses 13 sheets. Print a couple of spare blank flags in your accent color to fill gaps and make the ends drape nicely.

Questions

Is the banner maker free?

Yes, completely. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

What paper should I use?

Cardstock between 65 and 110 lb. Thinner paper curls and lets light through when hung in front of a window.

Why are my colors missing when I print?

Turn on "Background graphics" (Chrome, Edge) or "Print backgrounds" (Safari, Firefox) in the print dialog.
