Party Planning Timeline & Countdown

Enter your party date to get a dated countdown checklist for a baby shower or birthday, from invitations to party day. Tick tasks off and add it to your calendar.

What are you planning?

Your date and ticks are saved in this browser.

    Why a dated timeline helps

    Most party stress comes from tasks bunching up in the final week. Working backward from the date spreads the jobs out: invitations four to six weeks ahead, decor in the last two weeks, fresh food in the final two days.

    The baby shower plan follows the steps in our week-by-week baby shower guide; the birthday plan is shorter because invitations go out three to four weeks ahead.

    Tip: Do the decorations a full week early. Paper pieces keep perfectly in a flat box, and the last few days are always busier than you expect.

    Questions

    When should baby shower invitations go out?

    Four to six weeks before the shower, so guests can plan travel and shop the registry.

    When should birthday invitations go out?

    Three to four weeks before for kids' parties. Two weeks is fine for a small family gathering.

    Can I print the timeline?

    Yes. Use the Print button; the checklist prints cleanly without the site header and footer.
