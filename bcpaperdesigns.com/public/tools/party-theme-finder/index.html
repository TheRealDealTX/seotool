How to choose a party theme

The best themes start with the guest of honor. A toddler who lines up toy trucks will light up at a construction party; a parent-to-be who loves calm colors will feel at home with a woodland shower.

  • Start with color. A palette of three or four colors does more for a room than any single decoration.
  • Check the venue. Earthy themes suit parks and patios; bright themes lift a plain community room.
  • Think about photos. The banner and cake table are in every picture, so make those match the theme first.

Questions

How does the theme finder pick themes?

Each theme is tagged by occasion, age, mood, colors and subject. Your answers score every theme and the top three are shown.

Can I mix two themes?

Yes. Keep one palette and let the second theme appear in small details like cupcake toppers or favor tags.
