Woodland Baby Shower Decorations

Foxes, fawns, owls and hedgehogs gathered under paper pines for a cozy forest welcome.

Plan this party Make the banner

Woodland baby shower decorations are some of the easiest to make feel warm and handmade, because the forest already gives you the cast: a curious fox, a spotted fawn, a sleepy owl and a hedgehog tucked into fallen leaves. We built our woodland sets around those four friends on heavy 110 lb cardstock, and the same pieces carry over beautifully to a first woodland birthday party.

An animal theme baby shower works for any guest list and any gender reveal plan. Keep the base palette earthy, then lean pink with dusty rose or lean blue with a soft slate if you want a hint. The fox is the star in most rooms, so a fox themed baby shower is simply this theme with the fox placed front and center on the banner and cake.

What goes in the woodland baby shower paper set

Forest friends banner

Five-inch pennants alternating fox, deer, owl and hedgehog with WELCOME BABY in bark brown letters, strung on twine with mini clothespins.

Hello Little One door sign

An 8x10 sign with a fawn peeking around a birch trunk, mounted on chipboard so it hangs flat on the front door.

Tree-stump centerpieces

Three-tier paper pines set in a small wood slice with a standing fox or owl cutout glued to a bamboo skewer.

Cake topper

A 6-inch fox curled around the baby’s name or a short message, double-layered so it stays rigid in buttercream.

Cupcake toppers

Two-inch circles in four animal faces, a dozen of each, on toothpicks.

Water bottle labels

Wraparound labels printed with a repeating leaf pattern and sized for standard 16.9 oz bottles.

Favor tags

Two-inch acorn-shaped tags reading Thanks for helping us welcome our little one, punched for twine.

Advice cards

Five-by-seven cards titled Wise Words from the Woods with an owl at the top for guests to fill in.

Food tent cards

Folded 3.5x2-inch labels with a tiny hedgehog for each dish on the buffet.

Building a forest corner

Pick one wall for a backdrop and layer it: a kraft paper base, the forest friends banner across the top, and a cluster of paper pines in three heights below. A plaid throw over the gift table is a quick woodland party decorations upgrade.

On the tables, skip a busy patterned tablecloth and use cream linen or burlap runners so the cutouts stand out. Scatter a handful of faux acorns, pinecones and moss. If you go pink, swap moss green napkins for dusty rose and the whole room softens.

A dessert table the animals would raid

A naked cake dusted with powdered sugar looks like a stump after a snowfall and holds the fox topper well. Surround it with mushroom-shaped meringues, berry tarts and the animal cupcakes on a wood slice stand.

Make it yourself: paper pine trees

These layered trees fill centerpieces, the dessert table and the window ledges, and a dozen take about an hour.

  1. Cut three circles per tree from green cardstock: 5, 4 and 3 inches.
  2. Fold each circle into eighths and snip a small notch at the center point.
  3. Unfold and cut a slit from the edge to the center so each circle becomes a shallow cone.
  4. Overlap the slit by one wedge and glue to form the cone.
  5. Stack the three cones on a 10-inch skewer, largest at the bottom, with a dab of hot glue under each.
  6. Push the skewer into a wood slice drilled with a small hole or a block of floral foam hidden under moss.

Menu and party games

On the menu

  • Fox tail fruit cups: orange segments and cantaloupe layered in clear cups
  • Mushroom caprese bites: cherry tomatoes and mozzarella on pretzel stems
  • Trail mix bar: nuts, dried cranberries, yogurt chips and granola in small crates
  • Pigs in a sleeping bag: mini sausages in puff pastry with honey mustard
  • Acorn treats: chocolate kisses stuck to mini vanilla wafers with frosting
  • Forest berry punch: sparkling lemonade with blackberries and rosemary sprigs

Games and activities

  • Woodland bingo: print a set from our baby shower bingo generator and use acorns as markers
  • Name that baby animal: kit, fawn, owlet, hoglet and more on a matching sheet
  • Wishes for baby tree: guests hang leaf-shaped notes on a branch in a vase
  • Guess the acorns: a jar of acorn candies and a pencil for every guess
  • Owl advice cards: guests share one piece of wise parenting wisdom

Woodland Baby Shower FAQ

What colors work best for woodland baby shower decorations?

Earthy oranges, browns and moss greens on a cream base are the classic mix. For a girl, add dusty rose; for a boy, add slate blue. Keep metallics to a touch of copper so the forest feel stays natural.

Can I reuse the decor for a woodland birthday party?

Yes. Swap the Welcome Baby banner letters for a name and age, and change the cake topper message. The pines, centerpieces and animal cupcake toppers work for a first birthday without any changes.

How many woodland animal decorations do I need for 30 guests?

Plan one centerpiece per table of eight, about 36 cupcake toppers and 30 favor tags. Our party food calculator helps size the food to match.

Is a fox themed baby shower different from a woodland theme?

Mostly in emphasis. A fox themed baby shower uses the same forest palette but repeats the fox on the banner, cake and favors, while woodland spreads the spotlight across several animals.
