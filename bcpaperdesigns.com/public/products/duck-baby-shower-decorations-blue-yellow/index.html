Blue and Yellow Duck Baby Shower Decorations

Bubbles, rubber ducks and sky blue stripes for a splashy, cheerful welcome.

Plan this party Make the banner

A duck baby shower is cheerful from the first glance: a sunny yellow duck, a tub of blue bubbles and a few orange beaks to wake it all up. Our blue and yellow baby shower decorations stick to clean shapes and bold color so they read clearly from across the room.

The palette bends easily. Swap the blue for mint and you get a blue green yellow baby shower theme that feels a little more springlike, or keep it classic for a baby boy. Either way, baby duck baby shower details like footprints and bubbles tie every piece together.

Decorating like bath time

Hang white and clear balloons in a cluster above the dessert table for a soap-bubble look. Lay a light blue tablecloth and cover it with white bubbles cut in three sizes. A row of real rubber ducks marching down the runner costs little and makes guests smile.

For blue and green baby shower decorations, add mint napkins and a few eucalyptus sprigs. It keeps the same playful ducks but feels softer.

Duck baby shower centerpiece ideas

Small wash tubs, glass bowls of blue water beads, or tall vases with a duck bobbing on top all work. Keep centerpieces under 12 inches so guests can see each other across the table. One bathtub per table of eight is plenty.

Make it yourself: a bubble backdrop

A wall of paper bubbles behind the dessert table makes the best photo spot.

  1. Cut about 40 circles from white, pale blue and mint cardstock in 2, 4 and 6-inch sizes.
  2. Add a white crescent highlight to each blue circle with a paint pen.
  3. Arrange them on the floor first in a loose arc.
  4. Attach each circle to the wall with removable mounting putty.
  5. Place the rubber duck banner across the middle of the arc.
  6. Finish with three large ducks at the bottom as if they are swimming through.

Blue and Yellow Duck Baby Shower FAQ

Can I make blue and yellow baby shower decorations gender neutral?

Yes. Lean more on yellow and white with only touches of blue, or bring in mint for a blue green yellow mix. Ducks themselves are friendly and neutral.

How many centerpieces do I need for a duck baby shower?

One per table is standard, plus a larger one for the dessert or gift table. For 24 guests at three tables, make four.

Do you have a pink duck version?

Yes, our pink rubber ducky set uses the same idea with blush, hot pink and yellow.
