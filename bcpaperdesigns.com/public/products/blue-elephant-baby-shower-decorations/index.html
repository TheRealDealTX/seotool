Blue Elephant Baby Shower Decorations

Sky blue, navy and soft grey for a boy elephant baby shower that feels classic and calm.

Plan this party Make the banner

A blue elephant baby shower is one of the easiest themes to pull together for a baby boy: the colors are soft, the shape is instantly recognizable, and it suits every style from rustic to modern. Our elephant baby shower decorations in blue were built around three shades, sky, denim and navy, with grey to keep it gentle.

Everything we made for this boy elephant baby shower was printed on 110 lb cardstock and cut by hand, with layered ears and trunks so the elephants had real depth on the table. You can personalize nearly every piece, whether that means the baby’s name across the banner or the parents’ last name on the door sign.

What goes in the blue elephant baby shower paper set

“Oh Boy” banner

Seven 6-inch pennants in alternating sky and navy, with a small elephant on each end flag, hung across a window or mantel.

Elephant door sign

An 8x10 navy elephant with “Welcome Baby” in cream letters, hung with a 3M hook so the door is not damaged.

Cake topper

A sky-blue elephant holding a tiny navy “B” for baby, 5 inches wide on a single skewer.

Cupcake toppers

Two-inch circles in three alternating designs: elephant, star and the baby’s initial.

Table centerpieces

Double-layer elephants on skewers in white pitchers of blue hydrangea or baby’s breath.

Diaper cake elephant

A paper elephant head and trunk pinned to the top tier of a three-tier diaper cake.

Water bottle labels

Wraparound 8x2 labels with a row of walking elephants.

Favor tags

Round 2.5-inch tags saying “Thanks for coming to my shower” from the baby.

Thank-you cards

Flat 4x6 cards with a navy elephant and space for a photo-style note.

Three blues without clashing

The trick to elephant baby shower decorations for a boy is limiting blue to three tones and repeating them in the same order everywhere. Use navy for anything with words (it reads from across a room), sky blue for large surfaces like tablecloths and balloons, and denim for accents. Grey and cream fill the gaps.

Hang the banner first, then build the dessert table underneath. A burlap or white runner, two cake stands and the elephant centerpiece in the middle make a balanced vignette.

Make it yourself: diaper cake elephant

A diaper cake doubles as a centerpiece and a gift. This version gets a paper elephant face on top.

  1. Roll 40 to 50 size 1 diapers and secure each with a small rubber band.
  2. Build three tiers around a paper towel tube: 24, 15 and 8 diapers.
  3. Wrap each tier with 1.5-inch navy ribbon and secure with a pin.
  4. Cut an elephant head from grey cardstock and blue ears from sky cardstock; glue the ears behind the head.
  5. Mount the head on a skewer and push it into the top tier.
  6. Tuck a small stuffed elephant or a pacifier into the bottom tier.

Planning the timeline

Mail invitations five weeks out, finish paper pieces the week before, and assemble the diaper cake two days ahead. Our party planning timeline builds a dated checklist from your shower date.

Blue Elephant Baby Shower FAQ

What colors go with a blue elephant baby shower?

Navy, sky blue and grey are the core. Cream or white keeps it airy, and a touch of mustard or kraft paper can warm it up if your room feels cool.

Is an elephant baby shower only for boys?

No. The elephant baby shower works in pink for girls, yellow and grey for a gender-neutral party, or blue for a baby boy elephant baby shower. The shapes stay the same; only the palette changes.

When should I start making blue elephant decorations?

Give yourself two weekends. Print and cut the large pieces the first weekend, and save cupcake toppers and tags for the second so they stay crisp.
