Pink and Gold Giraffe Baby Shower Decorations

Blush pink, gold and a tall, gentle giraffe for a shower that is sweet and a little glamorous.

Plan this party Make the banner

Giraffe baby shower decorations in pink and gold are soft and pretty with a touch of glamour. A giraffe baby shower feels gentle and a little whimsical, and the long neck gives you a tall shape that fills a dessert table better than almost any other animal.

Our giraffe set was printed on 110 lb cardstock, hand-cut and finished with gold foil-look accents on the spots and lettering. We personalized it with the baby’s name or a phrase like “Look Who’s Growing,” and the same pieces carried over beautifully to a first birthday.

What goes in the pink and gold giraffe baby shower paper set

“Oh Baby” banner

Blush 5x7 pennants with gold letters and a giraffe peeking over each end flag.

Giraffe cake topper

A 7-inch giraffe with a small gold bow, glued double-sided over two skewers so the long neck stays straight.

Cupcake toppers

Two-inch circles alternating giraffe faces and gold spot patterns.

Tall centerpieces

Twelve-inch giraffes on dowels in vases of blush roses and eucalyptus.

Invitations

A 5x7 card with a giraffe and calf under a gold border.

Plates and napkin bands

Gold paper plates with blush napkin bands printed in giraffe spots.

Favor tags

Tags reading “Thanks for sticking your neck out for baby.”

Welcome sign

An 11x14 easel sign with the mom’s name and a giraffe silhouette.

Thank-you cards

Folded A2 cards with a single gold giraffe.

Pink and gold with a safari touch

Use blush as the base color for the tablecloth and balloons, then add gold in small doses: plates, a confetti scatter and the cake topper edge. The giraffe spot pattern works best in just one place, such as the runner or the napkins, so it reads as an accent instead of a busy print.

Height is the giraffe’s gift. Stagger the dessert table with cake stands at three levels and set the tallest centerpiece at the back, so the giraffe looks out over everything like it does on the savanna.

Make it yourself: growth-chart guest book

Guests sign a tall giraffe that later hangs in the nursery as a growth chart.

  1. Buy a 5-foot strip of canvas or heavy kraft paper, about 10 inches wide.
  2. Lightly pencil a tall giraffe running the full length, then paint it in tan with brown spots.
  3. Mark height lines every inch with a gold paint pen, labeling each foot.
  4. Hang it on the wall near the entrance with removable strips.
  5. Ask guests to sign along the neck and add a wish for the baby.
  6. After the shower, mount it in the nursery starting 6 inches off the floor.

Giraffe birthday party ideas

For a giraffe birthday party, swap the banner wording to “Happy Birthday,” add the age to the cake topper and keep everything else. Pink giraffe party decorations suit a first birthday perfectly. Our birthday decorations collection has more animal themes to mix in.

Pink and Gold Giraffe Baby Shower FAQ

What colors go with a giraffe baby shower?

Pink and gold for a girl, or butter yellow, tan and cream for a gender-neutral shower. Brown spots tie any palette back to the giraffe.

Can I use giraffe baby shower decorations for a birthday?

Yes. Change the banner wording and add an age to the cake topper. The centerpieces and plates carry over unchanged.

Do I need giraffe paper plates for a baby shower?

Not necessarily. Plain gold or blush plates with a spotted napkin band look just as themed and are easier to find.
