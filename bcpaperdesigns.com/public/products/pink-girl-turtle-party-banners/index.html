Printing a banner that looks store-bought

Use 65 lb or heavier cardstock if your printer allows it; our original sets were printed on 110 lb cardstock for crisp edges. Set your printer to best quality and do not scale to fit. Cut with a paper trimmer for straight lines, then trim the triangle or swallowtail points with scissors.

A happy birthday banner printable PDF prints one pennant per page so the letters are large. If you want a smaller banner, print two per page at 50 percent.

Stringing and hanging your banner

Punch two holes at the top corners of each pennant, about half an inch in. Thread twine or ribbon front to back to front so the string shows across the top. Leave 18 inches of tail on each end. Tape the ends to the wall with painter’s tape or wrap them around nails or hooks.

Make it yourself: a pink turtle party table

Turn the banner into a full turtle party decorations setup.

  1. Hang the happy birthday banner across the back wall.
  2. Center the age number pennant under it.
  3. Lay a teal tablecloth and a pink tulle runner.
  4. Add the turtle cake on a white stand in the middle.
  5. Surround it with cupcakes, each topped with a turtle or shell.
  6. Tuck a few real shells and a stuffed turtle among the desserts.

Pink Turtle Party Banners and a Happy Birthday Banner Printable FAQ

Where can I get a free happy birthday banner printable PDF?

Use our party banner maker: type Happy Birthday or any message, pick colors and print or save it as a PDF. It is free and works on any home printer.

What paper is best for a printable banner?

Cardstock between 65 and 110 lb holds its shape and keeps ink crisp. Regular copy paper works for a quick banner but tends to curl.

Can I make a thank you banner printable the same way?

Yes. Type THANK YOU in the banner maker and print it in the same colors as your party banner for matching photos.
