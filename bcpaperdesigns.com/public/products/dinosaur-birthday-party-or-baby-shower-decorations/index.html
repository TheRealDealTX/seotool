Dinosaur Birthday Decorations and Dinosaur Baby Shower Ideas

Stomping footprints, a smoking volcano and a herd of friendly dinos for little paleontologists.

Plan this party Make the banner

Dinosaur birthday decorations are pure fun to plan because kids already know the cast: the long neck, the three horns, the plates down the back and the big one with tiny arms. We drew ours as friendly, rounded dinos so they read as cheerful to a two-year-old and still cool to a seven-year-old.

The same set softens easily into dinosaur baby shower decorations. Trade the volcano red for mint and pale blue, hatch a baby dino out of an egg on the cake, and you have a sweet baby dinosaur party that never feels scary.

What goes in the dinosaur birthday decorations and dinosaur baby shower paper set

Dino roar banner

Six-inch scalloped pennants spelling ROAR with a dinosaur between each word, sized to stretch about eight feet across a doorway.

Dinosaur door decorations

A 9x12 sign reading Dinosaur Crossing plus a trail of paper footprints leading from the door to the party table.

Volcano centerpieces

A cone of brown cardstock with orange paper lava, two small dinos on skewers and a few strands of tissue for smoke.

Hatching egg cake topper

A cracked egg cutout with a baby dino peeking out and the child’s name or age underneath.

Cupcake toppers

Two-inch toppers in four dino shapes plus footprints, layered for a little dimension.

Personalized dinosaur birthday banner

A second, smaller banner with the child’s name on bone-shaped letters for the cake table.

Dig site favor tags

Tags for small bags of fossil cookies reading Thanks for digging in.

Fossil food labels

Tent cards shaped like bones for every snack.

Party hats

Cone hats with a row of paper back plates glued down the seam.

Turning the yard into a dig site

Outdoors, a kiddie pool of play sand with buried plastic skeletons becomes the main event. Mark it off with the Dinosaur Crossing sign and a few green paper fronds tucked into planters. Indoors, line the hallway with footprints cut from 110 lb cardstock so they hold up to little feet.

Use plain green plates and napkins and let the paper pieces carry the theme. Dinosaur birthday plates printed with characters can look busy next to handmade toppers, so we keep tableware solid and save the pattern for the banner.

Cake and dessert ideas with prehistoric flair

A green buttercream cake with a crushed-cookie dirt top and the hatching egg topper is simple and striking. Add chocolate rock candies around the base and a couple of toy dinos climbing the side. For a dinosaur baby shower, try a white cake with pastel speckles like an egg.

Make it yourself: a paper volcano centerpiece

Each volcano takes about 15 minutes and looks great on every table.

  1. Cut a quarter circle with a 10-inch radius from brown cardstock and roll it into a cone; glue the seam.
  2. Snip the top inch off to make an open crater.
  3. Cut jagged lava drips from orange and red paper and glue them down from the rim.
  4. Stuff orange and red tissue into the crater so it puffs out like smoke.
  5. Glue two dino cutouts to skewers and tape them inside the base so they stand beside the volcano.
  6. Set it on a green placemat and scatter a few rock candies around it.

Menu and party games

On the menu

  • Dino eggs: deviled eggs or grapes in a nest of shredded wheat
  • Fossil cookies: sugar cookies stamped with toy dino feet before baking
  • Brontosaurus bites: cucumber and cheese on small skewers
  • Volcano dip: guacamole with a salsa crater and tortilla chips
  • Dirt cups: chocolate pudding, cookie crumbs and a gummy dinosaur
  • Swamp juice: green apple punch with lime sherbet

Games and activities

  • Fossil dig: brushes and spoons to uncover skeletons in the sand box
  • Dino egg hunt: plastic eggs with tiny dinos hidden around the yard
  • Stomp relay: kids race in big paper dinosaur feet
  • Pin the tail on the dinosaur: a poster dino and paper tails
  • Dino mask craft: precut masks, crayons and elastic string

Dinosaur Birthday Decorations and Dinosaur Baby Shower FAQ

How far ahead should I make dinosaur birthday decorations?

Start two weeks out for a handmade set. Cut banners and toppers first, then assemble centerpieces the week of the party. Our party planning timeline builds a dated checklist for you.

Can this theme work as dinosaur baby shower decorations?

Absolutely. Use pastel mint, baby blue or blush instead of red and orange, swap the volcano for a nest of eggs and change the banner to say Welcome Baby. Baby shower dinosaur centerpieces look sweet with a single baby dino in an egg.

What are good baby dinosaur party supplies for a first birthday?

Keep it soft: a highchair banner, a smash cake with a hatching egg topper, footprint place cards and a few plush dinos. Avoid small toy pieces within reach of babies.
