Lion King Baby Shower Decorations

A little prince is on the way: blue, gold and a proud lion for a regal boy shower.

Plan this party Make the banner

Lion king baby shower decorations are a natural fit when parents feel this baby is the future ruler of the house. Our version swaps the usual orange and red for a soft royal blue, so it works beautifully as a lion king baby shower for a boy, while gold crowns and a tawny lion keep the savanna feel.

We designed this set around generic lions, crowns and acacia trees, printed on 110 lb cardstock and cut by hand, with a layered mane on the cake topper for real dimension. These ideas are inspired by the popular theme and are not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holder; no characters, logos or lyrics are used.

A royal savanna in blue and gold

Build the look in layers: a royal blue tablecloth, a tan burlap runner for grassland, and gold accents on top. A large paper sun or gold balloon behind the dessert table gives the “rising sun” look that people associate with lion king decorations, without copying any artwork.

Add greenery: potted grasses or a few palm fronds at the corners soften the paper pieces and fill space cheaply.

Make it yourself: paper mane centerpiece

This lion head sits on a small stand and becomes the star of the gift table.

  1. Cut a 7-inch circle from tan cardstock for the face and draw or print simple features.
  2. Cut thirty 1x5-inch strips in gold, tan and brown cardstock.
  3. Curl each strip around a pencil.
  4. Glue the strips around the back of the face, overlapping in two rings.
  5. Add a small gold crown on top and hot glue the lion to a 10-inch dowel.
  6. Stand the dowel in a pot filled with dried beans and topped with moss.

Lion cake toppers and dessert table

A two-tier cake in ivory buttercream with a blue ribbon and a gold-crowned lion on top is all you need. Surround it with paw-print cookies and cupcakes on wooden slices. If you want custom crown cupcake toppers or a different wording, you can reach us at info@bcpaperdesigns.com.

Lion King Baby Shower FAQ

How do I make a lion king baby shower for a boy without characters?

Focus on lions, crowns, acacia trees and a warm sunset palette, then make blue the main color. It reads clearly as the theme without using any copyrighted art.

What should a lion king baby shower cake topper look like?

A simple cub or lion silhouette with a small crown works best. Glue two printed layers over a skewer so it stays upright.

Are lion king baby shower thank you cards necessary?

They are a nice finishing touch. A gold paw print or crown on heavy cardstock matches the decor and keeps the theme going after the party.
