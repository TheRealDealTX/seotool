Pink Turtle Baby Shower Decorations

Slow and steady, she’s on her way: a beachy shower with pink turtles and sea glass blues.

Plan this party Make the banner

Turtle baby shower decorations fit a calm, beachy shower perfectly. A pink baby turtle hatching from the sand, a few sea glass blues and natural textures like driftwood and rope make the room feel relaxed and sweet.

This theme also works for a turtle birthday party. Keep the turtles and palette, change the wording and add a number cake topper. Baby turtle baby shower decorations and first birthday decor share almost every piece.

A beachy turtle shower setup

Lay a natural burlap runner over a white tablecloth and scatter shells and sea glass. Add driftwood and glass jars filled with sand and tea lights. Hang the banner in front of a sheer white curtain for a breezy backdrop.

Pink and teal balloons in a loose garland finish the look. Keep the pink soft so it feels like a sunrise on the beach. On the gift table, a small sign reading Shell-ebrate the Little One and a woven basket for cards keep things tidy.

For a turtle birthday party, add a sandbox or water table outside and set the cupcakes on a driftwood board. The same banner, centerpieces and toppers carry over with only a new message.

Baby shower turtle ideas for the dessert table

A buttercream cake with a sand-colored base, crushed graham crackers and a teal wave on top looks like a shoreline. Add the turtle topper as if she is heading to the water. Chocolate turtle candies and blue rock candy are easy treats.

Make it yourself: paper turtle favor boxes

Little turtle boxes for candy take about 10 minutes each. Print them on heavy 110 lb cardstock so they keep their shape in a favor basket.

  1. Print a pillow box template on pink cardstock.
  2. Cut and score along the curved lines.
  3. Fold and glue the side seam.
  4. Glue a teal hexagon shell pattern to the top.
  5. Add a small turtle head and legs cut from teal paper.
  6. Fill with candy and close the ends.

Pink Turtle Baby Shower FAQ

What colors go with turtle baby shower decorations?

Coral pink, sea glass and sandy beige for a girl. Swap pink for navy for a boy. Teal ties both together.

What are good turtle themed baby shower favors?

Chocolate turtle candies, mini sea salt scrubs or a small succulent in a shell. Tag each with a little turtle.
