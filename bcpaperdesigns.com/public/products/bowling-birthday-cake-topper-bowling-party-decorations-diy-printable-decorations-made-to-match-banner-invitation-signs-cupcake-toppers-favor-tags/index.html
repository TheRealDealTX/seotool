Bowling Birthday Cake Topper and Party Decorations

Strikes, spares and a cake topper that steals the show at the lanes or at home.

Plan this party Make the banner

A bowling birthday cake is the centerpiece of any bowling party, and the right topper makes even a plain sheet cake look custom. Our printable birthday cake topper shows a bowling ball crashing into a set of pins with the child’s name and age in bold retro letters, and it is designed to match the rest of the set.

Bowling parties are often held at the alley, where you get a lane and a table but little else. These printable decorations travel well: a banner, signs, cupcake toppers and favor tags fit into one folder and set up in ten minutes.

Bowling birthday cake ideas

A simple white sheet cake with a red and navy stripe border and the topper in the center is the easiest win. For something bigger, bake a round cake as the ball and stand candy pins made of marshmallows on top. Cake decorations bowling fans love include a lane of black fondant down the middle with mini pins at the end.

If you buy a cake from the store, ask for plain frosting and add the topper and a few cupcake toppers around the edge at home.

Setting up at the alley

Most alleys allow tape on their tables, so bring painter’s tape for the banner. Clip lane signs to the console and set centerpieces at the food table. Pack a small tote with scissors, tape, lighter and candles so nothing is forgotten.

Make it yourself: the printable birthday cake topper

A sturdy topper takes about 20 minutes and holds up for photos.

  1. Print the topper on heavyweight cardstock at 100 percent scale.
  2. Cut it out with small, sharp scissors or a craft knife.
  3. Print a second copy and glue the two back to back for strength.
  4. Hot glue two bamboo skewers to the back, two inches apart.
  5. Trim the skewers so three inches sit in the cake.
  6. Insert the topper just before serving so frosting does not soften the paper.

Bowling Birthday Cake Topper and Party FAQ

Can I use the printable birthday cake topper on cupcakes?

Yes. Print the cupcake size at two inches, or shrink the main topper to 40 percent. Glue each to a toothpick.

Where can I find bowling cupcake toppers free?

Our set is meant to inspire and teach you to make your own. You can design matching circles at home, or use the party banner maker for the letters.

How many favor bags do I need?

One per child plus two spares. For 12 kids, prep 14 bags with candy, a sticker and a tag.
