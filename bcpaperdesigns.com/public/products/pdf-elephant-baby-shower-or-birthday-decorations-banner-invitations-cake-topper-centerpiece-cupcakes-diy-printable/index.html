Printable Elephant Baby Shower Cake Topper and Decorations

Print, cut and assemble a full elephant set at home, from cake topper to thank-you cards.

Plan this party Make the banner

An elephant baby shower cake topper is one of the simplest ways to make a store-bought cake look custom, and it is the piece people ask about most. Our printable elephant files were made for hosts who like to make things themselves: download, print at home or at a copy shop, then cut and assemble. The same files work for a baby shower or a first birthday.

When we made these sets by hand, every piece went onto 110 lb cardstock and was assembled with skewers and hot glue. That approach still matters at home. Heavy paper is the difference between a topper that stands up and one that leans by dessert time.

Cake topper

A 6-inch elephant printed twice, glued back to back over a skewer so it looks finished from every side.

Printing tips for crisp results

Print at 100 percent scale (turn off “fit to page”) so cupcake toppers come out exactly 2 inches. Use the highest quality or “photo” setting and a matte cardstock between 80 lb and 110 lb. If your home printer cannot feed heavy paper, a copy shop can print the files on 100 lb cover stock for very little.

Let ink dry for 20 minutes before cutting, especially on dark grey areas, or you will smudge the edges.

Make it yourself: double-sided elephant cake topper

This is the key piece of any elephant baby shower decorations DIY project, and it takes about 15 minutes.

  1. Print the topper page twice on cardstock, with the second copy mirrored.
  2. Cut both elephants out carefully, using a craft knife inside the trunk curve.
  3. Lay one face down and run a line of hot glue up the center.
  4. Press a 10-inch bamboo skewer into the glue, leaving 4 inches below the feet.
  5. Line up the second elephant on top and press firmly for 30 seconds.
  6. Trim the skewer if needed so the topper sits 2 to 3 inches above the frosting.

Assembling the rest in one evening

Work in batches: cut all circles first, then glue all toothpicks, then all tags. A 1-inch or 2-inch circle punch speeds up cupcake toppers enormously. Banner letters go fastest with a paper trimmer. If you would rather print custom wording, our party banner maker creates matching pennant letters in your colors.

Printable Elephant Baby Shower Cake Topper FAQ

What paper should I use for an elephant baby shower cake topper?

Use 110 lb cardstock if your printer can feed it, or at least 80 lb. Glue two layers back to back so the topper stays rigid.

Are printable elephant decorations good for boys and girls?

Yes. Print in grey with pink accents for a girl, blue for a boy, or yellow for a gender-neutral shower. The elephant baby shower ideas for a boy and a girl use the same templates.

Can I use the printable set for a birthday?

Yes. Change the banner wording and add an age number to the cake topper for an elephant first birthday.
