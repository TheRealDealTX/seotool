Jungle Safari Baby Shower Decorations

Gentle baby animals, golden grasses and soft greens to welcome a new little cub.

Plan this party Make the banner

Safari baby shower decorations are gentler than their birthday cousins: fewer bold stripes, more baby animals and a soft palette of sage, wheat and linen. We drew a sleepy lion cub, a long-lashed giraffe, a little elephant and a round hippo, then set them among pale leaves so the room feels like a quiet morning on the plains.

A jungle safari baby shower suits any gender and any season. For a girl, add blush to the palette and you have pink safari baby shower decorations; for a boy, mix in a dusty blue. Either way, the animals and greenery do the heavy lifting.

What goes in the jungle safari baby shower paper set

Safari baby shower banner

Five-inch pennants with OH BABY spelled between pairs of baby animals, strung on natural jute.

Welcome sign

An 11x14 sign reading A Little Cub Is On The Way with a watercolor lion cub, set on an easel at the entry.

Grass and animal centerpieces

A low basket of faux pampas or dried grass with a giraffe and elephant on 12-inch skewers.

Cake topper

A cub curled on top of the word Baby, or the baby’s name if it has been shared.

Cupcake toppers

Two-inch circles with each baby animal on a sage background.

Hippo diaper raffle tickets

Small tickets guests fill in when they bring a pack of diapers.

Advice cards

Five-by-seven cards titled Advice for the Little One with a giraffe peeking from the corner.

Favor tags

Two-inch round tags reading Thanks for helping our little one grow.

Chair banner

A small Mom To Be pennant set for the guest of honor’s chair.

Soft savanna styling

Start with a linen or cream tablecloth and a runner of dried grasses or eucalyptus. Rattan chargers, wooden beads and a few potted ferns add texture without clutter. Hang the banner over the gift table, which becomes a natural backdrop for photos with the mom-to-be.

Keep animal print to small touches like a napkin band or the cake ribbon. A whole leopard tablecloth can overwhelm the delicate baby animals on the cutouts.

A jungle safari baby shower cake and brunch

A two-tier cake in pale sage buttercream with a ring of gold leaf and the cub topper looks polished in photos. If the shower is midday, a brunch spread of quiches, fruit and pastries is easy to make ahead. Arrange desserts on wooden boards at different heights so the table has a skyline like a stand of acacia trees.

Make it yourself: an animal ears headband for mom

A soft, wearable keepsake for the guest of honor that takes about 20 minutes.

  1. Cut two rounded lion ears from tan felt or 110 lb cardstock, about 2.5 inches tall.
  2. Cut smaller inner ears from wheat-colored paper and glue them on.
  3. Fold a half-inch tab at the base of each ear.
  4. Hot glue the tabs to a plain headband, three inches apart.
  5. Wrap a small strip of ribbon over each base to hide the glue.
  6. Add a tiny paper flower or bow beside one ear if you like.

Safari baby shower games that keep everyone mingling

Pick two or three games and keep them short so guests can chat. Animal-themed games are easy to print and score, and a diaper raffle helps the parents stock up.

Menu and party games

On the menu

  • Acacia fruit skewers: melon, berries and grapes on bamboo picks
  • Lion cub sliders: mini chicken salad sandwiches on golden rolls
  • Watering hole lemonade: sparkling lemonade with cucumber slices
  • Giraffe spot cookies: vanilla cookies with caramel icing spots
  • Savanna grain salad: quinoa, roasted vegetables and herbs
  • Hippo pudding cups: chocolate mousse topped with a candy hippo

Games and activities

  • Baby animal names: match each animal to its baby name, from cub to calf
  • Safari bingo: create a printable card set with our bingo generator
  • Diaper raffle: one ticket per pack, drawing at the end
  • Guess the bump: string around the belly and guests cut their guess
  • Wishes for baby: notes tucked into a small wooden safari jeep

Jungle Safari Baby Shower FAQ

What colors are best for safari baby shower decorations?

Sage, wheat, tan and linen are calm and gender neutral. Add blush for a girl or dusty blue for a boy. Use black only in tiny zebra-stripe details.

How is this different from your jungle safari birthday decor?

The shower version uses baby animals and softer colors. The jungle safari birthday set goes brighter with bold stripes, palm greens and explorer gear for kids.

Can I add rainforest baby shower decorations to this theme?

Yes. Add big monstera leaves, a sloth or toucan cutout and deeper greens. It shifts the mood from open savanna to a leafy rainforest while keeping the same banner and toppers.
