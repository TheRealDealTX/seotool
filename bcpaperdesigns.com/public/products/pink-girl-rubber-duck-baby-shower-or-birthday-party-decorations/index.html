Pink Rubber Ducky Baby Shower Decorations

A little yellow duck, a whole lot of pink and enough bubbles for every guest.

Plan this party Make the banner

Rubber ducky baby shower decorations in pink take the classic bath-time duck and give it a sweet, girly twist. Our set pairs a sunny yellow duck with blush, rose and bubblegum pink, and every cutout is printed on 110 lb cardstock so the bubbles and ducks hold their shape on skewers.

The same rubber duck decorations make an easy first or second birthday. Change the banner to the birthday girl’s name, add a number to the cake topper and the duck party decorations are ready to go again.

Pink bath-time styling

A blush tablecloth, a white runner and clusters of pink and clear balloons give the room a bubbly glow. Tie a few rubber ducks to balloon strings so they appear to float. A shower curtain ring garland, strung with pink ribbon, is a fun nod to bath time across the gift table.

Keep the yellow limited to the ducks themselves. That pop of color against all the pink is what makes the theme work.

Rubber duck birthday party twists

For a rubber duck birthday party, lean into play. Set out a water table with floating ducks, hand out duck-shaped sunglasses and serve a smash cake with a single duck on top. Rubber duck party supplies like bubble machines keep toddlers happily busy.

Make it yourself: floating duck balloons

This quick trick fills the ceiling with ducks for very little money.

  1. Inflate pink and clear helium balloons.
  2. Print duck cutouts at about 4 inches and cut them from cardstock.
  3. Punch a small hole at the top of each duck.
  4. Tie a duck to the bottom of each balloon string, 18 inches below the balloon.
  5. Trim the strings to varying lengths so the ducks bob at different heights.
  6. Weight a few balloons on the tables with small bags of jelly beans.

Pink Rubber Ducky Baby Shower FAQ

What is the difference between rubber duck baby shower decorations and duck baby shower decorations?

They overlap. Rubber duck focuses on the toy and bath time, while a broader duck theme can include real ducklings and ponds. Our pink set is the bath-time version.

How do I make rubber ducky decorations for a birthday?

Swap the shower wording for the child’s name and age, add a number cake topper and pick up a few real ducks for favors. The party banner maker lets you print any message in matching colors.

Can I mix blue into the pink duck set?

A touch of pale aqua makes the pink look like bath water. Keep it to one or two accents.
