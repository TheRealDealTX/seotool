Yellow and Grey Elephant Baby Shower Decorations

Sunshine yellow and soft grey: the gender-neutral elephant shower that works whether or not you know.

Plan this party Make the banner

Yellow and grey elephant baby shower decorations are our answer for parents who are waiting to find out, or who simply do not want pink or blue. Yellow is cheerful and warm, grey grounds it, and the elephant ties the whole thing together. It is a gender-neutral palette that still feels like a clear theme rather than a compromise.

We printed these sets on 110 lb cardstock in two yellows, a pale sunshine and a deeper mustard, so the pieces had contrast on the table. The same elephant shapes from our pink and blue sets carried over, which made it easy for guests to recognize the theme the moment they walked in.

Balancing yellow so it stays soft

Yellow can overwhelm a room fast. We used grey as the main color for tablecloths and backdrops, then layered yellow on top in paper pieces, flowers and balloons. Aim for roughly 60 percent grey and white, 30 percent yellow and 10 percent mustard or charcoal for detail.

Natural light helps. If you can, set the dessert table near a window; yellow paper glows in daylight and looks flat under warm indoor bulbs.

Make it yourself: elephant party favors for baby shower guests

These little boxes hold candy, seeds or a mini candle, and they are the kind of elephant party favors guests actually take home.

  1. Print a 2-inch cube box template on yellow 110 lb cardstock, one per guest.
  2. Score along every fold line with a butter knife and ruler before folding.
  3. Glue a grey elephant face to the front panel and two ears to the sides.
  4. Assemble with a glue stick on the tabs and let them dry flat for an hour.
  5. Fill with lemon drops or yellow jelly beans and close the lid.
  6. Tie a grey twine bow and a 2-inch thank-you tag on top.

Yellow and Grey Elephant Baby Shower FAQ

Is yellow and grey a gender-neutral baby shower theme?

Yes. Yellow and grey reads as gender neutral, which makes it a popular choice for parents waiting to find out or for a joint shower.

How do I mix yellow and grey elephant decorations with other colors?

Add white and one natural texture like kraft paper, wood or greenery. Avoid introducing a second bright color; it competes with the yellow.

Can I add a big banner as the main backdrop?

Absolutely. Our yellow elephant baby shower banner page covers sizes, wording and hanging tips.
