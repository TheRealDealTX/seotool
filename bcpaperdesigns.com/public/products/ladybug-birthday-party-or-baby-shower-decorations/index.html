Ladybug Birthday Party Decorations

Red spots, green leaves and a garden full of daisies for a sweet ladybug birthday or shower.

Plan this party Make the banner

A ladybug birthday party is bright, cheerful and one of the easiest themes to pull together. Red with black spots reads instantly from across a yard, and leaf green and white daisies soften it into a garden look. It is a natural fit for a first or second birthday, and with a little pink it becomes a sweet baby shower.

Our ladybug decorations were printed on 110 lb cardstock, hand-cut and layered with raised spots and slightly lifted wings, so each bug had a bit of shadow on the table. Every set could be personalized, and “Our Little Ladybug Is Turning One” was the wording we printed most often.

What goes in the ladybug birthday party paper set

Happy Birthday banner

Red 5x7 pennants with black spots and white letters, a ladybug flag at each end, hung on green twine.

Leaf name banner

Green leaf-shaped pennants spelling the child’s name for the high chair or the cake table.

Ladybug cake topper

A 5-inch ladybug perched on the birthday number, glued double-sided over two skewers.

Cupcake toppers

Two-inch ladybugs and daisies, twelve per sheet, backed with a second layer to hide the toothpick.

Ladybug birthday party centerpieces

Mason jars of white daisies with a paper ladybug on a skewer and a gingham ribbon on the jar.

Invitations

A 5x7 card with a ladybug crawling onto a leaf: “Fly on over.”

Favor tags

Two-inch round tags reading “Thanks for crawling by,” tied on with red baker’s twine.

Party hats

Red cone hats with black spots and two pipe-cleaner antennae on top.

Door sign

An 8x10 “Welcome to the Garden” sign with a flower border.

A garden party in red and green

Start with a white tablecloth, a green runner or a strip of faux grass, and let the red come from the paper pieces, balloons and fruit. Keep black to the spots and the lettering; too much of it makes the table feel heavy. Small potted daisies at each place setting look fresh and go home with guests as favors.

Outdoors, clip paper ladybugs onto tree branches, the fence and the backs of chairs with mini clothespins. A red-and-white gingham blanket on the lawn becomes the toddler zone.

Make it yourself: paper plate ladybugs

A five-minute craft for little guests. Prep everything before the party so the kids only glue.

  1. Use 7-inch red paper plates, or paint white ones red the night before.
  2. Cut a black half circle for each head and glue it at the top of the plate.
  3. Precut black 1-inch dots, six to eight per child, and set them out in bowls.
  4. Kids glue on spots with a glue stick and add two googly eyes.
  5. Tape on two short black pipe-cleaner antennae.
  6. Draw a black line down the middle with a marker to show the wings.

Ladybug baby shower decorations

For a ladybug shower, bring in petal pink and change the banner to “Our Little Ladybug Is On the Way.” Ladybug baby shower decorations look especially sweet with pink tulle on the gift table, white roses and pink-spotted cupcake toppers. You will find more soft shower ideas in our baby shower decorations collection.

Menu and party games

On the menu

  • Ladybug cupcakes with red frosting and mini chocolate chip spots
  • Strawberry and blueberry fruit skewers in a jar
  • Caprese ladybugs: cherry tomato halves with olive spots on basil
  • Mini sandwiches cut with a flower-shaped cutter
  • Sparkling red berry lemonade with lemon wheels
  • Watermelon wedges where the seeds double as spots

Games and activities

  • Paper plate ladybug craft (see the steps above)
  • Spot counting game for toddlers with numbered ladybugs
  • Garden scavenger hunt for a leaf, a flower and a smooth rock
  • Pin the spots on the ladybug, blindfolded
  • Planting sunflower seeds in small pots to take home

Ladybug Birthday Party FAQ

What age is a ladybug birthday party best for?

Ages one to five get the most out of it. It is a popular first birthday theme and a lovely garden party for preschoolers who like bugs.

Can ladybug decorations work for a baby shower?

Yes. Add pink, white flowers and new banner wording. Ladybug baby shower decorations feel sweet without being fussy.

How much food do I need for a ladybug party?

Plan one and a half cupcakes per child and a handful of fruit skewers per guest. Our party food calculator works it out from your guest count.
