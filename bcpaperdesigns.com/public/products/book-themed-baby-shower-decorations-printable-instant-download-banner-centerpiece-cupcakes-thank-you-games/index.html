Book Themed Baby Shower Decorations

Every guest brings a favorite story, and the baby goes home with a library.

Plan this party Make the banner

A book themed baby shower is one of the most meaningful parties you can throw: instead of a pile of onesies, the new parents go home with a shelf of stories. Ask each guest to bring a favorite children’s book with a short note inside the cover, and the decor follows naturally with alphabet blocks, open pages and bright primary colors.

Our printable baby shower decorations for this theme come as an instant download so you can print as many as you need at home. Print on heavy cardstock for the banner and centerpieces and on regular paper for games and signs.

Setting up the library table

Give the books their own spot near the entrance with a small shelf or crate, the Bring a Book sign and a cup of pens for inscriptions. As guests arrive, the shelf fills up and becomes the main decoration. Office baby shower decorations work the same way: one table, a banner and a stack of books feels festive without taking over a conference room.

Hang the storybook banner above the shelf and add a few paper hot air balloons or stars, nods to classic picture books without using any copyrighted characters.

Book themed baby shower centerpiece ideas

Stack books in heights of two to four, tie them with gingham ribbon and top with a small vase of daisies. Hollowed-out old books can hold succulents. Alphabet blocks in a glass jar make an easy table filler that the family can keep for the nursery.

Make it yourself: the guest ABC book

Guests each draw a page during the shower, and the family gets a one-of-a-kind book.

  1. Print 26 pages on cardstock, each with a large outlined letter at the top.
  2. Set them on a table with colored pencils and a sign explaining the activity.
  3. Each guest picks a letter and draws something that starts with it.
  4. Ask them to sign the bottom of their page.
  5. Punch three holes along the left edge of every page.
  6. Bind with ribbon or binder rings and add a cover with the baby’s name.

Book Themed Baby Shower FAQ

How do I word a book themed baby shower invitation?

Add a simple line such as Please bring a signed copy of your favorite children’s book in place of a card. Mention it on the invitation so guests can shop ahead.

Are these printable baby shower decorations?

Yes. Print at home on 8.5x11 cardstock, cut with scissors or a paper trimmer and assemble with glue dots and skewers. Use heavier 110 lb paper for the banner and centerpieces.

What are some book themed party supplies I should buy?

Primary-color plates and napkins, alphabet blocks, a small crate or shelf for gifts and gingham ribbon. The printables cover the rest.
