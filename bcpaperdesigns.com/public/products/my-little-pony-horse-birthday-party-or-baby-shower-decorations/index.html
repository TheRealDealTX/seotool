Pony and Horse Birthday Party Decorations

Saddle up for pastel ponies, rainbow manes and a horseshoe banner fit for a young rider.

Plan this party Make the banner

Horse birthday party decorations are perfect for kids who love riding, ponies or just galloping around the yard. Our set mixes pastel ponies with flowing rainbow manes and a few classic stable details like horseshoes, hay and rope.

People often search for my little pony when they want a pastel pony party. These ideas are inspired by that theme and are not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holder; we use our own generic ponies. The same set works as horse baby shower decorations, too.

A pastel stable party

Hang the horseshoe banner over the main table and set hay bales as seats outside. Add pastel balloons, gingham napkins and mason jars with wildflowers. A paper fence along the front of the dessert table finishes the look.

For horse birthday decorations with a more classic feel, swap pastels for brown, cream and red gingham. Add a pair of cowboy boots filled with sunflowers, a lasso of rope coiled around the cake stand and a few toy horses grazing between the cupcakes.

Every piece in our original set was printed on heavy 110 lb cardstock and hand-cut, which matters for a pony party because ponies on skewers need to stand tall in hay and buttercream. Personalize the stable sign with any message, from a name to a favorite pony’s name.

Dessert and snack corral

A rainbow layer cake with a pony topper is the showstopper. Serve carrot cupcakes, apple slices and caramel dip so the snacks are themed and simple. Line feed buckets with parchment and use them as serving bowls for popcorn and pretzels, and label each one with a stall-style tent card.

Our party food calculator tells you how many cupcakes and drinks to plan for your guest count.

Make it yourself: stick ponies

Kids ride them during games and take them home. Make one per guest plus a spare, and prep the heads a few days early.

  1. Cut a pony head from two sheets of cardstock, about 10 inches tall.
  2. Glue the pieces together around the top of a dowel.
  3. Cut yarn in pastel colors for the mane and glue along the neck.
  4. Add eyes and a nose with a marker.
  5. Tie a ribbon bridle around the nose.
  6. Write each child’s name on the dowel.

Menu and party games

On the menu

  • Hay bale treats: crispy rice squares with butterscotch
  • Carrot sticks and ranch in clear cups
  • Apple slices with caramel dip
  • Horseshoe sandwiches cut with a cookie cutter
  • Pony punch: pink lemonade
  • Rainbow mane cupcakes with swirled frosting

Games and activities

  • Stick pony races: relay laps around the yard on the homemade ponies
  • Pin the tail on the pony: a big poster pony and yarn tails
  • Horseshoe toss: plastic horseshoes and a stake in the lawn
  • Pony obstacle course: pool noodle jumps and cone weaves
  • Rosette ceremony: every rider earns a ribbon for something different

Pony and Horse Birthday Party FAQ

Can I use these as horse baby shower decorations?

Yes. Change the banner to Baby Shower or Welcome Baby and soften the palette to cream, sage and blush.

Are these official my little pony decorations?

No. These are generic pastel ponies inspired by the theme, not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holder.

What age is a pony party for?

Most kids love it from about age three to nine. Use the party theme finder to compare it with other ideas.
