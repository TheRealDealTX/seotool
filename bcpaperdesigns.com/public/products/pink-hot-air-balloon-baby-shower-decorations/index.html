Pink Hot Air Balloon Baby Shower Decorations

Oh, the places she’ll go: blush balloons, soft clouds and a sky full of wishes.

Plan this party Make the banner

Hot air balloon baby shower decorations feel hopeful and lighthearted, which is exactly the mood most families want for a new baby. Our pink version floats blush and peach balloons against a pale blue sky with puffy white clouds, and each balloon has a little woven basket in warm tan.

These hot air balloon decorations for baby shower spaces rely on height. Hang balloons from the ceiling, set taller centerpieces on the gift table and keep the tables themselves light so the eye drifts up.

What goes in the pink hot air balloon baby shower paper set

Cloud banner

Seven-inch cloud-shaped pennants with OH BABY and tiny balloons between letters, hung on white ribbon.

Hanging paper balloons

Three-dimensional balloons made from eight folded panels, 6 to 10 inches tall, hung on clear thread.

Welcome sign

An 11x14 sign reading Up, Up and Away, Baby Girl Is On Her Way.

Balloon centerpieces

A small woven basket with a 3D paper balloon on a dowel and a cloud of white tulle.

Cake topper

A pink balloon with the baby’s name across the basket.

Cupcake toppers

Two-inch balloons and clouds in blush, peach and white.

Where will she go map cards

Five-by-seven cards for guests to note a place they hope she visits.

Favor tags

Thanks for helping our little one take flight, for macarons or tea packets.

Chair sign

A small Mom To Be cloud for the guest of honor’s chair.

Filling the sky above the party

Hang the paper balloons at three heights over the dessert table and entry. Use fishing line and command hooks, and space them about 18 inches apart. White cotton batting stretched thin makes surprisingly convincing clouds tucked between them.

Real latex balloons in pink and peach, with a small paper basket tied below each, are a quick way to fill a larger room. Bunches of three look fuller than singles.

A dessert table that looks like a sunrise

Arrange desserts in an ombre from peach to pink to white. A tall pink cake with the balloon topper sits in the center, flanked by cake pops on a cloud stand and a jar of cotton candy. Air balloon baby shower ideas like this take little effort but photograph beautifully.

Make it yourself: a 3D paper balloon

These hang from the ceiling or sit on dowels in centerpieces.

  1. Print or trace eight identical balloon shapes on pink and peach cardstock.
  2. Fold each shape in half lengthwise.
  3. Glue the back half of one shape to the front half of the next, lining up edges.
  4. Before closing the last pair, lay a loop of thread along the center fold.
  5. Glue the final halves together so the balloon opens into a round shape.
  6. Glue a small paper basket below and hang from the thread.

Pink Hot Air Balloon Baby Shower FAQ

How do I hang hot air balloon baby shower decorations without damaging walls?

Use removable ceiling hooks and fishing line, or string a thin wire across the room and clip the balloons to it. Paper balloons are light enough that small adhesive hooks hold them easily.

Can I do a hot air balloon baby shower for a boy?

Yes. Swap pink and peach for sky blue, sage and a touch of mustard. The clouds and baskets stay the same.

When should I start making the 3D balloons?

Give yourself a week. Each takes about 15 minutes, and our party planning timeline spreads tasks across the weeks before the shower.
