Monster Birthday Party Decorations

Googly eyes, fuzzy horns and big smiles: the friendliest little monster bash on the block.

Plan this party Make the banner

Monster birthday party decorations are for the kid who loves silly faces and loud colors. Our little monsters are round, fuzzy and friendly, with one eye or three, never scary. The palette is a bright mix of purple, green, blue and orange, so every corner of the room feels like a party.

This theme became one of our most popular for a monster 1st birthday, because babies love bold colors and big eyes. Every piece was printed on 110 lb cardstock, hand-cut and layered, and we personalized banners with the child’s name: “Happy Birthday Little Monster Leo.”

Building a monster bash

Color is the whole point here. Use a different tablecloth on each table, or alternate colored plates and cups on one long table. Hang purple balloons in clusters and stick paper googly eyes on a few of them so they look like they are watching guests.

For a monster 1st birthday, keep a quieter corner for the smash cake: one color backdrop, a single little monster birthday banner and the high chair. It photographs better than a busy wall.

Make it yourself: monster masks

Kids make these at the party and wear them for photos. Set up a craft table with everything precut.

  1. Precut mask shapes from colored 110 lb cardstock, one per child.
  2. Cut eye holes with a craft knife ahead of time.
  3. Set out bowls of googly eyes, paper horns, teeth and pom-poms.
  4. Kids glue on features with glue dots, which dry instantly.
  5. Tape a craft stick to the bottom as a handle.
  6. Take a group photo of all the monsters together.

Monster party decorations on a budget

Googly eyes stuck on anything (cups, napkin rings, the fridge) do a lot of work. Crepe paper streamers twisted in two colors make easy “fur.” Our party banner maker prints colorful letters if you want a different message on the wall.

Monster Birthday Party FAQ

Is a monster theme good for a first birthday?

Yes. Little monster 1st birthday decorations are bright and cheerful and suit both boys and girls. Keep faces round and smiling for a baby party.

What goes on a happy birthday monster banner?

We like “Happy Birthday” with tiny eyes over each letter and a monster flag at each end. Add a second line with the child’s name.

What are easy monster birthday party supplies to make?

Cupcake toppers, masks and a banner are the easiest. Print them on heavy cardstock and add googly eyes.
