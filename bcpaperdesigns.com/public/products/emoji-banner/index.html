Emoji Party Banner and Emoji Party Decorations

Smiles, heart eyes and laughing faces for a birthday that is all good vibes.

Plan this party Make the banner

An emoji party banner turns any room into a giant group chat. Our banner pairs round yellow faces, heart eyes, laughing tears and a cool face with sunglasses, each about six inches across, with bold letters between them.

Emoji themes are perfect for tweens and kids who love expressive faces. It is bright, easy to DIY and needs nothing more than yellow cardstock and a black marker. We printed our original emoji banners on 110 lb cardstock so the circles hang flat, and any message can replace HAPPY BIRTHDAY, from a name to a graduation line.

A room full of smiles

Hang the emoji party banner across the main wall and add clusters of yellow balloons with faces drawn on in black marker. Use yellow plates, blue napkins and red cups for a bright, simple table.

Add a wall of paper faces as a backdrop, the same kind of look as the photo above, for selfies. Fifty circles in three sizes cover a 4x6 foot area, and removable mounting putty keeps the paint safe.

Let the guest of honor pick a signature face. If it is the cool sunglasses face, repeat it on the cake topper, the door sign and the favor tags so the emoji party decorations feel planned rather than random.

Emoji snack ideas

Yellow cupcakes with candy faces, smiley cookies and fruit cups keep the food fun. Label each dish with an emoji tent card that matches its flavor: a spicy face on the chips, heart eyes on the dessert. Round food is your friend here, from pizzas to cookies to mini pancakes, since every circle can become a face.

Make it yourself: the emoji face banner

A banner of hand-drawn faces takes about an hour, and older kids can help draw the expressions.

  1. Trace six-inch circles on yellow cardstock and cut them out.
  2. Draw faces with a black marker; add red hearts and blue tears.
  3. Cut letters from colored cardstock and glue to white circles.
  4. Punch two holes at the top of each circle.
  5. String them on twine in order, alternating faces and letters.
  6. Hang across the wall with painter’s tape.

Emoji Party Banner and Emoji Party FAQ

How do I make an emoji party banner at home?

Cut six-inch yellow circles, draw faces and alternate them with letters. For the letters, our party banner maker prints them in any color.

What age is an emoji party for?

Kids from about six to thirteen love it. It also works for teens with a more pared-back palette.

What other emoji party decorations do I need?

Yellow balloons, a cake topper, cupcake toppers and photo props. Add a backdrop wall of paper faces for photos.
