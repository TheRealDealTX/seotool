Fire Truck Birthday Party Decorations

Sirens, ladders and lots of red: a fire station party every little firefighter will remember.

Plan this party Make the banner

Fire truck birthday party decorations are pure fun: bright red, a flash of yellow and a reason to wear a helmet all afternoon. This theme is a favorite for preschoolers and kindergartners, and it is easy to style because the colors are already so recognizable.

Our fire truck sets were printed on 110 lb cardstock and hand-cut, with ladders, hydrants and Dalmatian spots that layered onto banners and cupcake toppers. We personalized them with the birthday child’s name, like “Station No. 5” for a fifth birthday. That little touch makes fireman decor feel custom rather than store-bought.

What goes in the fire truck birthday party paper set

Truck birthday banner

Red pennants with white letters and a fire truck end flag, 5x7 each.

Fire station sign

An 11x14 sign reading “Station No. [age], Captain [name].”

Fire truck cake topper

A 6-inch side-view engine with ladder, glued double-sided on skewers.

Fire truck cupcake toppers

Two-inch circles with trucks, hydrants and helmets, twelve to a sheet.

Invitations

Fire truck birthday invitations styled as an emergency call: “Alarm! Party at 2 p.m.”

Centerpieces

Red buckets holding a paper hydrant and a toy truck.

Favor tags

Tags reading “Thanks for helping put out my candles.”

Paper helmet bands

Printable red helmet fronts with each child’s name.

Turning the room into a fire station

Use a red tablecloth on the main table and black runners as roads. A ladder from the garage (laid flat on the table or leaned against the wall) makes an instant display shelf for cupcakes. Hang the banner across the front and stack a few red and yellow balloons at each end.

Fire truck printable signs help guests find their way: “Engine Bay” for the food, “Gear Up” for favors and “Fire Drill” for the game area. They are quick to make and kids love reading them.

Fire truck cupcakes and cake

Fire truck cupcakes are simple: red buttercream piped in a swirl, a pinch of yellow sprinkles and a printed topper. For the cake, a sheet cake frosted red with crushed graham crackers as a road and the fire truck cake topper standing up at the back looks great and serves a crowd.

Make it yourself: fire hose ring-toss game

A homemade game that keeps a crowd of four-year-olds busy for 20 minutes.

  1. Cut “flames” from orange and yellow cardstock and tape them to four empty bottles.
  2. Line the bottles up 5 feet from a starting line.
  3. Make rings from 12-inch red pool-noodle pieces taped into circles.
  4. Each child gets three tosses to “put out” a fire.
  5. Hand every player a paper firefighter badge at the end.

Menu and party games

On the menu

  • Hot dogs on a “fire grill” platter
  • Red fruit cups of strawberries and watermelon
  • Fire truck cupcakes with yellow ladders piped in frosting
  • “Fire hydrant” cheese puffs in red cups
  • Pretzel rods labeled “Fire Ladders”
  • Red punch labeled “Fire Hydrant Water”

Games and activities

  • Fire hose ring toss (see the project above)
  • Obstacle course with a crawl tunnel and a slide pole
  • Spray-bottle target practice outside at paper flames
  • Dalmatian spot coloring sheets
  • Visit or call ahead to your local station to ask about a truck drive-by

Fire Truck Birthday Party FAQ

What goes in fire truck birthday supplies?

At minimum, a truck birthday banner, a cake topper, cupcake toppers and invitations. Helmets, red cups and a few toy trucks fill out the table.

When should I send fire truck birthday invitations?

Send them three to four weeks ahead. Our party planning timeline lists every task by date.

Can I make a fireman party for a toddler?

Yes. Keep the games simple, the party to 90 minutes and the decorations at adult height so little hands do not pull them down.
