Kids Halloween Birthday Decorations and Party Ideas

Smiling pumpkins, friendly ghosts and a sprinkle of candy corn for an October birthday.

Plan this party Make the banner

Halloween birthday decorations let October kids have it both ways: a birthday and a costume party in one. Our kids version keeps things friendly, with smiling pumpkins, round little ghosts and a purple bat that looks more cuddly than spooky.

Because many families celebrate close to the holiday, we made the set as instant download printable files. Print, cut and set up the banner, centerpieces and cupcake toppers in an evening, and save the extras for next year.

A spooky-cute party room

Hang the Halloween birthday banner across a window with orange and purple balloons clustered at each end. Drape cheesecloth over a doorway for a soft cobweb look. Battery candles inside jack-o-lanterns light the room safely.

Keep decor at a kid-friendly level of scary. Little guests love ghosts and pumpkins; skeletons and blood can upset younger siblings.

Trick or treat dessert table

A two-tier orange cake with a ghost topper and a fondant pumpkin on the side is classic. Fill apothecary jars with candy corn, gummy worms and chocolate eyeballs, and let kids fill treat bags at the end.

Planning around trick-or-treat night

If the birthday falls within a week of Halloween, hold the party on a weekend afternoon so it never competes with trick-or-treating. Ask guests to come in costume, and tell parents on the invitation that face paint and masks are optional for little ones. A two-hour window from 2 to 4 pm leaves plenty of energy for the evening.

Print every piece at once: banner, toppers, labels, the birthday flags printable and the thank-you cards. One evening at the printer and a paper trimmer and the whole set is ready, which matters in a busy October.

Make it yourself: ghost lanterns

Simple, safe lanterns that glow on the tables and cost almost nothing, since you can save jars from the kitchen all month.

  1. Wrap empty jars in white tissue paper with Mod Podge.
  2. Let them dry for 30 minutes.
  3. Cut ghost eyes and mouths from black cardstock.
  4. Glue the faces onto the jars.
  5. Place a battery tea light inside each jar.
  6. Group three lanterns at different heights on each table.

Kids Halloween Birthday Decorations and Party FAQ

How do I make Halloween birthday decorations not too scary?

Choose round, smiling characters and bright colors like orange, purple and lime. Keep lighting warm, use battery candles instead of flames, and skip realistic gore so younger guests stay comfortable.

When should I send Halloween thank you cards printable?

Within two weeks of the party. Print them the same day as the decorations so they are ready, and add a costume photo of each guest inside the card for a fun keepsake.

Can I make my own Halloween birthday banner?

Yes. Our party banner maker lets you type a message, pick orange and purple, and print the letters.
