Common questions

What are current SBA loan rates?

With the prime rate at 7.00%, the maximum variable rate on an SBA 7(a) loan ranges from 10.00% for loans over $350,000 to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. Fixed-rate 7(a) caps run from 12.00% to 15.00%. SBA 504 loans priced in October 2026 at 6.59% to 6.97% fixed.

Who sets SBA loan rates?

Your lender sets your rate. The SBA only sets the maximum a lender may charge, as prime (or the SBA peg rate) plus a spread that depends on the loan amount. 504 rates are different: the SBA-backed portion is priced at a monthly bond sale.

What is an SBA-approved lender?

Any bank, credit union or other lender that has an agreement with the SBA to make guaranteed loans. Preferred Lenders (PLP) can approve SBA loans in-house, which is usually faster. Use the lender directory to compare lenders.

Do SBA rates change when the prime rate changes?

Variable-rate 7(a) loans reset with prime, usually monthly or quarterly. Prime rose from 6.75% to 7.00% on September 17, 2026, so maximum variable rates rose a quarter point the same day. Fixed 7(a) and 504 rates are locked at closing.
