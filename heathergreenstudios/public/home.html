The journal

Since 2009 this address has belonged to the print studio, the paper dress, the open call and the second-Saturday art walk. The journal keeps those threads going.

We write about how prints are made, how to tell an original from a reproduction, how to hang what you buy and where to see art in Bisbee. Everything here is written for working artists and curious collectors alike.

Old Bisbee
Bisbee, Arizona

A mining camp that became an art town

Tucked into the Mule Mountains near the Mexican border, Bisbee grew rich on copper and turquoise, then reinvented itself as a haven for artists after the mines closed. Today its steep streets are lined with galleries, studios and a monthly evening art walk.

Our guide covers the neighborhoods, the art walk, community art spaces and how to plan a visit.
