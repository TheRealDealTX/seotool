Privacy policy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

What we collect

This site doesn't use advertising trackers. When you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message so we can reply. Our web host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and maintenance.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are read and how visitors find them. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies and receives your IP address, browser details and the pages you visit. We don't use it to identify individuals. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or Google's opt-out add-on.

Fonts

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are requested.

How we use it

Messages are used only to respond to you. We don't sell or share personal information.

Your choices

To ask about or delete any message you've sent us, email info@heathergreenstudios.com.
