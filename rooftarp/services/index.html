Emergency or long-term?

It comes down to the realistic gap between now and the permanent repair.

Under about two weeks — a contractor is booked, materials are in stock, nothing is waiting on an adjuster — a standard tarp correctly anchored is the right answer and the cost-effective one.

Beyond a month — an insurance process, a material lead time, a post-storm backlog, a permit — UV degradation and repeated wind loading become the limiting factor. Installing a lightweight cover in that situation means paying for the job twice, and usually discovering the failure during the storm you needed it for.

We will tell you which situation you are in. If you are not sure, describe the timeline on the phone and we will give you a straight answer.
