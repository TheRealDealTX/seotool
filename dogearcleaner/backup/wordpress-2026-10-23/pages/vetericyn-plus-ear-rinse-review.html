How to Use Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse Properly

Technique matters. Even the best cleaner performs poorly if used incorrectly.

Here’s the basic process.

Step 1: Keep Your Dog Calm

Start somewhere quiet. Avoid rushing.

If your dog hates ear cleaning, use treats before and after. Build positive association slowly.

Step 2: Lift the Ear Flap

Expose the ear canal gently. Don’t force anything.

Look for:

  • Excess wax
  • Redness
  • Swelling
  • Strong odor
  • Dark discharge

If the ear looks severely inflamed, stop and contact your veterinarian.

Step 3: Apply the Rinse

Squeeze enough solution into the ear canal to coat the area without overflowing excessively.

Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse spreads easily because of its thin consistency.

Step 4: Massage the Ear Base

Massage the base of the ear for 20–30 seconds.

This loosens debris deeper inside the canal.

You’ll hear squishing sounds. That’s normal!

Step 5: Let Your Dog Shake

Stand back.

Seriously.

Dogs usually shake immediately afterward, helping remove loosened debris.

Step 6: Wipe the Outer Ear

Use gauze or cotton pads to wipe away residue.

Avoid pushing cotton swabs deep into the canal.

That mistake causes more problems than people realize.

Floppy-Eared Dogs

Dogs with heavy ear flaps trap heat and moisture more easily.

Examples include:

When This Product Is NOT Enough

This part matters.

Some dogs need veterinary treatment. Period.

No over-the-counter rinse should replace professional care when symptoms become serious.

Contact your veterinarian if you notice:

Tips for Better Ear Cleaning Results

Want better outcomes? Focus on habits, not intensity.

Stick to a Routine

Dry Ears After Water Exposure

Use Positive Reinforcement

Reward calm behavior consistently.

Don’t Overclean

More cleaning is not always better.

Excessive cleaning may irritate healthy ears and disrupt normal balance.

1. How often should I use Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse?

It may help clean irritated ears, but it is not a replacement for veterinary treatment of serious infections.

3. Is Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse safe for puppies?

Yes, the gentle non-toxic formula is generally considered safe for puppies when used as directed.

4. Does Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse sting during use?

Most dogs tolerate it well because the formula is alcohol-free and designed for sensitive ears.

5. Can I use Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse after swimming?

Yes, many owners use it after swimming to help flush moisture and debris from the ear canal.

6. Is Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse good for dogs with allergies?

It can help manage wax and debris buildup caused by allergies, although it does not treat the allergies themselves.

7. Does this ear rinse help with bad ear odor?

Yes, routine cleaning with Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse may help reduce mild odor caused by wax and trapped debris.
