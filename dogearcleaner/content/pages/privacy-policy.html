This policy explains what information Dog Ear Cleaner (dogearcleaner.com) collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: when you send a message, we receive your name, email address, topic and message. We use these only to reply to you and to improve our content.
  • Server logs: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of the request. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running.
  • Interactive tools: our symptom checker, schedule calculator, ear cleaner finder and dose calculator run entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us or stored on our servers. If you download a calendar file from the schedule tool, it's created on your device.

We don't run advertising networks, and we don't sell, rent or trade personal information.

Cookies

Dog Ear Cleaner itself does not set tracking cookies. Your browser may store a small amount of data locally (for example, to remember that the mobile menu is open). When you click an Amazon link and land on Amazon, Amazon may set cookies to track the referral for its Associates Program; that is governed by Amazon's privacy notice.

Third-party services

  • Amazon: affiliate links take you to Amazon.com, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: our typefaces are loaded from Google's font servers, which receive your IP address when fonts load.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted by Hostinger, which processes server logs on our behalf.

How long we keep information

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up, and are then deleted. Server logs are kept for a limited period by our host for security purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the UK and EU, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to how it's used. To make a request, use our contact form.

Children's privacy

Dog Ear Cleaner is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
