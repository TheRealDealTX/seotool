About Dog Ear Cleaner

An independent resource for safe, simple dog ear care, written by dog owners for dog owners.

Dog Ear Cleaner exists for one reason: to make dog ear care simple, safe and stress-free. Ears are easy to overlook until your dog is shaking its head at 2 a.m., and most of the advice online is either too technical, too vague or quietly written to sell one product.

Why we built Dog Ear Cleaner

If you've ever searched "how to clean dog ears", you've seen it: conflicting instructions, homemade vinegar recipes that sting, and "reviews" that read like ads. We built Dog Ear Cleaner as the resource we wanted ourselves: clear steps, honest product breakdowns and straight answers about when home care stops and the vet starts.

What you'll find here

  • Dog ear cleaner reviews: in-depth looks at Virbac Epi-Otic, Zymox, Vetericyn Plus, Pet MD and more, plus our best dog ear cleaner roundup.
  • How-to guides: starting with how to clean your dog's ears step by step.
  • Health and prevention: ear infection signs, cleaning routines, floppy-ear breed care and after-bath ear care.
  • Free interactive tools: a symptom checker, cleaning schedule calculator, ear cleaner finder and dose calculator.

How we research products

We want to be precise about this, because many review sites aren't. Dog Ear Cleaner does not run a lab, and we don't claim to have tested every product on a panel of dogs. Every verdict is built from:

  1. The full ingredient list and label directions, from the manufacturer and the product packaging.
  2. Published veterinary guidance on ear cleaning, ear anatomy and which ingredients suit which problems.
  3. Patterns in owner feedback: what large numbers of owners consistently praise or complain about (scent, sting, residue, nozzle design, value).
  4. Everyday usability: bottle size, nozzle, format, and how easy it is to use on a wriggly dog.

We score products on ingredient safety, suitability for their intended use, ease of use and value. If a product has real downsides, we list them, even on our top picks.
