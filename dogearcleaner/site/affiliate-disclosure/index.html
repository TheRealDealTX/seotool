What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a normal link with a tracking code. If you click it and buy something, the retailer knows you came from Dog Ear Cleaner and pays us a small percentage of the sale. You pay the same price you would anyway.

Why we use affiliate links

Researching ear cleaners properly takes time: reading ingredient lists, checking veterinary guidance and keeping recommendations up to date. Affiliate commissions let us do that and keep every guide and tool on Dog Ear Cleaner free, with no paywalls and no sponsored posts.

Our editorial promise

  • We never accept payment for a positive review or a place on a list.
  • Commission rates don't influence our rankings. Products are judged on ingredients, suitability, ease of use and value.
  • We include cons and limitations for every product, including our top picks.
  • Prices and availability change, so we don't print prices. Always check the current price on the retailer's site.

You can read more about our process on the About Dog Ear Cleaner page.
