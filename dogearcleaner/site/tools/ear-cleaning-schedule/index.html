How Often Should You Clean Your Dog's Ears? (Schedule Calculator)

Every dog is different. A Basset Hound that swims needs a very different routine to a German Shepherd that hates water. Answer five questions to get your dog's ear-cleaning schedule and save the dates to your calendar.

1. What are your dog's ears like?
2. How often does your dog swim or get bathed?
3. Does your dog have allergies (itchy paws, skin, seasonal flare-ups)?
4. What do the ears look like right now?
5. Ear infections in the last year?

How often should you clean your dog's ears?

Most dogs with healthy, upright ears only need their ears checked weekly and cleaned every few weeks, or when they look dirty. Dogs with floppy ears, hairy ear canals, allergies or a swimming habit build up moisture and wax faster, so they usually do better with weekly cleaning, plus a drying clean after every swim or bath.

Cleaning too often is a real problem too: it can irritate the canal and strip away its natural protection. The calculator above balances those risks using the factors that matter most.

What the calculator considers

  • Ear shape: floppy and hairy ears trap warmth and moisture. See our guide to floppy-ear breeds.
  • Water exposure: water left in the canal after swims and baths is a classic trigger. Our after-bath care guide covers drying.
  • Allergies: allergic dogs make up a large share of recurring ear infections, and cleaning is part of managing them.
  • Current state and history: a dog that has had repeated infections needs a tighter routine (and a vet's plan).

Turn the schedule into a routine

Pair the schedule with our dog ear cleaning routines and the right cleaner. For most routine schedules, Dog Ear Cleaner recommends a gentle, drying, alcohol-free cleaner such as Virbac Epi-Otic Advanced; for very sensitive ears, Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I clean my dog's ears?

For most healthy dogs, every 2 to 4 weeks or when the ears look dirty. Floppy-eared dogs, swimmers and allergic dogs often need weekly cleaning, plus a drying clean after swimming or bathing.

Can you clean a dog's ears too much?

Yes. Over-cleaning can irritate the ear canal and disrupt its natural balance. Unless a vet tells you otherwise, more than two or three times a week is usually too often for a healthy ear.

Should I clean my dog's ears after every bath?

If water gets into the ears, yes: a drying ear cleaner after the bath helps remove trapped moisture. Placing cotton balls loosely in the ear openings during the bath helps keep water out in the first place.

Do puppies need their ears cleaned?

Puppies need weekly ear checks, mainly to get them used to having their ears handled. Clean only when you see wax or dirt, using a gentle cleaner.

Do dogs with upright ears need ear cleaning?

Less often. Many dogs with upright ears only need a weekly look and an occasional clean. Clean when you see wax build-up or after swimming.
