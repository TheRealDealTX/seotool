How to use the dog ear symptom checker

Look at both ears in good light before you start. Lift each ear flap, sniff, and gently press around the base of the ear to see whether your dog flinches. Then tick every box that applies. The checker weighs red-flag signs (balance problems, facial drooping, bleeding, a swollen ear flap) far above everyday signs like light wax, so a single serious symptom is enough to recommend a same-day vet visit.

What the results mean

  • Home care: normal-looking wax with no pain or smell usually just needs a gentle cleaning. Follow our step-by-step guide to cleaning dog ears.
  • Vet visit this week: odor, discharge, redness or constant scratching suggest otitis externa (an outer ear infection). A vet needs to look down the canal and check a swab under the microscope to tell yeast from bacteria. Don't flush the ear right before the appointment, as it can wash away the evidence.
  • Vet today: head tilt, circling, flicking eyes, facial drooping, bleeding, severe pain or a pillow-like ear flap can mean a middle or inner ear problem, a ruptured eardrum or an aural hematoma. These are not home-care problems.

Why you shouldn't pour cleaner into a painful ear

If the eardrum is damaged, some cleaner ingredients can reach the middle ear and cause harm. That's why every reputable ear cleaner label says to ask a vet first if the ear is inflamed or painful. For a full rundown of warning signs, read ear infection signs every dog owner should know.

Once your vet has cleared the ear, a gentle maintenance cleaner such as Virbac Epi-Otic Advanced or Vetericyn Plus Ear Rinse helps keep it that way.

Frequently asked questions

Can I tell if my dog has an ear infection at home?

You can spot the warning signs at home: odor, discharge, redness, swelling, head shaking and scratching. But only a vet can confirm an infection and tell yeast from bacteria or mites, because that needs an otoscope exam and a look at a swab under the microscope.

Should I clean my dog's ears if I think they're infected?

Not before a vet has examined them. Cleaning can sting an inflamed ear, can be risky if the eardrum is damaged, and can wash away the debris the vet needs to sample.

Is a dog ear infection an emergency?

Most outer ear infections are not emergencies but should be seen within a few days. Head tilt, loss of balance, flicking eyes, facial drooping, bleeding or severe pain should be seen the same day.

What does a yeast ear infection smell like?

Owners often describe yeast as a musty, sweet or bread-like smell with brown, greasy wax. Bacterial infections more often smell foul and produce yellow or green discharge. These are clues, not a diagnosis.

Does this symptom checker replace a vet?

No. It is a free triage aid from Dog Ear Cleaner that helps you decide how quickly to act. When in doubt, call your vet.
