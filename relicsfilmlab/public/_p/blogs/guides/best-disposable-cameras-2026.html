The best disposable cameras of 2026

Disposable cameras are still the easiest way to put film in a hundred hands at once — at weddings, on holiday, or in a kid's backpack. These are the single-use cameras worth buying in 2026, plus how to get them developed.

Our picks

1. Kodak FunSaver (800 speed, with flash)

The classic. 27 exposures of Kodak 800-speed colour film, a reliable flash and the warm, punchy Kodak look. Best all-rounder.

2. Fujifilm QuickSnap Flash 400

Cooler, cleaner colour than the Kodak, with greens and blues that pop. Great for travel and outdoor events.

3. Ilford XP2 Super single-use

Black & white film that develops in standard colour (C-41) chemistry, so any lab can process it. Fantastic for weddings.

4. Ilford HP5 Plus single-use

True black & white with more grit and contrast. Needs a lab that does B&W.

5. Kodak Ektar H35 (reusable half-frame)

Not disposable — but if you'd buy more than three single-use cameras, a cheap reusable camera with a roll of film is better value and less waste.

Tips for better disposable photos

  • Use the flash indoors and in shade — even in daytime.
  • Stay at least 4 feet (1.2 m) from your subject; the lens is fixed-focus.
  • Wind fully after every shot.
  • Avoid leaving cameras in hot cars.

Where to develop a disposable camera

Any C-41 lab can process it — see where to develop film. Some labs ask you to send the whole camera; others want only the film.

Better than disposable: cheap point-and-shoots

A secondhand compact such as the Olympus Trip 35 or Canon Sure Shot Owl costs about the same as three disposables and gives you a sharper lens and better exposures, roll after roll. Browse our point and shoot collection.
