Where to develop film

The Relics lab no longer takes orders — but here's exactly how to get your film developed and scanned well, wherever you are.

Option 1: a mail-in film lab

  1. Place an order on the lab's website and choose develop-only, develop + scan, or prints.
  2. Put your rolls in a zip bag with the order number, then a padded envelope.
  3. Ship with tracking. Scans usually arrive by download link within a week.

Option 2: a local lab

Independent labs and camera stores often offer drop-off. Ask which processes they run in-house (C-41, B&W, E-6) and what scanner they use.

Option 3: develop at home

Black & white is cheap and easy; colour is doable with a kit. Follow our home developing guide.

What to ask a lab

  • Scan resolution and file format (JPEG vs TIFF)
  • Whether negatives are returned
  • Push/pull processing for over- or under-exposed film
  • Turnaround time

FAQ

Where can I get film developed near me?

Search for independent film labs in your area, or use a mail-in lab: pack the rolls in a padded envelope with your order number and ship them.

How much does film developing cost?

Typically $10–$20 per roll for develop and scan in the US, depending on scan resolution.

Can I still get disposable cameras developed?

Yes — any C-41 lab can process disposable cameras; some ask you to send the whole camera.

Can I drop off film at Relics?

Not any more. Relics' Long Beach lab and drop boxes have closed; use a mail-in lab or develop at home.
