Basic camera cleaning

A gentle clean makes a used camera look, feel and work better. Here is the routine we used on every camera at the Relics bench.

What you need

  • A rocket air blower (never canned air inside a camera)
  • A soft lens brush and microfibre cloth
  • Lens cleaning fluid and tissue
  • Cotton swabs and 99% isopropyl alcohol
  • A pencil eraser for battery contacts

Step by step

  1. Outside: wipe the body with a barely damp cloth; use a swab in the crevices.
  2. Film chamber: blow out dust and film chips. Don't touch the shutter curtains.
  3. Lens: blow, brush, then a drop of fluid on tissue — wipe in circles from the centre out.
  4. Viewfinder: clean the eyepiece only; leave the mirror and focusing screen to a pro.
  5. Battery contacts: rub with an eraser, then alcohol on a swab.
  6. Light seals: if the foam is sticky or crumbly, replace it with a seal kit.
