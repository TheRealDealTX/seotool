The Relics signal chain

Our lab paired Noritsu processors — consistent temperature and agitation — with a Fujifilm Frontier SP3000 scanner, loved for its golden skin tones and vivid greens. If you send film to a lab, ask which processor and scanner they use and what scan resolution you'll get.

FAQ

What film services does Relics offer?

Relics ran a professional film lab in Long Beach. Today we publish free guides to developing, scanning and printing film, and recommend the kits to do it at home.

What is the difference between C-41, E-6 and B&W?

C-41 is the process for colour negative film, E-6 for colour slide film, and black & white film uses its own developers. ECN-2 is for motion-picture film.

What scanner did the Relics lab use?

A Fujifilm Frontier SP3000, famous for its warm skin tones, paired with Noritsu processing.
