FAQ

What film should I buy first?

Kodak Gold 200 for sunny days or Kodak UltraMax 400 for everything else — both are affordable and forgiving. For black & white, Ilford HP5 Plus.

Which film does my Polaroid take?

600-series cameras (Sun 600, OneStep 600, Impulse) take Polaroid 600 film; SX-70, OneStep Land and Pronto cameras take SX-70 film. Instax Wide cameras take Instax Wide film.

How should I store film?

Keep it cool and dry — the fridge is ideal. Let it warm to room temperature for an hour before loading.
