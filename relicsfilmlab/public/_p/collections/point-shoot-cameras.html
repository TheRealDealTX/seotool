How to choose a point and shoot film camera

  • Fixed lens or zoom? Fixed-lens compacts like the Olympus Stylus Epic or Nikon L35AF usually have faster, sharper lenses. Zooms are more flexible but darker.
  • Batteries: most 90s compacts use CR123A or CR2 lithium cells — easy to find.
  • Flash override: look for a flash-off mode if you shoot at night.

FAQ

What makes a great point and shoot film camera?

A sharp lens, reliable autofocus, a flash you can switch off and common batteries.

Are point and shoot film cameras worth it?

Yes — they are the easiest way to shoot film, and the best ones produce results to rival SLRs.
