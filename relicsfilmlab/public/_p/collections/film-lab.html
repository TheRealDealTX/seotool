From the Long Beach film lab

For years Relics ran a professional film lab in Long Beach, developing and scanning rolls from across the US. Our film services guide shares what we learned — how to send film to a lab, what to ask for, and how to get lab-quality results at home.

FAQ

Is Relics still a film store in Long Beach?

Relics began as a Long Beach film lab and camera shop. Today Relics Film Lab is an online guide and curated shop: we recommend gear and link to Amazon, and we no longer process film in person.
