Super 8 movie cameras

Super 8 is the warm, flickering look of home movies, music videos and film-school shorts. Kodak still makes Super 8 film, and a good vintage Super 8 camera with a fast XL lens is all you need to start shooting.

What to look for in a Super 8 camera

  • An XL (existing light) lens and shutter for low light.
  • Reliable auto exposure — many cameras read the cartridge's notch for film speed.
  • 24 fps for a smoother, more cinematic look; 18 fps to stretch a cartridge.

FAQ

Is Super 8 film still made?

Yes. Kodak manufactures Vision3 50D, 200T and 500T colour negative and Tri-X black & white Super 8 cartridges.

How long is a Super 8 cartridge?

50 feet — about 3 minutes 20 seconds at 18 fps or 2 minutes 30 seconds at 24 fps.
