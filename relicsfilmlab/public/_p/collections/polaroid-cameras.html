FAQ

Do they still make Polaroid cameras?

Yes — Polaroid makes new cameras and fresh film for both vintage 600 and SX-70 cameras.

Where can I buy Polaroid camera film?

Polaroid 600, SX-70 and i-Type film are sold on Amazon and in camera stores; check which type your camera takes first.
