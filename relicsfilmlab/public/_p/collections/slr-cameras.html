Picking your first 35mm SLR

For learning, we recommend a mechanical or semi-automatic body with a 50mm lens: the Pentax K1000, Canon AE-1, Minolta X-700 or Olympus OM-10. All have huge, affordable lens ecosystems.

FAQ

What is the best 35mm SLR for beginners?

The Pentax K1000, Canon AE-1 Program and Minolta X-700 are the classic choices: simple, robust and well supported.

Do vintage SLRs need batteries?

Most need a small battery for the light meter; fully mechanical cameras like the K1000 and Nikon FM still fire without one.
