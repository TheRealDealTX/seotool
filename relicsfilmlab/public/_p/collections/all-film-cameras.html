Old cameras worth buying today

Some old film cameras are collectors' pieces; most are excellent working tools. If it can still take a film that is made today — 35mm, 120, Polaroid, Instax, Super 8 or 110 — it can still make beautiful pictures. Old movie cameras and reel cameras are covered in our Super 8 collection.

FAQ

Can old film cameras still be used?

Yes, as long as film for their format is still made. 35mm, 120, Polaroid and Super 8 film are all in production.

What is a CLA?

A clean, lube and adjust — a service that restores an old camera's shutter, meter and controls.
