Choosing film

Three things decide which film to load: your camera's format, how much light you'll have, and the look you're after. Faster film (higher ISO) copes with dim light but shows more grain; slower film is finer and more saturated but needs bright conditions.

ISOBest for
50–100Bright sun, landscapes, tripod work
200Sunny days, holidays
400The all-rounder — indoors and out
800+Night, concerts, interiors

FAQ

What film speed should I buy?

ISO 400 is the safest all-rounder. Choose 100–200 for bright sunny days and 800 or higher for indoors and night.

Colour or black & white?

Colour negative is the easiest to get developed anywhere. Black & white is cheap to develop at home and very forgiving.
