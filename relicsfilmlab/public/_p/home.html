Why Relics

A film lab's eye, applied to every listing

Relics started as a Long Beach film lab and camera store. We processed on Noritsu machines, scanned on a Fujifilm Frontier and bench-tested every camera we sold.

That experience is baked into every page: what each camera does brilliantly, which film to load, what tends to fail on a used copy and how to check it the day it arrives. Then we send you to Amazon, where you can compare live listings and buy with its checkout and returns.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Relics Film Lab?

Relics Film Lab is a film photography shop and guide that started as a film lab and camera store in Long Beach, California. We curate film cameras, film and lab gear and link you to the best current listings on Amazon.

Do you sell cameras directly?

No. Every “Shop on Amazon” button takes you to Amazon, where you buy from Amazon or an independent seller with Amazon's checkout, shipping and returns.

Are you still a film lab or camera store in Long Beach?

Relics was born in Long Beach and ran a film lab and camera shop there. Today we operate online and no longer process film or take walk-ins; our film services guide points you to great labs and home-developing kits.

How do returns work?

Returns go through your Amazon account. See returns & exchanges.

Why don't you show prices?

Vintage camera listings change daily, so we let Amazon show the live price, condition and seller rating.

Where should I develop my film?

Read where to develop film or learn to develop film at home.

Long Beach roots

Once the film store in Long Beach. Now everywhere.

For years Relics was the camera store and film lab Long Beach photographers relied on — the place to buy a refurbished camera, drop off a roll or learn to develop. The storefront and drop boxes have closed, but the know-how moved online so film shooters anywhere can use it.

Looking for a film camera shop near Los Angeles? Start with our SLR and point-and-shoot collections, or read where to develop film.
