The lesson plan

  1. Practise loading a reel in daylight with a sacrificial roll, then with your eyes closed.
  2. Load your real film in a changing bag.
  3. Mix developer, stop and fixer; bring everything to 20 °C.
  4. Develop, stop, fix, wash and hang to dry.
  5. Scan or contact-print your negatives.
