What is ECN-2?

ECN-2 is the colour negative process used for motion-picture stocks such as Kodak Vision3 50D, 250D and 500T. Vision3 has a carbon remjet backing that must be removed during processing.

Your options

  • A lab that runs ECN-2 — the best results with proper remjet removal.
  • At home — ECN-2 kits are available; a pre-bath in a borax solution softens remjet so it can be wiped away.
  • C-41 cross-processing — possible but gives colour shifts and risks contaminating a lab's machine; only do it at home with remjet removed first.

FAQ

What is ECN-2 film?

Motion-picture colour negative film, such as Kodak Vision3, developed in the ECN-2 process.

Can I develop ECN-2 film in C-41?

It can be done at home after removing the remjet layer, but colours shift and most labs won't accept it in their C-41 machines.
