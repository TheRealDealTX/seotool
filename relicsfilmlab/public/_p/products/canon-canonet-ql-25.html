Why we rate the Canon Canonet QL25

  • Rangefinder focusing is quick and precise, and the quiet leaf shutter is great for street photography.
  • Simple controls — there is very little to go wrong and very little to learn.
  • Affordable enough that you can take it anywhere without worrying.

It is a great fit for first-time film shooters, travellers and anyone who wants a camera that lives in a jacket pocket.

About Canon

Canon's film cameras span everything from the AE-1 and A-1 SLRs that defined the 1970s to the Sure Shot compacts that filled family albums in the 80s and 90s. FD-mount SLRs are mechanical and easy to service; the autofocus EOS bodies take the same EF lenses Canon still supports.

Buying a used Canon Canonet QL25: what to check

Most listings on Amazon come from independent sellers, and condition varies. Read the description and seller rating, and when it arrives:

  1. Open the back and look for light-seal foam that is sticky or crumbling.
  2. Make sure the lens extends and retracts smoothly and the lens cover opens fully.
  3. Fire the flash and check that the battery door contacts are free of corrosion.
  4. Look through the lens with the back open: haze, fungus or scratches show up against a bright light.

Film & batteries

It takes standard 35mm film (also called 135 film), which is still widely available in colour and black & white from Kodak, Fujifilm, Ilford and others. Check the battery compartment before buying — most cameras of this type use a common lithium, alkaline or silver-oxide cell that is easy to find.
