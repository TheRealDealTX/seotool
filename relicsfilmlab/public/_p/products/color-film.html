FAQ

What is the best 35mm colour film for beginners?

Kodak Gold 200 in good light or Kodak UltraMax 400 for everything else — both are affordable and forgiving.

Does film expire?

Yes. Keep it cool; expired film still works but needs extra exposure — roughly one stop per decade.
