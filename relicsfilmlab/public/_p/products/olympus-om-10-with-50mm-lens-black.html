Why we rate the Olympus OM-10 with 50mm Lens (Black)

  • An interchangeable-lens system with plenty of affordable used lenses to grow into.
  • Simple controls — there is very little to go wrong and very little to learn.
  • Affordable enough that you can take it anywhere without worrying.

It is a great fit for anyone learning photography properly, students and shooters who want full manual control and interchangeable lenses.

About Olympus

Olympus was obsessed with making cameras small. The Trip 35, the Pen half-frames, the OM SLRs and the Stylus/mju compacts were all the smallest of their kind, and their Zuiko lenses are famously sharp.

Buying a used Olympus OM-10 with 50mm Lens (Black): what to check

Most listings on Amazon come from independent sellers, and condition varies. Read the description and seller rating, and when it arrives:

  1. Fire the shutter at every speed — slow speeds should sound clearly longer than fast ones.
  2. Check the mirror box and focusing screen for dust, damage or degraded mirror foam.
  3. Test the light meter against your phone's light-meter app.
  4. Inspect the lens for haze, fungus and an oily or sticky aperture.

FAQ

What film does the Olympus OM-10 with 50mm Lens (Black) take?

It takes standard 35mm film (also called 135 film), which is still widely available in colour and black & white from Kodak, Fujifilm, Ilford and others.

Does the Olympus OM-10 with 50mm Lens (Black) need batteries?

Yes, it uses two LR44/SR44 button cells.

Is the Olympus OM-10 with 50mm Lens (Black) good for beginners?

Yes. A manual-focus SLR is the classic way to learn exposure, and this one is simple to operate. Start with a 400-speed film for flexibility.
