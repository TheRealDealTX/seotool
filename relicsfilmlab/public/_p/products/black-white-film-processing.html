Black & White Film Processing

B&W developing guide

Black & white film is the easiest film to process yourself — no strict temperatures, cheap chemistry and wonderful results. Here is everything you need for black & white film processing at home.

What you need

  • A daylight developing tank with reels
  • A changing bag (or a truly dark room)
  • Developer (Ilford ID-11, Kodak D-76 or HC-110), stop bath, fixer
  • A thermometer, measuring jugs and film clips

Classic developer times at 20 °C

FilmDeveloperTime
Ilford HP5 Plus 400ID-11 stock7.5 min
Kodak Tri-X 400D-76 stock6.75 min
Ilford FP4 Plus 125ID-11 stock8.5 min

Always confirm times against the manufacturer's current data sheet.
