AdviceWritten by film shooters

Three ways to scan film

  1. Dedicated film scanner — simple, consistent and great for 35mm.
  2. Flatbed with film holders — the most affordable way to scan 120 medium format.
  3. Camera scanning — a digital camera, a macro lens, a light panel and a film holder. Fastest and highest quality once set up.

Tips for better scans

  • Clean negatives with a blower before scanning — never wipe them dry.
  • Use a high-CRI light source for accurate colour.
  • Scan in RAW or 16-bit TIFF and invert with dedicated software for the best colour negatives.
