AdviceWritten by film shooters

Choosing a print

  • Measure your wall first — prints look best when they fill around two-thirds of the space above furniture.
  • A matte paper reduces glare; a gloss paper makes colours pop.
  • A simple black or natural-wood frame suits almost every photograph.
