Why it belongs on your shelf

Photography books teach in a way videos can't: you can sit with a picture, study the light and come back to it next year with fresh eyes. We recommend Edward Weston (Aperture Masters of Photography) to anyone who wants to look harder and shoot better.

Buying used photo books

  • Check the edition and whether it is hardcover or paperback.
  • Ask the seller about dust jackets, writing and water damage.
  • Out-of-print titles are often far cheaper used than new — compare a few listings.
