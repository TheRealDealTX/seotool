Why we rate the Hasselblad 503CX with Distagon 60mm

  • A big 120 negative delivers smooth tones and detail no 35mm camera can match.
  • Simple controls — there is very little to go wrong and very little to learn.
  • Affordable enough that you can take it anywhere without worrying.

It is a great fit for portrait, landscape and fine-art photographers who want the most out of every frame.

About Hasselblad

Hasselblad's V-system 6×6 cameras went to the Moon and into almost every fashion studio of the 20th century. Bodies, backs, finders and Zeiss lenses are all interchangeable.

Buying a used Hasselblad 503CX with Distagon 60mm: what to check

Most listings on Amazon come from independent sellers, and condition varies. Read the description and seller rating, and when it arrives:

  1. Check light seals and film-back light traps — medium-format backs often need new seals.
  2. Test the leaf shutter at all speeds and check that the lens and body couple correctly.
  3. Look for haze in the taking and viewing lenses (TLRs) and a bright, clean focusing screen.
  4. Confirm which backs, inserts and prisms are included — they are often sold separately.

Film & batteries

It takes 120 roll film, the medium-format standard still made by Kodak, Ilford and Fujifilm. Check the battery compartment before buying — most cameras of this type use a common lithium, alkaline or silver-oxide cell that is easy to find.
