AdviceWritten by film shooters

Printing film scans

  • Export at 1800 × 1200 pixels or more (300 dpi at 6 × 4 inches).
  • Match the 3:2 ratio of 35mm so nothing gets cropped.
  • Calibrate your screen, or print a test sheet first.

Home printing

Compact dye-sublimation printers make durable 4×6 prints in about a minute and are perfect for parties and travel.
