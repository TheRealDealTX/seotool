AdviceWritten by film shooters

Why we rate the Canon 514XL Super 8 Camera

  • A genuinely fast lens for its class, which means cleaner low-light shots and softer backgrounds.
  • Real film motion with the grain, colour and flicker that digital filters only imitate.
  • Simple controls — there is very little to go wrong and very little to learn.

It is a great fit for filmmakers, music-video directors and couples who want their wedding on real film.

About Canon

Canon's film cameras span everything from the AE-1 and A-1 SLRs that defined the 1970s to the Sure Shot compacts that filled family albums in the 80s and 90s. FD-mount SLRs are mechanical and easy to service; the autofocus EOS bodies take the same EF lenses Canon still supports.

Buying a used Canon 514XL Super 8 Camera: what to check

Most listings on Amazon come from independent sellers, and condition varies. Read the description and seller rating, and when it arrives:

  1. Check that the motor runs smoothly at every frame rate with fresh batteries.
  2. Test the light meter — many Super 8 cameras need the correct battery for accurate exposure.
  3. Look for haze in the zoom lens and check the power zoom works.
  4. Make sure the cartridge door closes firmly and the film gate is clean.

Buying through Amazon means you are covered by its A-to-z Guarantee if a camera doesn't arrive as described.

Film & batteries

It takes Super 8 film cartridges, which Kodak still produces in Vision3 colour negative and Tri-X black & white. Check the battery compartment before buying — most cameras of this type use a common lithium, alkaline or silver-oxide cell that is easy to find.

FAQ

What film does the Canon 514XL Super 8 Camera take?

It takes Super 8 film cartridges, which Kodak still produces in Vision3 colour negative and Tri-X black & white.

Is the Canon 514XL Super 8 Camera good for beginners?

Yes, if you can use a camcorder you can shoot Super 8. Use the built-in meter and keep shots short — a 50ft cartridge lasts about 3 minutes at 18fps.

Where can I get my film developed?

Any lab that processes the format will do — see our guide to where to develop film, or learn to develop film at home.
