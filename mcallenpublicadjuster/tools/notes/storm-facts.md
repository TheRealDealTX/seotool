## Key recent episodes

- April 21, 2023: up to an hour of continuous hail across southern Hidalgo County; reports up to 2.75 in (baseball) near Peñitas and McCook, 2.5 in in Mission, 1.25 in in McAllen. KMFE ASOS gust 62 mph.
- April 28, 2023: severe wind event overnight (Apr 28–29 local); NWS storm surveys found estimated gusts to ~74 kt (~85 mph) in McAllen, shingles >50% off a two-story building on S 29th St, aircraft flipped at McAllen-Miller Intl Airport; damage in Mission, Pharr, San Juan, Edinburg, Weslaco, Donna, Progreso.
- May 8, 2025: supercell with hail up to 3 in reported in north McAllen (Shayland), 2 in near Dove & McColl (Edinburg/McAllen line); microburst estimated ~74 kt in north McAllen near Edinburg line.
- April 20, 2012: softball-size (4.5 in) hail reported at McAllen High School; golf ball to baseball hail for 20+ minutes across McAllen/Mission/Pharr.
- March 29, 2012: 2.75 in hail at Edinburg Municipal Airport area.
- July 23, 2008: Hurricane Dolly — widespread minor to moderate structural damage across the Lower Rio Grande Valley; heavy rainfall/flooding.
- July 25–26, 2020: Hurricane Hanna — landfall Padre Island (Kenedy County) as Cat 1; tropical-storm impacts and major rainfall flooding in Hidalgo County.
- February 14–20, 2021: prolonged freeze; McAllen International reported a low of 21°F; lows in the lower 20s to upper teens across southern Hidalgo County.
