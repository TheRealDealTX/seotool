## Fire Damage & Smoke-Related Losses

Fire damage doesn’t end when the flames are out. Smoke infiltration, soot residue, and heat exposure can impact structural components, electrical systems, HVAC units, and personal or business property—often in ways that aren’t immediately visible.

### Fire-related claims we handle include:

- Structural fire damage

- Smoke and soot contamination

- Water damage from firefighting efforts

- Odor remediation issues

- Partial losses and total losses

We conduct detailed inspections to identify both obvious and hidden damage, then prepare a claim that reflects the full scope of the loss—not just what’s visible at first glance.

## Water Intrusion & Flood-Related Claims

Water damage is one of the most misunderstood—and frequently underpaid—types of insurance claims. Whether from severe weather, plumbing failures, or sudden flooding, water can compromise building materials quickly and lead to long-term issues if not properly addressed.

### We assist with claims involving:

- Storm-related water intrusion

- Burst pipes and plumbing failures

- Roof leaks and drainage issues

- Mold-related secondary damage

- Flood damage where coverage applies

Water losses often involve coverage disputes and exclusions. We carefully analyze your policy to determine what applies and build documentation that supports every eligible portion of the claim.

We also help with:

## Reopening Previously Settled Claims

Many policyholders assume a closed claim is final. In reality, Texas insurance policies often allow claims to be reopened under certain conditions—typically within a defined timeframe.

We review and pursue reopened or supplemental claims for losses that were:

- Underpaid

- Improperly documented

- Settled before full damage was discovered

### A closed claim may still qualify if:

- Additional damage surfaced later

- Repairs revealed hidden issues

- Pricing or scope was incomplete

- The settlement didn’t reflect actual repair costs

In many cases, property owners leave money on the table simply because they were unaware of their rights. We help determine whether additional recovery is possible.

## When Professional Representation Makes Sense

You don’t need a public adjuster for every claim—but when stakes are high, expertise matters.

Consider professional help if:

- Your claim is delayed or disputed

- The settlement offer doesn’t cover repairs

- The insurer keeps requesting more information

- You’re overwhelmed by documentation requirements

- Your claim involves extensive or hidden damage

Early involvement can prevent costly missteps, but we can step in at nearly any point in the process.
