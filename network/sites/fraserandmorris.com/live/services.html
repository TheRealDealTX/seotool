Services

Garden design services, survey to planting

Full garden design, planting plans, build management and aftercare for gardens in London and the countryside.

Service 01

Full garden design

Our complete design service takes a garden from first visit to finished planting plan.

  • Consultation visit and design brief
  • Measured topographical survey
  • Concept layouts and mood boards
  • Final scaled masterplan
  • Construction and setting-out drawings
  • Detailed planting plan and plant schedule

Service 02

Planting design

For new borders, a tired garden or a layout built by someone else. Planting is our speciality.

  • Planting plans for borders or whole gardens
  • Seasonal and year-round schemes
  • Plants sourced from specialist nurseries
  • Plants set out and placed by us on planting day
  • Bulb and meadow planting
  • Container and roof-terrace planting

Service 03

Build management

We work with a small group of trusted landscape contractors and oversee the build.

  • Tender documents and contractor selection
  • Regular site visits during construction
  • Coordination with architects and builders
  • Quality checks on materials and finishes
  • Lighting and irrigation coordination
  • Final snagging

Service 04

Aftercare & consultancy

Gardens change. We help them settle in and keep evolving.

  • Seasonal aftercare visits
  • Editing and replanting borders
  • Garden consultations and advice visits
  • Maintenance schedules for gardeners
  • Planting for events and seasons
  • Advice on existing trees and plants

The process

Five steps to a new garden

Every project is different, but most follow this path from first conversation to planting day.

1

Consultation

We visit, walk the garden with you and talk about how you want to use it.

2

Survey

A measured survey records levels, boundaries, trees, soil and aspect.

3

Concept

Sketch layouts and mood images explore ideas before anything is fixed.

4

Design

A detailed plan, drawings and planting plan, costed and ready to build.

5

Build & plant

We oversee the landscapers and set out every plant ourselves.

Questions

About our services

How does the garden design process work?

It starts with a consultation visit. We then produce a measured survey, a concept design for discussion, a final layout and a detailed planting plan. We can also manage the build with trusted landscapers and set out the planting ourselves.

How long does it take to design a garden?

A small garden can be designed in a few weeks. Larger gardens usually take two to three months from survey to final planting plan, depending on how quickly decisions are made.

Do you build the gardens you design?

We work with a small group of trusted landscape contractors who build our designs, and we oversee the work on site. We set out and place every plant ourselves.

Can you just help with planting?

Yes. Many of our projects are planting-only: new borders, replanting a tired garden or a seasonal planting plan for an existing layout.
