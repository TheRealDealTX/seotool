Crafting with kids, the low-stress way

Lay down a cheap shower curtain or kraft paper, set out only the supplies needed for one project, and let kids lead. The goal is process, not a perfect result — and that's exactly why these projects work for toddlers through tweens.

Always supervise small parts and glue guns, choose washable, non-toxic paints, and keep anything under about 1.75 inches away from children under three.
