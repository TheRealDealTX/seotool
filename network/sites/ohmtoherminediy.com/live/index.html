Good questions

DIY questions moms ask us most

What are easy DIY projects for beginners?

Start with a painted side table, a terracotta pot makeover, a simple farmhouse wreath or mason jar crafts — they're inexpensive, quick and very forgiving.

How can I decorate my home on a budget?

Paint is the biggest bang for your buck, followed by thrifted furniture makeovers, dollar store decor hacks and handmade accents. Plan it with our free makeover budget planner.

Do I need to sand furniture before using chalk paint?

Usually not completely — clean it well and scuff glossy surfaces lightly. Sand and prime only for very slick finishes or stain bleed-through.

What crafts can I do with toddlers?

Process art like painting, play dough, sticker collages and nature crafts are perfect. Always supervise small parts and use washable, non-toxic supplies.
