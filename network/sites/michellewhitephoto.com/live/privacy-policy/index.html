Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Michelle White Photo (“we”, “us”) collects through michellewhitephoto.com and how it is used.

Information you give us

When you send an inquiry through the contact form or by email, we receive the details you choose to share — typically your name, email address, event date, location and message. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and, if you book, to provide our services.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and performance. We do not use advertising trackers.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with service providers who help us run the business (for example, email and gallery hosting), and only as needed to provide our services.

Retention

Inquiry details are kept only as long as needed to respond and, for clients, for as long as required for business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@michellewhitephoto.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
