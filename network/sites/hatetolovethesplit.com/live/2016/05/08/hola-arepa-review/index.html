Hola Arepa Review: From Food Truck to Our Favorite Minneapolis Restaurant

By the Split · May 8, 2016 · 2 min read

  1. Home
  2. Random Thoughts
  3. Hola Arepa Review: From Food Truck to Our Favorite Minneapolis Restaurant

We first found Hola Arepa the way everyone did: chasing a food truck around downtown Minneapolis on a weekday lunch. Now it's a full restaurant on Nicollet Avenue, and it's our go-to whenever we need a night off from the house.

From Hola Arepa food truck to restaurant

The Hola Arepa food truck built a cult following for its arepas: griddled corn cakes split open and stuffed with fillings. The restaurant kept the truck's spirit, bright, loud and friendly, and added a full bar and a bigger menu. If you loved the truck, you'll love the restaurant.

What to order

  • Arepas, obviously. Crispy outside, soft inside, packed with braised meats, beans, cheese and slaw. Get two. Trust us.
  • Something fried to share. Plantains or yuca are perfect with a group.
  • A cocktail. The bar leans tropical and fresh, which helps in a Minnesota winter.

Tips for visiting

  • It gets busy on weekends. Go early or be ready to wait with a drink.
  • The patio is the move in summer.
  • Bring friends so you can try more of the menu.
✦
Our order

Two arepas each, one side of plantains for the table, and one cocktail we always say we'll share and never do.

The verdict: one of the best meals in Minneapolis, and proof that sometimes the best thing you can do for your house is leave it for a few hours.
