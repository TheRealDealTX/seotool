So, what's a split?

Half a flight up, half a flight down, and completely ours.

A split-level is that very 1970s house where you walk in the front door and immediately have to choose: up to the kitchen and bedrooms, or down to the family room. Ours came with popcorn ceilings, three layers of bathroom wallpaper and a mouse problem. We hated it a little.

So we started fixing it, one room at a time, and writing it all down. Honest how-tos, real costs and the things we'd do differently.
