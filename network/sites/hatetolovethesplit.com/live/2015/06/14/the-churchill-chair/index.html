The Churchill Chair (and a Little Jimmy Rig)

By Emelia · June 14, 2015 · 2 min read

  1. Home
  2. Living Room
  3. The Churchill Chair (and a Little Jimmy Rig)

The living room finally has a reading chair. Not just any chair: a secondhand Restoration Hardware Churchill leather chair we found online for a fraction of what it costs new.

Why the Churchill?

The Churchill is Restoration Hardware's take on a classic English library chair: rolled arms, a tight back, nailhead trim and leather that gets better as it gets beat up. It's compact enough for our split-level living room and looks like it has been reading books since 1940.

The jimmy rig

The chair had one problem: a back leg that wobbled. The previous owner's fix was a folded-up coaster. Ours was slightly better, but let's be honest, it's a jimmy rig (or jury rig, if you're picky):

  1. Flipped the chair and found the leg's corner block had come loose.
  2. Scraped out old glue, re-glued the block with wood glue, and clamped it overnight.
  3. Added two new screws through the block into the frame.

No more wobble. The coaster has been retired with honors.

✦
Leather care

Dust weekly, condition twice a year with a leather conditioner, and keep the chair out of direct sun. Scratches on aniline leather often buff out with a warm finger.

Now I just need to read more books. Which, conveniently, is next week's post.
