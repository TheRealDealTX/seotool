LG TV repair in Kolkata

LG makes some of the most popular televisions in Kolkata homes, and a few of its LED models share one very well-known weakness: the backlight. If your LG TV has sound but a black screen, you are in good company, and it is very repairable.

We repair LG LED, NanoCell and LCD televisions across the city, from the backlight and power supply to the mainboard, T-con and webOS software.

Common LG TV faults we repair

Black screen with sound

The classic LG LED fault. LEDs in the strips burn out, often one after another. We replace the full set with quality strips.

Screen goes dark after a few seconds

Picture appears then disappears: the backlight is shutting down to protect itself from a failed LED. Same repair, same result.

TV won't power on

Power supply board faults, from blown fuses and capacitors to failed regulators. Repaired at component level.

webOS slow, apps not loading

Corrupted software or worn storage. We reload the system and repair the eMMC where needed.

Magic Remote not pairing

Pairing, IR and Bluetooth module faults, plus the settings that often cause the problem in the first place.

Lines or half-screen

T-con board and ribbon cable faults are repairable; panel faults are assessed honestly before any spend.

LG models we work on

LG UHD and Full HD LED, NanoCell, older LG LCD televisions, and board-level work on LG OLED models. If you're not sure what you have, the model number is on the sticker on the back of the TV. Send us that and a short description of the fault.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my LG TV have sound but no picture?

On LG LED televisions this is almost always the backlight. One or more LEDs in the strips have failed, which breaks the whole chain. Replacing all the strips is the reliable fix.

Can you repair LG OLED TVs?

We handle power, mainboard, sound and software faults on LG OLED sets. OLED panels have no backlight, so 'no picture' faults on an OLED have different causes, which we diagnose on the bench.

Are you LG's official service centre?

No, we are an independent multi-brand repair centre and not affiliated with LG. For in-warranty sets, contact LG first.
