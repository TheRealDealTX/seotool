Samsung LED TV repair in Naihati

Naihati is a busy junction on the Sealdah main line in North 24 Parganas. We repair Samsung LED and Smart TVs for Naihati, Garifa, Halisahar and the nearby towns.

Samsung sets from the Naihati area most often come to us with dark screens (backlight) and no-power faults after voltage fluctuations.

Samsung TV problems we fix in Naihati

Backlight failure (sound, no picture)

Samsung's direct-lit LED models are known for LED strip failures. We replace the full strip set so one weak strip doesn't take the rest down months later.

Red standby light blinking

A blinking standby LED is the power board or mainboard reporting a fault. We read the pattern and test the rails to find it.

Vertical lines or half screen

Often the T-con board or the panel's ribbon cables; sometimes the panel bonding. We test each before quoting.

Stuck on Samsung logo / boot loop

Corrupted firmware or a failing eMMC chip. A software reload or storage replacement brings Tizen back.
