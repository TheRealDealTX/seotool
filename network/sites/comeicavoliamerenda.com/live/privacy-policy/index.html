Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect very little, and we never sell it.

Last updated October 12, 2026

Who we are

Come i Cavoli a Merenda (comeicavoliamerenda.com) is an independent informational website about earrings and ear piercings. Contact: info@comeicavoliamerenda.com.

What we collect

We do not require accounts. If you use the contact form or email us, we receive the name, email address and message you send, and use them only to reply. Our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested, time) for security and troubleshooting.

Cookies and local storage

We don’t set advertising or tracking cookies. The Earring Size Tool saves your screen calibration in your browser’s local storage so you don’t have to repeat it; it never leaves your device. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of delivering them.

Sharing

We don’t sell or rent personal information. We share it only if required by law.

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete any message you sent by emailing info@comeicavoliamerenda.com.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
