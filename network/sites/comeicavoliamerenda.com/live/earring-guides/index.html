Earring Guides

Actual-size charts, gauges, backs and the everyday questions nobody else answers properly.

Earrings are tiny, and the numbers that describe them are confusing: millimeters for stud faces, gauges for posts, carats for stones. These guides turn the numbers into something you can picture, then cover the practical stuff like backs, storage and that smell. Start with the actual-size stud chart or the gauge size chart.
