Potara Earrings: The Dragon Ball Fusion Earrings Explained

Potara earrings explained: how Dragon Ball's fusion earrings work, which ear each fighter wears, and how to find comfortable cosplay versions for everyday.

Pop Culture4 min readUpdated October 17, 2026

Quick answer

  • Potara earrings belong to the Supreme Kais in Dragon Ball and fuse two people who each wear one.
  • One fighter wears an earring on the left ear and the other on the right; Goku and Vegeta's fusion is Vegito.
  • Dragon Ball Super adds a one-hour limit for non-Kais; fusions involving two Kais are permanent.
  • Cosplay pairs are usually a ring with a round bead; clip-ons work if your ears aren't pierced.

Potara earrings are some of the most recognizable earrings in anime. In Dragon Ball, they're a sacred accessory of the Supreme Kais, and when two people each wear one on opposite ears, they fuse into a single, much stronger fighter. Here's how the lore works, the scenes fans remember, and how to wear your own pair without fusing with anyone.

What are Potara earrings?

In the Dragon Ball universe, the Potara are earrings worn by the Kais, the deities who watch over the universes. Every Supreme Kai wears them as standard. Their real power appears when a pair is split between two people: one wears an earring on the right ear, the other on the left, and the two are pulled together and merged.

Visually, they're simple. Each earring is a ring through the lobe with a round bead or orb hanging from it. The color varies between characters and adaptations, and Dragon Ball Super shows different Kais with different colored stones.

How Potara fusion works in the story

Potara fusion is introduced in the Buu arc of Dragon Ball Z. The best-known example is Vegito, the fusion of Goku and Vegeta, who briefly overwhelms Majin Buu. The fusion separates after Vegito is absorbed by Buu, which fans often treat as the reason the original rules seemed permanent but weren't absolute.

Dragon Ball Super later clarified the rules. Here's the simplified version fans usually go by:

Who fusesHow long it lastsExample
Two non-KaisAbout one hour (per Super), sometimes lessVegito in the Future Trunks arc
Two KaisPermanentFused Zamasu in Super
Kai with another person (Z era)Treated as permanentThe Elder Kai and a witch; Shin and Kibito

Kefla, the fusion of Caulifla and Kale in the Tournament of Power, is another fan favorite. Lore discussions get detailed fast, so treat the table as a quick reference rather than the final word.

The "one ear each" rule is great for real-life styling too. Split a pair with a friend, sibling or partner, and you've got the most wholesome matching jewelry on the internet.

Potara earrings vs the Fusion Dance

Dragon Ball has two famous fusion methods, and mixing them up is a common cosplay slip. The Fusion Dance, which Goku teaches Goten and Trunks in the Buu arc, needs two fighters of similar power to perform a synchronized pose. It produces fusions like Gotenks and Gogeta, and it has a strict time limit.

Potara fusion needs no dance at all, just the earrings. That's why Vegito and Kefla wear Potara and Gogeta and Gotenks don't. Fusion Dance characters also share a distinctive vest instead. If you're building a fusion costume, check which method created your character before you buy earrings, because the wrong accessory is the first thing fellow fans will notice.

Potara earrings for cosplay

Most Potara earrings you'll find are fan-made or third-party cosplay pieces, with some licensed merchandise appearing over the years. They're easy to recognize: a metal ring with a round bead, usually yellow, green or another bright color depending on the character.

  • For Supreme Kai cosplay, wear both earrings, one in each ear.
  • For Vegito or Gogeta-adjacent looks, remember Vegito is the Potara fusion; Gogeta comes from the Fusion Dance and doesn't wear Potara.
  • For Zamasu or Kefla, match the bead color to the version you're cosplaying.
  • For couples or duo cosplay, split one pair, left ear and right ear.

Choosing a comfortable pair

Potara beads are larger than everyday jewelry, so weight matters. Acrylic or resin beads stay light. Glass and metal look richer in photos but tug on the lobe over a long day.

  1. Check the closure. Hinged rings or hooks are easier than full hoops that need a gap opened with pliers.
  2. Know the ring size. A lobe ring of roughly 10–14 mm inner diameter sits close like the anime version.
  3. Check the gauge. Standard lobe piercings usually take 20g or 18g; thicker wire may not fit. See our earring gauge guide.
  4. Mind the metal. Cheap cosplay rings may contain nickel. Titanium, niobium or nickel-free steel are gentler.
  5. No piercings? Clip-on or magnetic Potara are common and perfect for a one-day event.

Wearing Potara earrings day to day

A single Potara earring is surprisingly wearable. One bright bead in one lobe, with a plain stud or small hoop in the other ear, is exactly the kind of mismatched pairing we love. It reads as playful and color-blocked, and fellow fans will get the joke immediately.

Smaller interpretations, like a tiny bead on a thin huggie, make the look office-friendly. If you've got multiple lobe piercings, place the Potara in the first hole and keep the rest minimal; our double lobe guide has pairing ideas. And as with any novelty jewelry, wait until new piercings are fully healed before switching in cosplay pieces, and see a doctor if a piercing looks infected.

Frequently asked questions

Which ear do Potara earrings go on?

In the story, each person wears one earring on opposite ears: one on the right, the other on the left. Supreme Kais wear one in each ear.

How long does Potara fusion last?

In Dragon Ball Super, fusion between non-Kais lasts about an hour, while a fusion of two Kais is permanent. Older Z-era fusions involving a Kai were treated as permanent.

Who is the Potara fusion of Goku and Vegeta?

Vegito. Gogeta is a different fusion of the same two characters, created with the Fusion Dance rather than Potara earrings.

Are Potara earrings safe to wear?

Usually, if the ring is a hypoallergenic metal and the bead is lightweight. Avoid wearing them in a fresh piercing and swap the hooks if your ears react.
