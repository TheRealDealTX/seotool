Jason Aldean Earrings: The Low-Key Country Look, Decoded

Jason Aldean earrings explained: how many he usually wears, the small hoop-and-stud style behind his look, and how to copy it with the right size and metal.

Celebrity4 min readUpdated October 17, 2026

Quick answer

  • In most photos Jason Aldean wears small earrings in both ears — so two in total, one per lobe.
  • The style is understated: small hoops or studs rather than anything long or flashy.
  • To get the look, try a huggie hoop around 8–10 mm or a small stud in a dark or silver-tone metal.
  • He has worn earrings for much of his career, so they read as part of his signature rather than a trend.

Jason Aldean earrings are one of those details fans notice once and then can't stop seeing. Under the ball cap and next to the stubble, there's usually a small flash of metal in each ear — and it's a big part of why people search "how many earrings does Jason Aldean wear." The short answer: in most photos, he wears one small earring in each lobe.

What makes the look worth studying isn't the hardware itself, which is simple. It's how well a tiny earring works with a rugged, no-fuss style. Here's what he tends to wear, why it works, and how you can borrow it.

How many earrings does Jason Aldean wear?

Going by the bulk of his concert, red-carpet and promo photos, Aldean typically wears two earrings — one in each earlobe. He doesn't appear to have cartilage piercings or a stacked ear; the look is a classic single lobe piercing on both sides.

The exact pieces vary from photo to photo. Fans point to small hoops in some shots and studs in others, and the earrings have been part of his image since early in his career. Country music outlets that look back at his "then and now" photos tend to note that the earrings are one of the things that never really changed.

The style: small, close-fitting and quietly masculine

The appeal of the Jason Aldean earrings look is restraint. Nothing dangles, nothing is oversized, and nothing competes with the hat. A small earring sitting close to the lobe adds a bit of edge without tipping into "statement jewelry."

  • Size: small — think huggies and little hoops rather than big rings.
  • Placement: standard lobe piercing, centered, on both ears.
  • Finish: silver-tone or darker metals suit the denim-and-black-tee palette best.
  • Symmetry: matching on both sides, which keeps it clean and intentional.

It's the same principle you see across a lot of guys with earrings: a smaller, close-fitting piece looks deliberate and wears well every day.

How to get a similar look

You don't need anything custom to copy this. The trick is getting the size right, since an earring that's even a few millimeters too big shifts the vibe from understated to flashy.

PieceSize to tryWhy it works
Huggie hoop8–10 mm inner diameterHugs the lobe; reads as a small ring
Small seamless hoop10–12 mmSlightly more visible, still low-key
Flat or bezel stud4–5 mmSubtle glint; nothing moves

Tip: most standard lobe earrings are 20g or 18g. If your piercing is older and you've worn heavier jewelry, a thicker 16g huggie can look closer to the solid, chunky feel of a man's earring.

Choosing a metal that lasts

Because this is an everyday look, pick something you can sleep, shower and sweat in. Stainless steel and titanium are durable and budget-friendly. Sterling silver gives the bright silver tone but tarnishes, so it needs an occasional polish. Solid gold or white gold costs more but holds up for years.

If your ears react to cheap jewelry, skip mystery "silver-tone" alloys. Implant-grade titanium and 14k-plus solid gold are the safest bets for sensitive skin.

If you don't have pierced ears yet

Getting both lobes pierced is the most straightforward piercing there is, but it's still worth doing properly. Look for a professional piercer — ideally an APP (Association of Professional Piercers) member — who uses a needle and implant-grade titanium or solid gold starter jewelry. Lobes typically take around 6–8 weeks to heal on the surface, with full healing taking a few months.

Wait until you're healed before switching to snug huggies, and see a doctor if you notice spreading redness, heat, swelling or fever.

Hoops vs. studs: which version suits you?

Since Aldean seems to switch between small hoops and studs, it's worth deciding which version fits your face and routine. Hoops add a little movement and are easier to spot from a distance, which suits stage lights, beards and longer hair. Studs sit flat, stay put under hats and headphones, and are the lowest-maintenance option for work.

A good test is to try both for a week. If you find yourself fiddling with a hoop or catching it on a collar, a stud is probably your everyday piece, with the hoop saved for nights out.

Part of the reason Jason Aldean earrings get so much attention is contrast. Country style is often associated with plain, practical clothing, so a small pair of earrings stands out without feeling out of place. It's a good lesson for anyone easing into earrings: start small, keep it symmetrical, and let the jewelry be one detail rather than the whole outfit.

Frequently asked questions

How many earrings does Jason Aldean wear?

In most photos he wears two — one small earring in each earlobe. The exact style varies between small hoops and studs.

What kind of earrings does Jason Aldean wear?

Small, close-fitting pieces such as huggie hoops or studs, usually in a silver or darker tone. He doesn't tend to wear dangling or oversized earrings.

What size hoop gives the Jason Aldean look?

An 8–10 mm huggie hoop is a good starting point. Go up to 10–12 mm if you want it slightly more visible.

Does Jason Aldean have cartilage piercings?

He's generally photographed with lobe earrings only, not cartilage piercings or a stacked ear.
