Earring Size Tool: See Studs & Gauges at Actual Size

Calibrate your screen with a bank card, then preview any stud size from 2mm to 14mm at true scale, and convert ear gauges to millimeters and inches.

The copper coin is a US penny (19.05 mm) for scale.

Make it truly actual size

Screens differ. Hold a credit card (or any ID-1 card) against the screen and drag until the dashed outline matches it exactly. We remember the setting on this device.

Match a bank card here

How to use it

  1. Hold any standard bank, ID or gift card flat against your screen.
  2. Drag the calibration slider until the dashed outline matches the card edges exactly.
  3. Now slide through stud sizes. What you see is the real diameter of the earring face.

Without calibration we use the standard CSS scale (96 px per inch), which is close on most laptops but can be off on phones and high-density displays.

Ear gauge converter

Gauge numbers run backwards: the bigger the number, the thinner the post. Most lobe earrings are 20g or 18g; cartilage jewelry is usually 16g. Pick a size to see it in millimeters and inches.
