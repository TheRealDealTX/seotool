# San Marcos / Hays County verified fact sheet (researched 2026-10-20)

**Confidence levels:** **High** means I read it on the primary or official source myself. **Med** means it came from an official source but only through a search snippet, or there is a small caveat. **UNVERIFIED** means writers must not use it.

## 1. City of San Marcos building codes

**Adopting ordinance (High).** Ordinance No. 2023-09 adopted the 2021 ICC codes and the 2020 NEC. It passed first reading Jan 17, 2023 and second reading Feb 7, 2023. Source: https://sanmarcostx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3330/Building-Code-Ordinance-02072023-PDF

**Effective date (High).** The city's Codes & Ordinances page says: "As of April 1, 2023, the following codes are in effect." Source: https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/1289/Codes-Ordinances

**Adopted codes (High).** City Code Sec. 14.002, per the ordinance:
- IBC 2021 (as amended; Appendices C, F, I)
- IRC 2021 (as amended; Appendices AH, AO, AR, AS, AU, AW)
- IECC 2021
- IEBC 2021
- IPMC 2021
- IMC 2021
- IPC 2021
- IFGC 2021
- IFC 2021 (as amended)
- ISPSC 2021
- NEC 2020 "as amended or as adopted by the State of Texas"

The same list appears on the city's construction requirements sheet (revised 5-22-2024): https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/9618/City-of-San-Marcos-Construction-Requirements-and-Notes---85-by-11-PDF

**Notable local amendments (High, same ordinance):**
- **Reroofing:** Both the IBC and the IRC amendments add this exemption (IBC 105.2 #14, IRC R105.2 #11): "Roof covering replacement permits are not required unless the weight of the replacement roof is heavier than the existing roof covering."
- **Foundations:** All foundations must be designed by a licensed engineer, who must submit a sealed letter of conformance (IBC 107.11, IRC R106.1.1.1).
- **ETJ projects:** A project in the extraterritorial jurisdiction that gets City water, wastewater or electric service must obtain a building permit (Sec. 101.5.1).

**Newer codes (UNVERIFIED).** I found no evidence that San Marcos has adopted the 2024 I-Codes. The city's Municode page returned 403. Writers should say "2021 codes" and note that this is current per the city website.

- Phone: 512.805.2630

## 3. Hays County (unincorporated areas)

**Development Services (High for the URL).** It handles development, septic (OSSF), food establishment and junkyard permits in unincorporated areas, with applications through MyGovernmentOnline.
- https://www.hayscountytx.gov/595/Development-Permits
- https://www.hayscountytx.gov/permitting

The county site blocked direct fetches, so the details come from search snippets (Med). The phone number 512-393-2150 was found only on a third-party page (Med-low).

**OSSF authority (Med).** Texas Health & Safety Code Ch. 366 and 30 TAC Ch. 285. Source: https://www.hayscountytx.gov/496/Septic-Permits

**Fire code (Med).** Commissioners Court adopted the **2018 International Fire Code** with local amendments on Dec 17, 2019, effective Jan 1, 2020, under Local Gov't Code Ch. 233 Subch. C.
- Scope: new commercial and public buildings, new multifamily, remodels of 50% or more of those, plus subdivision water supply and access-road review.
- Sources: https://www.hayscountytx.gov/895/Prevention-Compliance-Division and https://haysfreepress.com/2020/01/15/commissioners-adopt-countywide-fire-code

**Single-family homes (Med).** A county FAQ says the county does not inspect single-family construction itself. It requires the HB 2833 third-party inspections (foundation, framing/mechanical, final). Source: https://www.hayscountytx.gov/m/faq?cat=46

**Safe statement for writers:** Hays County does not issue residential building permits or enforce a residential building code in unincorporated areas. It does regulate septic, development and floodplain, and applies the fire code to commercial and multifamily work. Avoid stronger claims.

Older Hays declarations also exist: 1970, 1972, 1991, 1997, TS Charley 1998 (DR-1239), and Rita 2005. Hays is **not** in DR-4879 (July 2025 floods) or any other 2025–2026 declaration through the data I pulled.

**Memorial Day flood, May 23–24, 2015 (High).** Source: NWS EWX summary, https://www.weather.gov/media/ewx/wxevents/ewx-20150524.pdf
- The Blanco River at Wimberley rose from about 5 ft at 9pm to about 41 ft by 1am, including 5 ft every 15 minutes from 10:45 to 11:45pm.
- The San Marcos River near San Marcos crested at 43.08 ft at 5:15am on May 24.
- NWS issued Flash Flood Emergencies for Wimberley (11:24pm) and San Marcos (1:25am).
- Rain totaled 6–8 inches widely, with a maximum of 12–13 inches in the Blanco headwaters over 4–6 hours.
- Death toll (13) and homes destroyed (~350) come from news reports, not NWS (Med). Source: https://www.npr.org/2025/07/16/nx-s1-5467719/wimberley-texas-flood-2015

**October 30–31, 2015 flood (High).** Source: https://www.weather.gov/media/ewx/wxevents/ewx-20151031.pdf
- Two-day rainfall: 18.46 inches at 1 WNW Buda, 15.63 inches at San Marcos 5.8 N, and 12.61 inches at San Marcos 2.9 WNW.
- 4 tornadoes were confirmed across south-central Texas, none of them in Hays County.
- The $33.8M HUD CDBG-DR award to San Marcos covered both 2015 floods: https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/1536/Blanco-River-Flood-Mitigation-Project---

**Halloween flood, Oct 30–31, 2013 (High).** Source: https://www.weather.gov/media/ewx/wxevents/ewx-20131031.pdf
- 6–10 inches fell across Hays County, peaking at 12–14 inches from Wimberley to Driftwood.
- 7.50 inches fell at 7 W San Marcos.
- The San Marcos River flooded near Martindale.

**October 17–18, 1998 flood (High).** Source: https://www.weather.gov/ewx/wxevent-1998flood
- NWS estimated "over 30 inches" of rain over a small area south of San Marcos in 36 hours.
- Homes were washed off foundations along the Guadalupe River from Canyon Lake to Seguin.

**Winter Storm Uri, Feb 10–18, 2021 (High).** Source: https://www.weather.gov/media/ewx/wxevents/ewx-20210218.pdf
- Snowfall included 7.0 inches at 5 WNW San Marcos and 5.9 inches at Kyle on Feb 15.

**May 9, 2024 hailstorm (Med–High).** Source: https://www.kut.org/energy-environment/2024-05-10/hail-severe-storm-hays-county-texas-hill-country-weather
- Hail and wind hit San Marcos, smashing windows and toppling trees onto homes.
- NWS reported hail up to about 6 inches in Johnson City (Blanco County), not San Marcos.
- Hays County Judge Ruben Becerra signed a local disaster declaration. This was a local declaration, not a FEMA one.
- NWS story map: https://arcg.is/1DrLHS

**UNVERIFIED events:**
- April 2021 San Marcos hail: KXAN reported roof damage, but the page was blocked, so I could not confirm the date.
- Any 2016 or 2023 hail event specific to San Marcos, Kyle or Buda.
- A January 2024 tornado in Hays County. The NWS Jan 21–25, 2024 story map exists, but I did not confirm a Hays tornado.

## 6. FEMA flood and NFIP

**NFIP participation (High).** The city states it participates in NFIP: https://sanmarcostx.gov/4773/Repetitive-Loss-Area-Analysis

**NFIP community numbers (Med).** City of San Marcos is 485505 and Hays County unincorporated is 480321, per FIRM panels.

**CRS class (UNVERIFIED).** I could not confirm it. The city lists a "CRS update" project: https://sanmarcostx.gov/3021/Projects

**New flood maps (High).** Source: https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/402/FEMA-Flood-Map-Revision-Process
- The revision started in 2016 and the new maps took effect **January 17, 2025**.
- It covered the Blanco River, the San Marcos River, and portions of Sink, Purgatory, Willow Springs and Cottonwood Creeks.

**Floodplain resources (High).**
- Page: https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/398/Property-Insurance

## 7. TWIA

**TWIA counties (High).** The catastrophe area is Aransas, Brazoria, Calhoun, Cameron, Chambers, Galveston, Jefferson, Kenedy, Kleberg, Matagorda, Nueces, Refugio, San Patricio and Willacy, plus part of Harris County (cities east of Highway 146). **Hays County is not included.** Source: https://www.twia.org/about-us/overview/
