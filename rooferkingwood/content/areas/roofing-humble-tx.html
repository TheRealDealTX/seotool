Humble is one of the oldest towns in this corner of Harris County, and its roofs show it. Within a few miles you can go from small frame houses near Main Street to large two-story homes in subdivisions built in the last decade. A roofer in Humble TX has to be comfortable with both, and with the airport-corridor storms that roll through this side of Houston.

Old Town Humble and Older Housing

The streets around Old Town Humble have homes dating back many decades, some built long before modern building codes. On these houses we regularly find:

  • Plank or board decking with gaps that need re-sheathing before new shingles
  • Multiple roof layers left from past re-roofs
  • Low-slope porch and addition roofs that were tied in poorly
  • Undersized attic ventilation that shortens shingle life in Texas heat

Commercial buildings along Main Street and the older business strips often have flat or low-slope roofs, which fall under our commercial roofing work.

Newer Subdivisions Around Humble

East and south of town, much of the growth came in planned subdivisions such as Fall Creek, built mostly from the early 2000s onward. These roofs are architectural shingles on complex layouts, and many are now reaching the age where the first round of replacements begins. Typical issues include cracked pipe boots, nail pops, and shingles that were never sealed down properly. Many of these homes also have long, low-slope sections over patios that collect leaves and hold water near the wall flashing, a detail a careful roofer in Humble TX should check on every visit. Fall Creek and similar communities have active HOAs, so roof color and material changes usually need approval first.

Check before you sign: If you live in a deed-restricted subdivision, ask your HOA about approved shingle colors before you choose a product. It avoids delays once the crew is scheduled.

Storm Patterns a Roofer in Humble TX Sees

Humble sits on flatter, more open ground than Kingwood, especially near the Bush Intercontinental Airport side. Spring storms that build west of Houston often arrive here with hail and straight-line winds. Instead of whole pines landing on houses, we more often see hail bruising, wind-lifted tabs and dented vents and gutters. Repair or Replace?

Many Humble roofs can be repaired: a few replaced shingles, a new pipe boot, fresh flashing at a chimney. When the roof is past 15 to 20 years, has widespread granule loss or has been patched repeatedly, replacement usually makes more sense. As a planning range, architectural shingle replacement in the Houston area runs about $4.50 to $7.50 per square foot installed.
