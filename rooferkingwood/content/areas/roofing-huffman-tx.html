Huffman feels different from Kingwood even though it sits just across Lake Houston. Lots are larger, the roads are quieter and most properties have no HOA at all. Roofing Huffman TX homes means planning for open-water wind on the lake side, big pines on wooded tracts and a mix of shingle and metal roofs on houses, barns and shops.

Lake Houston Shoreline Homes

Homes along the east shore of Lake Houston face west across miles of open water, which is exactly the direction many storms arrive from. Gusts build over the lake and hit the first row of roofs hard. On these homes we most often repair:

  • Ridge caps blown loose or missing
  • Shingle tabs creased or lifted on the lake-facing slope
  • Drip edge and starter strips pulled up along the eaves
  • Loose or damaged flashing around chimneys and dormers

Proper nailing, a sealed starter course and high-wind-rated ridge caps make a measurable difference here. Some lakefront homes also sit on raised foundations, which makes for taller, more exposed roofs.

Rural Acreage Along FM 2100 and Huffman-Cleveland Road

Away from the water, much of Huffman is wooded acreage and small farms north and east of the lake. Homes range from older frame houses and manufactured homes to newer custom builds. With no HOA standing in the way, many owners choose metal roofing for the main house as well as barns and workshops. As a planning range, standing-seam metal runs about $10 to $16 per square foot installed in the Houston area, while architectural shingles run about $4.50 to $7.50.

Metal shop roofs: If an older barn or shop roof leaks at the screws, the panels may be fine. Replacing dried-out fasteners and washers is often enough.

Storms on the East Side of the Lake

Huffman takes the full range of Gulf weather: spring hail, summer thunderstorms and tropical systems from June through November. Hurricane Ike in 2008 and Hurricane Beryl in 2024 both brought down trees and stripped shingles across the area. After a storm, our storm damage roof repair crews can tarp leaks and document damage for your insurer. We can meet the adjuster, but we are not public adjusters.

Roofing Huffman TX Homes: What to Expect

Many Huffman roofs are older than the homes around them suggest, because rural properties tend to stay with the same owner for a long time. We often find roofs past 20 years, multiple layers, or plank decking under the shingles. A tear-off lets us check and replace bad decking before new underlayment goes down. Without an HOA, you can choose color and material freely, though a few subdivisions do have deed restrictions worth checking.

Because many properties sit on septic, well water and long gravel drives, we plan material delivery and dumpster placement with you ahead of time so trucks do not damage soft ground or drain fields. That planning is part of roofing Huffman TX properties the right way.
