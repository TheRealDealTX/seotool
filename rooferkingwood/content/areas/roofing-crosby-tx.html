Open Ground and Storm Exposure

Unlike Kingwood's closed pine canopy, much of Crosby is open pasture with scattered tree lines. Storms moving up from the Gulf or across east Harris County have little to slow them. That makes hail and straight-line wind the main threats, more than falling trees, though large oaks and pines near homes still cause their share of damage. After major storms like Hurricane Beryl, we see:

  • Hail bruising and granule loss on shingle roofs
  • Dented ridge vents, gutters and metal panels
  • Wind-lifted shingles and blown-off ridge caps
  • Loose trim and flashing on barns and shops

Our storm damage roof repair service covers documentation, tarping and repairs. We can meet your adjuster, but we are not public adjusters and never waive deductibles.

Older Country Homes

Many Crosby homes sit on family land and have been there for decades. Their roofs often show it: shingles past 20 years old, patched sections that never quite matched, two layers stacked together, or roofs that were added onto as the house grew. A full tear-off is usually the right call so we can check the decking and correct the tie-ins between old and new sections.

Ventilation is the other common weak spot. Many older Crosby homes have a few small box vents and no soffit intake, so attics bake through long Texas summers and the shingles age faster from underneath. When we replace a roof as your roofer in Crosby TX, we size intake and exhaust ventilation for the attic instead of simply copying what was there. Gutters matter too on open lots, since heavy rain sheets off wide roofs and can wash out soil along the foundation.

Before a replacement: If your home has had additions over the years, point them out during the inspection. Tie-ins between roof sections are one of the most common leak sources we find.

Metal Roofing on Crosby Acreage

Metal is a natural fit for many Crosby properties. Barns and shops already use it, and without an HOA many owners put it on the house too. Standing-seam metal runs roughly $10 to $16 per square foot installed as a Houston-area planning range, compared with about $4.50 to $7.50 for architectural shingles. See our metal roofing page for the trade-offs.

Newport and Other Subdivisions

Newport, built around its golf course, is Crosby's best-known planned community and has deed restrictions with HOA approval for exterior changes. Homes there are mostly architectural shingle roofs, and many are reaching replacement age. Other smaller subdivisions around Crosby and Barrett Station may have their own rules, so check your paperwork before choosing a color or material.
