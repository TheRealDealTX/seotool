How to use the project finder

Every project on Custom Southern Co. is tagged by season, how long it takes, what it costs and how tricky it is. Choose what matters to you and the list updates instantly, or hit Surprise me to pick at random.

Most of our projects start with something secondhand: a cutting board, an old window, a light globe or a stack of hymnals. Browse the DIY & Repurpose and Seasonal Decor collections for even more ideas.
