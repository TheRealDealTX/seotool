How we decide what gets a report

Any hail at or above quarter size, or wind at or above 58 mph

Those are the National Weather Service's severe thresholds and the point where asphalt shingles start taking damage. When a warning covers Converse, Kirby, Windcrest, Universal City, Live Oak, Schertz, Cibolo, Selma or St. Hedwig, we pull the warning text, spotter reports and outage numbers and publish a report within a day or two.
