Free Insurance Claim Calculators for Wyoming

Seven calculators and planners built from real appraisal work. Check the carrier’s math and decide your next step — no sign-up, and nothing you enter leaves your browser.

Award Net-Gain Projector

Size the disputed amount, test where an award might land and subtract appraiser and umpire fees.ACVCalculator

ACV & Holdback Worksheet

List damaged items with age and life to estimate the first ACV check and the depreciation held back.▲Calculator

Roof Area, Pitch & Waste Calculator

Turn a footprint into true roof squares using pitch and waste, then check the estimate’s numbers.%Calculator

Deductible Comparison Calculator

Convert a percentage wind/hail deductible to dollars and compare it with a flat deductible.◷Planner

Claim Timeline Builder

Hail Test-Square Scorer

Enter hits per test square on each roof facet to see which slopes qualify and how much roof that is.

Using the calculators together

  1. Claim Timeline Builder — Protect your deadlines first
  2. Hail Test-Square Scorer — Score each roof slope after a hailstorm

  4. Deductible Comparison Calculator — See what the deductible takes
  5. ACV & Holdback Worksheet — Split the payment into ACV and holdback
  6. Award Net-Gain Projector — Test whether appraisal pays off
  7. Appraisal Ready-Meter — Score your file before a demand
