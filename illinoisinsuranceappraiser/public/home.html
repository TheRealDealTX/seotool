A missed roof slope, siding that can’t be matched, a finished basement priced as bare concrete, code items nobody listed. An Illinois Insurance Appraiser exists for the gap between “we accept coverage” and a payment that actually covers the repair.

Five steps

How the appraisal clause moves a claim

Appraisal turns an open-ended argument into a defined process with a binding result. Here is how an Illinois Insurance Appraiser takes a file from first call to signed award.

  1. 1

    Free review

    We read the policy, the carrier’s estimate and your photos, then tell you plainly whether appraisal fits.

  2. 2

    Our own valuation

    We inspect, measure and build an independent line-item estimate priced for your Illinois ZIP code.

  3. 3

    Written demand

    Appraisal is demanded in writing and we’re named as your appraiser within the policy’s window.

  4. 4

    Panel work

    We work line by line with the carrier’s appraiser. Most disputes narrow sharply here.

  5. 5

    Signed award

    Items still open go to a neutral umpire. Two of three signatures set the amount of loss.

Questions

Questions people ask an Illinois Insurance Appraiser

Straight answers about how appraisal works, what it costs and when it’s the wrong move.

All questions Claim terms glossary
What does an Illinois Insurance Appraiser do?

We value property damage when you and your insurer agree a loss is covered but disagree on what it costs. As your named appraiser we inspect, build an independent line-item estimate, work with the carrier’s appraiser and, if needed, present unresolved items to a neutral umpire.

How is an appraiser different from a public adjuster?

A public adjuster handles and negotiates the whole claim. An appraiser works inside the policy’s appraisal clause on one question: the amount of loss. We don’t negotiate outside appraisal and we don’t give legal advice.

When should I call an Illinois Insurance Appraiser?

When coverage is accepted, you have the carrier’s estimate, and the gap between it and your contractor’s number is meaningful — usually more than about 10% or several thousand dollars.

Is the appraisal award binding?

On the amount of loss, yes, once any two of the three panel members sign it. That’s why the file should be complete before appraisal is demanded.

Can appraisal get me Section 155 penalties?

No. Section 155 of the Illinois Insurance Code is a remedy a court may consider in a lawsuit. Appraisal only sets the amount of loss. Talk to an Illinois attorney about legal remedies.

What does it cost?

Each side pays its own appraiser and usually half the umpire. We put our fee in writing before any work starts, and the first conversation is free.
