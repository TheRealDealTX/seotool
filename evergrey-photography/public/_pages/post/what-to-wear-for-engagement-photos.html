What to Wear for Engagement Photos: A Vancouver Island Guide

July 30, 20264 min readBy Evergrey Photography

Figuring out what to wear for engagement photos is one of the most common questions we get, and for good reason. Your outfits shape the whole feel of the gallery. On Vancouver Island, where one session might move from a mossy forest to a windy beach, the right choices also need to be practical. Here is how we guide couples before their engagement sessions.

Start with your location

Choose your location first, then dress to complement it. Colours that blend with your setting make images feel cohesive and timeless. Still deciding where to go? Our guide to the best engagement photo locations in Victoria is a good place to start.

Forest sessions

Think Goldstream, Mount Douglas or the trails around East Sooke. Everything around you is green and brown, so earthy tones feel natural:

  • Rust, terracotta, mustard, olive, cream and warm brown
  • Deep burgundy or plum for a moodier autumn feel
  • Avoid bright greens, which can disappear into the background

Beach and coastal sessions

Dallas Road, Witty's Lagoon or a rocky stretch near Sooke call for lighter, airy palettes:

  • Soft blues, sand, white, oatmeal, dusty rose and sage
  • Flowy fabrics that catch the wind look beautiful by the ocean
  • Darker tones like navy or charcoal look striking against a grey winter sea

City and downtown sessions

Victoria's Inner Harbour, brick laneways and heritage architecture can handle a bit more polish:

  • Black, camel, cream, charcoal and jewel tones
  • Tailored coats, blazers and a slightly dressier look
  • A bold colour on one partner can pop against brick and stone

Coordinate, don't match

Pick a palette of three or four colours and dress from it, rather than wearing identical outfits. One partner might wear a cream dress while the other wears a warm brown sweater and olive trousers. You look like a pair without looking like a uniform.

  • Choose one partner to carry the main colour or pattern; let the other stay neutral.
  • Avoid large logos, busy small patterns and neon colours, which pull attention away from you.
  • Keep formality similar. If one person is in a gown, the other should not be in a hoodie.

Add texture

Texture is the secret to outfits that look rich in photos. Knits, linen, wool, denim, suede, lace and flowing chiffon all add depth, especially in softer West Coast light. A chunky sweater or a wool coat also adds warmth and gives you something natural to do with your hands.

Fit matters as much as colour. Clothes that are slightly too big or too tight become more noticeable on camera, especially when you are sitting, hugging or walking. Choose pieces you can move in comfortably, and do a quick test at home: sit down, hold hands, lean in for a hug. If anything pulls or bunches, swap it out.

Plan for wind and weather

Coastal sessions are often breezy. A little wind can be magic, with hair and fabric moving in the light, but it helps to be ready:

  • Bring a warm layer you are happy to be photographed in.
  • Pin or tie back hair if you prefer it controlled, or embrace the movement.
  • Very short or lightweight skirts can be tricky in strong wind; a midi or maxi length is easier.
  • Pack a clear umbrella just in case. Our rainy day photo tips apply to engagement sessions too.

Shoes for the terrain

Many favourite spots involve rocky beaches, roots and uneven trails. Wear sturdy shoes to walk in and bring your nicer pair to change into once we arrive. Clean white sneakers, leather boots and loafers all photograph well. Heels and sand rarely get along.

If you are bringing a dog to your session, pack a towel and treats as well. Wet paws and sandy fur are part of the fun, but you will want to keep your outfits clean for the walk back.

Outfit changes

Most sessions comfortably allow one outfit change. A popular combination is one casual look and one dressier look, or one outfit per location. Wear the outfit that is easiest to change out of first, and bring a bag to keep the second one clean and wrinkle free.

Hair and makeup

You do not need professional hair and makeup for an engagement session, but many couples like to book a trial with their wedding artist on the same day. It is an efficient way to test your wedding look and walk away with photos of it. If you are doing your own makeup, aim for your everyday look with a little extra definition, and skip heavy shimmer or SPF-heavy foundation, which can reflect light. Matte powder is your friend on a warm day. For partners, a fresh haircut a few days before the session, not the day of, tends to look the most natural.

Final touches

  • Iron or steam everything the night before.
  • Bring lip balm, a comb and a small mirror.
  • Wear your engagement ring and get a manicure if you would like ring close-ups.
  • Bring something personal: a dog, a blanket, a thermos of coffee, a favourite book.

Most of all, wear what makes you feel like yourselves. When you are comfortable, it shows. Ready to plan your session? Check the light for your date with our golden hour calculator and get in touch through our contact page.

Frequently asked questions

Should couples match for engagement photos?

Coordinate rather than match. Choose a palette of three or four complementary colours and let one partner carry the main colour or pattern while the other stays neutral.

What colours look best in engagement photos?

It depends on your location. Earthy tones suit forests, light neutrals and soft blues suit beaches, and black, camel and jewel tones suit city sessions.

Can we change outfits during our engagement session?

Yes. Most sessions allow one outfit change, often a casual look and a dressier look, or one outfit per location.

What shoes should I wear for a beach or forest session?

Wear sturdy shoes to walk in and bring a nicer pair to change into. Boots, loafers and clean sneakers all photograph well on uneven ground.
