How Much Does a Wedding Photographer Cost in Victoria, BC?

September 8, 20264 min readBy Evergrey Photography

If you are researching wedding photographer cost in Victoria, BC, you have probably noticed that prices vary a lot. One photographer lists a package at $1,500 and another starts at $5,000, and it is not always obvious why. This guide breaks down typical price ranges, what goes into them and how to find an affordable photographer in Victoria without ending up with photos you regret.

Typical price ranges (estimates)

The ranges below are general estimates for the Victoria and Vancouver Island market, in Canadian dollars, before tax. Individual photographers will fall above and below them.

Type of coverageTypical range (CAD, estimate)What it usually includes
Elopement or micro wedding (1 to 4 hours)About $1,200 to $3,000Ceremony, portraits, a small group of guests, online gallery
Full wedding day (6 to 10 hours)About $3,000 to $6,500+Getting ready through reception, online gallery, sometimes a second photographer
Engagement session (1 to 2 hours)About $350 to $700One or two locations, edited gallery; often discounted when booked with a wedding

Albums, prints, extra hours, a second photographer and travel outside the region are usually priced on top. Want a quick number for your own plans? Our pricing estimator lets you pick coverage hours and add-ons and shows an estimate instantly.

What drives the price

Hours of coverage

The single biggest factor. Eight hours on the day typically means another two to three times that in culling, editing, backing up and delivering images.

Experience and consistency

A photographer who has handled hundreds of different lighting situations, venues and timelines will consistently deliver, whether the ceremony is under bright noon sun or in a dim heritage church. You are paying for that reliability.

Second photographer

A second shooter captures both sides getting ready, guest reactions during the ceremony and alternate angles. It adds cost but can be worth it for larger weddings.

Editing and delivery

Careful, consistent editing takes time. So does a well organized gallery with high resolution downloads and print rights.

Business costs

Professional cameras and backups, insurance, gallery hosting, software, accounting and travel all sit behind the price. A professional photographer in BC also needs to account for GST.

About tax: In British Columbia, the 5% federal GST generally applies to photography services provided by a GST-registered business. Ask whether a quoted price includes tax so you can compare like with like.

How to find an affordable photographer in Victoria without regret

Looking for an affordable photographer in Victoria, BC is completely reasonable. Photography is one budget line among many. The trick is to save money in ways that do not affect the photos you will actually keep.

  1. Reduce hours, not quality. Do you need coverage of the last two hours of dancing? Many couples end coverage after the first dance and a few open floor songs.
  2. Consider a weekday or off-season date. Some photographers offer lower rates from November to March or on weekdays. A misty February elopement on the coast can be breathtaking.
  3. Keep everything close together. Getting ready, ceremony and reception at one venue saves travel time and lets your photographer spend more time photographing.
  4. Skip the album for now. You can always order one later from your digital gallery.
  5. Ask about elopement or micro wedding collections. Short, focused coverage can deliver stunning portraits at a fraction of a full day price.

It also helps to think about value over time. Flowers fade and the cake is gone by midnight, but your photos are what you will hand to your children and look back on at every anniversary. Spending a little more on the right photographer, and a little less elsewhere, is a trade many couples tell us they are glad they made.

Where not to cut corners

Very low prices sometimes mean something important is missing. Before you book, check:

  • Do they shoot with two memory card slots or another form of real-time backup?
  • Is there a written contract that spells out deliverables, timelines and cancellation terms?
  • Can they show you full wedding galleries, not just highlight images?
  • Do they carry liability insurance? Many Victoria venues require it.
  • Do they have a plan if they are sick or injured on the day?

Our article on questions to ask your wedding photographer goes deeper on each of these.

How to compare quotes fairly

Two quotes that look far apart are often closer than they seem once you line them up. When you gather proposals, put them side by side and check the same things for each:

  • Hours on the day. Six hours and nine hours are very different amounts of work.
  • Who is photographing. The lead photographer, an associate, or a team of two.
  • What you receive. An online gallery, high resolution downloads, print rights, an album or prints.
  • Travel. Ceremonies up-island, on the Gulf Islands or in Tofino may involve ferry fares, mileage or accommodation.
  • Tax. Whether GST is included or added on top.

Once everything is on equal footing, the decision usually comes down to whose work moves you most and who you feel comfortable with. That is worth paying a little more for, because the person behind the camera shapes how relaxed you feel all day, and relaxed couples simply photograph better.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Victoria, BC?

As a rough estimate, full-day wedding photography in Victoria typically falls between about $3,000 and $6,500 CAD or more, while elopements and micro weddings often range from about $1,200 to $3,000 CAD. Prices vary by photographer and coverage.

Is GST charged on wedding photography in BC?

Photography services from a GST-registered business generally include the 5% federal GST. BC PST does not usually apply to photography services, but always ask whether a quote includes tax.

How can I find an affordable photographer in Victoria, BC?

Choose fewer hours, consider a weekday or off-season date, keep locations close together and skip extras like albums until later. Avoid cutting corners on backups, contracts and insurance.

Do you offer engagement sessions on their own?

Yes. We photograph engagement and couples sessions on their own or as an add-on to a wedding collection.
