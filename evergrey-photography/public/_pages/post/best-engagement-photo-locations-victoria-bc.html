The 12 Best Engagement Photo Locations in Victoria, BC

September 14, 20264 min readBy Evergrey Photography

Choosing among the engagement photo locations in Victoria, BC is a happy problem. Within a short drive you can stand on an ocean bluff, wander a lantern-lit alley or disappear into rainforest. We have built this list from years of sessions around the city. Each spot comes with notes on the vibe, the best light and how much walking is involved. A quick comparison table sits at the end.

1. Dallas Road and Clover Point

Windswept bluffs, wildflowers in spring and views across the Strait of Juan de Fuca to the Olympic Mountains. It is easy to reach, and the sunsets over the water are wonderful. Bring a layer, because it is almost always breezy.

2. Beacon Hill Park

Ponds, stone bridges, giant trees and open meadows, right next to downtown. Camas blooms paint the meadows blue in spring. Beacon Hill Park offers a lot of variety without much walking.

3. Fisgard Lighthouse and Fort Rodd Hill

The red-and-white lighthouse, historic fortifications and sweeping views of the harbour mouth. This is a Parks Canada site with set hours and admission, and professional photography may need a permit, so check ahead.

4. Oak Bay Waterfront and Willows Beach

A sandy beach, tidy seaside streets and calm water facing east. Mornings here are lovely, with soft light over the water and fewer people.

5. Mount Douglas (PKOLS)

A short drive or a moderate hike takes you to a 360-degree summit view over the Saanich Peninsula, the city and the islands. Sunset up here is spectacular, and the forest trails below are moody and green.

6. Gonzales Hill

A small, rocky hilltop with a historic observatory building and big views over the ocean and Oak Bay. It feels secluded but is only a few minutes from the road.

7. Fan Tan Alley and Chinatown

Canada's narrowest street and the colourful lanes of Canada's oldest Chinatown suit couples who love an urban, editorial feel. Go early in the morning, before the shops open, for an empty alley.

8. The Inner Harbour

The Parliament Buildings, the Empress and boats bobbing in the harbour. Come at blue hour, when the Parliament Buildings' outline lights up, for something truly iconic.

9. Goldstream Provincial Park

Towering cedars, moss, ferns and the river. It is gorgeous in soft rain, and in late autumn the salmon run brings the forest to life. This is a BC Parks site, so check the commercial photography permit requirements.

10. Witty's Lagoon

A forest trail that opens onto a sandy spit, a lagoon and the ocean, all in one session. It is a CRD Regional Park in Metchosin, and the walk in is easy to moderate.

11. Botanical Beach (Port Renfrew)

A sandstone shelf, tide pools and crashing waves, about a two-hour drive from Victoria. Time your visit with a low tide and make a day of it. It is worth every kilometre.

12. Hatley Park

A castle, formal gardens and maples that blaze in autumn. We cover it in depth in our Hatley Castle engagement photos guide.

Planning tips for any Victoria location

  • Check the wind. Victoria's waterfront spots, especially Dallas Road, can be gusty. Pin up long hair or embrace the windswept look, and bring a warm layer.
  • Watch the tides. Beaches like Willows, Witty's Lagoon and Botanical Beach change completely between high and low tide. Low tide gives you more room to roam and reveals tide pools.
  • Think about crowds. Downtown and the Inner Harbour get busy on summer weekends. Weekday evenings and early mornings are far quieter.
  • Allow for travel time. Combining two nearby spots works well. Combining Botanical Beach with downtown in a single session does not.
  • Have a rain plan. Forests like Goldstream look magical in drizzle, and covered spots downtown work in a downpour. A clear umbrella photographs beautifully.

Whatever you choose, the best engagement photos happen when you are comfortable. Pick a place that feels good to walk around in, wear something you can move in (our outfit guide can help), and let us handle the rest.

How to choose

Think about where you feel most like yourselves. If you met hiking, choose the forest. If Sunday mornings mean coffee by the water, choose the beach. Plan around the light with our golden hour calculator, or let our location finder narrow it down. Prefer something cozier? An in-home couple session might be perfect.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most popular engagement photo location in Victoria, BC?

Dallas Road, Beacon Hill Park and Hatley Park are perennial favourites thanks to their easy access, variety and beautiful light.

Do I need a permit for engagement photos in Victoria parks?

Some locations, including many BC Parks, Parks Canada sites and Hatley Park, may require a permit for professional photography. Check with the managing authority before your session.

What time of day is best for engagement photos?

The hour before sunset gives warm, flattering light at most outdoor locations. Early mornings are best for quiet urban spots like Fan Tan Alley.

Can we visit two locations in one session?

Yes, as long as they are close together, such as Hatley Park and Esquimalt Lagoon, or Beacon Hill Park and Dallas Road.
