Victoria wedding photography

Photographs that feel like being there again

Fog rolling off the Strait of Juan de Fuca. Rain-dark cedars in Goldstream. That last orange sliver of sun over Dallas Road. Vancouver Island weddings have a mood all of their own, and we love leaning into it.

Our approach to Victoria wedding photography is mostly documentary: we watch for the glances, the belly laughs and the quiet hands-held moments, then step in with gentle direction when the light is too good to waste. The result is a gallery that looks like your day, not a template.

When couples search for the best wedding photographers in Victoria, BC, they're usually looking for three things — someone calm, someone who knows the local light, and someone whose pricing is clear from the start. That's exactly how we work.

Questions couples ask

Where are you based and where do you travel?

We're based in Victoria, BC and photograph weddings and elopements across Greater Victoria, Sooke, the Saanich Peninsula, the Cowichan Valley, Nanaimo and Tofino. Ferry trips to the Gulf Islands and Vancouver are easy to arrange, and travel further afield is always welcome.

How much does wedding photography cost?

Elopements start at $1,650 and full wedding coverage starts at $2,700 (CAD, plus GST). Our interactive estimator builds a quote from your hours, add-ons and location in about thirty seconds.

How far ahead should we book?

Most couples book 9–14 months out for summer Saturdays. Weekday elopements and shoulder-season dates (April, May, October) often have openings on shorter notice — just ask.

When will we see our photos?

You'll get a sneak peek within a week and the full, hand-edited online gallery within six to eight weeks, with print rights and a private download link.

What happens if it rains?

On the West Coast we plan for it. Clear umbrellas, forest canopies and covered heritage spots make for some of our favourite images — read our rainy day wedding photo tips.
