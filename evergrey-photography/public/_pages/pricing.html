    Pricing questions

    Are you an affordable photographer in Victoria, BC?

    We aim to be clear rather than cheap: elopements from $1,650 and weddings from $2,700, with no hidden fees. Weekday and off-season dates, shorter coverage and skipping the album are all easy ways to bring the total down. Our guide to wedding photographer cost in Victoria, BC explains what drives pricing.

    Do your prices include GST?

    Listed prices are in Canadian dollars before 5% GST. The estimator adds GST so you can see the full number.

    Do you offer payment plans?

    Yes. After the 30% retainer, you can split the balance into monthly payments, as long as it's paid off a month before your date.

    Is travel included?

    Travel anywhere in Greater Victoria, the Saanich Peninsula and the West Shore is included. Further afield we add a flat travel fee, shown in the estimator.

    Can we customise a package?

    Every package can be adjusted. Use the estimator as a starting point and tell us what you have in mind.
