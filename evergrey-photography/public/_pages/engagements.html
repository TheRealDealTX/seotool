Engagements

A session that feels like a date, not a photoshoot

Most couples tell us they're “awkward in photos.” Give us ten minutes.

An engagement session is the easiest way to get comfortable in front of the camera before your wedding — and to come away with photos you'll actually want on the wall. We'll walk, talk, play a few silly prompts and chase the good light. You won't be asked to hold a stiff pose for long.

Favourite spots include the driftwood along Dallas Road, the gardens of Hatley Castle, and the ferns of Goldstream — see our list of the best engagement photo locations in Victoria, BC. Prefer something intimate? An in-home couple session with coffee, records and your dog on the sofa is one of our very favourite things to photograph.

Not sure what to wear? Our engagement outfit guide has palettes for forest, beach and city settings.

Questions couples ask

When should we book our engagement session?

Anywhere from a year to two months before the wedding. If you want the photos for save-the-dates, plan a session at least four months out.

What if it rains?

We'll reschedule if you'd like, or lean in with umbrellas and a forest location. Some of our favourite sessions happened in drizzle.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes please. Bring a helper to take them home partway through if you'd like a few photos of just the two of you.

Do you photograph anniversaries and proposals?

Yes — the same session works beautifully for anniversaries, and we're happy to help plan a surprise proposal.
