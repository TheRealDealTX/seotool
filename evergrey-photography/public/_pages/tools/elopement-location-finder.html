Choosing an elopement location on Vancouver Island

The island packs a remarkable range of scenery into a short drive: old-growth rainforest, cobble and sand beaches, rocky headlands, Garry oak meadows, vineyards and mountain summits. The right spot depends on how you want the day to feel — and on practical things like walking distance, privacy and the season.

Once you have a shortlist, check the light with our golden hour calculator and read the Vancouver Island elopement guide for licences, permits and a sample timeline. Rain on the forecast? Forest canopies are your friend — see how Kasha and Justin made the most of it at Aylard Farm.

Questions

Do we need a permit to elope at these locations?

Many regional, provincial and national parks require a permit for ceremonies or commercial photography, and the rules change. We confirm the current requirements for your chosen spot during planning.

What's the best season for a beach elopement?

Summer brings the warmest, driest weather, but late spring and early autumn offer softer light and quieter beaches. Winter storms are dramatic if you dress for them.

Can our families come?

Yes. Easy-access spots like French Beach, Dallas Road or Hatley Park suit guests with limited mobility; choose 'A few steps' in the quiz.

Are the drive times exact?

They're rough estimates from downtown Victoria without traffic or ferries. Always allow extra time on the Malahat and Highway 14.
