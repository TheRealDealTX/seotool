Why San Antonio Homeowners Hire a Public Adjuster

When you file a claim, your insurance company sends its own adjuster to decide what it owes. That adjuster works for the carrier. A San Antonio public adjuster is licensed by the Texas Department of Insurance to represent only you. We inspect the damage, write a complete estimate, read your policy, and present and negotiate the claim until it's paid correctly.

San Antonio is one of the most hail-prone cities in the United States. Storms building over the Hill Country regularly drop large hail across Bexar, Comal and Guadalupe counties, and severe thunderstorms bring damaging straight-line winds. Add hard freezes, burst pipes, slab leaks and house fires, and San Antonio policyholders file a lot of claims. Many of them are underpaid.

Our public adjusters handle San Antonio insurance claims every day. That includes hail and wind roof claims, water damage, fire and smoke, denied claims, underpaid claims, appraisal disputes, and commercial and rental property claims, from Stone Oak to the South Side and from Alamo Ranch to Converse.

A Public Adjuster in San Antonio Can Help When:

  • The carrier offered a repair when the roof needs replacing
  • Your claim was denied for "wear and tear," "prior damage" or "cosmetic damage"
  • The settlement doesn't cover your contractor's bid
  • The first check was mostly depreciation
  • You have water, fire or smoke damage and don't know where to start
  • You manage a commercial, church, HOA or rental property claim

Texas Claim Rules Every San Antonio Policyholder Should Know

Prompt payment

Chapter 542 of the Texas Insurance Code sets deadlines for carriers to acknowledge, investigate, accept or deny, and pay claims.

Pre-suit notice

Chapter 542A requires written notice before a weather-related lawsuit. We're not attorneys, but we document claims so your options stay open.

Public adjuster licensing

Chapter 4102 requires public adjusters to be licensed and caps fees at 10%. Roofers can't act as your adjuster.

Policy deadlines

Your policy sets deadlines for notice, proof of loss and recovering depreciation. Don't let them pass.

General information only, not legal advice.

San Antonio Public Adjuster FAQ

What does a public adjuster in San Antonio do?

A San Antonio public adjuster is licensed by the Texas Department of Insurance to represent you, the policyholder, on a property insurance claim. We inspect the damage, write an estimate, read your policy, and present and negotiate the claim with your insurance company. The carrier's adjuster works for the insurance company. We work for you.

How much does a San Antonio public adjuster cost?

There is no upfront fee. Our fee is a percentage of the claim recovery and Texas law caps it at 10%. If we do not recover money, you do not owe a fee.

Should I hire a public adjuster before or after I file?

Either works, but earlier is better. Bringing in a San Antonio public adjuster before the carrier inspects means the damage is documented on your terms before the first estimate is written.

Can a public adjuster help if my claim was already paid or denied?

Yes. Many San Antonio claims we handle were already paid too little or denied. We review the carrier's estimate and reasons and supplement or re-present the claim.

Is a public adjuster the same as a roofer or a lawyer?

No. Texas law does not allow a roofer to act as your adjuster, and we do not do repairs. We are not attorneys and do not give legal advice or file lawsuits. We handle the claim itself.

Will filing a claim raise my rates?

Texas law limits how carriers can use weather claims in setting premiums, but every policy and carrier is different. We can walk through your situation during the free claim review.

What areas around San Antonio do you serve?

We serve all of San Antonio and Bexar County, plus Converse, Live Oak, Universal City, Schertz, Cibolo, New Braunfels, Boerne, Helotes, Alamo Heights, Stone Oak, Seguin and surrounding Comal, Guadalupe, Kendall, Medina and Wilson County communities.

How fast can you inspect my property?

In most of the San Antonio area we can schedule an inspection within a few days of your call, and sooner after a major storm.
