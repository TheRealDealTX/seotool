The Appraisal Clause, Explained for San Antonio Homeowners

Most Texas homeowner policies contain an appraisal clause. It's one of the most useful tools a San Antonio policyholder has for an underpaid claim.

What it resolves

Appraisal decides the amount of loss. It doesn't decide whether damage is covered. If the carrier has denied coverage outright, appraisal usually isn't the right tool.

How it works

  1. Either side demands appraisal in writing.
  2. Each side names a competent, disinterested appraiser.
  3. The two appraisers try to agree. If they can't, they select an umpire.
  4. An award signed by any two of the three sets the amount of loss.

Costs

Each side usually pays its own appraiser and splits the umpire's fee. Check your policy wording.

When it makes sense in San Antonio

Appraisal works well on hail and wind claims where the carrier admits damage but has priced repairs far below the real San Antonio market cost, or has approved a partial roof instead of a full replacement.
