After a fire, the policyholder is dealing with displacement, a building that may be unsafe, and an adjuster who is already writing the estimate. A San Antonio fire damage public adjuster handles the structure, smoke and soot cleaning, contents inventory and additional living expenses so the full loss is paid.

Smoke travels farther than flames

Smoke and soot get into HVAC ducts, attic insulation, cabinets and wall cavities well beyond the burned room. We document smoke migration so cleaning and replacement are priced throughout the house.

Contents inventory

The contents claim is often worth as much as the structure. We build a room-by-room inventory with replacement pricing instead of leaving you to remember everything you owned.

Additional living expense (ALE)

Loss of use coverage pays for temporary housing, extra food costs and mileage while the home is repaired. We track and submit it so it is not left on the table.

Fire & Smoke Damage Claims FAQ

Do I need a public adjuster for a fire & smoke damage claim in San Antonio?

You aren't required to hire one, but a licensed San Antonio public adjuster documents and negotiates the claim for you. On fire & smoke damage claims, that often changes what gets paid.

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. Our fee is a percentage of what we recover, capped at 10% under Texas law.

Do you serve areas outside San Antonio?

Yes. We cover all of Bexar County and surrounding Comal, Guadalupe, Kendall, Medina, Wilson and Atascosa County communities.
