What golf ball hail does to a Converse home

Most of the damage is not visible from the ground

Converse's housing stock is largely 1990s to 2020s builds with three-tab or architectural asphalt shingles, aluminum gutters and slab-mounted AC condensers. Here is what Converse Public Adjuster inspects and photographs on every roof, and what a rushed carrier inspection tends to miss.

Roof covering

Shingle bruising & granule loss

Hail fractures the fiberglass mat under the granules. The roof leaks months later; the carrier calls it "wear." We document impacts per test square with a chalk-and-tape method that holds up in appraisal.

Roof metals

Vents, flashing, drip edge

Soft metals record hail size and direction like a fingerprint. Dented turtle vents and pipe jacks prove the hail was big enough to damage shingles too.

Exterior

Gutters, downspouts, fascia

Aluminum gutters dent from the top and split at seams. Carriers frequently pay to "repair" a section that cannot be color-matched.

Windows & siding

Screens, glazing beads, siding

Wind-driven hail at 60 mph shreds screens and chips vinyl and Hardie siding on the storm-facing elevation. Sept 11's storm moved southwest, so northeast-facing walls took it.

Mechanical

AC condenser fins

Crushed fins cut efficiency and are a covered peril. Fin combing is rarely an adequate repair on 1.5"+ hail.

Interior

Water intrusion after the storm

Stained ceilings and wet insulation from the 2"+ rain that followed the hail are part of the same claim, if they are tied to the storm-created opening.

Site

Fences, sheds, patio covers

Cedar privacy fences, metal carports and polycarbonate patio covers are covered "other structures" and are routinely left off the carrier's estimate.

Vehicles

Hail-smashed cars

Auto damage runs through your auto comprehensive policy, not the homeowners policy. We can point you in the right direction, but our work is the property claim.

Texas rules that matter after a hail storm

The clock started on September 11

Insurance in Texas runs on deadlines, and most of them favor whoever knows they exist. These are the ones Converse homeowners should have in mind now.

Report the loss promptly

Most Texas homeowner policies require "prompt" notice, and many set a one-year outside limit for weather claims. Report now, even if you are not sure of the damage. Reporting is not the same as accepting an offer.

YOUR POLICY · CONDITIONS SECTION
15 days to acknowledge

Once you report, the carrier has 15 days to acknowledge the claim and start its investigation, and 15 business days after receiving everything it needs to accept or reject it.

TEX. INS. CODE CH. 542 · PROMPT PAYMENT
5 business days to pay

After a carrier accepts a claim it has 5 business days to pay. Late payment adds statutory interest and attorney's fees on top of the claim.

TEX. INS. CODE §542.057–.060
Public adjuster fees are capped

A Texas public adjuster's commission may not exceed 10% of the insurance settlement. No retainer, no hourly billing, nothing due until the carrier pays.

TEX. INS. CODE §4102.104
Roofers cannot adjust your claim

It is illegal in Texas for a roofing contractor to act as a public adjuster on a roof it will repair. If a roofer offers to "handle the insurance," that is your cue to call us.

TEX. INS. CODE §4102.163
Pre-suit notice, then two years

Weather-related claims require 60 days' written notice before any lawsuit, and most policies limit suits to two years from the loss. Appraisal is usually faster and cheaper than either.

Questions Converse homeowners are asking this week

Straight answers

My roof looks fine from the yard. Should I still file a claim for the Sept 11 storm?

Get it inspected before you decide. Hail of 1.5" to 2.5" fractures shingles without tearing them off, and the damage shows up as leaks a year later, after the policy's filing window has closed. Our inspection is free and we will tell you if there is nothing there.

The insurance company already sent an adjuster and paid me. Is it too late?

No. A paid claim can be supplemented or reopened, and in our experience most first payments on hail roofs in Bexar County leave out gutters, metals, siding, fence, code upgrades and overhead and profit. We review what was paid against what was damaged, at no cost.

What does a public adjuster cost?

Converse Public Adjuster charges a percentage of what we recover for you, never more than 10%, which is the maximum Texas allows. There is no retainer, no hourly billing and no fee if we recover nothing. On a reopened claim our fee applies only to the additional money.

A roofer knocked on my door and offered to handle the insurance for me. Is that normal?

It is common in Converse after a storm, and it is illegal. Texas Insurance Code §4102.163 prohibits a contractor from adjusting a claim on a property it will repair. Use a roofer to build the roof and a public adjuster to settle the claim.

Will filing a hail claim raise my rates?

Texas law bars a carrier from cancelling or non-renewing a homeowners policy based only on weather-related claims. Rates in 78109 and 78244 move with the area's storm history, and the Sept 11 storm is now part of that history whether or not you file.

The carrier says the damage is "cosmetic" or "wear and tear." What now?

Those are the two most common denial reasons on hail roofs, and both are arguable. We document impact density per test square, compare to the metals on the same slope and cite the storm data on this site. If the carrier holds its position, you can demand appraisal under your policy, and we prepare that demand for you.

What is the appraisal clause?

Nearly every Texas property policy lets either side demand appraisal when the amount of loss is disputed. Each side picks an appraiser, the appraisers pick an umpire, and the panel sets the amount. It is binding on the dollar figure, does not require a lawyer and usually resolves in weeks. It is the core of our practice.

Do you handle vehicles damaged by the hail?

Vehicle hail damage runs through your auto policy's comprehensive coverage and is not something a public adjuster handles. We are happy to explain the process on the phone.
