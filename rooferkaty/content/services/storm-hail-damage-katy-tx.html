What Hail and Wind Do to a Katy Roof

Hail damage

Hailstones crush the granules on asphalt shingles and can fracture the mat underneath. That bruise may not leak right away, but it exposes the asphalt to sun and speeds up aging. Larger hail can crack shingles outright, puncture vents and dent metal flashing. Soft metals like gutters, downspouts and AC fins often show the clearest evidence.

Wind damage

Strong gusts lift shingle edges, break the adhesive strips and crease the shingle so it no longer lies flat. Wind also tears off ridge caps and can peel back sections near eaves and rakes. The May 2024 derecho and Hurricane Beryl in July 2024 both left wind damage across the Houston area that some owners did not discover until later leaks.

Falling limbs and debris

Mature trees in older Katy and West Houston neighborhoods can drop branches that puncture decking. Any impact like this needs prompt attention.

Signs You Need Hail Damage Roof Repair in Katy

You do not need to climb a ladder to spot clues. Walk around the house after the storm and look for:

  • Dents or spatter marks on gutters, downspouts, mailboxes and patio furniture
  • Granules piled at downspout outlets or in the gutters
  • Shingle pieces, ridge caps or vent covers in the yard
  • Dented or dinged AC condenser fins
  • New water stains on ceilings or in the attic
  • Neighbors getting roofs inspected or replaced after the same storm

Our storm damage checklist walks you through what to photograph from the ground, and our post on how to spot hail damage on your Katy roof shows what damage looks like up close.

Safety first: Do not climb onto a wet or storm-damaged roof. Shingles can be slick and decking may be weakened. Leave the roof-level inspection to a trained crew.

Working With Your Insurance Company

Hail and wind damage are covered perils under many Texas homeowners policies, but every policy is different. Some have separate wind and hail deductibles, often a percentage of your dwelling coverage. Some pay actual cash value for older roofs instead of replacement cost. Check your policy or ask your agent so you understand what to expect.

We help homeowners document storm damage and meet the insurance adjuster, but we are not public adjusters and do not negotiate claims on your behalf. We also never waive or cover your deductible. Watch Out for Storm Chasers

After big storms, out-of-town crews knock on doors across Katy. Some do fine work, but many are gone by the time a warranty issue comes up. Be cautious of anyone who:

  • Offers to pay or waive your deductible
  • Pressures you to sign a contract on the spot
  • Asks for large payments before materials arrive
  • Cannot give you a local phone number and references

A local Katy roofer will still be here when you need them next season.

Upgrading After Hail Damage

If your roof needs replacement after a storm, it is a good moment to consider Class 4 impact-resistant shingles or metal. Many homeowners in hail-prone areas choose them, and some insurers offer discounts for impact-rated roofs. Ask your agent before you decide, and keep in mind that your HOA may still need to approve the color and product.
