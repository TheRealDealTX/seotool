Common Roof Repairs We Handle in Katy

Katy homes share a lot of the same construction details, so the same problems show up again and again. The repairs we make most often include:

  • Pipe boot failures. The rubber collars around plumbing vents crack in our UV and heat, often within ten years. This is one of the most common leak sources we find.
  • Missing or creased shingles. Strong gusts from spring storms and tropical systems break the seal strips and fold shingles back.
  • Step and wall flashing. Two-story homes have long roof-to-wall transitions where flashing can be missing, loose or poorly sealed.
  • Valley leaks. Valleys carry a lot of water during heavy rain, and worn or damaged valley shingles let it through.
  • Vent and exhaust caps. Damaged turtle vents, ridge vents and bath fan caps can leak or let in pests.
  • Nail pops. Nails that back out lift the shingle above them and open a path for water.

How We Diagnose a Leak

Water rarely enters the roof directly above the stain on your ceiling. It runs along decking, rafters and the top of drywall before it shows. Our roof repair process starts inside when possible:

  1. We check the attic for wet insulation, staining on the decking and daylight around penetrations.
  2. On the roof, we inspect everything uphill of the leak: flashing, boots, vents, valleys and shingles.
  3. We photograph the cause and show you what we found before recommending a fix.

This is the same documentation you get with our free roof inspection, so you can see the problem yourself instead of taking anyone's word for it.

Active leak? Move valuables, put a bucket under the drip and, if the ceiling is bulging with water, carefully puncture it to let it drain in a controlled spot. Stay off the roof and call us.

Roof Repair in Katy TX After Storms

When severe thunderstorms, hail or tropical systems move through, repair calls spike. Some damage is obvious, like shingles in the yard. Other damage, like hail bruising or broken seal strips, is easy to miss from the ground and may only show up as a leak weeks later. After events like the 2024 derecho and Hurricane Beryl, many Katy-area roofs had wind damage that owners did not notice right away.

If your roof took a hit, we can make temporary repairs or tarp exposed areas, then document the damage in case you decide to file an insurance claim. Our storm and hail damage page explains that process in more detail.

Materials That Last in Katy's Climate

A repair is only as good as what goes into it. We replace pipe boots with durable flashings rather than relying on caulk, use new metal for flashing rather than bending old pieces back into place, and seal and hand-tab replacement shingles so they stay down in the wind. Where shingles are discontinued, we choose the closest match available and tell you honestly how it will look.

Preventive Maintenance Saves Money

Many repairs we make could have been caught early. A quick check each spring before hail season and each fall before the end of hurricane season helps. Look for granules in gutters, missing shingles, lifted ridge caps and stains on ceilings. Keep gutters clear so water does not back up under the roof edge.
