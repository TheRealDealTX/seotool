Richmond has more history than most of the towns we serve. As the Fort Bend County seat on the Brazos River, it has historic homes downtown, established neighborhoods from the 1970s through the 1990s, and newer master-planned communities spreading along the Grand Parkway and FM 359. Roofing in Richmond TX means knowing how to handle all three, because a century-old wood-frame home and a two-year-old production build have very little in common up on the roof.

Historic Homes Near Downtown Richmond

Around the historic downtown, homes may have steep pitches, dormers, original board decking and chimneys that need careful flashing. Some have been re-roofed over older layers, which hides deck problems. When we replace these roofs, we tear down to the boards, repair or overlay decking where needed and add ventilation that many older houses never had. We take care with trim, porches and landscaping that are often decades old.

Mature Neighborhoods Like Pecan Grove

Pecan Grove, along FM 359 and FM 1464, is one of the area's most established communities, with homes built over several decades and a canopy of large pecan and oak trees. Those trees bring their own roof problems:

  • Limbs that break in thunderstorms and tropical winds and puncture decking
  • Leaves and pecans that pack valleys and gutters, backing water under shingles
  • Shade that keeps north-facing slopes damp, encouraging algae and moss
  • Branches that scrape shingles and wear away granules

Keeping trees trimmed and gutters clear goes a long way in neighborhoods like this.

After a storm: If a large limb hit your roof, do not assume it is fine because there is no leak yet. A cracked rafter or punctured deck can let water in during the next heavy rain.

New Communities in Richmond

West and south of the older areas, communities like Harvest Green, Long Meadow Farms, Aliana, Grand Mission and Veranda have added thousands of homes. These newer roofs are generally architectural shingles on complex two-story rooflines. We often find installation issues such as missing kick-out flashing and caulk-only pipe boots, and storm damage on young roofs is common after hail. HOAs here have active architectural review, so we provide specs and samples for approval before replacement.

Because many of these communities were built within a few years of each other, a single hailstorm can damage roofs on entire streets at once. When that happens, door-to-door crews arrive quickly. Take your time, ask for local references and a written scope, and avoid anyone who offers to cover your deductible, which is illegal in Texas.

Brazos River Weather and Storm Exposure

Richmond's position on the Brazos means it gets the full force of Gulf moisture moving inland. Heavy tropical rains, strong thunderstorms and occasional hail are part of life here, and the area saw wind damage from the 2024 derecho and Hurricane Beryl. Roof leaks often show up during long, wind-driven rain events rather than short storms, which is why roofing in Richmond TX has to account for flashing and underlayment details, not just shingles. If you suspect damage, our storm and hail damage service includes photo documentation you can use if you file a claim.
