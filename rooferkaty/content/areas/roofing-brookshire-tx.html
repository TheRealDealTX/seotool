Brookshire's Mix of Homes and Buildings

Older in-town homes

The original town center has older wood-frame and brick homes, some with several layers of roofing or patched sections from decades of repairs. These roofs often need decking replacement once we tear down to the boards, and some lack modern ventilation entirely.

New subdivisions

Communities such as Sunterra, Jordan Ranch and nearby Cane Island have brought thousands of new homes to the area. They typically have architectural shingles on production-builder rooflines. Young roofs still get storm damage and installation defects, so inspections matter here too.

Acreage, barns and commercial buildings

Outside town you will find farms, horse properties, workshops and metal commercial buildings along the I-10 corridor and toward the Brazos River. Exposed-fastener metal panels are everywhere, and they need different care than shingles.

Metal Roofing in Brookshire

Metal is far more common in Brookshire than in Katy's master-planned neighborhoods. The most frequent problems are rubber washers that dry out and crack in the sun, screws that back out from daily heat cycling, rusted panels at the eaves and leaking skylight panels. Replacing fasteners and resealing laps can add years to a sound metal roof. When panels are too far gone, a new metal roof is a long-lasting fix for both homes and outbuildings.

Barn owners: Check the screws along the ridge and eaves each spring. Loose or missing fasteners are the most common cause of leaks on exposed-fastener metal roofs.

Storm Exposure in Waller County

Brookshire is surrounded by open farmland and the Katy Prairie, so wind has a long, unobstructed run. Spring storms can bring hail and strong straight-line gusts, and tropical systems moving inland bring sustained wind and heavy rain. Metal buildings with large open doors are vulnerable to uplift, and older homes with worn shingles lose tabs quickly. After the 2024 derecho and Hurricane Beryl, many rural properties had both roof and outbuilding damage. If your property took a hit, our storm and hail damage team can document it and make repairs.

Roofing in Brookshire TX: Permits and HOAs

Inside city limits, the City of Brookshire handles permitting. Unincorporated Waller County properties follow county rules, and many rural tracts have no HOA. Newer subdivisions, on the other hand, usually have active HOAs with architectural review for roof color and material. We confirm requirements for your address and help with HOA paperwork when it applies.
