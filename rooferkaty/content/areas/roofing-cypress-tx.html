Two Generations of Cypress Housing

Established neighborhoods

Areas closer to FM 1960 and US-290 include subdivisions built from the 1980s through the early 2000s, such as Coles Crossing and Cypress Creek Lakes. Many of these homes are on their second or third roof, and owners often find a patchwork of repairs from past storms. Older decking, outdated ventilation and mature trees overhanging the roof are common. Limbs scraping shingles and leaves clogging valleys and gutters cause more trouble here than in newer sections.

Newer master-planned communities

Farther west and north, communities like Bridgeland, Towne Lake, Fairfield and Blackhorse Ranch are full of homes built in the last 10 to 20 years. Roofs are usually architectural shingles, often on large two-story homes with complex rooflines. Many are approaching their first replacement, especially after a hailstorm or two.

Storm Patterns in Northwest Harris County

Cypress sees the same severe spring thunderstorms that move across the Houston area, and hail swaths often track across the 290 corridor. Lakeside communities like Towne Lake and Bridgeland are open to wind sweeping across the water, which can lift shingles on exposed rows of homes. The 2024 derecho and Hurricane Beryl brought down trees and lifted shingles across northwest Harris County. If a storm just passed through, start with our storm damage checklist, then have the roof checked up close.

Tree check: In older Cypress neighborhoods, have overhanging limbs trimmed back several feet from the roof. Branches rubbing in the wind wear through shingles faster than you might expect.

What a Roofer in Cypress TX Should Check

When we inspect a Cypress roof, we pay close attention to:

  • Hail bruising on shingles and dents on gutters, vents and AC units
  • Wind-lifted tabs and torn ridge caps on lakeside and corner lots
  • Valleys and gutters packed with leaves under mature trees
  • Attic ventilation, especially on older homes with a single row of turtle vents
  • Roof-to-wall flashing on two-story homes in newer communities

If we find storm damage, our insurance claim help includes photo documentation and meeting your adjuster on site. We are not public adjusters, and we never waive deductibles.

HOAs and Permits in Cypress

Most Cypress homes are in unincorporated Harris County, so permitting differs from homes inside a city. HOAs are a bigger factor. Master-planned communities typically maintain approved roof colors and require architectural review before any replacement. Older subdivisions may have simpler rules, but it is still worth checking your deed restrictions. We supply product specs and color samples for your HOA application.

Repair or Replace?

For a newer Bridgeland or Fairfield roof with a few lifted shingles, a roof repair is usually enough. For a 20-year-old roof with granule loss and hail hits on several slopes, a roof replacement often makes more sense. We give you photos and a straight answer either way.
