CenTrust Bank SBA loans (IL)

CenTrust Bank, a Northbrook, Illinois SBA lender, is now a division of SmartBiz Bank, N.A. after SmartBiz acquired it in 2025.

CenTrust Bank, National Association, was a small national bank in Northbrook, Illinois, on Chicago's North Shore. In March 2025 SmartBiz Loans, the online SBA loan platform, completed its acquisition of the bank after conditional approval from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency. The bank was renamed SmartBiz Bank, N.A., and it continues to serve its existing customers as Centrust Bank, a division of SmartBiz Bank, N.A.

Even before the deal, CenTrust was an SBA specialist. It ran a dedicated SBA lending group, and SBA data shows it making 7(a) loans in many states rather than only in Illinois. SmartBiz had already worked with partner banks to fund SBA loans for its online applicants; owning a bank lets it make those loans itself, and the OCC approval allows the bank to expand small-business lending nationwide.

For borrowers, the practical result is a bank whose business model is built around SBA 7(a) lending, with much of the application process online. Typical loans are working capital, refinancing of higher-cost business debt, equipment and smaller business acquisitions, along with some owner-occupied real estate.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Centrust, now part of SmartBiz Bank, suits a borrower who wants an SBA-focused lender and is comfortable applying online and working remotely. Common uses are 7(a) loans for working capital, refinancing merchant cash advances or other expensive debt, equipment and smaller acquisitions. Read your documents to see which entity is the lender of record and who services the loan, ask for the spread over prime and every fee, and compare the offer with at least one local SBA lender.

SBA programs to ask CenTrust Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is CenTrust Bank an SBA lender?

Yes. CenTrust has long been an active SBA 7(a) lender, and since March 2025 it has been part of SmartBiz Bank, N.A., a bank built around SBA lending. It makes loans in many states, not only Illinois, much of it through an online application.

Is CenTrust Bank part of SmartBiz?

Yes. SmartBiz Loans completed its acquisition of CenTrust Bank, N.A. on March 17, 2025, and the bank was renamed SmartBiz Bank, N.A. It continues to serve existing customers as Centrust Bank, a division of SmartBiz Bank. Check your loan documents for the lender of record.

What SBA rates does CenTrust Bank offer?

CenTrust sets rates within SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) rates top out at 10.00% for loans over $350,000 and 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. Ask for the spread over prime and all fees before you accept an offer.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from CenTrust Bank?

CenTrust Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
