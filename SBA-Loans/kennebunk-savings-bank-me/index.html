Kennebunk Savings SBA loans (ME)

Kennebunk Savings Bank is a mutual savings bank in southern Maine that makes business loans, including SBA-guaranteed financing, to local companies.

Kennebunk Savings Bank is a mutual savings bank headquartered in Kennebunk, Maine. As a mutual, it has no stockholders. It is owned in effect by its depositors, which lets it take a long-term view and reinvest in the communities it serves. It has operated in York County since the 1800s.

Its branches cover southern Maine, and it has expanded into seacoast New Hampshire. The local economy mixes tourism, hospitality, marine trades, construction, healthcare and a growing number of professional and remote businesses. The bank's commercial customers reflect that mix, from inns and restaurants to builders and medical practices. Many of those customers are family-owned and have banked locally for years.

Kennebunk Savings is known locally for community giving, including a program that donates a share of its earnings to local nonprofits. On the business side, it offers commercial real estate, equipment and working capital loans, and uses SBA programs when a deal needs more flexibility than a conventional loan. Decisions are made locally.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Southern Maine is full of seasonal and owner-run businesses, and that is where an SBA loan from Kennebunk Savings tends to help. A 7(a) loan can finance the purchase of a restaurant, inn or trades business with a 10-year term, or real estate with up to 25 years, easing payments in the slow months. Because high coastal property values push down payments up, a 504 loan with a Maine CDC can keep equity near 10%. Bring several years of monthly sales to show how seasonality works.

SBA programs to ask Kennebunk Savings about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Kennebunk Savings Bank make SBA loans?

Kennebunk Savings Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. It serves businesses in southern Maine and the New Hampshire seacoast. Talk with a commercial lender about which SBA programs it uses and how it handles seasonal businesses.

What does it mean that Kennebunk Savings is a mutual bank?

A mutual bank has no outside stockholders, so it does not answer to quarterly earnings pressure the way a public bank does. For borrowers, that can mean a steadier, relationship-based approach. Loan approvals still depend on cash flow, credit, collateral and equity, including on SBA loans.

What SBA loan rate could I get from Kennebunk Savings Bank?

The bank sets your rate under SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums range from 10.00% for loans above $350,000 to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. A 504 loan's CDC portion is fixed, recently about 6.57% for a 25-year term.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Kennebunk Savings?

Kennebunk Savings sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
