Morton Community Bank SBA loans (IL)

Morton Community Bank is a locally owned bank based in Morton, Illinois, near Peoria, that serves businesses and farms across central Illinois.

Morton Community Bank is headquartered in Morton, Illinois, a village just southeast of Peoria. It is a privately held community bank that has grown from its home town into a network of offices across central Illinois. The bank markets itself on local ownership and local decision-making, and most of its lenders live in the communities they serve.

Its footprint centers on the Peoria area and Tazewell County, with offices reaching other central Illinois towns and cities. That region mixes manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture and a large number of family-owned businesses. Morton Community Bank serves all of them, with separate teams for agricultural, commercial and consumer lending. Lenders tend to know their customers personally.

Typical business clients include contractors, manufacturers and suppliers, medical and professional practices, retailers and farm-related businesses. Many have banked with it for years. For companies that need a longer amortization or a smaller down payment than a conventional loan offers, the bank can use the SBA 7(a) or 504 programs.

How business owners use SBA loans here

In central Illinois, an SBA loan through Morton Community Bank most often supports a business purchase or succession sale, an owner-occupied building, or new equipment for a growing company. A local bank may be more flexible on a deal it understands, such as a well-known local business changing hands. Ask whether it handles SBA underwriting in-house, whether it is an SBA Preferred Lender, and which central Illinois CDC it works with for 504 loans.

SBA programs to ask Morton Community Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Does Morton Community Bank make SBA loans?

Yes. Morton Community Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Talk with a commercial lender at your nearest office about 7(a) or 504 financing. Ask whether it is an SBA Preferred Lender and how long approval usually takes.

Where does Morton Community Bank lend?

The bank is headquartered in Morton, Illinois, near Peoria, and serves the Peoria and Tazewell County area plus other central Illinois communities. It focuses on businesses and farms within its region rather than lending nationally.

What SBA rate can I expect from Morton Community Bank?
