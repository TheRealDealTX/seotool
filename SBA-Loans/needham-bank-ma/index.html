Needham Bank SBA loans (MA)

Needham Bank is a Greater Boston commercial bank that grew from a local savings bank into a larger business lender, most recently by absorbing BankProv.

Needham Bank is headquartered in Needham, Massachusetts, a suburb southwest of Boston. It operated for generations as a mutual savings bank, then converted to stock ownership when its holding company, NB Bancorp, went public in 2023. Despite the change in ownership structure, it still operates as a locally headquartered bank focused on the Boston area, with lending decisions made in Massachusetts.

In November 2025 NB Bancorp completed its acquisition of Provident Bancorp, and BankProv merged into Needham Bank. That added branches in the Merrimack Valley and southern New Hampshire to Needham's base in the western and southern Boston suburbs. Former BankProv branches have been rebranded as Needham Bank. The combined bank is noticeably larger than Needham was on its own, which raises the size of loans it can hold.

Needham has built up commercial lending, including commercial real estate, construction, business banking and specialty lending. Small business clients include professional services firms, contractors, health care practices, retailers and property owners across the region. Many clients bring both their business and personal banking to the bank, and lenders usually expect the operating accounts to move with the loan.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A Greater Boston owner would likely look at Needham Bank for an SBA loan when buying commercial space for the business, where a 504 loan keeps the down payment near 10% on expensive property, or when buying an existing practice or service company with a 7(a) loan. Former BankProv customers in northern Massachusetts and southern New Hampshire now have the same option. Ask whether SBA deals are handled by a dedicated team and how quickly the bank can approve.

SBA programs to ask Needham Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Needham Bank offer SBA loans?

Needham Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask its business banking team which SBA programs it currently offers, the loan sizes it targets, and whether it acts as first-lien lender on 504 projects with a Massachusetts CDC.

What happened to BankProv?

BankProv, the bank of Provident Bancorp in Amesbury, Massachusetts, merged into Needham Bank when NB Bancorp completed its acquisition in November 2025. BankProv customers were converted to Needham Bank systems and branches are being rebranded.

Where does Needham Bank lend?

Needham Bank is based in Needham and serves Greater Boston, with a strong presence in the western and southern suburbs. Since the BankProv merger it also has branches in the Merrimack Valley and southern New Hampshire.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Needham Bank?

Needham Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
