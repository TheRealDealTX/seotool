St. Mary's Bank SBA loans (NH)

St. Mary's Bank is a member-owned credit union in Manchester, New Hampshire, widely recognized as the first credit union in the United States.

Despite the word bank in its name, St. Mary's Bank is a credit union. It was founded in 1908 in Manchester, New Hampshire, by French-Canadian mill workers and parishioners of St. Mary's parish, under the name La Caisse Populaire Ste-Marie. It is commonly cited as the first credit union in the United States.

Today it is a member-owned financial cooperative serving Manchester and communities across southern New Hampshire. Membership is open to people who live or work in its eligible area, and business owners can join to open business accounts and apply for commercial credit. As a cooperative, it returns value to members through rates and fees rather than to outside shareholders, and its decisions are made locally in Manchester.

St. Mary's Bank offers business checking, commercial real estate loans, equipment and vehicle loans, and lines of credit to local small businesses. Its business clients tend to be owner-operated firms in the Manchester and Merrimack Valley area, such as contractors, retailers, restaurants and professional offices. Loans tend to be modest in size, and the credit union generally wants to see the business's operating accounts held there as well.

How business owners use SBA loans here

For a Manchester-area business, an SBA loan through a credit union like St. Mary's Bank is most useful when a conventional commercial loan falls short on down payment or term. Common cases are buying a building for your own business, refinancing equipment, or funding working capital with a longer payback. Credit unions face caps on member business lending, so larger or more complex deals may be routed to the SBA structure or to a partner. Expect membership to be required before you apply.

SBA programs to ask St. Mary's Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is St. Mary's Bank a bank or a credit union?

St. Mary's Bank is a credit union, owned by its members, even though its name says bank. It began in Manchester, New Hampshire in 1908 and is widely recognized as the first credit union in the country. Deposits are insured by the NCUA rather than the FDIC.

Does St. Mary's Bank make SBA loans?

St. Mary's Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask its business lending team which SBA programs are available now, the loan sizes it prefers, and whether you must become a member before applying. Most credit unions require membership.

Can I get an SBA loan at a credit union in Manchester NH?

Yes, some credit unions are approved SBA lenders, and St. Mary's Bank is one Manchester option to ask. Rates are capped by the SBA the same way they are at banks. Compare its offer with a local bank and, for real estate, a 504 loan through a New Hampshire CDC.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from St. Mary's Bank?

St. Mary's Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
