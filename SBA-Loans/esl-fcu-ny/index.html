ESL Federal Credit Union SBA loans (NY)

ESL Federal Credit Union is a large Rochester, New York credit union that began as a savings institution for Eastman Kodak employees.

ESL Federal Credit Union is headquartered in Rochester, New York. It started in 1920 as Eastman Savings and Loan, serving employees of Eastman Kodak, and the ESL initials come from that original name. Over time it became a federal credit union with membership open to people who live or work across its broader community area.

ESL is one of the largest financial institutions based in the Rochester area, with branches across Monroe County and nearby Finger Lakes counties. It is known locally for community giving and for a full consumer lineup, from mortgages and auto loans to checking accounts. Its long history with Kodak families means many Rochester business owners already have personal accounts there before they ever ask about a business loan.

On the business side, ESL offers business checking, commercial real estate loans, equipment financing, lines of credit and SBA loans. Business clients tend to be Rochester-area owner-operated firms, including professional practices, contractors, retailers and manufacturers. Business members can usually expect a relationship banker who handles both lending and deposit services, with decisions made locally in Rochester rather than at a distant headquarters.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A Rochester owner might use ESL for an SBA loan when buying a building for the business, purchasing an existing company from a retiring owner, or financing equipment over a longer term than a standard loan allows. Western New York has many family businesses changing hands, and a 7(a) loan helps a buyer with limited cash close the deal. Because ESL is a credit union, you will need to become a member, and its member business lending has regulatory limits that the SBA guarantee can help work around.

SBA programs to ask ESL Federal Credit Union about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does ESL offer business and SBA loans?

Yes, ESL Federal Credit Union offers business loans, including commercial real estate loans, equipment financing and lines of credit, and it appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask which SBA programs it offers now and the loan sizes it prefers.

What does ESL stand for?

ESL comes from Eastman Savings and Loan, the name the institution used when it was founded in 1920 to serve Eastman Kodak employees. It later became ESL Federal Credit Union and opened membership to a much wider Rochester-area community.

Do I need to be an ESL member to get an SBA loan?

Generally yes. Credit unions lend to members, so you or your business will need to qualify and join before the loan closes. Membership is based on where you live, work or do business in its service area. ESL can confirm eligibility when you apply.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from ESL Federal Credit Union?

ESL Federal Credit Union sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
