PCB Bank SBA loans (CA)

PCB Bank, formerly Pacific City Bank, is a Los Angeles Koreatown bank with an active SBA lending business.

PCB Bank is a commercial bank headquartered in Los Angeles, in the Koreatown area. It was founded as Pacific City Bank and later shortened its name to PCB Bank. Its holding company, PCB Bancorp, trades on Nasdaq. It operates as a business-focused commercial bank and is one of the larger Korean-American banks in the United States.

The bank grew up serving the Korean-American business community in Southern California and has since added branches and loan production offices in other states with large Korean-American populations. Several of its lending offices focus on SBA and commercial real estate loans rather than deposits. That network lets it serve borrowers well outside Los Angeles.

Its business clients include restaurants, retailers, wholesalers, gas stations, hotels and motels, and owners of commercial property. Many are first- or second-generation immigrant entrepreneurs, and staff can often work with borrowers in Korean as well as English. Bankers are used to cash-intensive businesses and to deals where a buyer is purchasing a business from another member of the same community.

How business owners use SBA loans here

PCB Bank is a common choice for buying an existing small business, such as a restaurant, liquor store, dry cleaner or motel, where a 7(a) loan covers business value that a conventional loan will not. It is also used for owner-occupied commercial real estate, either through a 7(a) real estate loan with a 25-year term or a 504 structure. Borrowers who prefer to work in Korean may find the process easier here. Compare its rate and fees with at least one other SBA lender before committing.

SBA programs to ask PCB Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does PCB Bank offer SBA loans?

Yes, SBA lending is a core business line for PCB Bank, and it appears in SBA data as an active lender. Ask its SBA team which programs fit your deal, whether it can approve under Preferred Lender authority, and the rate and fees for your loan size.

Is PCB Bank the same as Pacific City Bank?

Yes. PCB Bank is the Los Angeles bank originally called Pacific City Bank. It shortened its name, and its parent company is PCB Bancorp. Older documents and reviews may still use the Pacific City Bank name.

Can PCB Bank help with an SBA loan in Korean?

PCB Bank was built around the Korean-American business community, and many of its bankers work with clients in Korean and English. Ask the branch or loan office near you. SBA loan documents themselves are in English.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from PCB Bank?

PCB Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
