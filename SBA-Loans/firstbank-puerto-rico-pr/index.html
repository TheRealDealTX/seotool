FirstBank Puerto Rico SBA loans (PR)

FirstBank Puerto Rico is one of the island's largest banks and offers SBA loans to small businesses in Puerto Rico, Florida and the Virgin Islands.

FirstBank Puerto Rico is the main subsidiary of First BanCorp., a publicly traded bank holding company headquartered in San Juan. It is one of the largest banks in Puerto Rico, offering consumer, mortgage, commercial and small-business banking. It also operates in Florida and in the US and British Virgin Islands.

In 2020 FirstBank completed its acquisition of Banco Santander Puerto Rico, adding branches, customers and commercial loans and strengthening its position on the island. Its business clients range from colmados, restaurants and retailers to medical practices, manufacturers, tourism businesses and commercial property owners. The deal made FirstBank an even larger player in the island's banking market.

SBA programs apply in Puerto Rico just as they do in the states, and FirstBank uses them to finance working capital, equipment, business acquisitions and real estate. After hurricanes and other disruptions, SBA lending has also played a role in helping island businesses rebuild and recapitalize. Its scale also means borrowers can access treasury services, merchant processing and commercial credit lines in one place.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A Puerto Rico business owner would most likely use FirstBank for a 7(a) loan to buy equipment, refinance costly short-term debt, purchase an existing business or acquire a commercial building. Because island property and insurance costs can be high, longer SBA terms help keep payments manageable. Bilingual service makes the process easier for Spanish-speaking owners. Florida businesses can also apply through FirstBank's Florida operation, so ask which team handles SBA loans in your area.

SBA programs to ask FirstBank Puerto Rico about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Can I get an SBA loan in Puerto Rico from FirstBank?

Yes. Puerto Rico businesses are eligible for SBA programs, and FirstBank Puerto Rico appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Contact its commercial or small-business lending team to confirm which programs it offers and the documents you will need.

Does FirstBank Puerto Rico make SBA loans in Florida?

FirstBank operates in Florida as well as Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands. Florida businesses can ask its local commercial bankers about SBA financing. Program availability can vary by market, so confirm whether SBA loans are originated by the Florida team or centrally from Puerto Rico.

What SBA loan rates apply at FirstBank Puerto Rico?

SBA rate caps are the same in Puerto Rico as on the mainland. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums range from 10.00% for loans over $350,000 to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. FirstBank sets your rate within those limits.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from FirstBank Puerto Rico?

FirstBank Puerto Rico sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
