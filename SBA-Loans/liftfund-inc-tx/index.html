LiftFund SBA loans and microloans (TX)

LiftFund is a nonprofit CDFI based in San Antonio that makes small business loans, including SBA microloans, to owners banks often turn away.

LiftFund is a nonprofit lender headquartered in San Antonio, Texas, and a certified Community Development Financial Institution. It was known for years as Accion Texas, part of the Accion network of microlenders, before rebranding as LiftFund. As a nonprofit, it raises capital from government programs, banks, foundations and other investors, and uses it to lend where conventional banks usually do not.

LiftFund started in Texas and has since expanded to lend in a number of other states, mostly across the South. Much of its lending is done remotely, so borrowers do not need to live near a LiftFund office. Its mission centers on entrepreneurs with limited access to bank credit, including low- and moderate-income owners, women and minority business owners.

Its loans are generally small, from start-up and first-time business loans to working capital and equipment financing. LiftFund also pairs lending with free business coaching and education for borrowers. Coaching can cover budgeting, credit, marketing and record keeping, which helps first-time borrowers build the history they need to qualify for larger bank or SBA loans later.

How business owners use SBA loans here

LiftFund is a fit for owners who need a smaller amount than a bank wants to make, or who do not yet have the credit history, collateral or time in business banks require. As an SBA microlender it can lend up to $50,000 for working capital, inventory, supplies or equipment, though SBA microloans cannot be used to buy real estate. It has also been active in SBA's Community Advantage lending for somewhat larger loans. Expect closer coaching and more flexible underwriting, with rates that may run higher than a bank's.

SBA programs to ask LiftFund about

  • Microloan. Loans up to $50,000 through nonprofit intermediaries, usually with free coaching. Good for start-ups and very small businesses.
  • Community Advantage. Mission-based 7(a) lending for borrowers that banks often turn down: newer businesses, thin credit files and underserved communities.
  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is LiftFund an SBA microlender?

Yes, LiftFund is an SBA microloan intermediary and a certified CDFI based in San Antonio. SBA microloans go up to $50,000 for working capital, inventory, supplies and equipment. LiftFund also offers its own small business loan products, so ask which one fits your need.

Is LiftFund the same as Accion Texas?

Yes. LiftFund is the San Antonio nonprofit formerly known as Accion Texas. It changed its name as it grew beyond Texas. The mission, lending to small business owners who lack access to bank credit, stayed the same.

Who qualifies for a LiftFund loan?

LiftFund focuses on small business owners who struggle to get bank financing, including start-ups and owners with limited credit history. You still need a workable plan and the ability to repay. Eligibility and loan options vary by state, so check whether it lends where your business operates.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from LiftFund?

LiftFund sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
