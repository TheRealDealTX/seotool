Bar Harbor Bank & Trust SBA loans (ME)

Bar Harbor Bank & Trust is a Maine-based community bank with branches across Maine, New Hampshire and Vermont that offers SBA-backed business loans.

Bar Harbor Bank & Trust is the bank subsidiary of Bar Harbor Bankshares, a publicly traded holding company. The bank started on Mount Desert Island in Maine and still keeps its headquarters in the town of Bar Harbor. For most of its history it served coastal Maine communities, where seasonal tourism, fishing, boatbuilding and hospitality shape the local economy.

The bank's footprint changed in 2017 when Bar Harbor Bankshares acquired Lake Sunapee Bank Group, the parent of Lake Sunapee Bank in New Hampshire. That deal added branches in New Hampshire and Vermont, and the Lake Sunapee branches were folded into the Bar Harbor name. Today the bank operates across all three northern New England states.

Business customers include hotels and inns, restaurants, marine businesses, contractors, healthcare practices and other owner-managed companies. The bank offers commercial real estate, equipment and working capital loans alongside cash management and trust services. SBA lending is one tool it uses for businesses that need longer terms or lower down payments than a conventional loan allows.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Seasonality is the big issue for many Bar Harbor Bank & Trust borrowers. A 7(a) loan with a 10-year term can spread payments out so a summer-heavy hotel or restaurant is not squeezed in the off season, and a 25-year term on real estate helps even more. Buyers of an inn or marina often pair the bank with a 504 loan through a regional CDC. Be ready to show several years of monthly revenue so the lender can see how cash flow moves through the year.

SBA programs to ask Bar Harbor Bank & Trust about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Bar Harbor Bank & Trust offer SBA loans?

Bar Harbor Bank & Trust is listed in SBA lending data as a participating lender. It serves businesses in Maine, New Hampshire and Vermont. Ask a commercial lender which programs it currently uses, such as 7(a), 504 or SBA Express, and whether it has Preferred Lender status for faster approvals.

Can I get an SBA loan from Bar Harbor Bank & Trust in New Hampshire or Vermont?

Yes, its branch network covers New Hampshire and Vermont as well as Maine, largely because of the 2017 Lake Sunapee Bank acquisition. Businesses in those states can apply through local commercial bankers. As with any lender, approval depends on cash flow, credit, collateral and owner equity.

What are SBA loan rates at Bar Harbor Bank & Trust?

The bank prices each loan, but SBA caps the maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps range from 10.00% for loans above $350,000 to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. The 504 debenture portion is fixed, recently about 6.57% for a 25-year term.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Bar Harbor Bank & Trust?

Bar Harbor Bank & Trust sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
