Community National Bank SBA loans (VT)

Community National Bank is an independent community bank based in Derby, Vermont, serving the Northeast Kingdom and central Vermont.

Community National Bank is headquartered in Derby, Vermont, close to the Canadian border. Its holding company is Community Bancorp. It is one of a shrinking number of independent banks based in Vermont, and it has deep roots in the Northeast Kingdom. Being locally headquartered means credit decisions are made close to home rather than by a bank based out of state.

The bank's branches stretch across northern and central Vermont, from Orleans, Caledonia and Essex counties toward Lamoille, Washington County and the Montpelier and Barre area. There are several unrelated banks called Community National Bank in other states, so check that you are looking at the Vermont one. Lenders here know these small markets well.

Its business clients are the small firms that make up rural Vermont's economy: farms and agricultural businesses, contractors, lodging and hospitality, retailers, manufacturers and professional offices. The bank offers commercial real estate, equipment and working capital loans along with business deposit services. Borrowers can generally expect bankers who understand seasonal cash flow and rural property values.

How business owners use SBA loans here

In northern Vermont, SBA loans often help with deals that look thin on paper to a conventional underwriter: an inn or general store changing hands, a small manufacturer buying equipment, or a contractor buying a shop building. A 7(a) loan can stretch the term and accept a smaller down payment. Seasonal tourism businesses may want an SBA-backed line of credit. For larger owner-occupied real estate, ask about a 504 loan with a Vermont CDC, with Community National on the first mortgage.

SBA programs to ask Community National Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is Community National Bank in Vermont an SBA lender?

The Vermont-based Community National Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask its commercial lenders which SBA programs it uses today and whether it handles approvals in-house. Make sure you are contacting the Derby, Vermont bank, not a same-name bank elsewhere.

Where is Community National Bank located?

Community National Bank is headquartered in Derby, Vermont. Its branches serve the Northeast Kingdom and parts of central Vermont, including communities around Montpelier and Barre. It is an independent bank and is owned by Community Bancorp.

What SBA loan rate could I get in Vermont?

SBA rates are capped nationally, so the limits are the same in Vermont as anywhere. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums run from 10.00% for loans over $350,000 to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term and risk.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Community National Bank?

Community National Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
