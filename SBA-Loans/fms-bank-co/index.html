FMS Bank SBA loans (CO)

FMS Bank is a community bank in Fort Morgan, Colorado that serves farm, ranch and small-business customers on the northeastern plains.

FMS Bank is a community bank headquartered in Fort Morgan, the seat of Morgan County on Colorado's northeastern plains. The name comes from its earlier identity as Fort Morgan State Bank. It is a small, locally focused institution rather than part of a large regional chain, and it lends mostly in the communities where its staff live and work.

The economy around Fort Morgan is built on agriculture, food processing, trucking and the businesses that support them. That shapes the bank's loan book. It makes agricultural operating and equipment loans as well as commercial loans to Main Street businesses such as auto repair shops, restaurants, retailers, contractors and professional offices.

For a small rural bank, SBA guarantees help manage risk on loans that are larger than it would otherwise hold, or on businesses with limited collateral. They also let the bank offer longer repayment terms than a typical conventional commercial note, which helps borrowers keep monthly payments manageable. For the borrower, that usually means a lower monthly payment and more room in the budget.

How business owners use SBA loans here

In a market like Morgan County, SBA loans often finance the handoff of a family business to a new owner, the purchase of a shop building, or equipment for a trucking or ag-services company. A 7(a) loan can stretch equipment payments to 10 years, and real estate up to 25. Agricultural producers usually look to FSA programs instead, but ag-related businesses such as repair shops or input suppliers can qualify for SBA. Bring clean financial statements and expect the banker to know the local market well.

SBA programs to ask FMS Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does FMS Bank in Fort Morgan offer SBA loans?

FMS Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. It serves businesses in Fort Morgan and the surrounding northeastern Colorado area. Contact a loan officer to confirm which SBA programs it offers and whether it processes them in-house or with outside help.

Can an agriculture-related business get an SBA loan from FMS Bank?

Many ag-related businesses qualify for SBA loans, such as equipment dealers, repair shops, trucking firms and processors. Farms and ranches themselves are usually financed through USDA Farm Service Agency programs instead. FMS Bank makes both agricultural and commercial loans, so a banker can tell you which route fits.

What is the SBA loan rate at FMS Bank?

FMS Bank sets your rate, capped by SBA. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums are 10.00% for loans over $350,000, 11.50% for $250,001 to $350,000, 13.00% for $50,001 to $250,000 and 13.50% for $50,000 or less.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from FMS Bank?

FMS Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
