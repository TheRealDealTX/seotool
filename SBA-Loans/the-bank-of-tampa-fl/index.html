The Bank of Tampa SBA loans (FL)

The Bank of Tampa is a locally owned Tampa Bay bank focused on businesses, professionals and their owners, with SBA loans among its lending options.

The Bank of Tampa is an independent, locally owned bank headquartered in Tampa, Florida. It has stayed independent while many Florida community banks sold to larger regional companies, and it positions itself as a relationship bank for business owners, professionals and families in the Tampa Bay area. That independence means credit decisions are made in Tampa.

Its offices are concentrated in Hillsborough and Pinellas counties. The bank offers commercial lending, treasury management and wealth and trust services. Clients include medical and dental practices, law and accounting firms, family-owned companies, contractors, nonprofits and commercial real estate owners. Many of those relationships span the business, the owner's personal banking and the owner's family, which is typical of a private banking model.

Because it focuses on established businesses and their owners, much of its lending is conventional. SBA loans add flexibility for clients who want longer terms, a smaller down payment, or financing for an acquisition or practice purchase that a conventional structure would make harder. Those uses tend to come up during ownership changes and expansions.

How business owners use SBA loans here

The Bank of Tampa's client base points to SBA uses like buying into a medical, dental or professional practice, purchasing an office condo or building, or financing a family business transition. A 7(a) loan can cover goodwill in a practice purchase with a 10-year term, and a 504 loan can help with owner-occupied property in a high-cost market. Expect a relationship approach, which often means moving operating accounts to the bank, and a lender who knows the Tampa Bay market well.

SBA programs to ask The Bank of Tampa about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does The Bank of Tampa offer SBA loans?

The Bank of Tampa appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. It serves businesses and professionals in the Tampa Bay area. Ask a commercial banker which SBA programs it uses and whether SBA financing is right for your deal or a conventional loan would work better.

Can I use an SBA loan from The Bank of Tampa to buy a medical or dental practice?

Practice acquisitions are a common use of SBA 7(a) loans because they can finance goodwill with terms up to 10 years. Under current SBA rules, buyers generally need at least 10% equity, and lenders may ask for more. The Bank of Tampa works with many professional clients, so ask about its experience.

What are SBA loan rates at The Bank of Tampa?

The bank sets rates within SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums are 10.00% for loans over $350,000, 11.50% for $250,001 to $350,000, 13.00% for $50,001 to $250,000 and 13.50% for $50,000 or less.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from The Bank of Tampa?

The Bank of Tampa sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
