Wallis Bank SBA loans (TX)

Wallis Bank is a Texas community bank, founded in the small town of Wallis, that has become one of the most active SBA lenders in the Houston area.

Wallis Bank is headquartered in Wallis, Texas, a small town in Austin County west of Houston. The bank traces its roots to 1906 and operated for many years as Wallis State Bank before changing its name to Wallis Bank in 2019. It remains a community bank, but it has grown well beyond its home town.

Its branches now cover much of Greater Houston, including Sugar Land and other suburbs, and it has expanded into San Antonio. Wallis Bank has also opened offices outside Texas, including in the Atlanta area, Arizona and California, largely to support business and SBA lending. That reach is unusual for a bank that started in a town of a few thousand people.

SBA lending is central to its business. Wallis Bank is an SBA Preferred Lender and has reported being the largest SBA 7(a) lender by dollar volume in the Houston district in recent years. Common borrowers include hotel owners, restaurants, gas stations and convenience stores, medical practices and other owner-operated businesses buying property or another company.

Formerly known as Wallis State Bank.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Wallis Bank is a strong option when SBA is the main event, not a side product. Its lenders regularly close 7(a) loans for business acquisitions, hotels, gas stations and owner-occupied buildings, and Preferred Lender status means it can approve many loans without waiting on SBA. Come with a clear use of funds, your equity injection, tax returns and a business plan or seller financials. Ask about rate structure, fees and prepayment terms, and compare with another Houston SBA lender.

SBA programs to ask Wallis Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is Wallis Bank a good SBA lender?

Wallis Bank focuses heavily on SBA lending and is an SBA Preferred Lender, so it can approve many 7(a) loans in-house. It has reported being the top SBA 7(a) lender by dollar volume in the Houston district. That experience helps, but still compare its rate, fees and terms with other lenders.

Where does Wallis Bank make SBA loans?

The bank is headquartered in Wallis, Texas, west of Houston, with branches across Greater Houston and in San Antonio. It also has offices outside Texas, including in the Atlanta area and Arizona. Ask whether it lends in your state if you are outside Texas.

What are Wallis Bank's SBA loan rates?

Wallis Bank prices within SBA limits. With prime at 7.00%, the maximum variable 7(a) rate is 10.00% for loans over $350,000, 11.50% for $250,001 to $350,000, 13.00% for $50,001 to $250,000 and 13.50% for $50,000 or less.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Wallis Bank?

Wallis Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
