Florida First Capital Finance Corporation, Inc. (FL)

SBA loan rates, programs and alternatives for this Certified Development Company in Florida.

Florida First Capital Finance Corporation is a Certified Development Company (CDC). CDCs provide the SBA-backed 40% portion of 504 loans for owner-occupied real estate and long-life equipment, alongside a bank first mortgage.

Rates on a 504 loan through Florida First Capital

The CDC portion of a 504 loan is priced at the SBA's monthly debenture sale, so the rate is the same at every CDC. The October 2026 effective rates were:

504 debenture termEffective fixed rate

CDCs differ in how fast they move, which banks they work with, and the fees they charge for packaging.

Before you apply

  1. Ask whether the lender is an SBA Preferred Lender and how many SBA loans it closed last year in your industry.
  2. Request the spread over prime, the fixed-rate option, and all fees in writing.
  3. Get at least one more quote. Our lender directory lists other SBA lenders in Florida.

Questions

Does Florida First Capital offer SBA loans?

Florida First Capital Finance Corporation, Inc. appears in SBA's public lending data as a participating SBA lender. Confirm which programs it offers today and whether it has Preferred Lender (PLP) status, which lets it approve loans without waiting on the SBA.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Florida First Capital?

The 504 portion is priced at the SBA's monthly debenture sale; in October 2026 that was 6.59% to 6.97% fixed. The partner bank prices its first mortgage separately.
