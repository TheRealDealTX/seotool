Citizens Community Federal SBA loans (WI)

Citizens Community Federal N.A. is an Eau Claire, Wisconsin bank that began as a credit union and now makes SBA loans in Wisconsin and Minnesota.

Citizens Community Federal N.A. is headquartered in Eau Claire, Wisconsin, and owned by Citizens Community Bancorp, which trades on Nasdaq under CZWI. It started in 1938 and spent decades as Citizens Community Federal Credit Union. Members voted in 2001 to convert to a mutual savings bank, and the company later became publicly owned. It now operates as a national bank.

The bank serves western and central Wisconsin, centered on the Chippewa Valley around Eau Claire, along with markets in Minnesota. It has shifted its focus from consumer lending toward commercial and agricultural banking over the past decade, partly through acquisitions of other community banks in the region. Lending decisions stay regional.

Its business clients include manufacturers, contractors, dairy and other farm operations, healthcare providers and Main Street businesses. SBA lending is a regular part of its commercial banking, and the company has cited SBA loan sale gains in its public earnings reports. That suggests an established SBA process rather than an occasional sideline.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A Chippewa Valley or Minnesota borrower might use Citizens Community Federal for a 7(a) loan to buy a business, purchase or expand a building, or finance equipment. Because the bank sells guaranteed portions of its SBA loans, it has reason to keep its SBA pipeline active. Ask whether it is a Preferred Lender, who underwrites SBA deals and how long closing usually takes. For larger real estate projects, ask about pairing with a Wisconsin CDC on a 504 loan.

SBA programs to ask Citizens Community Federal about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Citizens Community Federal make SBA loans?

Yes. Citizens Community Federal N.A. is an active SBA lender, and its parent company has reported gains on SBA loan sales in its earnings. Talk with a commercial banker about 7(a) or 504 loans for acquisitions, real estate or equipment.

Is Citizens Community Federal a credit union?

Not anymore. It began as Citizens Community Federal Credit Union in Eau Claire, converted to a savings bank in 2001, and now operates as a national bank, Citizens Community Federal N.A. You do not need credit union membership to borrow.

What rate will I pay on an SBA loan from Citizens Community Federal?

The bank sets your rate within SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps are 10.00% for loans over $350,000, 11.50% for $250,001 to $350,000, 13.00% for $50,001 to $250,000 and 13.50% at $50,000 or less.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Citizens Community Federal?

Citizens Community Federal sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
