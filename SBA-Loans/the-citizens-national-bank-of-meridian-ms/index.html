Citizens National Bank of Meridian SBA loans (MS)

The Citizens National Bank of Meridian is a long-established Mississippi community bank that lends to local businesses, including through SBA programs.

The Citizens National Bank of Meridian is a Mississippi-chartered community bank based in Meridian, in the east-central part of the state. It is one of the older independent banks in Mississippi and has stayed locally owned rather than selling to a regional or national holding company. That matters for business owners, because loan decisions are made by people who know the local economy.

The bank's home market is Lauderdale County and the surrounding east Mississippi counties, and over time it has added offices in other Mississippi markets. It offers the usual mix of deposit accounts, treasury services and commercial lending. Business borrowers tend to be owner-operated companies: medical and professional practices, contractors, retailers, timber and agriculture-related firms, and service businesses.

Because it is a full-service commercial bank, Citizens National handles conventional loans as well as government-guaranteed lending. SBA loans give it a way to say yes to deals that fall a little short of conventional standards, such as a thin down payment, a younger business, or collateral that does not fully cover the loan.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A Meridian-area owner would most likely come to Citizens National for a 7(a) loan to buy an existing local business, refinance expensive debt, or fund equipment and working capital. For an owner-occupied building, the bank can act as the first-lien lender in a 504 deal alongside a Mississippi CDC, which keeps the down payment near 10%. A relationship-driven bank like this usually wants your deposit accounts too, so expect the conversation to cover operating accounts and treasury services, not just the loan.

SBA programs to ask Citizens National Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Citizens National Bank of Meridian make SBA loans in Mississippi?

Yes. The Citizens National Bank of Meridian appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. It mainly serves businesses in Meridian and its other Mississippi markets. Call a commercial lender to confirm which SBA programs it offers today and whether it can approve loans under SBA delegated authority.

What SBA loan rate could I get from Citizens National Bank?

The bank sets your rate, but it cannot exceed SBA maximums. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your actual rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit history and cash flow.

Is Citizens National Bank of Meridian a good fit for a business purchase?

It can be, especially if the business is in east Mississippi and the bank already knows the market. SBA 7(a) loans for acquisitions run up to 10 years and generally need at least 10% equity injection under current SBA rules. Bring tax returns for the seller's business and a clear transition plan.
