Idaho Central Credit Union (ID)

SBA loan rates, programs and alternatives for this credit union in Idaho.

Idaho Central Credit Union (ICCU) is the largest credit union in Idaho. Credit unions can make SBA loans to member businesses just like banks.

Maximum SBA rates at Idaho Central CU

Every SBA lender must stay at or below the SBA's caps, which are tied to the prime rate (7.00%). A loan over $350,000 can't carry a variable rate above 10.00%.

Questions

Does Idaho Central CU offer SBA loans?

Idaho Central Credit Union appears in SBA's public lending data as a participating SBA lender. Confirm which programs it offers today and whether it has Preferred Lender (PLP) status, which lets it approve loans without waiting on the SBA.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Idaho Central CU?

The lender sets your rate below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% to 13.50%. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
