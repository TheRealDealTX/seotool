First Financial Bank SBA loans (AR)

First Financial Bank is a community bank headquartered in El Dorado, Arkansas, serving south and east Arkansas and parts of north Mississippi.

First Financial Bank is headquartered in El Dorado, the seat of Union County in south Arkansas. It began in the 1930s as First Federal Savings and Loan Association and took the First Financial Bank name in 1999. It is not related to the similarly named banks in Abilene, Texas or Cincinnati, Ohio, which is a common source of confusion.

The bank has expanded beyond Union County over time. It acquired Cornerstone Bank in Senatobia, Mississippi, and in 2019 bought The First National Bank of Wynne, adding several east Arkansas counties. It has also moved into Little Rock. Its main office is in downtown El Dorado, where the bank renovated a new headquarters building.

Clients include small and mid-size businesses typical of south Arkansas: timber and energy-related firms, agriculture, healthcare, retailers, contractors and professional practices. The bank offers commercial real estate, equipment and operating loans, and uses SBA programs where a borrower needs a longer term or a lower down payment. Lenders are based in the communities they serve.

How business owners use SBA loans here

For a south or east Arkansas business, First Financial Bank would most likely use a 7(a) loan to finance a business purchase, an owner-occupied building or equipment, especially in smaller towns where property values limit conventional loan amounts. A 504 loan with an Arkansas CDC can work for larger real estate projects. Ask whether SBA loans are underwritten in El Dorado, how long approvals take and whether the bank has Preferred Lender status.

SBA programs to ask First Financial Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is First Financial Bank in El Dorado the same as First Financial Bank in Texas?

No. First Financial Bank of El Dorado, Arkansas is a separate community bank. It is not affiliated with First Financial Bank of Abilene, Texas or First Financial Bank of Cincinnati, Ohio. Check the bank's location before you apply or compare reviews.

Does First Financial Bank in Arkansas make SBA loans?

Yes. First Financial Bank of El Dorado appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender in Arkansas. Speak with a commercial lender about 7(a) or 504 financing, and ask which offices handle SBA applications and how long approval usually takes.

What SBA loan rates does First Financial Bank charge?

The bank sets your rate below SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps range from 10.00% for loans above $350,000 to 13.50% for $50,000 or less. Loan size, term, collateral and credit determine where you land.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from First Financial Bank?
