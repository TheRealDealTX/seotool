Heritage Family Credit Union SBA loans (VT)

Heritage Family Federal Credit Union is a member-owned credit union based in Rutland, Vermont that offers business loans, including SBA-guaranteed financing.

Heritage Family Federal Credit Union is a member-owned financial cooperative headquartered in Rutland, Vermont. Like other credit unions, it is not-for-profit and returns earnings to members through rates and fees rather than paying shareholders. Its roots are in the Rutland area, and it has grown into one of the more visible credit unions in that part of the state.

Most of its members are individuals and families, but it also serves small businesses. Business members tend to be local: contractors, retailers, restaurants, landlords, farms and service companies in Rutland County and nearby central Vermont towns. Business owners usually need to become members before they can borrow. Many of them are sole proprietors or family firms that also keep personal accounts at the credit union.

Credit unions face federal limits on how much business lending they can hold. SBA-guaranteed loans help a credit union like Heritage Family serve business members within those limits, and they let it offer longer terms than its conventional commercial loans. The SBA-guaranteed portion of a loan generally does not count toward that cap, which gives the credit union more room to help local owners grow.

How business owners use SBA loans here

For a Rutland-area owner who already banks with Heritage Family, an SBA 7(a) loan is a way to finance a building, a business purchase or equipment without leaving a familiar institution. Credit union SBA programs are often smaller and more hands-on than a big bank's, so expect personal service but possibly slower turnaround on complex deals. For larger real estate projects, ask whether the credit union works with a Vermont CDC on 504 loans.

SBA programs to ask Heritage Family Credit Union about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Heritage Family Federal Credit Union offer SBA loans?

Heritage Family Federal Credit Union is listed in SBA lending data as a participating lender. It serves business members mainly in the Rutland area and central Vermont. Ask its business lending team which SBA programs it offers today and how large a loan it can handle.

Do I need to be a member to get a business loan from Heritage Family?

Generally yes. Credit unions lend to members, so you or your business will usually need to open an account and meet the membership requirements first. Membership is typically tied to where you live or work. Ask the credit union how eligibility works for a business entity.

Are credit union SBA loan rates lower than bank SBA rates?

Not automatically. Every SBA lender works under the same maximum rates. With prime at 7.00%, 7(a) caps run from 10.00% to 13.50% depending on loan size. A credit union may price below the cap, but compare full offers, including fees, term and collateral requirements.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Heritage Family Credit Union?

Heritage Family Credit Union sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
