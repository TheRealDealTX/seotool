Coastal States Bank SBA loans (SC)

Coastal States Bank is a Hilton Head Island community bank that also runs a national SBA and government-guaranteed lending team.

Coastal States Bank is headquartered on Hilton Head Island, South Carolina. Its community banking offices are in Hilton Head and Bluffton, and it also serves Savannah and metro Atlanta. Locally, it lends to businesses, professionals and real estate owners in the Lowcountry. It is a relatively young bank compared with many community banks in the Southeast.

Beyond its home market, the bank operates a Government Guaranteed Lending group that makes SBA loans to borrowers across the country, with lenders based in several regions. It received SBA Preferred Lender status in 2018, which lets it approve qualifying SBA loans without sending each file to the SBA. Its lenders work across many industries.

That combination means two kinds of clients: local Lowcountry businesses that want a hometown bank, and owners elsewhere who find Coastal States specifically for SBA financing. For the second group, the draw is a lender that does government-guaranteed loans every day. Those borrowers often never visit a branch, working instead with an SBA lender assigned to their region.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Coastal States is set up for borrowers who want a lender that does SBA loans regularly, even if the business is nowhere near Hilton Head. Typical uses include buying an existing business, purchasing or building owner-occupied property, and funding expansion with a 7(a) loan. Preferred Lender status can shorten approval time. Because its SBA team lends nationally, you may work mostly by phone and email with a regional lender. Compare its quote with a local bank and a 504 loan through your state's CDC.

SBA programs to ask Coastal States Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Coastal States Bank make SBA loans outside South Carolina?

Yes. Coastal States Bank describes itself as a national SBA lender, and its Government Guaranteed Lending team works with borrowers in many states. Ask which lender covers your region, which programs fit your deal, and how much of the process can be done remotely.

Is Coastal States Bank an SBA Preferred Lender?

Coastal States Bank received SBA Preferred Lender Program status in 2018. Preferred Lenders can approve eligible loans under delegated authority, which usually speeds up the process. Confirm current status and expected timelines with the bank when you apply.

Where is Coastal States Bank located?

Coastal States Bank is headquartered on Hilton Head Island, South Carolina, with a community office in Bluffton. It also serves Savannah, Georgia and the Atlanta area, and its SBA lenders are based in several parts of the country.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Coastal States Bank?

Coastal States Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
