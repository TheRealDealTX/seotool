Heritage Bank SBA loans (KY)

Heritage Bank, Inc. is a community bank based in Burlington, Kentucky that lends to small businesses across Northern Kentucky and the Cincinnati area.

Heritage Bank, Inc. is a community bank headquartered in Burlington, in Boone County, Kentucky. It is not related to the several other banks across the country that also use the Heritage Bank name. Its home market is Northern Kentucky, the fast-growing suburbs just south of Cincinnati across the Ohio River.

Boone, Kenton and Campbell counties have seen strong growth in logistics, distribution and light industry, helped by the Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky International Airport in Boone County. Heritage Bank serves the small businesses that grow up around that activity: contractors, trucking and logistics firms, retailers, restaurants, medical practices and real estate investors.

The bank offers commercial real estate, equipment and working capital lending alongside business deposit accounts. SBA programs give it a way to finance start-ups, acquisitions and owner-occupied buildings for Northern Kentucky companies that need longer terms or lower down payments. Local loan officers know the area's growth corridors and commercial real estate market, which can help when a property appraisal or a project budget needs a second look.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Northern Kentucky's growth means many owners are buying or building their own space instead of leasing. Heritage Bank could fund that through a 7(a) loan with up to 25 years on real estate, or a 504 loan with a Kentucky CDC for larger projects. Contractors and logistics firms may also use 7(a) for equipment and trucks. Because Cincinnati is right across the river, ask whether the bank will lend to an Ohio-based business or property if that matters to your deal.

SBA programs to ask Heritage Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is Heritage Bank in Burlington, KY the same as other Heritage Banks?

No. Heritage Bank, Inc. in Burlington, Kentucky is a separate community bank from other institutions named Heritage Bank, such as the one based in Washington state. SBA records list it as Heritage Bank, Inc. Make sure you are contacting the Northern Kentucky bank.

Does Heritage Bank make SBA loans in Northern Kentucky?

Heritage Bank, Inc. appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. It serves businesses in Boone, Kenton and Campbell counties and the wider Cincinnati area. Ask a commercial lender which SBA programs it currently offers and its typical loan sizes.

What SBA loan rates does Heritage Bank offer?

The bank sets your rate, capped by SBA. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums are 10.00% for loans over $350,000 and up to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. A 504 loan's debenture portion is fixed, recently about 6.57% for a 25-year term.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Heritage Bank?

Heritage Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
