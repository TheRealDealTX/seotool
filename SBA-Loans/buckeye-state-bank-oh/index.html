Buckeye State Bank SBA loans (OH)

Buckeye State Bank is an Ohio community bank based in Powell, north of Columbus, that makes SBA-guaranteed loans alongside its conventional business lending.

Buckeye State Bank is a state-chartered community bank headquartered in Powell, Ohio, in the northern suburbs of Columbus. It began in 1885 as The Citizens Bank of De Graff, a small-town bank in Logan County, and moved its main office to Powell in 2018 as it shifted its focus toward the Columbus market. The bank still keeps offices in west-central Ohio where it started.

The bank grew south when it acquired Rockhold Bank, a community bank in Ross County, adding offices around Bainbridge and the Chillicothe area. The result is a small branch network that spans growing Columbus suburbs and rural southern Ohio towns. Decisions are made locally, which matters for borrowers whose loan does not fit a national bank's scorecard.

Its business clients are mostly owner-operated companies: contractors, professional practices, service firms, retailers and family businesses buying property or equipment. The bank participates in the SBA 7(a) program and uses it for deals that need a longer term or lower down payment than a conventional loan allows. Expect a relationship approach, with deposit accounts and treasury services usually part of the package.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A borrower in central Ohio would most often bring Buckeye State Bank a 7(a) loan for buying an existing business, purchasing an owner-occupied building or refinancing expensive equipment debt. Because it is a smaller bank, an SBA guarantee lets it take on loans larger or riskier than it would hold alone. Ask whether it has Preferred Lender status, since that affects how fast approval comes. For a larger building project, ask whether it will act as the first-lien lender on a 504 loan with a Columbus-area CDC.

SBA programs to ask Buckeye State Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Buckeye State Bank make SBA loans?

Yes. Buckeye State Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating 7(a) lender. Call its business lending team to confirm which SBA programs it offers today, the loan sizes it prefers, and whether it can approve loans in-house under SBA's Preferred Lender Program.

Where is Buckeye State Bank located?

Its headquarters is in Powell, Ohio, a suburb north of Columbus. The bank started in De Graff in Logan County and also has offices in southern Ohio from its acquisition of Rockhold Bank, including the Bainbridge area of Ross County.

What rate will I pay on a Buckeye State Bank SBA loan?

The bank sets your rate, but it cannot exceed SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps range from 10.00% for loans over $350,000 to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. Your actual rate depends on size, term, collateral and cash flow.
