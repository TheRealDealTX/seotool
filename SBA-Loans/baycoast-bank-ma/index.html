BayCoast Bank SBA loans (MA)

BayCoast Bank is a savings bank on the Massachusetts South Coast that grew out of Fall River's Citizens-Union Savings Bank.

BayCoast Bank is a savings bank headquartered in Swansea, Massachusetts. Its roots are in Fall River, where it operated for well over a century before moving its main offices to Swansea. The bank was known as Citizens-Union Savings Bank until it adopted the BayCoast Bank name in 2012. It operates as a community-focused savings bank.

Its branches cover the South Coast and southeastern Massachusetts, centered on Fall River, Swansea, Somerset and nearby communities, and it also reaches across the state line into Rhode Island. That footprint follows the old mill cities and the small towns between them. Lenders tend to know these local markets street by street.

BayCoast lends to individuals and to local businesses, with commercial real estate, construction, equipment and working capital loans. Typical business clients are family-owned companies, trades, restaurants, marine and fishing-related firms, and owners of mixed-use buildings. Its lenders are used to older industrial and mixed-use properties, which can be harder to finance than newer buildings, and that experience matters when an SBA loan involves real estate.

How business owners use SBA loans here

On the South Coast, an SBA loan through a local bank like BayCoast often helps a long-time employee or family member buy out a retiring owner, since a 7(a) loan can cover goodwill that a conventional loan will not. Owner-occupied buildings in Fall River and nearby towns are another fit, either as a 7(a) real estate loan or a 504 loan with a Massachusetts CDC. SBA Express can work for smaller equipment purchases or a working capital line for seasonal businesses.

SBA programs to ask BayCoast Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does BayCoast Bank make SBA loans?

BayCoast Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask its commercial lenders which SBA programs it uses today, its usual deal sizes, and whether it partners with a Massachusetts CDC as the first-lien lender on 504 real estate projects.

Is BayCoast Bank the old Citizens-Union Savings Bank?

Yes. BayCoast Bank is the Fall River institution formerly called Citizens-Union Savings Bank. It changed its name to BayCoast Bank in 2012 and later moved its headquarters to Swansea. Accounts and loans carried over to the new name.

Where does BayCoast Bank lend?

BayCoast focuses on southeastern Massachusetts, centered on Fall River, Swansea, Somerset and surrounding South Coast towns, plus nearby Rhode Island. Businesses outside the area can ask, but local banks generally prefer borrowers in their market.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from BayCoast Bank?

BayCoast Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
