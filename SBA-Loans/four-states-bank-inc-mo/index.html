Four States Bank SBA loans (MO)

Four States Bank is a community bank headquartered in Carthage, Missouri, serving businesses in the Joplin and Springfield areas.

Four States Bank is headquartered in Carthage, Missouri, in Jasper County. Its name comes from the Four States region, where Missouri, Kansas, Oklahoma and Arkansas meet near Joplin. The bank is a subsidiary of Four States Bancshares and recently opened a new headquarters building in downtown Carthage, which the state described as a significant local investment.

Its offices are in Carthage, Joplin and Springfield, so it serves the two largest business centers in southwest Missouri. This is a region with a mix of manufacturing, trucking and logistics, healthcare, agriculture and a large base of small, family-run businesses. Many of these companies need real estate, equipment and working capital financing as they grow or change hands.

As a community bank, Four States Bank offers commercial real estate, equipment and operating loans along with deposit and treasury services. Business owners deal directly with local lenders. SBA loans give the bank a way to help borrowers with less equity, a shorter track record or a purchase price that needs a longer repayment term.

How business owners use SBA loans here

In southwest Missouri, Four States Bank would most likely use SBA 7(a) loans for buying an existing business, purchasing an owner-occupied building, or funding equipment for trucking, construction or manufacturing firms. A smaller bank can be flexible and responsive, but ask whether it underwrites SBA loans in-house or works with an outside SBA service provider, and whether it has Preferred Lender status. For larger property deals, ask which Missouri CDC it partners with on 504 loans.

SBA programs to ask Four States Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.
