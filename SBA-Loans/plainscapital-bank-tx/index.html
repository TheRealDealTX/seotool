PlainsCapital Bank SBA loans (TX)

PlainsCapital Bank is a Dallas-based Texas commercial bank, owned by Hilltop Holdings, that started in Lubbock and lends across the state's major metros.

PlainsCapital Bank is headquartered in Dallas and is the banking arm of Hilltop Holdings, a Dallas financial services company that also owns PrimeLending and HilltopSecurities. The bank traces its roots to Lubbock, where it began as a West Texas bank before expanding statewide. It still has a strong presence in Lubbock alongside its Dallas headquarters.

Today PlainsCapital operates in most major Texas markets, including Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin, Houston, San Antonio and West Texas. It focuses on commercial banking for privately held companies, business owners and professionals, along with private banking and treasury services. Its lenders tend to know the Texas economy by region, from energy and agriculture in the west to services and real estate in the metros.

Business clients include mid-size companies, medical and professional practices, real estate investors and owner-occupants, and family businesses. SBA lending is one tool among many. The bank typically uses it when a borrower's equity, collateral or time in business does not fit its conventional loan standards. Many SBA borrowers here are growing companies that already bank with PlainsCapital and need a longer repayment term for a building or acquisition.

How business owners use SBA loans here

With PlainsCapital, an SBA 7(a) loan usually makes sense when a business is buying a company, buying out a partner, or purchasing an owner-occupied building with less money down than a conventional loan requires. For larger real estate or equipment projects, ask about a 504 structure with a Texas CDC. As a bank focused on relationships, it may expect your operating accounts. Ask whether SBA loans are underwritten by a dedicated team and how long approval and closing typically take.

SBA programs to ask PlainsCapital Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does PlainsCapital Bank make SBA loans?

Yes. PlainsCapital Bank is listed in SBA lending data as a participating lender in Texas. Contact a commercial banker in your city to ask which SBA programs it offers, the loan sizes it prefers, and whether it holds SBA Preferred Lender status.

Who owns PlainsCapital Bank?

PlainsCapital Bank is owned by Hilltop Holdings, a Dallas-based financial holding company. Hilltop also owns PrimeLending, a mortgage lender, and HilltopSecurities. The bank itself is headquartered in Dallas and began in Lubbock. Hilltop is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange.

What will a PlainsCapital SBA loan cost?

Your rate is set by the bank under SBA maximums. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% for loans over $350,000 to 13.50% for $50,000 or less. Ask for the spread over prime, total fees and prepayment terms.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from PlainsCapital Bank?

PlainsCapital Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
