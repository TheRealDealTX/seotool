Lake Ridge Bank SBA loans (WI)

Lake Ridge Bank, the Madison-area community bank once known as Monona Bank, lends to small and mid-size businesses around Dane County.

Lake Ridge Bank is a community bank headquartered in Monona, Wisconsin, on the southeast edge of Madison. For most of its history it operated as Monona Bank, and many local business owners still search for it under that name. The bank changed its name to Lake Ridge Bank as it grew beyond the city of Monona itself.

The bank's offices are concentrated in Madison and surrounding Dane County communities. That keeps its lending focused on the greater Madison economy, which mixes state government, university-linked companies, health care, construction trades, restaurants and a steady stream of owner-operated service businesses. Lenders know the local market well, from east side retail corridors to the light industrial parks around Madison, and can judge Dane County property values without outside help.

Lake Ridge serves business clients alongside consumers, with commercial loans, owner-occupied real estate financing, equipment loans, lines of credit and treasury services. Borrowers who banked with Monona Bank keep the same institution, just under the newer name. Business owners can expect to work with a local relationship banker who handles both the loan request and the day-to-day banking, rather than a call center.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A Dane County owner would most likely use Lake Ridge Bank for a 7(a) loan to buy an existing local business, such as a restaurant, trade contractor or clinic, where the seller is retiring and collateral is thin. Owner-occupied property is the other common case. Madison commercial space is not cheap, and a 504 loan, with the bank on the first lien and a Wisconsin CDC on the second, keeps the down payment near 10%. Smaller equipment or working capital needs may fit SBA Express.

SBA programs to ask Lake Ridge Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is Lake Ridge Bank the same as Monona Bank?

Yes. Lake Ridge Bank is the same Monona, Wisconsin bank that operated for years as Monona Bank. The name changed, but the institution, its local ownership focus and its business lending continued. If you search for Monona Bank SBA loans, this is the bank you want.

Does Lake Ridge Bank offer SBA loans?

Lake Ridge Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask a commercial lender which programs it offers now, typical loan sizes, and whether it acts as the first-lien lender on SBA 504 projects with a Wisconsin CDC.

What rate will Lake Ridge Bank charge on an SBA loan?

The bank sets your rate, but it cannot exceed SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your actual rate depends on size, term, collateral and cash flow.
