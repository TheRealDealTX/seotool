Banc of California SBA loans

Banc of California is a large California commercial bank, combined with PacWest and Pacific Western Bank in 2023, that also appears in SBA records outside the state.

Banc of California is a California-based commercial bank headquartered in Los Angeles. Although SBA data lists this record under Colorado, the bank is a California institution. In 2023 it merged with PacWest Bancorp, the parent of Pacific Western Bank, and the combined company kept the Banc of California name. Pacific Western Bank no longer operates under its old name.

The merger made Banc of California one of the larger banks based in California. It operates branches across Southern California and other parts of the state, and the former Pacific Western Bank business brought commercial lending offices in several other states, which may explain the Colorado listing. Confirm coverage for your state directly.

The bank focuses on commercial clients: privately held businesses, real estate owners and investors, entertainment and venture-backed companies, and nonprofits. It offers commercial real estate loans, lines of credit, treasury management and specialty lending. SBA loans are a smaller piece of its overall business than at a community bank, but remain an option for qualifying borrowers.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Banc of California makes the most sense for an established business that wants a large bank's treasury services along with financing. An SBA 7(a) loan could support an acquisition or owner-occupied real estate purchase, and a 504 loan with a California CDC can fund a larger building. Because SBA is not its main focus, ask early whether it has an SBA team in your area and how long approval takes. A dedicated SBA lender may move faster on smaller loans.

SBA programs to ask Banc of California about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Banc of California offer SBA loans?

Banc of California appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. SBA loans are a smaller part of its business than commercial real estate and corporate banking, so ask a business banker whether SBA 7(a) or 504 financing is available for your deal and location.

Why is Banc of California listed in Colorado?

Banc of California is headquartered in Los Angeles. Its 2023 merger with PacWest Bancorp brought in Pacific Western Bank's offices and lending operations, which reached beyond California. SBA records sometimes list a lender under a location outside its home state for that kind of reason.

What are Banc of California's SBA rates?

The bank prices under SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% for loans over $350,000 to 13.50% for $50,000 or less. The 25-year 504 debenture rate was about 6.57% in November 2026.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Banc of California?

Banc of California sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
