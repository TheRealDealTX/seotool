Canyon View Credit Union SBA loans (UT)

Canyon View Federal Credit Union is a Salt Lake City credit union that operated for decades as University First Federal Credit Union.

Canyon View Federal Credit Union is a member-owned credit union headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah. For many years it was University First Federal Credit Union, often called UFirst, and it grew up on and around the University of Utah campus. It changed its name to Canyon View as it broadened its membership beyond the university community.

Membership now covers people who live or work in Salt Lake County and several surrounding counties. The credit union runs branches across the Salt Lake Valley and has extended into other parts of the state, including St. George. It describes itself as having around two dozen branches, and members can also use shared credit union branches and ATMs.

Alongside consumer accounts and loans, Canyon View offers business checking and business lending for members. Its business borrowers are mostly small, owner-operated companies along the Wasatch Front, from contractors and retailers to professional services firms. Business lending is a smaller part of the credit union than consumer lending, so owners should ask early about loan sizes and the documents needed.

How business owners use SBA loans here

For a Salt Lake Valley business, an SBA loan through a credit union like Canyon View makes most sense for a modest, straightforward need: equipment, working capital, refinancing higher-cost debt, or buying a small owner-occupied space. The SBA guarantee lets a credit union say yes to a longer term or smaller down payment than its standard business loan. Larger acquisitions or construction projects may be better suited to a bank with a dedicated SBA team, or to a 504 loan through a Utah CDC. Membership is required.

SBA programs to ask Canyon View Credit Union about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is Canyon View Credit Union the same as University First?

Yes. Canyon View Federal Credit Union is the Salt Lake City credit union formerly named University First Federal Credit Union, or UFirst. It changed its name as membership expanded beyond the University of Utah community. Member accounts carried over to the new name.

Does Canyon View Federal Credit Union offer SBA loans?

Canyon View appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask its business lending staff whether SBA loans are offered today, what loan sizes they handle, and how long approval takes. Smaller credit union SBA programs often focus on simpler loans.

Who can join Canyon View Credit Union for a business loan?

Membership is generally open to people who live or work in Salt Lake County and certain nearby Utah counties, and to their businesses. You will need to join before a loan closes. Ask the credit union to confirm your eligibility at the start.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Canyon View Credit Union?

Canyon View Credit Union sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
