Bravera Bank SBA loans (ND)

Bravera Bank is a Dickinson, North Dakota bank, known until 2021 as American Bank Center, that lends to farms, ranches and businesses across the region.

Bravera Bank is a community bank headquartered in Dickinson, in western North Dakota. It operated as American Bank Center until June 2021, when it announced the Bravera name. Before that, the institution had used earlier names including Bank Center First, based in Bismarck. The new name was chosen to unite the bank and its affiliated businesses under one brand.

Bravera's branches are spread across North Dakota, from the Bakken and western ranch country to Bismarck and other central and eastern communities, with locations in neighboring states as well. The Bravera brand also covers related insurance and wealth businesses. Most of its markets are small towns where the local bank is a key source of business credit.

Agriculture is a large part of its customer base, alongside small and mid-size businesses tied to energy, construction, trucking, retail and health care. That mix means lenders are used to cyclical cash flow and weather-driven revenue swings. Its bankers generally know local markets and families well, which can matter when a small-town business changes hands.

How business owners use SBA loans here

In North Dakota, an SBA loan through Bravera is likely to support a main street or energy-related business rather than a farm, since most farm credit runs through conventional or FSA programs. Typical uses are buying a business in a small town where few buyers have large down payments, building or buying a shop or warehouse, or financing trucks and heavy equipment over a longer term. For owner-occupied real estate, ask about a 504 loan with a North Dakota CDC.

SBA programs to ask Bravera Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is Bravera Bank the same as American Bank Center?

Yes. Bravera Bank is the Dickinson, North Dakota bank that was called American Bank Center until it announced its new name in June 2021. Customers kept their accounts and loans, and the bank's lending focus on businesses and agriculture continued.

Does Bravera Bank offer SBA loans?

Bravera Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask a business banker which SBA programs it actively offers, whether it handles 504 first liens with a North Dakota CDC, and how long approvals usually take.

Can I use an SBA loan for a farm or ranch?

SBA loans can fund some agricultural businesses, but many farm and ranch loans go through conventional bank programs or the USDA Farm Service Agency instead. A lender like Bravera can tell you which option fits your operation and whether SBA rules allow it.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Bravera Bank?

Bravera Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
