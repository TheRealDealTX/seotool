West Coast Community Bank SBA loans (CA)

West Coast Community Bank is the new name of Santa Cruz County Bank, a Santa Cruz community bank with a long record of SBA lending on the Central Coast.

West Coast Community Bank is headquartered in Santa Cruz, California. Until April 1, 2025, it was called Santa Cruz County Bank, a name many local business owners still use. The new name matches its holding company, West Coast Community Bancorp, and reflects a wider territory than a single county. Its roots remain in Santa Cruz County.

The rebrand followed a merger. On October 1, 2024, 1st Capital Bancorp merged into West Coast Community Bancorp, and 1st Capital Bank, based in the Monterey and Salinas area, merged into Santa Cruz County Bank. The combined bank now serves businesses across Santa Cruz, Monterey, Santa Clara and San Luis Obispo counties.

Santa Cruz County Bank built a reputation as an active SBA and agricultural lender, serving growers, wineries, hospitality businesses, tech and professional firms, and Main Street shops. West Coast Community Bank carries that business forward. It offers commercial real estate, lines of credit and equipment loans, and uses SBA programs for borrowers who need longer terms.

How business owners use SBA loans here

On the Central Coast, high property values make SBA loans especially useful. A 7(a) or 504 loan can let a business buy its own building with 10% to 20% down instead of the larger down payment a conventional loan would need. West Coast Community Bank also sees business acquisitions, winery and hospitality deals, and working capital needs. If you banked with Santa Cruz County Bank or 1st Capital Bank, ask your existing lender, since the teams carried over.

SBA programs to ask West Coast Community Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is West Coast Community Bank the same as Santa Cruz County Bank?

Yes. Santa Cruz County Bank changed its name to West Coast Community Bank on April 1, 2025, after merging with 1st Capital Bank. It is the same institution, headquartered in Santa Cruz, and existing SBA loans and relationships continued under the new name.

Does West Coast Community Bank make SBA loans?

Yes. The bank continues the SBA lending that Santa Cruz County Bank was known for, including 7(a) loans and 504 projects. Ask about loans for owner-occupied buildings, business acquisitions and working capital across Santa Cruz, Monterey, Santa Clara and San Luis Obispo counties.

What are West Coast Community Bank's SBA rates?

The bank sets rates within SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% for loans over $350,000 to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. The 25-year 504 debenture rate was about 6.97% in October 2026.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from West Coast Community Bank?

West Coast Community Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
