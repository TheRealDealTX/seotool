Centennial Bank SBA loans (FL)

Centennial Bank is the Conway, Arkansas subsidiary of Home BancShares, with a large branch network in Florida where SBA records list it.

Centennial Bank is the banking subsidiary of Home BancShares, a publicly traded holding company based in Conway, Arkansas. The bank grew through a long series of acquisitions, combining several community banks under the Centennial name. Although its roots are in Arkansas, a large share of its branches and deposits are in Florida, which is why SBA data lists it there.

Its Florida offices stretch from the Panhandle to Central and South Florida, including the Florida Keys. Centennial also operates in Alabama and, since acquiring Happy State Bank in 2022, across much of Texas. It also runs Centennial Commercial Finance Group, a lending unit based in New York that makes larger commercial loans across the country.

For small businesses, Centennial works like a regional community bank: local lenders, commercial real estate loans, equipment financing and deposit services. Common clients include hospitality and tourism businesses, medical practices, contractors and real estate owner-occupants. SBA loans fill gaps where a conventional loan would need too much cash down or too short a term.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A Florida business owner might use Centennial for a 7(a) loan to buy a hotel, restaurant or service business, or for a 504 loan on an owner-occupied building with a Florida CDC. Because Centennial is a large regional bank with a conservative credit culture, expect solid documentation requirements and clear collateral. Ask which office handles SBA loans in your market, whether approvals are done in-house, and how its SBA pricing compares with its conventional commercial real estate terms.

SBA programs to ask Centennial Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Centennial Bank offer SBA loans in Florida?

Yes. Centennial Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender listed under Florida, where it has a large branch network. Ask a local commercial lender which SBA programs it offers in your area and who underwrites them.

Is Centennial Bank a Florida bank or an Arkansas bank?

Both, in practice. Centennial Bank is headquartered in Conway, Arkansas, as the subsidiary of Home BancShares. A large portion of its branches are in Florida, and it also operates in Texas and Alabama. Its Florida offices make local lending decisions within the bank's credit policy.

What are Centennial Bank's SBA loan rates?

Centennial sets the rate, but SBA caps apply. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) rates cannot exceed 10.00% on loans over $350,000 or 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Compare the spread, fees and term with at least one other lender.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Centennial Bank?

Centennial Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
