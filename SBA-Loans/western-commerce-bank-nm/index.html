Western Commerce Bank (NM)

SBA loan rates, programs and alternatives for this bank in New Mexico.

Western Commerce Bank is a community bank based in Carlsbad, New Mexico, serving businesses in southeastern New Mexico. Local ownership means credit decisions are made close to the borrower.

Maximum SBA rates at Western Commerce Bank

Every SBA lender must stay at or below the SBA's caps, which are tied to the prime rate (7.00%). A loan over $350,000 can't carry a variable rate above 10.00%.

Before you apply

  1. Ask whether the lender is an SBA Preferred Lender and how many SBA loans it closed last year in your industry.
  2. Request the spread over prime, the fixed-rate option, and all fees in writing.
  3. Get at least one more quote. Our lender directory lists other SBA lenders in New Mexico.

Questions

Does Western Commerce Bank offer SBA loans?

Western Commerce Bank appears in SBA's public lending data as a participating SBA lender. Confirm which programs it offers today and whether it has Preferred Lender (PLP) status, which lets it approve loans without waiting on the SBA.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Western Commerce Bank?

The lender sets your rate below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% to 13.50%. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
