Entities like this can include small specialty lenders, lending affiliates or organizations that made a limited number of SBA loans in a given period. How do I check whether an Arizona SBA lender is legitimate?

Ask the lender to confirm its SBA participation in writing and name the programs it uses. You can contact your local SBA district office in Phoenix or use Lender Match on SBA.gov. Be cautious of anyone who asks for large upfront fees or guarantees approval before reviewing your financials.
