Arizona Capital Source SBA 504 loans (AZ)

Arizona Capital Source is a Tucson nonprofit Certified Development Company that provides SBA 504 financing.

Arizona Capital Source is a nonprofit Certified Development Company (CDC) based in Tucson, Arizona. CDCs are the community lenders that make the SBA-backed portion of 504 loans, so Arizona Capital Source works alongside banks rather than in place of them: the bank funds the first mortgage and the CDC funds the second, fixed-rate piece.

Its focus is 504 financing for owner-occupied commercial real estate and long-life equipment. A typical project is a business buying, building or renovating the property it operates from, with the bank lending about 50% of the cost, the CDC about 40% and the owner putting in about 10%. Third-party lender databases also show it handling some 7(a) loans and working outside Arizona.

Because a CDC packages and services the 504 portion over 10 to 25 years, the choice of CDC matters: it affects how fast your loan closes and who you deal with after closing. Ask Arizona Capital Source which banks it partners with in your area, its timeline from application to debenture sale, and what fees it charges for packaging.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Use Arizona Capital Source when your business wants to own its building or buy major equipment and you want a long fixed rate on part of the financing. The 504 debenture portion priced at 6.57% for 25 years in November 2026, which often beats a bank's own fixed rate. You can bring your own bank or ask the CDC for a referral. Your business generally has to occupy at least 51% of an existing building.

Questions

What is Arizona Capital Source?

Arizona Capital Source is a nonprofit Certified Development Company in Tucson that provides SBA 504 loans. It funds the SBA-backed second piece of a 504 project, usually about 40% of the cost at a fixed rate, while a bank funds the first mortgage and you contribute about 10%.

How does a 504 loan through a CDC work?

A bank lends about 50% of the project with a first lien, the CDC lends up to 40% through an SBA-guaranteed debenture with a fixed rate set at a monthly bond sale, and you put in about 10%, more for start-ups or special-purpose buildings. The CDC packages the SBA side and services it for the life of the loan.

What SBA loan rate should I expect in Arizona?

SBA rate caps are the same nationwide. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums are 10.00% for loans over $350,000, 11.50% from $250,001 to $350,000, 13.00% from $50,001 to $250,000 and 13.50% for $50,000 or less. Any quote above those levels is a red flag.

What rate will I get on a 504 loan through Arizona Capital Source?

The CDC portion of a 504 loan carries a fixed rate set at the SBA's monthly debenture sale, the same for every CDC. The November 2026 sale priced the 25-year debenture at 6.57% including fees. The bank's first mortgage is priced separately, so ask for both rates.
