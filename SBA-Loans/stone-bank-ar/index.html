Stone Bank SBA loans (AR)

Stone Bank is a Little Rock-based Arkansas community bank, founded in Mountain View, that offers business and SBA-guaranteed loans.

Stone Bank is an Arkansas community bank that takes its name from Stone County in the Ozark foothills, where it started in Mountain View. As it grew, it moved its headquarters to Little Rock: in 2023 it bought the former Bank OZK headquarters building on Chenal Parkway in west Little Rock, and it opened the renovated building as its new headquarters in January 2025.

The bank offers personal and business banking, with commercial lending as a core focus. Its business customers are mostly Arkansas companies: retailers, contractors, healthcare providers, hospitality businesses, and owners buying or improving commercial property. Tourism and small manufacturing are part of the economy in north-central Arkansas, and the bank's loan mix reflects that.

SBA lending has been one of the ways Stone Bank serves business owners beyond its home county. Government guarantees let a smaller bank take on loans for start-ups, acquisitions and real estate that might not fit its conventional lending limits. They also give borrowers longer repayment periods, which helps cash flow in the early years after a purchase or expansion.

How business owners use SBA loans here

An Arkansas owner would most likely use Stone Bank for a 7(a) loan to buy a business, purchase or renovate a commercial building, or add equipment and working capital in one package. Smaller banks often know the SBA paperwork well because it is a meaningful part of their business, which can help first-time borrowers. Ask whether the bank can approve loans under SBA delegated authority, how long closing usually takes, and whether it will consider a start-up with the minimum 10% equity injection.

SBA programs to ask Stone Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Stone Bank make SBA loans?

Stone Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender in Arkansas. It offers commercial loans to small businesses, and SBA guarantees are part of that toolkit. Contact a commercial lender to confirm current programs, loan size limits and whether it serves your part of the state.

What rate will Stone Bank charge on an SBA 7(a) loan?

The rate is negotiated, but it cannot exceed SBA caps. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) maximums are 10.00% above $350,000, 11.50% from $250,001 to $350,000, 13.00% from $50,001 to $250,000 and 13.50% for $50,000 or less.

Can I use a Stone Bank SBA loan to buy commercial real estate?

Yes, SBA loans can finance owner-occupied commercial property. A 7(a) loan allows terms up to 25 years for real estate. A 504 loan pairs the bank's first mortgage with a CDC debenture and usually needs about 10% down. Your business must generally occupy at least 51% of an existing building.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Stone Bank?

Stone Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
