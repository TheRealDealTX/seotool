Hanover Community Bank SBA loans (NY)

Hanover Community Bank is a Long Island-based commercial bank with a dedicated SBA lending operation serving the New York metro area.

Hanover Community Bank is a New York State-chartered commercial bank headquartered in Mineola, on Long Island. Its parent, Hanover Bancorp, is publicly traded. The bank serves businesses, real estate investors and consumers with branches on Long Island and in New York City. It operates as a mid-size community bank with a commercial lending focus.

SBA lending is a larger part of Hanover's business than at many community banks its size. Its 2021 acquisition of Savoy Bank, a Manhattan bank known for SBA and small business lending, brought in an experienced government-guaranteed lending team. Hanover has since promoted SBA loans as a core product. For borrowers, that history means staff who handle SBA files routinely.

Its business clients include family-owned companies, restaurants, medical and professional practices, contractors and owners of mixed-use and commercial property across the five boroughs, Nassau and Suffolk counties. Many of these borrowers are first-generation owners or family businesses that need a lender comfortable with New York property values, mixed-use buildings and business acquisitions, which is where SBA terms often help close the gap.

How business owners use SBA loans here

A New York borrower would typically approach Hanover for a 7(a) loan to buy a business, buy into a partnership, or purchase owner-occupied commercial property. Because the bank has a dedicated SBA team, it is a reasonable first call for deals that conventional banks turn down for short operating history or limited collateral. In a high-cost market, the 25-year term on SBA real estate loans keeps payments manageable. Smaller working capital and equipment needs may fit SBA Express. SBA programs to ask Hanover Community Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Hanover Community Bank NY offer SBA loans?

Yes, SBA lending is one of Hanover Community Bank's stated focus areas, strengthened by its acquisition of Savoy Bank. Ask about current programs, loan size ranges and whether a loan can be approved under Preferred Lender authority, which usually speeds up closing.

Where is Hanover Community Bank located?

Hanover Community Bank is headquartered in Mineola, New York, on Long Island. It has branches in Nassau and Suffolk counties and in New York City. Its SBA lenders may also work with borrowers elsewhere in the region, so ask if you are outside that footprint.

What happened to Savoy Bank?

Savoy Bank, a Manhattan bank active in SBA lending, was acquired by Hanover Bancorp in 2021 and merged into Hanover Community Bank. Former Savoy borrowers are now Hanover customers, and much of the SBA lending experience moved to Hanover with the deal.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Hanover Community Bank?

Hanover Community Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
