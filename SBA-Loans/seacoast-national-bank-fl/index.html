Seacoast Bank SBA loans (FL)

Seacoast National Bank, known as Seacoast Bank, is a Stuart-based Florida bank that offers SBA loans to small businesses throughout the state.

Seacoast National Bank, which markets itself as Seacoast Bank, is headquartered in Stuart on Florida's Treasure Coast. Its parent, Seacoast Banking Corporation of Florida, is publicly traded and is one of the largest banks based in Florida. The bank has been part of the Treasure Coast economy for most of the past century.

Over the last decade Seacoast grew quickly by acquiring other Florida banks, which extended its reach to South Florida, Orlando, Tampa Bay, the Gulf Coast and North Florida. Today it operates only in Florida, which makes it a statewide lender with a local focus rather than a national bank. Borrowers in most major Florida metro areas can reach a Seacoast banker.

Its business clients include professional practices, contractors, hospitality and restaurant businesses, healthcare providers, marine businesses and commercial real estate owners. Seacoast offers commercial lending, treasury management and wealth services, with SBA loans as part of its small-business lending lineup. Larger clients can also access treasury tools and commercial real estate financing as they grow, so the SBA loan is often the start of a broader relationship.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Florida's steady flow of business sales and new arrivals makes acquisition lending a big part of SBA activity, and Seacoast's statewide footprint suits a buyer acquiring a practice, franchise or service company anywhere in Florida. Owner-occupied real estate is another common use, through 7(a) or a 504 loan with a Florida CDC. Because Seacoast has absorbed many smaller banks, ask who your SBA contact will be and whether approvals are made locally or by a central team.

SBA programs to ask Seacoast Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Seacoast Bank offer SBA loans?

Seacoast National Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender and serves businesses across Florida. Ask a business banker which SBA programs it offers, such as 7(a), 504 or SBA Express, and whether it approves loans under SBA delegated authority.

Can I get a Seacoast Bank SBA loan outside the Treasure Coast?

Yes. Seacoast is headquartered in Stuart but has offices across much of Florida, including South Florida, Orlando, Tampa Bay and other markets. Borrowers typically work with a banker in their region. It does not operate outside Florida, so out-of-state businesses should look elsewhere.

What SBA loan rates does Seacoast Bank charge?

Seacoast sets each rate below SBA maximums. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps are 10.00% for loans over $350,000, 11.50% for $250,001 to $350,000, 13.00% for $50,001 to $250,000 and 13.50% for $50,000 or less.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from Seacoast Bank?

Seacoast Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
