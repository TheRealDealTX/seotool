Horizon Bank SBA loans (Indiana)

Horizon Bank is a commercial bank headquartered in Michigan City, Indiana, with branches across Indiana and Michigan.

Horizon Bank is headquartered in Michigan City, Indiana, near the Lake Michigan shore in the northwest corner of the state. It is the bank subsidiary of Horizon Bancorp, which trades on Nasdaq. Several unrelated banks in Nebraska, Texas and other states also use the Horizon name, so confirm you are looking at the Indiana bank.

Horizon has grown through a series of acquisitions of smaller Indiana and Michigan banks and branches. Today its footprint runs through northern and central Indiana and across much of Michigan. That acquisition history means some customers first knew their local branch under a different bank name before it became Horizon.

The bank serves consumers and businesses, with commercial real estate, equipment, working capital and agricultural lending, along with treasury management. Business clients range from small family firms to mid-size manufacturers and distributors. Horizon is large enough to handle bigger commercial deals but still lends through local market teams, so small business owners usually work with a banker based in their region.

How business owners use SBA loans here

With a footprint full of manufacturing towns and family businesses, Horizon is the kind of regional bank an Indiana or Michigan owner would approach for a 7(a) loan to buy a company from a retiring founder, or to finance equipment and a building together. For owner-occupied plants and warehouses, a 504 loan, with Horizon on the first lien and an Indiana or Michigan CDC on the second, can provide a long fixed rate. Ask whether SBA loans go through a dedicated team.

SBA programs to ask Horizon Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does Horizon Bank in Indiana offer SBA loans?

Horizon Bank of Michigan City, Indiana appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask its commercial bankers which SBA programs it actively uses, typical loan sizes, and whether it has Preferred Lender status for faster approvals.

Is Horizon Bank Indiana the same as Horizon Bank in Texas or Nebraska?

No. Several separate banks use the Horizon name. This page covers Horizon Bank headquartered in Michigan City, Indiana, which operates in Indiana and Michigan. Check the website and headquarters before you apply so you contact the right lender.

What rate will Horizon Bank charge on an SBA loan?

Horizon sets the rate, within SBA limits. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. The rate you get depends on your cash flow, collateral, term and loan size.
