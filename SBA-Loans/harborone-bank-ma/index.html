HarborOne Bank SBA loans (MA)

HarborOne Bank grew out of a large Brockton, Massachusetts credit union and merged into Eastern Bank in November 2025.

HarborOne Bank is based in Brockton, Massachusetts. It began as a credit union and operated for years as HarborOne Credit Union, one of the larger credit unions in the state, before converting to a savings bank charter in 2013. The holding company, HarborOne Bancorp, later became publicly traded. The bank also built a sizable mortgage business under the HarborOne Mortgage name.

Its branches are concentrated south of Boston and in Rhode Island, where it expanded through the 2019 acquisition of Coastway Bancorp in Warwick. Business clients range from small retailers and restaurants to contractors, professional firms and commercial real estate investors. The bank has also run small-business education programs for local entrepreneurs.

Eastern Bankshares completed its acquisition of HarborOne on November 1, 2025, and HarborOne Bank merged into Eastern Bank the same day. HarborOne branches, customers and loans are now part of Eastern Bank, and the HarborOne Mortgage business was folded into Eastern in early 2026. If you are looking for a former HarborOne banker, you will now reach them through Eastern Bank, which is also an SBA lender.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Former HarborOne business customers on the South Shore and in Rhode Island now apply through Eastern Bank. Typical SBA uses are unchanged: a 7(a) loan for an acquisition, a buildout or working capital, or a 504 loan to buy your own building. Ask Eastern for the SBA lender covering your area, whether it can approve under Preferred Lender authority, and get a written term sheet before you commit to a seller or landlord.

SBA programs to ask HarborOne Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Is HarborOne Bank part of Eastern Bank now?

Yes. Eastern Bankshares completed its acquisition of HarborOne Bancorp on November 1, 2025, and HarborOne Bank merged into Eastern Bank. SBA loans for former HarborOne customers are now handled by Eastern Bank. Existing HarborOne loans are serviced by Eastern.

Was HarborOne Bank a credit union?

Yes. HarborOne operated as HarborOne Credit Union in Brockton before converting to a savings bank charter in 2013. The conversion let it expand commercial lending, including SBA loans, beyond the business lending limits that apply to credit unions. It later formed a public holding company, HarborOne Bancorp.

What SBA loan rates does HarborOne Bank charge?

Rates are set loan by loan within SBA limits. With prime at 7.00%, the maximum variable 7(a) rate is 10.00% for loans over $350,000 and up to 13.50% for loans of $50,000 or less. A 504 loan's debenture portion is fixed, about 6.57% for 25 years recently.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from HarborOne Bank?

HarborOne Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
