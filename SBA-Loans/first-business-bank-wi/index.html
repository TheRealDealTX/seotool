First Business Bank SBA loans (WI)

First Business Bank is a Madison, Wisconsin commercial bank that focuses on business clients and offers SBA loans in Wisconsin and the Kansas City area.

First Business Bank is the bank subsidiary of First Business Financial Services, a publicly traded company headquartered in Madison, Wisconsin. Unlike most community banks, it does not run a large retail branch network. It was built to serve businesses, business owners and executives, and most of its deposits and loans come from commercial relationships.

The bank operates in Madison, Milwaukee and northeast Wisconsin, and in the Kansas City metro area. It entered Kansas City in 2014 by acquiring Alterra Bank, which was later rebranded as First Business Bank. Its services include commercial lending, treasury management, equipment finance, asset-based lending and private wealth management. Its parent trades on Nasdaq.

Clients are typically established, privately held companies such as manufacturers, distributors, professional services firms and commercial real estate owners. SBA lending lets the bank serve smaller or growing businesses and ownership transitions that need longer terms or lower equity than a conventional loan. The bank's bankers tend to work with a smaller number of clients in more depth than a retail-focused lender would.

How business owners use SBA loans here

Because First Business Bank concentrates on commercial clients, it tends to fit owners of established companies more than very early start-ups. A common use is a 7(a) loan to fund a management buyout or acquisition, where SBA terms of up to 10 years and lower equity requirements make the deal work. Manufacturers may use a 504 loan for a plant or heavy equipment, with debenture limits up to $5.5 million. Expect detailed underwriting and a banker who will also pitch treasury services.

SBA programs to ask First Business Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does First Business Bank offer SBA loans in Madison and Milwaukee?

First Business Bank appears in SBA lending data as a participating lender and serves businesses in Madison, Milwaukee and other Wisconsin markets, as well as Kansas City. Ask a commercial banker which SBA programs it uses and whether it handles them through a dedicated SBA team.

Is First Business Bank a good fit for buying a business?

It can be, especially for acquisitions of established Wisconsin or Kansas City companies. SBA 7(a) loans can finance business purchases with terms up to 10 years, and current SBA rules generally call for at least 10% equity injection. The bank will look closely at historical cash flow and the buyer's experience.

What SBA loan rates does First Business Bank charge?

Rates are priced per deal within SBA limits. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. For 504 projects, the CDC debenture is fixed, recently about 6.97% for 25 years.
