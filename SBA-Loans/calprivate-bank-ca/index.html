CalPrivate Bank SBA loans (CA)

CalPrivate Bank is a La Jolla-based business bank that lends to companies, professionals and real estate owners in coastal Southern California.

CalPrivate Bank is a state-chartered commercial bank headquartered in La Jolla, in San Diego. It is the subsidiary of Private Bancorp of America. The bank opened in 2006 and operated as San Diego Private Bank before taking the CalPrivate Bank name as it expanded beyond San Diego County. It operates as a business-focused institution with a small number of offices.

Its offices run along coastal Southern California, including San Diego, Orange County and the Los Angeles area, with a more recent office in Santa Barbara County. The bank positions itself as a private and business bank rather than a mass-market retail lender. Offices are few compared with big regional banks, so clients usually work closely with a named relationship manager who handles both credit and deposits.

CalPrivate's clients are mostly operating businesses, professional practices, business owners and real estate investors. For a small business, that usually means a banker who expects full financials and wants the deposit relationship along with the loan. Clients who keep their operating accounts and owner deposits at the bank are generally in a stronger position when they ask for credit, including SBA loans.

How business owners use SBA loans here

In Southern California, SBA financing with a business bank like CalPrivate is often about real estate and transitions. A 504 loan lets an owner buy a building with around 10% down, which matters when property prices are this high. A 7(a) loan suits buying a dental or medical practice, an insurance agency or another cash-flowing business where hard collateral is limited. Expect the bank to look closely at debt service coverage and the owner's experience rather than just credit score.

SBA programs to ask CalPrivate Bank about

  • 7(a). The SBA's main loan, up to $5 million, for working capital, equipment, buying a business, refinancing and real estate.
  • 504. Long-term fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment. The bank funds about 50%, a CDC up to 40%, and you put in about 10%.
  • SBA Express. A 7(a) loan up to $500,000 that the lender approves under its own authority, so it closes faster. The rate cap is the same as standard 7(a).

Program availability changes. Ask the lender which SBA programs it closes most often and whether it has SBA Preferred Lender status, which lets it approve loans in-house.

Questions

Does CalPrivate Bank offer SBA loans?

CalPrivate Bank is listed in SBA lending data as a participating lender. Ask its relationship managers which SBA programs it actively offers, its typical loan size, and whether it can approve SBA loans in-house as a Preferred Lender or must send files to the SBA.

Is CalPrivate Bank the same as San Diego Private Bank?

Yes. CalPrivate Bank is the bank that previously operated as San Diego Private Bank. It adopted the CalPrivate name as it opened offices beyond San Diego County. The parent company is Private Bancorp of America, based in La Jolla.

What SBA rate can I expect from CalPrivate Bank?

Rates are negotiated, but they must stay under SBA maximums. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps range from 10.00% to 13.50% depending on loan size. For real estate, compare a 504 loan, where the CDC portion recently priced near 6.57% for 25 years.

What rate will I get on an SBA loan from CalPrivate Bank?

CalPrivate Bank sets your rate at or below the SBA maximum. With prime at 7.00%, variable 7(a) caps run from 10.00% on loans over $350,000 to 13.50% on loans of $50,000 or less. Your rate depends on loan size, term, collateral, credit and cash flow.
