How to choose an SBA lender

Volume matters. Lenders that close many SBA loans a year are usually Preferred Lenders, approve in-house, and know which deals the SBA will accept. Community banks can still be the better fit when you want a local relationship or a smaller loan. See the top SBA lenders by volume.

SBARates.com tracks the SBA's maximum loan rates and public SBA lending data so you can compare lenders before you apply. We're not a lender or a government site, and nothing here is a loan offer.
