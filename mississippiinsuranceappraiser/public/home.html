The short version

Covered isn’t the same as paid in full

Most underpaid Mississippi claims aren’t denied. They’re short: a roof slope left off, decking nobody priced, a floor that can’t be matched, a code upgrade the estimate skipped. Mississippi Insurance Appraiser exists for the space between “covered” and “paid in full.”

We work on one question — the amount of loss. As your Mississippi Insurance Appraiser, we inspect the property ourselves, write our own estimate and defend it in front of the carrier’s appraiser and, when needed, a neutral umpire.

  • Our own measurements. Never a marked-up copy of the carrier’s estimate.
  • Numbers that hold up. Priced to survive an umpire’s review, not to impress on day one.
  • Straight answers. If your claim needs an attorney or a supplement instead, we say so on the first call.

Process

From first call to signed award

The appraisal clause turns an open-ended argument into a defined process with a binding result. Here’s how Mississippi Insurance Appraiser moves a file through it.

  1. 1

    Free file review

    We read the policy, the carrier’s estimate and your photos, and tell you whether appraisal fits — or whether you need someone else.

  2. 2

    Independent scope

    On-site inspection, measurements and a line-item estimate priced for your part of Mississippi.

  3. 3

    Written demand

    Appraisal is demanded in writing and your appraiser is named inside the policy’s window.

  4. 4

    Appraiser to appraiser

    We work the differences line by line with the carrier’s appraiser. Many files narrow sharply here.

  5. 5

    Umpire & award

    Anything left goes to a neutral umpire. Two signatures set the amount of loss.

FAQ

Questions people ask a Mississippi Insurance Appraiser

Straight answers about how appraisal works, what it costs and when it’s the wrong move.

More answers Claim terms
What does a Mississippi Insurance Appraiser do?

We value property damage when you and your insurer agree the loss is covered but disagree on what it costs. As your appointed appraiser we inspect, build an independent line-item estimate, work with the carrier’s appraiser and, if needed, present unresolved items to a neutral umpire.

Is an appraiser the same as a public adjuster?

No. A public adjuster handles and negotiates the whole claim. An appraiser works inside the policy’s appraisal clause on one question: the amount of loss. We don’t negotiate claims outside appraisal and we don’t give legal advice.

Can appraisal settle a wind-versus-flood argument on the Coast?

Appraisal can value covered wind damage. Whether damage was caused by wind or by flood is often a coverage question, and coverage disputes generally sit outside appraisal. Those may need a Mississippi attorney.

Is the appraisal award binding?

Under a typical clause, an award signed by any two of the three panel members sets the amount of loss. That’s why the file should be complete before anyone demands appraisal.

What does it cost?

Each side usually pays its own appraiser and splits the umpire. Our fee is agreed in writing before work starts, and the first conversation is free.

Do you cover my county?

Yes. We work in all 82 Mississippi counties, from DeSoto County to Hancock County and from the Delta to the eastern state line.
