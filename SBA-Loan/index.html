Who can't get an SBA loan

SBA 7(a) and 504 loans are for for-profit small businesses. Nonprofits, passive real-estate investors, lenders, and businesses that restrict who can use them (like many private clubs) are generally not eligible. Nonprofit child care centers can use SBA Microloans.
