SBA loans for environment, conservation and wildlife organizations

NAICS 813312 · Program that usually fits: Usually not eligible

Organizations in this code are usually nonprofits, which can't use SBA 7(a) or 504 loans. For-profit environmental businesses, such as remediation or consulting firms, are classified under other NAICS codes and can qualify. Nonprofits can look at CDFIs, foundation program-related investments or conventional loans.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Land and facilities
  • Program equipment

What to use instead

Look at loans from a CDFI or local loan fund, a bank's nonprofit lending group, or program-related investments from foundations. If you also run a for-profit affiliate, that business may qualify for SBA financing on its own.

Questions

Can a conservation organization business get an SBA loan?

Usually not, because most organizations in this industry are nonprofits, and SBA 7(a) and 504 loans require a for-profit business.

What is the NAICS code for environment, conservation and wildlife organizations?

NAICS 813312. Lenders use it to check SBA size standards and eligibility.
