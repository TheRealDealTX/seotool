Restaurant SBA loans: rates and eligibility

Full-Service Restaurants · NAICS 722511 · Program that usually fits: 7(a)

Restaurants are one of the most common SBA borrower types. Lenders look for owner experience in the industry and usually want a down payment of at least 10% on a startup or change of ownership.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Buying an existing restaurant
  • Kitchen equipment and build-out
  • Franchise fees
  • Working capital for opening

Questions

Can a restaurant business get an SBA loan?

Yes, if the business is for-profit, meets SBA size standards for NAICS 722511, and can show it can repay. Restaurants are one of the most common SBA borrower types.

What is the NAICS code for full-service restaurants?

NAICS 722511. Lenders use it to check SBA size standards and eligibility.
