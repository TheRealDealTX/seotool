SBA loans for snack bars, juice bars and coffee shops

Snack and Nonalcoholic Beverage Bars · NAICS 722515 · Program that usually fits: 7(a)

Snack bars, juice bars, coffee shops and smoothie stands all sit under NAICS 722515, and most of the SBA lending in this category is franchise lending: a branded coffee or smoothie concept with a build-out budget and a franchisor's sales data. Independent shops qualify too, with a bit more evidence that the location will work.

Who qualifies

Franchisees opening a coffee or smoothie unit, operators buying an existing shop, and owners adding a second location. Lenders want a lease as long as the loan, a contractor's build-out bid, and either franchisor unit economics or an operating history.

Which SBA program fits

7(a) for a new unit

Covers the franchise fee, build-out, equipment and opening working capital on a 10-year term.

SBA Express for smaller shops

Kiosks and small shops under $500,000 can close faster through Express.

7(a) for buying a shop

Purchases of an existing shop, including goodwill, are eligible.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Franchise fee and build-out
  • Espresso machines, blenders, refrigeration and POS
  • Leasehold improvements including plumbing and ventilation
  • Opening inventory and the first months of payroll
  • Buying an existing shop

Typical loan amounts, down payment and terms

Most loans in this category run $100,000 to $500,000. Equity injection is 10% to 20%, higher for first-time owners and independent concepts. Lenders focus on sales per square foot and drive-through potential.

Questions about juice bar business loans

Can I get an SBA loan to open a coffee or juice bar?

Yes. Coffee shops, juice bars, smoothie stands and snack bars are eligible for 7(a) and Express loans. Franchise units are approved most often; independents need a strong location analysis or an existing shop's numbers.

How much does a juice bar SBA loan cover?

Typically $100,000 to $500,000, covering build-out, equipment, the franchise fee if any, and opening working capital. The SBA allows up to $5 million, but single shops rarely need more.

What down payment is needed for a snack bar loan?

10% to 20% of total project cost. A franchisee with experience in the brand can be at the low end; a first-time independent owner at the high end.

What is the interest rate on a coffee shop SBA loan?

A loan between $50,001 and $250,000 is capped at prime plus 6, or 13.00% today, and between $250,001 and $350,000 at 11.50%. Expect pricing near the cap for start-ups.

Is a kiosk or food truck eligible?

Yes. Kiosks and mobile units are eligible businesses. Lenders treat the kiosk or truck as equipment, usually on a 7-to-10-year term, and will want a location agreement or route plan.
