Medical practice loans: SBA rates and terms

Medical Practices · NAICS 621111 · Program that usually fits: 7(a)

A medical practice loan is one of the strongest SBA applications a lender sees: physicians default rarely, revenue is insured, and the collateral problem that keeps banks away from new practices is exactly what the SBA guarantee solves. Lenders will finance 100% of a practice purchase or a start-up for a physician with a clean credit file.

Who qualifies

Physicians buying into a practice, buying a retiring doctor's practice, or opening their own office. Lenders want board certification or eligibility, a production history or employment contract, and for a purchase, the seller's tax returns and a practice valuation. Student debt is expected and doesn't disqualify you.

Which SBA program fits

7(a) for practice purchase or start-up

Covers the purchase price including goodwill, build-out, equipment and working capital, up to $5 million, usually on a 10-year term.

504 for the office building

Buying the medical office or building a new one fits a 504 loan at a fixed rate, with the practice's own 7(a) covering equipment and working capital.

7(a) for a partner buy-in

Partial buy-ins are eligible when the buyer will own at least 20% and the practice can show it supports the payment.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Buying an existing practice, including goodwill and patient records
  • Opening a new office: build-out, exam rooms and furnishings
  • Imaging, lab and EHR equipment
  • Working capital through credentialing and the first billing cycles
  • Buying the medical office building

Typical loan amounts, down payment and terms

Practice purchases run $300,000 to $2 million, and start-ups $250,000 to $1 million. Many SBA lenders finance 100% of a practice purchase for physicians, with no equity injection, because the cash flow covers it. Terms are 10 years for the practice and 25 for real estate.

Questions about medical practice loans

Can a doctor get an SBA loan to buy a practice?

Yes. Practice purchases are a core 7(a) use. Many lenders offer 100% financing to physicians with good credit, covering goodwill, equipment and working capital. The seller's tax returns and a valuation support the loan amount.

How much can I borrow for a medical practice?

Up to $5 million on a 7(a) loan. Lenders size the loan to the practice's cash flow after your salary, typically keeping total debt service under 75% of available cash flow.

Does student loan debt stop me from getting a practice loan?

No. Lenders expect physicians to carry student debt and underwrite around it, as long as payments are current. Income-driven repayment plans are accepted at their actual monthly payment.

What interest rate does a medical practice SBA loan have?

Loans over $350,000 are capped at prime plus 3, or 10.00% today. Physicians with strong credit often see prime plus 1.5 to 2.5. Some lenders offer fixed rates for the full term on medical loans.

Can I use an SBA loan to start a practice from scratch?

Yes. Start-up practice loans fund build-out, equipment and 6 to 12 months of working capital. Lenders want a business plan with payer mix and a credentialing timeline, and they prefer a physician who has practiced in the area already.
