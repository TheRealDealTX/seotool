Golf course financing: SBA loans and rates

Golf Courses and Country Clubs · NAICS 713910 · Program that usually fits: 7(a) or 504

Public, daily-fee golf courses can qualify. Private clubs that limit membership for reasons other than capacity are not eligible for SBA loans, which rules out many country clubs.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Buying a course
  • Clubhouse renovation
  • Irrigation and maintenance equipment
  • Refinancing existing debt

Example: a $3,500,000 loan

Questions

Can a golf course business get an SBA loan?

Yes, if the business is for-profit, meets SBA size standards for NAICS 713910, and can show it can repay. Public, daily-fee golf courses can qualify.

What is the NAICS code for golf courses and country clubs?

NAICS 713910. Lenders use it to check SBA size standards and eligibility.
