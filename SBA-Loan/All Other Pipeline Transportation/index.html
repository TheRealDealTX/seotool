SBA loans for pipeline Transportation

All Other Pipeline Transportation · NAICS 486990 · Program that usually fits: 504 or 7(a)

The 504 program fits long-life equipment and facilities at a fixed rate. Projects must meet SBA size standards for the industry, so larger pipeline operators may not qualify as small businesses.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Pipe, pumps and compression equipment
  • Yards and facilities
  • Working capital for contracts

Example: a $2,000,000 loan

At today's maximum variable 7(a) rate of 10.00% over 20 years, the payment would be about $19,300 a month. Most well-qualified borrowers get a lower rate. On a 504 project, the SBA-backed 40% would be fixed at 6.97% (October 2026, 25-year). Try your own numbers.

Questions

Can a pipeline business get an SBA loan?

Yes, if the business is for-profit, meets SBA size standards for NAICS 486990, and can show it can repay. The 504 program fits long-life equipment and facilities at a fixed rate.

What is the NAICS code for all other pipeline transportation?

NAICS 486990. Lenders use it to check SBA size standards and eligibility.
