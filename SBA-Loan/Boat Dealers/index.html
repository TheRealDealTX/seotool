Boat dealer financing with an SBA loan

Boat Dealers · NAICS 441222 · Program that usually fits: 7(a) or 504

Boat dealer financing through the SBA works the same way it does for RV and car dealers: the SBA finances the dealership, the marina slips, the service shop and the purchase of an existing dealer, but not the boats on the lot. A separate floor-plan line covers inventory, and lenders want it committed before they close.

Who qualifies

Operators buying an existing dealership, dealers buying their waterfront property, and marine service businesses adding storage or a service yard. Lenders ask for manufacturer agreements, a floor-plan commitment, and three years of financials from any seller. Seasonality is expected; lenders underwrite to a full-year cash flow.

Which SBA program fits

504 for waterfront real estate

Finances the lot, showroom, docks and service building at a fixed rate with 10% down.

7(a) for buying a dealership

Covers goodwill, parts inventory, equipment and working capital, up to $5 million.

7(a) for storage and service

Adding dry-stack storage, lifts or a rigging shop fits a 10-year loan.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Buying the dealership and marina real estate
  • Buying an existing boat dealer
  • Service bays, lifts and rigging equipment
  • Dry-stack or rack storage buildings
  • Parts inventory and working capital

Typical loan amounts, down payment and terms

Real-estate projects run $1 million to $5 million; dealership purchases $500,000 to $3 million. Equity is 10% to 15% on a 504 and 10% to 20% on a 7(a). Boat inventory is excluded from SBA loans, so a floor-plan line is part of every structure.

Example: a $1,200,000 loan

At today's maximum variable 7(a) rate of 10.00% over 25 years, the payment would be about $10,904 a month. Most well-qualified borrowers get a lower rate. On a 504 project, the SBA-backed 40% would be fixed at 6.97% (October 2026, 25-year). Try your own numbers.

Questions about boat dealer financings

Can a boat dealer get an SBA loan?

Yes, for the real estate, the business purchase, service equipment and working capital. The SBA doesn't finance boat inventory, so the dealer also needs a floor-plan line from a bank or manufacturer finance company.

Does the SBA finance floor plans for boat dealers?

No. Inventory floor plans are excluded from 7(a) and 504 loans. The lender will review your floor-plan terms to confirm the dealership's cash flow covers both loans.

What down payment does boat dealer financing need?

10% for a 504 real-estate project, 15% for a start-up, and 10% to 20% for a 7(a) purchase. Waterfront land you already own can count as equity.

What is the term on a marine dealership SBA loan?

25 years for real estate on a 504 or a real-estate-heavy 7(a). Dealership purchases and equipment run 10 years.

What rate will a boat dealer pay?

The 504 SBA portion priced at 6.97% fixed for 25 years in October 2026. A 7(a) loan over $350,000 is capped at prime plus 3, or 10.00% today.
