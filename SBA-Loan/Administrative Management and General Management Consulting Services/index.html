SBA loans for management Consultants

Administrative Management and General Management Consulting Services · NAICS 541611 · Program that usually fits: 7(a)

Consulting firms have few physical assets, so lenders look closely at cash flow, client concentration and the owner's experience. SBA 7(a) loans are a common way to finance buying a practice from a retiring owner.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Hiring ahead of new engagements
  • Buying a consulting practice
  • Office build-out
  • Working capital while waiting on receivables

Example: a $350,000 loan

At today's maximum variable 7(a) rate of 11.50% over 10 years, the payment would be about $4,921 a month. Most well-qualified borrowers get a lower rate. Try your own numbers.

Questions

Can a management consulting business get an SBA loan?

Yes, if the business is for-profit, meets SBA size standards for NAICS 541611, and can show it can repay. Consulting firms have few physical assets, so lenders look closely at cash flow, client concentration and the owner's experience.

What is the NAICS code for administrative management and general management consulting services?

NAICS 541611. Lenders use it to check SBA size standards and eligibility.
