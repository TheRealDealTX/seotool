Gym business loans: SBA rates and eligibility

Gyms and Fitness Centers · NAICS 713940 · Program that usually fits: 7(a)

A gym business loan from an SBA lender is usually a franchise loan in disguise: most approved gym projects are branded boutique studios or franchise clubs, because the franchisor's membership data gives the lender something to underwrite. Independent gyms can qualify too, but expect to show presales or an existing member base.

Who qualifies

Franchisees opening a studio, operators buying an existing club, and established gyms expanding to a second location. Lenders want a lease that runs as long as the loan, a build-out budget from a contractor, and a membership projection with evidence behind it, such as presale numbers or a waitlist.

Which SBA program fits

7(a) for a new studio

Covers the franchise fee, build-out, equipment and 6 months of operating cash in one loan, on a 10-year term.

SBA Express for small equipment

Equipment refreshes under $500,000 can go through Express for a faster close.

504 for buying the building

Gym owners who buy their building use a 504 for the real estate and a 7(a) for equipment.

What borrowers use the money for

  • Franchise fee and training
  • Build-out: flooring, locker rooms, HVAC and sound
  • Cardio and strength equipment
  • Membership software and marketing for the presale
  • Buying an existing gym from its owner

Typical loan amounts, down payment and terms

Boutique studios run $250,000 to $600,000; full-size clubs $750,000 to $2.5 million. Equity injection is 10% to 20%, with lenders at the higher end for first-time owners and unproven concepts. Lenders focus on break-even membership, so a strong presale lowers the equity they ask for.

Questions about gym business loans

Can I get an SBA loan to open a gym?

Yes. Gyms and fitness studios are eligible, and franchise studios are approved regularly. Independent concepts need more evidence: a strong presale, an experienced owner, or an existing location with good numbers.

How much money do I need to open a gym with an SBA loan?

Plan on 10% to 20% of the total project cost in cash. For a $400,000 boutique studio, that's $40,000 to $80,000, plus any working capital the lender wants you to hold back.

What is the term on a gym business loan?

10 years for build-out, equipment and working capital. If you buy the building and it's more than half of the project, the loan can run 25 years.

What interest rate will I pay on a gym SBA loan?

A loan between $250,001 and $350,000 is capped at prime plus 4.5, or 11.50% today, and above $350,000 at 10.00%. Franchise borrowers with experience are usually priced below the cap.

Do lenders require a presale before funding a gym?

Many do for new studios. A presale of 150 to 300 founding members, depending on the concept, shows the lender the location will reach break-even. Franchisors often make this a condition too.
