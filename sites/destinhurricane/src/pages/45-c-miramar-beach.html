Different county, different playbook

Orders and shelters

Walton County Emergency Management, not Okaloosa. Zones are lettered A–E and, in the county's words, represent the level of threat from storm surge, not wind or storm category.

Alerts

Alert Walton at alertwalton.org, and NOAA Weather Radio programmed for Walton (SAME 012131).

Forecasts

NWS Tallahassee. Our tracker pulls alerts for both offices.

Shelter

Freeport High School is the county's primary shelter for the general population, pets and registered special-needs clients.

Storm notes for Miramar Beach

Miramar Beach sat inside the Isaias Hurricane Warning and the 6-to-9-foot surge forecast that ran from the Alabama line to Grayton Beach. Tornadoes are part of the picture too: Walton County's preparedness guide pictures an EF1 tornado over Sandestin in June 2023, a reminder that rain bands and summer storms can do roof damage without a hurricane.

Routes out run along US-98, either east to US-331 and the Clyde B. Wells Bridge toward Freeport and I-10, or west through Destin to the Mid-Bay Bridge. Both involve bridges that may close once sustained winds reach about 40 mph.

Resort condos and rentals

Much of Miramar Beach and Sandestin is condominium and short-term rental property. Typical claim issues:

  • Master-policy versus HO-6 responsibilities, set by each condominium's declaration.
  • Special assessments after the association pays a large hurricane deductible, and the loss-assessment rules in s. 627.714 and s. 627.70132(4). See the condo guide.
  • Lost rental income during repairs, which depends on the coverage form.
  • Owners who live elsewhere finding damage late. Florida's one-year notice clock still runs from the October 9, 2026 landfall.

The Lawgical Firm's Destin office on Emerald Coast Parkway is a short drive west. Ask for a free review if an Isaias claim is stuck.
