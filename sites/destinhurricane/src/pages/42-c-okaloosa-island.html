Two bridges, one road

US-98 runs the length of the island as Miracle Strip Parkway. To the west, the Brooks Bridge crosses Santa Rosa Sound into Fort Walton Beach and the route north on SR-85. To the east, the Marler Bridge crosses East Pass into Destin. There is no other way off by car. Okaloosa County's guidance is that bridge closures are considered once sustained winds reach 40 mph, so the window to leave closes before the worst of the storm arrives.

What a storm does here

  • Surge from the Gulf and the sound. With water on both sides, low parcels can flood from the Gulf beach or from Santa Rosa Sound.
  • Wind at height. Upper floors of towers see stronger wind than ground-level instruments record. Balcony doors, windows and rooftop equipment take the load.
  • Measured gusts. The island's pier sensor recorded 61 knots in Sally and 45 knots in Michael. In Isaias, gale-force winds bent stop signs and thrashed trees here and in Fort Walton Beach.
  • Base land next door. Eglin Air Force Base property adjoins the developed island; base areas follow base evacuation instructions.

Claims on the island

Most island housing is condominium or rental property, so claims tend to involve a master policy, an HO-6, and often lost rental income. Garages, lobbies and ground-floor units face wind-versus-surge disputes. Unit owners should report to their HO-6 carrier promptly even while the association's claim proceeds, and track any special assessment so a loss-assessment claim can be filed on time. The condo claims guide covers the deadlines.
