Water on every side

South Walton's coastal dune lakes are rare features: shallow lakes just behind the dunes that occasionally break through to the Gulf at their outfalls. In a storm, surge can push in through those outfalls while rain raises the lakes from behind, flooding nearby roads and yards. Behind the lakes lies Choctawhatchee Bay, and the communities along 30A from Dune Allen to Grayton Beach and beyond sit on a narrow, low strip in between.

The Isaias surge forecast ran 6 to 9 feet up to Grayton Beach and 4 to 7 feet east of it. Sally's damage in 2020 was mostly rain: 13.96 inches at South Walton High School, and Alaqua Creek above record flood stage with up to 4 feet of water in homes along SR-20 west of Freeport.

Getting out, and getting help

  • North: US-331 over the Clyde B. Wells Bridge to Freeport, then I-10 at DeFuniak Springs.
  • Shelter: Freeport High School, 12615 US-331 Business, Walton County's primary shelter.
  • Alerts: Alert Walton, NWS Tallahassee, NOAA Weather Radio SAME 012131.
  • Fire rescue: South Walton Fire District (non-emergency 850-267-1298).
  • Power: CHELCO serves much of South Walton; check your bill for your provider.

Claims along 30A

Second homes and rentals dominate many 30A neighborhoods, and high-value homes can exceed NFIP flood limits ($250,000 building), so private excess flood coverage is common. Claims here often turn on wind versus surge near the beach and the dune lakes, on high-end finishes that are hard to match, and on owners discovering damage late. Florida's notice deadlines run from landfall regardless of when you visit, so arrange an inspection soon after a storm. Guides: surge and flood claims · underpaid claims · claim lawyer guide.
