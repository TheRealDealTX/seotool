What is the same everywhere on this coast

  • Evacuation orders come by zone, A to E. Look yours up.
  • Bridges may close when sustained winds reach about 40 mph, and every route out of the beaches crosses at least one.
  • Homeowners policies exclude surge; flood needs a separate policy that usually takes 30 days to start.
  • Florida's one-year claim clock runs from landfall: October 9, 2026 for Isaias.
