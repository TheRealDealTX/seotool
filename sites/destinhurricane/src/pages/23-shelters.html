Is a shelter open right now?

Openings are announced storm by storm. Okaloosa: AlertOkaloosa, the county website, or the shelter information line 850-609-7000. Walton: Alert Walton, waltoncountyem.org or 850-892-8065. Or dial 2-1-1.

Walton County hurricane shelters

Walton County names one primary shelter in its preparedness guide and opens others as a storm requires:

Freeport High School

12615 US-331 Business, Freeport, FL 32439

Getting there from the beaches

From Santa Rosa Beach and 30A, US-331 north over the Clyde B. Wells Bridge leads to Freeport. From Miramar Beach, US-98 east to US-331.

In past storms Walton has warned that the shelter does not provide bedding, and has offered rides to residents who need transport through its citizen information line once activated. Register for Alert Walton at alertwalton.org so the order and the shelter announcement reach your phone.

Power and water outages

Isaias knocked out power to almost all of Destin. Electricity on this coast comes from two main providers; the name on your bill tells you which one to call.

  • Florida Power & Light (FPL) serves much of Destin, Fort Walton Beach and Okaloosa Island (the former Gulf Power territory). Outages: 1-800-468-8243 or the FPL outage map.
  • CHELCO (Choctawhatchee Electric Cooperative) serves members across Walton and Okaloosa counties, including much of South Walton. Use its outage reporting line or app.
  • Water: follow your utility's boil-water notices after a storm, such as Destin Water Users in Destin. Okaloosa County posts county notices at myokaloosa.com.

Restoration runs in priority order: hospitals, water plants and emergency services first, then main lines that serve the most customers, then neighborhood lines and individual services.
