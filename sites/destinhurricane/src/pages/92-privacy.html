Claim review requests

When you request a free review we collect your name, phone, email (optional), property city or ZIP, the status of your claim, the storm, and anything you choose to tell us about the claim and your insurer. It goes to The Lawgical Firm, P.A. so the firm can contact you. If you tick the text-message box, the firm may text you about your inquiry; message and data rates may apply and you can reply STOP at any time. We do not sell your information.

Storm alert sign-ups

If you subscribe, we store your email, optional mobile number, area and topics, and use them only to send the alerts you chose. Every message lets you unsubscribe.

Evacuation zone lookup

The address you type is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder and to Okaloosa County or Walton County GIS services to find the zone. We do not keep addresses; short-lived technical caches may hold a lookup result.

Calculators and damage log

Calculators run in your browser. The damage log is stored only in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us.

Analytics and third parties

We use Google Analytics to understand how pages are used. Pages also load data and images from NOAA, the National Weather Service, Windy.com and Google Fonts. These services receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser type.
