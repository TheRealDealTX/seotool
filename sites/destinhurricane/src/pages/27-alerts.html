Use two official systems, at least

Websites and inboxes fail when cell towers and power go down. Okaloosa and Walton both run their own notification systems, and the federal ones work even if you never sign up for anything.

AlertOkaloosa

Okaloosa County's emergency notifications for Destin, Fort Walton Beach, Niceville and the rest of the county: evacuation orders, shelter openings, boil-water notices. Sign up through AlertOkaloosa.com.

Alert Walton

Walton County's mass-notification system for Miramar Beach, Santa Rosa Beach, 30A and Freeport, including evacuation orders. Register at alertwalton.org.

Wireless Emergency Alerts

Automatic phone alerts for Hurricane, Storm Surge, Extreme Wind and Tornado Warnings. Make sure emergency alerts are switched on in your phone's settings.

NOAA Weather Radio

Battery or hand-crank. Program SAME code 012091 for Okaloosa or 012131 for Walton so it sounds for your county.
