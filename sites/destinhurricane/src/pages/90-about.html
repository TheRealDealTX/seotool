Where the storm data comes from

  • Tropical storms and advisories: NOAA's National Hurricane Center.
  • Alerts, forecasts, observations and radar: the National Weather Service offices in Mobile (Okaloosa County) and Tallahassee (Walton County), via api.weather.gov; observations from Destin Executive Airport (KDTS); radar from Eglin AFB (KEVX).
  • Evacuation zones and shelter sites: Okaloosa County Public Safety's public GIS layers and Walton County's published evacuation-zone layer and preparedness guide.
  • Address matching: the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder.
  • Storm history: NHC Tropical Cyclone Reports and advisories.
