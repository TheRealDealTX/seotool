What the firm handles

The firm's own practice list covers residential and commercial property insurance claims, including:

  • Hurricane and windstorm
  • Roof damage and roof leaks
  • Water damage
  • Plumbing leaks
  • Mold
  • Hail damage
  • Fire
  • Sinkholes
  • Theft and vandalism
  • Construction defect claims
  • Commercial property claims

Flood insurance (NFIP) claims are not on that list. If you have a flood claim alongside a wind claim, ask the firm whether it can help with the flood side or should refer it.

How a claim is handled

  1. Review the policy

    Coverage, exclusions, endorsements, deductibles and roof payment schedules.

  2. Inspect the property

    Roof, water and structural damage, looked at the way an adjuster should have.

  3. Estimate the loss

    Line-item estimates and, where cause is disputed, engineering and contractor reports.

  4. Advocate

    Present and negotiate the claim; use appraisal, mediation or litigation when needed.

Fees

The case review is free and there is no upfront fee. In the firm's words, it does not get paid unless you get paid. Before you sign, ask for the written fee agreement and how case costs are handled.
