Claim Hurricane Today is an informational website published by The Lawgical Firm, P.A., a Florida law firm that represents policyholders in property insurance claims. The guides on this site explain Florida's hurricane claim rules in plain English so homeowners can act quickly and protect their deadlines, whether or not they ever hire a lawyer.

What the firm handles

According to the firm's website, its practice covers residential and commercial property insurance claims, including:

  • Hurricane and windstorm damage
  • Roof damage and roof leaks
  • Water damage and plumbing leaks
  • Mold
  • Fire, hail, sinkhole, theft and vandalism claims
  • Construction defect claims
  • Commercial property claims

The firm reviews claims that have been delayed, underpaid or denied, and works through the policy, the insurer's estimate and the applicable Florida procedures with its clients. Learn more at the firm's hurricane and windstorm page.

Fees

The firm's website states that there are no upfront fees and that the firm does not get paid unless the client gets paid. The specific terms of any representation are set out in a written fee agreement provided before you hire the firm.
