This policy covers claimhurricanetoday.com, published by The Lawgical Firm, P.A. (“we”). Last updated October 2026.

Information you send us

When you submit the free case review form, we receive the details you enter: name, phone number, email address, property city or ZIP code, claim type and your description, together with the page you sent it from, the time and your IP address. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and evaluate whether the firm can help. It is stored on our web host and may be sent by email to the firm's intake staff.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it except with the firm's personnel and service providers who help us operate the website and respond to inquiries, or when required by law.

Information that stays in your browser

The deadline calculator remembers the dates you last entered, and the checklist remembers which boxes you ticked. Both use your browser's local storage on your own device. That information is not sent to us. You can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.

Server logs and cookies

Like most websites, our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and troubleshooting. This site does not set advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information from your browser.

Text messages and calls

If you agree on the form to be contacted, we may call, text or email you about your inquiry. You can ask us to stop at any time; for texts, reply STOP.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards for the information we receive, but no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Please do not send confidential details through the form. We keep inquiries as long as needed to respond and to meet legal and professional obligations.
