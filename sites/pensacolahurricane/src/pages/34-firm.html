How the firm handles hurricane claims

  • Review your policy — coverage, exclusions, endorsements, deductibles and roof payment schedules.
  • Inspect your property — with an adjuster's eye for roof, water and structural damage.
  • Estimate the damages — line-item estimates and, when needed, engineering, contractor and accounting reports.
  • Advocate for what you're owed — presenting the claim, negotiating, and pursuing appraisal or litigation when needed.

Fees

Case reviews are free and there are no upfront fees. The firm works on a contingency basis — no attorney fee unless there is a recovery. Ask about how case costs are handled under the written fee agreement before you sign.
