A Pensacola hurricane preparedness timeline

WhenWhat to do
Before June 1Review insurance (wind, flood, hurricane deductible). Buy flood insurance now — NFIP policies usually take 30 days to start. Trim trees, check the roof, buy shutters or cut plywood and label each piece.
Storm in the 7-day outlookCheck your zone. Refill prescriptions. Top up water, food and batteries. Make sure the car is serviced.
5 days outFill gas tanks and generator fuel cans. Get cash. Photograph and video your home inside and out.
Watch issued (~48 h)Put up shutters, bring in outdoor items, move boats, secure important papers in a waterproof bag. Decide where you will go.
Warning issued (~36 h)If ordered to evacuate, go. Otherwise finish preparations, freeze water, fill bathtubs, charge everything, set fridge to coldest setting.
Tropical-storm winds arriveStay inside, away from windows. Do not drive. Keep the NOAA radio on for tornado and extreme wind warnings.

Protecting a Pensacola home

Most homes built in Escambia and Santa Rosa counties since the early 2000s fall under Florida Building Code wind requirements; in this area design wind speeds generally run from roughly 140 to 160 mph or more depending on how close you are to the coast. Older homes benefit most from:

  • Opening protection — rated shutters or impact glass on every window, slider and garage door. A breached opening lets wind pressurize the house and lift the roof.
  • Roof-to-wall connections — hurricane clips or straps. A wind mitigation inspection documents these and can significantly lower the wind portion of your insurance premium.
  • Secondary water barrier — sealed roof decking keeps rain out when shingles blow off, the most common damage in Sally and Ivan.

Florida's My Safe Florida Home program has offered free wind-mitigation inspections and matching grants for qualifying homeowners when funded.

Don't forget the paperwork

A ten-minute video walk-through of every room, closet, appliance and the roof is the single most valuable thing you can do for a future insurance claim. Upload it to cloud storage before the storm. Then use our storm damage log afterwards.
