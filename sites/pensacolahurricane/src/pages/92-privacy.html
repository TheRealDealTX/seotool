Privacy Policy

Alert sign-ups

When you subscribe to alerts we store your email address, optional mobile number, community and alert preferences, and the date you signed up. We use them only to send the storm alerts and updates you chose. We do not sell your information. You can unsubscribe from any email or reply STOP to any text.

Evacuation zone checker

The address you enter is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder and to county GIS services to find your zone. We do not store addresses; short-lived technical caches may hold a lookup result for performance.

Calculators and damage log

Calculators run entirely in your browser. The storm damage log is saved only in your own browser's local storage and never sent to us.

Third-party content

Pages load images and data from NOAA, the National Weather Service, Windy.com and Google Fonts, which receive standard request information such as your IP address.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options listed on the alerts sign-up confirmation.
