Don't rely on any single alert

Websites, apps and email can be delayed or go down when power and cell towers fail. Use at least two of the official systems below, and keep a battery or hand-crank NOAA Weather Radio.

AlertEscambia

Escambia County's official emergency notification system — evacuation orders, shelter openings and boil-water notices by call, text and email. Sign up through the Escambia County BeReady page.

Santa Rosa County alerts

Santa Rosa County Emergency Management's notification system for Milton, Pace, Navarre and Gulf Breeze. Register at santarosa.fl.gov.

Wireless Emergency Alerts

Automatic phone alerts for Hurricane, Storm Surge, Extreme Wind and Tornado Warnings. Make sure “Emergency Alerts” are on in your phone settings.

NOAA Weather Radio

Pensacola-area transmitters are operated by NWS Mobile — find the nearest frequency on the NOAA Weather Radio station list. Program your radio for Escambia (SAME 012033) or Santa Rosa (SAME 012113).
