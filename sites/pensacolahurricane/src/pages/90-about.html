About Pensacola Hurricane

An independent, plain-English storm center for the people who live, work and vacation around Pensacola Bay.

Pensacola Hurricane brings together the information Pensacola-area households look for every hurricane season: live National Hurricane Center tracking, National Weather Service alerts and forecasts, evacuation zones and shelters from Escambia and Santa Rosa counties, preparedness tools, and guidance for insurance claims and recovery.

Where our data comes from

  • Storms and advisories: NOAA National Hurricane Center
  • Alerts, forecasts, observations and radar: National Weather Service, Mobile/Pensacola office (api.weather.gov)
  • Evacuation zones and shelter sites: Escambia County and Santa Rosa County public GIS services
  • Address geocoding: U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder

We do not change official forecasts. When official information and this website differ, the official source is correct.

Not a government site

Pensacola Hurricane is not affiliated with NOAA, the National Weather Service, FEMA, the State of Florida, Escambia County, Santa Rosa County or the City of Pensacola. In an emergency, call 911 and follow instructions from local officials.

Educational content

Articles about insurance and claims are general education, not legal or insurance advice. Where we mention outside professionals or resources — including Florida property-insurance attorneys — we do so to help readers find further help; see our disclaimer.
