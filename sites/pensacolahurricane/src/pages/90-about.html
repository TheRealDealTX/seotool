About Pensacola Hurricane

Storm claim help and hurricane information for Pensacola, Escambia County and Santa Rosa County, sponsored by The Lawgical Firm, P.A.

Pensacola Hurricane helps Pensacola-area homeowners, condo owners and businesses deal with hurricanes before and after the storm — and connects people whose hurricane insurance claims have been denied, delayed or underpaid with The Lawgical Firm, P.A., a Florida property insurance claims law firm.

Where our storm data comes from

  • Storms and advisories: NOAA National Hurricane Center
  • Alerts, forecasts, observations and radar: National Weather Service, Mobile/Pensacola office
  • Evacuation zones and shelter sites: Escambia County and Santa Rosa County public GIS services
  • Address geocoding: U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder

We do not change official forecasts. Pensacola Hurricane is not affiliated with NOAA, the National Weather Service, FEMA, the State of Florida, Escambia County, Santa Rosa County or the City of Pensacola. In an emergency, call 911 and follow instructions from local officials.
