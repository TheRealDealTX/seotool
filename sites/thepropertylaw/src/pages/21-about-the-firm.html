Residential property claims

The firm handles homeowner claims involving water damage, roof damage and roof leaks, hurricane and windstorm damage, sinkholes, fires, plumbing leaks, mold, construction defect claims, theft and vandalism, and hail damage. Its focus is claims that have been denied, delayed or underpaid.

Commercial property claims

For commercial owners and community associations, the firm handles claims on office buildings, condominiums, hotels and motels, restaurants, supermarkets, medical facilities, religious institutions and industrial properties, including business interruption claims prepared with forensic accountants.

About this site

The Property Law is a legal information resource. Its guides explain Florida property insurance statutes and policy terms in plain language and cite the statutes they rely on. Pages are reviewed against the current Florida Statutes and show a “last reviewed” date. They are general information, not legal advice for any particular claim; see the disclaimer.
