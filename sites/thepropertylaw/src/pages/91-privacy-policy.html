Privacy policy

Effective October 2026

This policy explains how thepropertylaw.com, operated for {{FIRM_LEGAL}}, handles information.

Information you give us

When you submit the free case review form, we collect what you enter: your name, phone number, email address, property city or ZIP code, claim type, property type and your description. We also record the page you sent it from, the time, and your IP address to prevent abuse.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, evaluate whether the firm can help, and contact you by phone, text or email about your inquiry. We do not sell your information. We do not share it outside the firm except with service providers who help us operate the website or communications, or when required by law.

Information collected automatically

Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and operation. This site does not use advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Security and retention

Form submissions are stored on our hosting account in a location that is not publicly accessible and are retained as long as needed to respond and to meet the firm's legal and professional obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete the information you submitted, or to stop contacting you, by calling {{PHONE}}. You can opt out of text messages by replying STOP.

Confidentiality

Submitting the form does not create an attorney-client relationship. Please do not send confidential details until the firm agrees to represent you.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
