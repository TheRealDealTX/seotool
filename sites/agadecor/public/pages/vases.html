Styling notes

How to style vases & glass

✦

Cylinders

Small to large, for floating candles, submerged blooms or tall branches.

✦

Gold & ceramic

Mini hexagons and compotes for low, lush garden arrangements.

✦

Bud clusters

Five to nine bud vases down a banquet table read like a runner.

✦

Candle glass

Mercury votives and hurricanes bring the warm glow photographers love.
