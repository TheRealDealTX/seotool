Modern luxury bridal style, North Shore edition

AGA Décor JournalUpdated October 20265 min read

In this article
  1. The North Shore look
  2. Five editorial tricks to borrow
  3. Décor for North Shore venues

Flip through any luxury bridal magazine or North Shore lifestyle title and you’ll notice the same thing: the weddings look effortless. They aren’t. That magazine-ready look is the product of careful décor decisions — and it’s very achievable for your own celebration.

The North Shore look

Chicago’s North Shore — Evanston, Wilmette, Winnetka, Glencoe, Highland Park, Lake Forest — has its own wedding aesthetic. Lakefront light, historic estates, country clubs and garden conservatories reward décor that’s refined rather than loud: tonal florals, crisp linens, candlelight, and one or two moments of real drama.

Five editorial tricks to borrow

  1. A tight palette. Editorial weddings rarely use more than three colours. Pick a main tone, a supporting tone and a metal.
  2. One statement per room. A hanging install, a flower wall or a dramatic head table — not all three.
  3. Texture over colour. Velvet, linen, satin, glass and greenery in the same hue look richer than five colours.
  4. Candles, everywhere. Photographers love candlelight; it flatters skin and makes every surface glow.
  5. Details at eye level. Menus, place cards and chargers appear in close-up shots — make them beautiful.

Décor for North Shore venues

  • Country clubs: keep centrepieces low near the windows to preserve views of the greens or the lake.
  • Gardens and conservatories: let the setting lead with seasonal florals and lanterns for evening.
  • Historic estates: echo the architecture with candelabra, classic whites and antique gold.
  • Lakefront terraces: plan for wind — weighted vases, hurricanes for candles, and low arrangements.
