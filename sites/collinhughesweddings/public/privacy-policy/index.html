Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

Collin Hughes Weddings (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through collinhughesweddings.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry we receive the details you enter: your names, email address, wedding date, venue and message. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your inquiry, plan your photography and deliver your gallery. We never sell or rent personal information.

Your photographs

Wedding images may be shared in our portfolio only with the couple’s permission. You can ask us to remove any image at any time.

Retention & your rights

Inquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to respond and for our business records. You may ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing info@collinhughesweddings.com.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log standard request data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@collinhughesweddings.com.
