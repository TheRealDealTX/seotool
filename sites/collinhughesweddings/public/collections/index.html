FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does wedding photography cost?

Every wedding is different, so we build a quote around your hours, locations and any extras like an engagement session or album. Send us your date and plans and we will reply with a tailored proposal and full collection guide.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. We photograph weddings throughout Minnesota and western Wisconsin with no travel fee inside the Twin Cities metro, and we travel for destination weddings in the UK, Europe and across the US.

Is there a second photographer?

Our Signature and Heirloom collections include a second photographer. For smaller weddings a single photographer is usually all you need.

When will we get our photos?

You will receive a sneak peek within a week and your full, edited gallery within eight weeks of the wedding.

How many photos will we receive?

It depends on coverage, but most full-day weddings receive between 600 and 900 finished images, all high resolution and with printing rights.

How do we book our date?

Your date is reserved with a signed agreement and a retainer. The balance is due a month before the wedding.

Do you photograph elopements and small weddings?

Absolutely. Courthouse weddings, backyard ceremonies and mountaintop elopements are some of our favourite days to photograph.
