Destination weddings

Wherever you say I do

Destination wedding and elopement photography — English country houses, rugged coastlines and cities across Europe and the US.

Passport ready

The same care, anywhere

A destination wedding asks a lot of the people you invite — so the photographs should be worth the journey. We travel light, arrive early and scout the location so we know where the light falls before the day begins.

The UK holds a special place for us. Weddings like Maddy & Ben’s in Surrey — village church, walk across the fields, a hilltop at golden hour — are exactly the kind of day we love. We also photograph elopements on coastlines, in mountains and in old European cities.

  • Location scouting the day before
  • Help planning a timeline around local sunset
  • Advice on permits, weather windows and back-up spots
  • Travel quoted at cost, upfront

Do you charge travel fees for destination weddings?

Travel is quoted at cost — flights, accommodation and ground transport — with no mark-up. We will always give you the full figure upfront.

Where have you photographed destination weddings?

Our favourite destination is the English countryside, including Surrey, the Cotswolds and the Lake District, along with coastal elopements and city weddings in Europe and across the US.

Can you photograph an elopement abroad?

Yes. Elopements are a wonderful way to marry somewhere spectacular. We can help with location ideas, timing for the best light and local logistics.
