About RJB LED LCD TV Service Centre

A Kolkata TV repair workshop built around one idea: fix the part that failed, and be honest about the rest.

Book a repair Email us

Repair, not replacement

RJB LED LCD TV Service Centre repairs flat-screen televisions for Kolkata households, from older LCD sets to the latest 4K Smart TVs.

The technology keeps changing, from CCFL-lit LCD to LED, QLED and OLED. What doesn't change is the approach: diagnose properly, repair at component level, test thoroughly and explain everything in plain language.

That means fewer unnecessary board swaps, lower bills, and TVs that keep working long after the manufacturer has stopped making spare parts for them.

  • Multi-brand: Samsung, Sony, LG, Videocon and every major brand sold in India.
  • Every technology: LCD (CCFL), LED, QLED, Smart TV and OLED boards.
  • Independent: no brand affiliation, so our advice is about your TV, not a sales target.

How we work

1

Tell us the fault

Email or use the form with your TV brand, model number and what it is doing. A photo of the screen helps a lot.

2

Diagnosis

We test the set on the bench, find the failed part and explain the fault in plain language.

3

Your go-ahead

You get a clear quote before any work starts. No approval, no charge for parts.

4

Repair & test

We repair, then burn-in test the TV for hours so the fault does not come straight back.
