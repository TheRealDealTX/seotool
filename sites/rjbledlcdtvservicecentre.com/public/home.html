TV symptom checker

What is your TV doing?

Pick the symptom closest to yours to see the usual causes and whether it is worth repairing.

Symptom 1 of 7

Sound but no picture

Most often the LED backlight. Shine a torch at the screen: if you can faintly see the picture, the panel is working and only the lighting has failed.

Likely culprits
LED backlight strips, backlight driver on the power board, or (less often) the T-con board.
Our verdict
Usually repairable. Backlight strip replacement is our most common job.
Get this diagnosed

Symptom 2 of 7

Lines on the screen

Vertical lines that are always in the same place usually point to the panel's COF (chip-on-film) bonding. Lines that change with the picture often come from the T-con board or its ribbon cable.

Likely culprits
Panel COF/bonding, T-con board, LVDS/V-by-One ribbon cables.
Our verdict
Board and cable faults are repairable; panel bonding faults need assessment first.
Get this diagnosed

Symptom 3 of 7

TV won't turn on

No standby light at all suggests the power supply. A standby light that blinks in a pattern is the TV reporting an error code, which tells us where to look.

Likely culprits
Power supply board (capacitors, MOSFETs, PWM IC), standby circuit, mainboard short.
Our verdict
Power supply faults are among the most reliably repairable.
Get this diagnosed

Symptom 4 of 7

Dark patches or uneven light

Dark corners, a dim band or 'cloudy' patches usually mean one or more LEDs in the backlight array have failed or the diffuser sheets have shifted.

Likely culprits
Individual LED failure, lens caps fallen off, diffuser or reflector sheet damage.
Our verdict
Repairable. We replace the strips and re-seat the optical sheets.
Get this diagnosed

Symptom 5 of 7

Flickering or restarting

A picture that flickers, or a TV that restarts by itself, often has a weak power rail or corrupted firmware.

Likely culprits
Dried-out capacitors, backlight driver, firmware/eMMC corruption.
Our verdict
Usually repairable at board level or with a software reload.
Get this diagnosed

Symptom 6 of 7

Picture but no sound

Check the TV is not set to an external speaker or ARC output first. If it is, the audio amplifier or speakers may have failed.

Likely culprits
Audio amplifier IC, speaker coils, mainboard audio section, settings.
Our verdict
Repairable, often the same day once diagnosed.
Get this diagnosed

Symptom 7 of 7

Smart features not working

Apps that crash, Wi-Fi that drops, or a TV stuck on the logo are usually software or storage (eMMC) problems rather than hardware damage.

Likely culprits
Firmware, eMMC storage chip, Wi-Fi module, mainboard.
Our verdict
Often fixed with a software reload; eMMC replacement when storage has failed.
Get this diagnosed

Frequently asked questions

Do you repair all TV brands?

Yes. We specialise in Samsung, Sony, LG and Videocon LED and LCD televisions, and we also repair Panasonic, Philips, Onida, TCL, Mi, OnePlus, Haier, Vu, Hisense and most other brands sold in India.

Is it worth repairing an LED TV or should I buy a new one?

Most common faults (backlight strips, power supply, mainboard, ports and software) cost a fraction of a new TV to fix. The exception is a cracked or physically damaged panel, where a replacement panel can approach the price of a new set. We tell you honestly which side of that line your TV is on.

Can you repair a TV with a broken screen?

A physically cracked panel cannot be repaired, only replaced. Lines, colour bands and half-screen faults are different: they are often caused by boards or cables and can usually be fixed.
