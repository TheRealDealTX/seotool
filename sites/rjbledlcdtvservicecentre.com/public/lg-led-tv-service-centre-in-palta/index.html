LG LED TV repair in Palta

Palta, just north of Barrackpore and home to the waterworks that supply much of Kolkata, is covered by our LG LED TV service along with Ichapur and Shyamnagar.

We repair LG LED and NanoCell sets here: backlight strips, power supply boards, webOS faults and Magic Remote pairing problems.

LG TV problems we fix in Palta

TV won't power on

Power supply board faults, from blown fuses and capacitors to failed regulators. Repaired at component level.

webOS slow, apps not loading

Corrupted software or worn storage. We reload the system and repair the eMMC where needed.

Magic Remote not pairing

Pairing, IR and Bluetooth module faults, plus the settings that often cause the problem in the first place.

Lines or half-screen

T-con board and ribbon cable faults are repairable; panel faults are assessed honestly before any spend.

For the full rundown on LG faults and models, see our LG LED LCD TV service centre page.

How it works

  1. Tell us the fault. Email or use the form with your TV brand, model number and what it is doing. A photo of the screen helps a lot.
  2. Diagnosis. We test the set on the bench, find the failed part and explain the fault in plain language.
  3. Your go-ahead. You get a clear quote before any work starts. No approval, no charge for parts.
  4. Repair & test. We repair, then burn-in test the TV for hours so the fault does not come straight back.
