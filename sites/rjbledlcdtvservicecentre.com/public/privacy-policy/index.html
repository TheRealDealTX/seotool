What we collect

When you email us or use the contact form, we receive the details you provide: your name, email address, optional phone number, locality, and information about your TV and its fault.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and carry out any repair you ask for. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log standard request data such as your IP address.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to handle your repair and any follow-up. To ask us to delete your information, email info@rjbledlcdtvservicecentre.com.
