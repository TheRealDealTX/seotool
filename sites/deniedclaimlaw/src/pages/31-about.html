About {{FIRM_LEGAL}}

This reference is written and maintained by {{FIRM_LEGAL}}, a Florida law firm that represents property owners in disputes with their property insurers.

What the firm handles

{{FIRM}} represents Florida homeowners and businesses whose property insurance claims have been denied, underpaid or delayed. Its listed practice areas include residential property insurance claims involving:

  • Roof damage and roof leaks
  • Hurricane and windstorm damage
  • Hail damage
  • Water damage and plumbing leaks
  • Fire
  • Mold
  • Sinkholes
  • Theft and vandalism
  • Construction defect claims

The firm also handles commercial property insurance claims. For details, visit thelawgicalfirm.com.

How fees work

There are no upfront fees. The firm is paid only if you recover, under a written fee agreement that explains the fee and how case costs are handled. The initial case review is free.

Why this site exists

Florida's property insurance laws changed repeatedly between 2021 and 2023, and policyholders often learn the rules only after a denial. Denied Claim Law collects the key statutes in one place with links to the official text, so you can understand your position before you decide what to do. Every guide carries a “last reviewed” date, and statutes are cited to the Florida Legislature's Online Sunshine website.
