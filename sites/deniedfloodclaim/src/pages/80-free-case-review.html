What to have ready

  • The denial or payment letter, and the date printed on it
  • Your policy declarations page (flood, homeowners, or both)
  • Any proof of loss you signed, and when you sent it
  • The insurer's estimate and your contractor's estimate
  • Photos and video of the damage and the water line
About flood (NFIP) policies. The Lawgical Firm handles Florida residential and commercial property insurance claims, including hurricane and windstorm, water damage, roof and mold claims. Its website does not list NFIP flood claims as a practice area. If your claim is purely an NFIP dispute, the firm will tell you whether it can help; if it cannot, look for other counsel promptly because the federal deadlines keep running.

What happens next

  1. We review your submission and contact you by phone or email.
  2. A short conversation about the policy, the dates and what the insurer said.
  3. A straight answer on whether the firm can take the claim. If it can, you will receive a written fee agreement to review before anything is signed. The firm's stated terms are no upfront fees and no attorney fee unless there is a recovery.
