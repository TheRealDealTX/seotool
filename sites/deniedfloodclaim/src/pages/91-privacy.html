Privacy policy

This policy explains how DeniedFloodClaim.com, operated for {{FIRM_LEGAL}}, handles information. Last updated October 2026.

Information you give us

When you submit the free case review form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, property city or ZIP code, claim type, the description you write, and your acknowledgment. We also record the page you submitted from, the time, and your IP address to prevent abuse.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, evaluate whether the firm can help with your claim, and contact you by phone, text or email about it. We do not sell your information. We do not share it outside the firm except with service providers that host this website and deliver email, or where required by law.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested and time) for security and troubleshooting. The deadline calculator stores the dates you enter only in your own browser's local storage so they are there when you return; they are not sent to us. This site does not use advertising cookies.

Retention and security

Form submissions are stored in a protected location on our web server that is not publicly accessible, and may also be delivered by email to the firm's intake staff. We keep inquiries as long as needed to respond and to meet the firm's professional record-keeping obligations.

Confidentiality

Submitting the form does not create an attorney-client relationship. Please do not include confidential details beyond a brief description until a lawyer has agreed to represent you.
