DeniedFloodClaim.com is an information resource on Florida flood insurance claims, sponsored by {{FIRM_LEGAL}}, a Florida property insurance claims law firm.

What the firm does

The Lawgical Firm represents property owners in disputes with their insurance companies over denied, delayed and underpaid claims. According to the firm's website, its practice covers Florida residential property insurance claims, including:

  • Hurricane and windstorm damage
  • Water damage and plumbing leaks
  • Roof damage and roof leaks
  • Mold
  • Sinkholes
  • Fire
  • Hail damage
  • Theft and vandalism
  • Construction defect claims

The firm also handles commercial property insurance claims.

A note on flood insurance

The firm's website does not list National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) claims as a practice area, and this site does not represent that the firm handles NFIP litigation. The flood guides here are general information for Florida property owners. Many hurricane disputes involve a homeowners or windstorm policy as well as a flood policy, and the firm can review the property insurance side of those claims. In a free case review, the firm will tell you whether your particular claim is one it can take on.

Fees

The firm's stated terms are no upfront fees, and no attorney fee unless there is a recovery. The specific terms, including how case costs are handled, are set out in a written fee agreement that you review before hiring the firm.
