About The Lawgical Firm and this site

Florida Home Law is a free information site sponsored by {{FIRM_LEGAL}}, a Florida law firm that represents property owners in insurance claim disputes.

The firm

{{FIRM_LEGAL}} focuses on property insurance claims for residential and commercial property owners. Its principal office is in Orlando, and it has an additional office in Destin, serving homeowners across Florida.

Claims the firm handles

  • Hurricane and windstorm damage
  • Roof damage and roof leaks
  • Water damage and plumbing leaks
  • Mold
  • Fire
  • Hail damage
  • Sinkholes
  • Theft and vandalism
  • Construction defect claims
  • Commercial property claims

The firm offers free case reviews and states that there are no upfront fees — it does not get paid unless you get paid. Fees and costs are set out in a written agreement before any representation begins.
