Privacy policy

Effective October 2026. This policy explains what information floridahomelaw.com collects and how {{FIRM_LEGAL}} uses it.

Information you give us

When you submit the free case review form, we collect the information you enter: your name, phone number, email address, property city or ZIP code, the type and status of your claim, and the description you write. We also record the page you submitted from, the date and time, and your IP address, to prevent spam and abuse.

How we use it

We use this information to evaluate your inquiry and to contact you about it by phone, text message or email. We do not sell your information. We do not share it outside the firm except with service providers who help us operate the website and receive inquiries, or when required by law.

Text messages

If we text you about your inquiry, message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts.

Information collected automatically

Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and troubleshooting. This site does not use advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them.

Security and retention

Form submissions are stored in a location that is not publicly accessible and are kept only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and meet the firm’s legal and professional obligations.

Confidentiality

Submitting the form does not create an attorney-client relationship. Please do not include confidential or sensitive details until a lawyer has agreed to represent you.
