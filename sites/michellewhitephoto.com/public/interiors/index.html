Interiors & architecture

Interior photography

Editorial interior and architectural photography for designers, architects, builders and publications in Salt Lake City and beyond.

Spaces with a story

Rooms that breathe

A finished space deserves photographs that do it justice — and that make people want to step inside.

Interior shoots are slow and deliberate. Each room is studied for its best hour of light, styled with care alongside the designer, and photographed with straight verticals, balanced exposures and colour that matches the materials in real life.

The result is a set of clean, warm, editorial images ready for portfolios, websites, award submissions and magazine features — with detail shots and vignettes that tell the story of how a home actually feels to live in.

Interiors FAQ

Before the shoot

How long does an interior shoot take?

Most residential projects are photographed in a half or full day, depending on the number of rooms and the shot list.

Do you help with styling?

Yes. Light styling is part of every shoot, and the studio is happy to work alongside your own stylist or design team.

Can images be licensed to other parties?

Usage is agreed up front. Additional licences for builders, vendors or publications can be arranged so everyone involved can share the work.

When will we receive the final images?

Edited, retouched images are typically delivered within two weeks of the shoot.
