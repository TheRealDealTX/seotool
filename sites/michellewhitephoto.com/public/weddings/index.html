Weddings & elopements

Utah wedding photography

Natural, editorial wedding and elopement photography in Salt Lake City, Park City and across the American West.

Your day, as it was

Unposed, unhurried, unforgettable

Your wedding should look like yours — not like a template.

Coverage is documentary at heart: getting ready, the first look, the ceremony, the toasts and the dance floor are photographed as they happen. Portraits are relaxed and quick, usually timed for the last hour of light when Utah’s mountains and valleys glow.

From temple and chapel weddings in Salt Lake City to ski-resort celebrations in Park City and desert ceremonies in the south of the state, every timeline is planned around where you’ll be and when the light will be at its best.

Elopements

Small ceremony, big landscape

Elopements and micro-weddings are about presence: just the two of you (or a handful of favourite people), a meaningful place, and the time to actually enjoy it.

Help is available with the parts that feel daunting — choosing a location, permits, the best season and time of day, and a weather back-up plan. Popular choices include alpine lakes in the Uinta and Wasatch ranges, red-rock country around Moab and quiet city spots in Salt Lake.

Wedding FAQ

Planning questions

How many photos will we receive?

It depends on the length of coverage, but a full-day wedding typically delivers six hundred to nine hundred finished images. Every image is individually edited — no filler, no near-duplicates.

Do you travel outside Utah?

Yes. Destination weddings and elopements are welcome. Travel within the Wasatch Front is included; other locations are quoted at cost.

Can you help with our timeline?

Absolutely. Every couple receives a custom photography timeline built around sunset, travel time between locations and family photo lists.

Is a second photographer included?

A second photographer is included in full-day and weekend collections and can be added to any other collection.

When do we get our photos?

A sneak peek arrives within a week, and the full online gallery is delivered within six weeks.
