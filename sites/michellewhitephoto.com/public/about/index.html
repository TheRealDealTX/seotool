About Michelle White Photography

A Salt Lake City studio for weddings, elopements and interiors — built on natural light and an unhurried eye.

The studio

Light first. People always.

Michelle White Photo exists for one reason: to make photographs that still feel true long after the day is over.

The studio works the same way whether the subject is a couple, a family or a beautifully finished room. Find the best light. Slow down. Pay attention to the things nobody thinks to ask for — a hand on a shoulder, a grandmother’s laugh, the way late sun falls across a kitchen island.

That means fewer stiff poses and more real moments, colour that looks like real life rather than a filter, and galleries that are carefully edited by hand. It also means being calm, organised and quietly helpful on the day, so you can forget the camera is there.

What guides the work

Natural light

Light is planned for, not fixed later. Timelines are built around golden hour and the best windows in the room.

Real moments

Gentle direction when it helps, then stepping back so real connection can happen on its own.

Timeless edits

True-to-life colour and soft, film-inspired tones that won’t date the way trends do.

Calm presence

Organised, unobtrusive and kind — the person your family is glad was there.

Two disciplines, one eye

Weddings & interiors

Wedding work teaches you to anticipate — to be in the right place a second before something happens. Interior work teaches patience, precision and respect for light and line. Each makes the other better.

Couples get the compositional care of an editorial shoot; designers get a photographer who knows how to make a room feel lived-in and warm, not cold and staged.
