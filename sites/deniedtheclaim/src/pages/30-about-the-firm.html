About The Lawgical Firm, P.A.

Denied the Claim is an information site published by {{FIRM_LEGAL}}, a Florida law firm that represents homeowners and property owners in disputes with their property insurance companies.

What the firm handles

The firm represents policyholders whose property insurance claims were denied, delayed or underpaid. Its practice covers residential and commercial property insurance claims, including:

  • Hurricane and windstorm damage
  • Water damage and plumbing leaks
  • Roof damage and roof leaks
  • Fire damage
  • Mold
  • Hail damage
  • Sinkholes
  • Theft and vandalism
  • Construction defect claims
  • Commercial property claims

How fees work

Case reviews are free and there are no upfront fees. The firm is paid only if you recover. The specific terms, including how case costs are handled, are set out in a written fee agreement you will see before you hire the firm.

About this site

The guides here are written to help Florida homeowners understand what happens after an insurer denied the claim: what the law requires of insurers, what the common denial reasons mean, and what dispute options exist. Each guide cites the Florida statutes it relies on and shows the date it was last reviewed. Laws change, and deadlines depend on your policy and date of loss, so treat the guides as a starting point rather than advice about your situation. See the disclaimer.
