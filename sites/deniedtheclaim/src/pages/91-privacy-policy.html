Privacy policy

Effective: October 2026

What we collect

When you submit the free case review form, we collect what you enter: your name, phone number, email address, property city or ZIP code, claim type, claim status and any description you write, together with the page you sent it from, the time, and your IP address (used to prevent spam and abuse). The site does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

{{FIRM_LEGAL}} uses this information to respond to your request, evaluate whether it can help with your claim, and contact you by phone, text or email about your inquiry. We do not sell your information. We may share it with service providers that host the website or deliver email, solely to operate the site, or when the law requires it.

Retention and security

Form submissions are stored on the website’s server in a location that is not publicly accessible and are kept as long as needed to respond to you and to meet the firm’s professional obligations. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure; please do not send confidential details through the form.

Third-party services

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests the font files.
