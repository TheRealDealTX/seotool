Gift message generator

Pick an occasion and a tone, add their name, and get a polished card message in one tap. Shuffle for more ideas.

Make the message your own

Generated messages are a starting point. The most memorable cards add one specific detail only you could write — a shared memory, an inside joke, or why you chose this particular gift. Try the formula from our guide to what to write in a gift card: open with them, say one specific thing, close with a wish.

Choosing the right tone

  • Heartfelt — partners, family, close friends and meaningful milestones.
  • Witty — friends, siblings and anyone who'd rather laugh than cry.
  • Formal — clients, colleagues, bosses and acquaintances.

Then present it beautifully: heavyweight card, good pen, matching envelope. See how to wrap like a luxury boutique.

FAQ

Questions about the gift message generator

How many messages are in the generator?

Sixty-three — three for every combination of seven occasions and three tones — plus rotating sign-offs.

Can I use these messages for business gifts?

Yes. Choose the Client occasion or the Formal tone for professional relationships.
