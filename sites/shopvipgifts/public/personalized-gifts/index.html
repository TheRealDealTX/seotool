Personalized gifts with a VIP finish

Engraving, monograms and custom keepsakes that turn a beautiful object into one that could only ever be theirs.

Personalization is the fastest way to make a gift feel VIP. A name, a date or a set of coordinates changes an object from "nice" to "irreplaceable" — and makes it almost impossible to re-gift.

The trick is restraint. The most elegant personalized gifts whisper rather than shout.

How to personalize a gift tastefully

  • Go small. Initials or a date are more elegant than a full name and a quote.
  • Hide it. Inside a ring, on a watch caseback, under the lid of a box — private messages feel intimate.
  • Check spelling twice. Engraving can't be undone. Confirm names, dates and monogram order (traditionally first, last, middle for a single person).
  • Order early. Personalization adds days or weeks to delivery — plan with our holiday gift planning timeline.

FAQ

Personalized Gifts: common questions

What is the correct monogram order?

For one person, the traditional three-letter monogram places the last-name initial in the center and larger, with first and middle initials on either side. For simple engraving, first-middle-last in a row is perfectly acceptable.

How long does personalization take?

Anywhere from same-day for simple engraving to two to four weeks for custom artwork or monogrammed leather. Order early for holidays.

Can personalized gifts be returned?

Usually not. Double-check spelling and details before you approve the proof.

What should I engrave on a gift?

Short and meaningful works best: initials, a significant date, coordinates, or a two-to-five-word phrase.
