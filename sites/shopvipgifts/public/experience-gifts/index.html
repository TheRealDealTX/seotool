Experience gifts: VIP moments they'll never forget

Spa days, tasting menus, weekends away and once-in-a-lifetime moments — the gifts that become stories.

Research on happiness consistently finds that experiences bring longer-lasting joy than objects. They create anticipation beforehand, connection during, and memories afterward. That makes experience gifts the ultimate VIP gift — especially for people who already have everything.

The secret is presentation: an experience should still feel like something to unwrap.

How to give an experience gift beautifully

The risk with experiences is that there's nothing to open on the day. Solve it with a physical "teaser": a box containing an item related to the experience plus a printed card with the details. A cooking class becomes an apron and a recipe card; a getaway becomes a packed travel pouch.

Book smart

  • Choose flexible or refundable bookings when you can — or give a dated voucher so they can pick the time.
  • Check their calendar quietly with a partner or friend before booking a fixed date.
  • For couples, book the experience for two — shared memories are the point.

FAQ

Experience Gifts: common questions

Are experience gifts better than physical gifts?

For many people, yes — experiences tend to create longer-lasting happiness and stronger memories. They're ideal for people who have everything or who value time over things.

How do I wrap an experience gift?

Place a printed voucher or itinerary inside a box with a related item (robe for a spa day, apron for a cooking class) and wrap it like any other present.

What's a good experience gift for parents?

A professional family photo session, a weekend away, theater tickets or a chef's dinner are all well-loved by parents.

What if they can't make the date?

Choose refundable or flexible bookings, or buy a gift voucher for the venue so they can choose their own date.
