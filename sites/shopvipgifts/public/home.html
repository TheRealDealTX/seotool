What makes a VIP gift?

It isn't the price tag. It's the feeling of being seen.

A VIP gift tells someone you paid attention — to the coffee they order, the trip they keep mentioning, the watch they'd never buy for themselves. We call it the VIP formula: personal, premium, presented beautifully.

  • Personal — chosen for one person, not for "someone like them."
  • Premium — the best version of a thing, even when the thing is small.
  • Presented — wrapping, timing and a handwritten note do half the work.

House rules

Three VIP gifting rules we live by

Upgrade something they use every day. A beautiful version of an ordinary object gets noticed every single morning.

— Rule 1: Daily luxury

Spend a third of your effort on presentation. Wrapping, timing and a handwritten note do half the emotional work.

— Rule 2: The reveal matters

Listen for "someday". "I'd love to try that someday" is the best gift brief you'll ever get.

— Rule 3: Pay attention

FAQ

VIP gift questions, answered

What is a VIP gift?

A VIP gift is a present that makes someone feel truly valued — personal to them, the best quality you can manage and beautifully presented. Read what makes a gift feel VIP.

What are good VIP gift ideas for clients?

Champagne with crystal flutes, executive gift boxes, engraved pens with leather journals and gourmet hampers are reliable client VIP gifts. See our corporate gifts guide.

Do I have to spend a lot on a VIP gift?

Not at all. Our guide to luxury gifts under $100 shows how quality, personalization and presentation make modest gifts feel special.
