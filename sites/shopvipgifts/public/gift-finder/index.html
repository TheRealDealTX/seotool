How the Gift Finder works

Our Gift Finder scores more than thirty hand-picked gift ideas from our gift guides against your answers. Who the gift is for and your budget carry the most weight, the occasion fine-tunes the list, and each interest you pick boosts ideas that match their personality.

The six highest-scoring ideas are shown with a match score, typical price range and a link to the guide where you'll find more context and presentation tips.

Tips for better results

  • Pick their interests, not yours. Think about how they spend a free Saturday.
  • Be honest about budget. We'll still show one or two ideas slightly above it when they're a great fit.
  • Run it twice. Try different interest combinations to explore new directions.

Once you've chosen, make it VIP with a handwritten note — our gift message generator can help.
