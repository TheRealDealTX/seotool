25 client appreciation gift ideas that build loyalty

Thoughtful client gifts at every budget — and the timing and personalization tricks that make clients remember you.

  2. $50–$150

  4. For client teams
  5. When to send client appreciation gifts
  6. What to write

Client appreciation gifts aren't about the object — they're about showing a client they matter beyond the invoice. Here are 25 ideas organized by budget, plus the details that make them land.

Under $50

  1. Single-origin chocolate tasting box.
  2. A bag of coffee from a roaster in their city.
  3. A business book you genuinely think they'd enjoy, with a note on why.
  4. Luxury hand cream and lip balm set.
  5. A potted succulent or desk plant.
  6. A charitable donation to a cause they care about.

$50–$150

  1. Executive gift box with gourmet treats and a notebook.
  2. Leather-bound journal and engraved pen.
  3. Crystal tumblers with a small-batch spirit (where appropriate).
  4. Three months of specialty coffee or tea.
  5. A gourmet hamper for their whole team.
  6. A premium umbrella or leather tech organizer.
  7. Artisan olive oil and balsamic collection.

$150+ for top clients

  1. Vintage champagne with crystal flutes.
  2. A wine club membership.
  3. A chef's tasting dinner for the client and their partner.
  4. Tickets to a game, concert or show they love.
  5. Noise-cancelling headphones for a frequent traveler.
  6. A personalized leather portfolio.
  7. A golf lesson or round at a premium course.

For client teams

  1. Catered lunch delivered to their office.
  2. A cookie or pastry tower for a celebration.
  3. A gourmet popcorn and treats bar.
  4. A team experience such as a cooking class.
  5. A large shareable gift basket.

When to send client appreciation gifts

  • Project milestones: a launch, a closing, a renewal.
  • Relationship anniversaries: one, three or five years of working together.
  • Off-season: mid-January or Thanksgiving week, to avoid the December pile.
  • Personal moments: a promotion, a new baby or a new office.

What to write

Keep it short, specific and free of sales talk. Mention a moment from your work together and thank them for something concrete. For example:

Thank you for trusting us with the spring launch — your team made it a joy. Here's to the next one.

Our gift message generator has a client mode with formal, warm and witty options. And check our corporate gift etiquette guide before sending.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I spend on a client appreciation gift?

Most businesses spend $50–$150 on regular clients and $150–$500 on their most important relationships, within any gift policy limits.

When is the best time to send client gifts?

Milestones and relationship anniversaries are more memorable than December, when clients receive the most gifts.
