What to write in a gift card: messages for every occasion

A simple three-line formula and dozens of examples — heartfelt, witty and formal — so you never stare at a blank card again.

The card is often the part of a gift people keep. Yet most of us freeze when faced with the blank space. Here's a formula that works every time, plus examples to borrow.

The three-line formula

  1. Open with them: use their name and the occasion.
  2. Say one specific thing: a memory, a quality you admire, or why you chose the gift.
  3. Close with a wish: for the year ahead, the next chapter, or simply their happiness.
Happy birthday, Mia. I still laugh about our disastrous camping trip — here's to many more adventures (with better tents). Love, Sam

Birthday messages

  • "Another year of you is worth celebrating properly. Happy birthday."
  • "You're not getting older, you're becoming a limited edition."
  • "Wishing you a year of easy mornings and big laughs."

Anniversary messages

  • "Every year with you is my favorite year so far."
  • "Thank you for choosing me — and choosing me again every day."
  • "Still the only person I'd share the last slice of pizza with."

Thank-you messages

  • "Thank you doesn't quite cover it, but it's a start."
  • "Your kindness meant more than you know."
  • "Consider this a down payment on all the favors I still owe you."

Client and business messages

  • "Thank you for your trust and partnership this year."
  • "Partners like you make the work worth doing."
  • "With sincere appreciation from all of us — here's to the year ahead."

Holiday messages

  • "The best part of this season is the people in it — and you're at the top of my list."
  • "Wishing you warmth, rest and the people you love."
  • "I checked the list twice — you were definitely on the nice side. Mostly."

Tips for a better card

  • Write by hand, in pen, on heavy card stock.
  • Draft it on your phone first so the card has no crossings-out.
  • Match the tone to the relationship: playful for close friends, warm-formal for clients.
  • Sign off thoughtfully: "With love," "Warmly," or "Cheers," rather than just your name.

Frequently asked questions

What do you write in a gift card for someone you don't know well?

Keep it warm and simple: their name, the occasion and a sincere wish, such as 'Wishing you a wonderful birthday and a great year ahead.'

How long should a gift card message be?

Two to four sentences is ideal: long enough to feel personal, short enough to fit neatly.
