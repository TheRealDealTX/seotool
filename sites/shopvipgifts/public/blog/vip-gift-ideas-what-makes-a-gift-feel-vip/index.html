VIP gift ideas: what makes a gift feel VIP

The three-part formula behind every gift that makes someone feel like a VIP — plus 25 ideas to put it into practice.

Gifting guide3 min readUpdated 2026-10-14
In this guide
  1. The VIP gift formula
  2. 25 VIP gift ideas by recipient
  3. Common mistakes that make gifts feel ordinary
  4. Find your VIP gift in a minute

A VIP gift isn't defined by price. Some of the most treasured presents cost less than dinner for two; some expensive ones end up in a drawer. What separates them is how they make the recipient feel: seen, valued and celebrated. Here's how to give that feeling on purpose.

The VIP gift formula

After years of studying what people remember about gifts, we've boiled it down to three ingredients. Hit all three and almost any gift will feel VIP.

1. Personal

The gift must clearly be for this person — not "a man in his forties" or "a client." It reflects something they've said, something they love, or a moment you shared. Personal doesn't have to mean engraved; it means specific.

2. Premium

Give the best version of a thing, even if the thing is small. The best olive oil they've ever tasted beats a mediocre kitchen gadget. A superb pair of socks beats an average sweater. Quality signals care.

3. Presented

Wrapping, timing and a handwritten note do half the emotional work. A gift that arrives beautifully wrapped, on the right day, with a few honest sentences, feels ten times more valuable than the same item in a shipping box.

People forget what you gave them, but they remember how it felt to open it.

25 VIP gift ideas by recipient

For a partner

  1. Fine jewelry tied to a shared memory — pearls, a pendant or engraved band.
  2. A surprise weekend away, with a packed bag waiting at the door.
  3. A chef's tasting dinner at the restaurant they keep mentioning.
  4. A framed star map of the night you met.
  5. A couples spa day — book it for two.

For parents

  1. A professional family photo session.
  2. A linen-bound photo book of the year.
  3. A fresh flower subscription for three months.
  4. A cashmere throw for their favorite chair.
  5. A recipe heirloom board engraved with a family recipe.

For friends

  1. A single-origin chocolate tasting box.
  2. A specialty coffee subscription.
  3. Concert or theater tickets — with you as their plus-one.
  4. A curated "open one a day" gift box.
  5. A private celebration night for a milestone birthday.

For clients and colleagues

  1. Vintage champagne with crystal flutes.
  2. An engraved fountain pen and leather journal.
  3. A gourmet hamper the whole team can share.
  4. A coffee subscription from a roaster near their office.
  5. A charitable donation in their name to a cause they support.

For the person who has everything

  1. A travel upgrade or airport lounge pass.
  2. A pottery or cooking class for two.
  3. A custom illustrated portrait of their home or pet.
  4. A restored and reframed old family photograph.
  5. Your time: a planned day doing exactly what they love.

Common mistakes that make gifts feel ordinary

  • Buying for yourself. If you'd love it, ask whether they would too.
  • Going generic at the last minute. A gift card with no note signals "I forgot." Plan with our gift planning timeline.
  • Skipping the card. The note is often what people keep. Try our gift message generator if words don't come easily.
  • Over-branding business gifts. A big logo turns a gift into marketing.

Frequently asked questions

What is a VIP gift?

A VIP gift is one that makes the recipient feel specially valued. It's personal to them, the best quality you can manage within your budget, and beautifully presented.

Do VIP gifts have to be expensive?

No. A $40 chocolate tasting box with a heartfelt note can feel more VIP than a $400 generic gadget. Quality and thoughtfulness matter more than price.

What is a good VIP gift for a client?

Shareable luxury consumables such as champagne, gourmet hampers or specialty coffee, presented with a handwritten card, are safe and appreciated.
