Experience gifts vs material gifts: which is better?

What happiness research says about giving experiences vs things — and how to choose (or combine) the two.

Gifting guide3 min readUpdated 2026-09-30
In this guide
  1. What research suggests
  2. When a material gift is better
  3. Quick comparison
  4. The VIP move: combine them

Should you give a weekend away or a beautiful watch? A cooking class or a new set of knives? The answer depends on the person — but research gives some useful clues.

What research suggests

A substantial body of consumer-psychology research has found that experiential purchases tend to produce longer-lasting satisfaction than material ones. Several reasons are commonly cited:

  • Anticipation: looking forward to an experience is itself enjoyable.
  • Memory improves them: we tend to remember the highlights and forget the minor hassles.
  • Less comparison: it's harder to feel your trip was "worse" than someone else's, whereas objects invite upgrades.
  • Connection: experiences are often shared, and studies on gift-giving have found that experiential gifts can make recipients feel closer to the giver.

When a material gift is better

Objects still win in plenty of situations:

  • Daily-use upgrades: a better wallet, watch or coffee machine creates small moments of joy every day.
  • Keepsakes: jewelry and engraved pieces carry meaning for decades.
  • Scheduling is impossible: new parents, busy executives and those with health constraints may find experiences stressful.
  • Professional relationships: experiences can feel too personal for clients you don't know well.

Experience giftsMaterial gifts
Best forPeople who have everything, couples, milestonesDaily-use upgrades, keepsakes, clients

The VIP move: combine them

The most memorable gifts often blend both: a physical item that hints at an experience.

  • A silk robe + a spa booking.
  • Pearl earrings + a dinner reservation for the same night.
  • A leather passport wallet + a weekend getaway.
  • A chef's apron + a cooking class for two.

Frequently asked questions

Are experience gifts better than material gifts?

Research generally finds experiences create longer-lasting happiness, but material gifts are better for daily-use upgrades, keepsakes and professional relationships.

How do I present an experience gift?

Pair a printed voucher with a related physical 'teaser' item and wrap it, so there's still a moment to unwrap.
