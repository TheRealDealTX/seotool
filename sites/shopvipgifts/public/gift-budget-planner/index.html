How to set a gift budget

Start with a total you're genuinely comfortable spending — ideally decided before you start browsing. Then list everyone you're buying for, including the easy-to-forget people: hosts, teachers, neighbors, clients and colleagues.

Closer relationships usually get a larger share. The planner's auto-split button applies typical weights (a partner gets four shares, a parent or child three, siblings and clients two, friends one and a half, everyone else one), rounded to the nearest $5. Adjust any amount and the summary updates instantly.

FAQ

Questions about the gift budget planner

Is my gift list stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

How does auto-split work?

Each person gets a number of shares based on their relationship, and your total is divided proportionally, rounded down to the nearest $5.

Can I print or share my list?

Use the Copy list button to copy a clean text version you can paste into notes, email or a spreadsheet.
