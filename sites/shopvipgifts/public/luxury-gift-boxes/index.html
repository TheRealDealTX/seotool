Luxury gift boxes & baskets worth unboxing

Curated VIP gift boxes for foodies, wine lovers, homebodies and wellness fans — and a step-by-step method to build your own.

A luxury gift box is the ultimate crowd-pleaser: multiple small delights, layered reveals and presentation built in. Done well, it feels like a private concierge assembled something just for them.

Here are our favorite VIP gift box themes, followed by tips for building a custom basket that looks boutique-made.

How to make a gift box feel luxurious

  1. Pick a theme. A clear concept (spa night, Italian dinner, coffee morning) feels curated. Random items feel like leftovers.
  2. Choose one hero item. Build around a single standout piece — a candle, a bottle, a cashmere item — and support it with smaller treats.
  3. Use a rigid box. A sturdy box with a lid, lined with tissue and crinkle paper, instantly reads as premium.
  4. Layer by height. Tall items at the back, small items in front, nothing loose.
  5. Finish with a note. A handwritten card on heavy stock is the final touch.

FAQ

Luxury Gift Boxes & Baskets: common questions

What should go in a luxury gift box?

One hero item (such as a candle, bottle or cashmere piece), three to five supporting treats that match a theme, quality filler, and a handwritten card.

How much does a luxury gift basket cost?

Most range from $50 to $250. Building your own lets you spend more on the hero item and less on packaging.

How do I ship a gift box without damage?

Use a rigid inner box, fill every gap so nothing moves, wrap bottles in bubble wrap, and ship in an outer carton with at least two inches of padding.

What's a good gift box for a client?

Shareable gourmet boxes and chocolate tasting boxes are safe and appreciated. See our corporate gifts guide.
