Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Shop VIP Gifts ("we", "us") collects when you use shopvipgifts.com and how we use it.

Information you give us

If you use our contact form or join our email list, we receive the details you provide — typically your name, email address and message. We use this only to reply to you or to send the emails you requested. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or writing to info@shopvipgifts.com.

Interactive tools

Our Gift Finder, Gift Message Generator and Anniversary tool run entirely in your browser. The Gift Budget Planner saves your list in your browser's local storage on your device so it's there when you return; it is never sent to us. You can clear it with the planner's Clear button or by clearing your browser data.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and performance purposes. We do not use advertising cookies.

Third-party services

We load web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when fonts are requested. Links to other websites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site (such as hosting and email) or where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us at info@shopvipgifts.com and we'll respond promptly.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
