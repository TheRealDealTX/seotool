You can pay your **Davao City Water District** (DCWD) bill at DCWD's own offices and collection points, through e-wallets such as GCash and Maya, via online banking, or at accredited payment centers like Bayad outlets and partner stores. Whichever method you choose, have your account number ready and pay before the due date to avoid penalties and disconnection.

Payment partners change from time to time, so always confirm the current list on DCWD's official website or Facebook page before you pay.

## Davao City Water District payment options at a glance

| Payment method | Where | What you need | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| DCWD offices and collection points | Main office and satellite offices around the city | Bill or account number, cash | Most direct; can get official receipts and ask questions |
| E-wallets (GCash, Maya) | Phone app, Bills/Pay Bills section | Account number, name, amount | Convenient; a small convenience fee may apply |
| Online and mobile banking | Your bank's app or website | Account number, enrolled biller | Check whether your bank lists DCWD as a biller |
| Payment centers | Bayad outlets, partner stores, some malls | Bill or account number, cash | Keep the receipt; posting can take a few days |
| Over-the-counter at banks | Partner banks | Bill and payment slip | Ask the bank if DCWD is an accredited biller |

## Paying at DCWD offices

DCWD has a main office and several district or satellite offices across Davao City, where you can pay bills at the cashier windows. Paying in person is a good choice if you:

- Need an official receipt right away.
- Have arrears, a disconnection notice or a billing dispute to discuss.
- Want to apply for a new connection, transfer of account or reconnection.

Office locations and hours can change, especially on holidays, so check DCWD's official channels before going. Bring your latest bill or at least your account number, and expect queues near the due date or at the start of the month.

## Paying through GCash and Maya

E-wallets are the most convenient option for many Davaoeños.

1. Open the app and go to **Bills** or **Pay Bills**.
2. Choose the **Water Utilities** category and select Davao City Water District (look for DCWD).
3. Enter your account number exactly as printed on your bill, plus the account name if asked.
4. Enter the amount due and confirm.
5. Save the screenshot or digital receipt until the payment appears on your next bill.

A small convenience fee may apply depending on the app and biller, and fees change over time. If you are still deciding which e-wallet to use, see our [GCash vs Maya guide](/tech/gcash-vs-maya-ewallet-guide/).

> **Tip:** Double-check the account number before confirming. A payment sent to the wrong account can take a long time to correct.

## Online banking

Many banks let you enrol DCWD as a biller in their app or online banking site. Search for "Davao City Water District" or "DCWD" in the biller list. If you cannot find it, your bank may not be a partner; use another channel or ask the bank. Payments made late at night or on weekends may be posted on the next banking day.

## Payment centers and partner stores

Accredited payment centers such as Bayad outlets and other bills-payment counters in malls and stores usually accept DCWD bills. These are handy if you prefer cash but don't want to go to a DCWD office. Keep your receipt, because it may take a few days before the payment shows in DCWD's records.

Some residents also pay through local cooperatives, rural banks or community collection partners. These arrangements vary, so check that any outlet is officially accredited by DCWD before handing over cash.

## How to read your DCWD water bill

Your bill contains several key pieces of information. Knowing where they are saves time when paying online.

| Bill item | What it means |
|---|---|
| Account number | Your unique customer number; needed for every payment |
| Billing period | The dates covered by the meter readings |
| Previous and present reading | Meter readings at the start and end of the period |
| Consumption (cu. m.) | Water used, in cubic metres: present reading minus previous reading |
| Current charges | Charges for this period's consumption, plus any fees |
| Arrears | Unpaid amounts from previous bills |
| Total amount due | What you need to pay now |
| Due date | Last day to pay without a penalty |

One cubic metre equals 1,000 litres. If your consumption suddenly jumps without a change in household habits, check for leaks in toilets, faucets and pipes before contacting DCWD.

## Avoiding penalties and disconnection

- **Pay on or before the due date.** Late payments may incur a penalty charge.
- **Watch for disconnection notices.** Unpaid bills can lead to disconnection, and reconnection may involve extra fees.
- **Keep receipts.** Digital or paper proof helps resolve any posting problems.
- **Update your contact details.** DCWD may send advisories about water interruptions and billing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I pay my Davao City Water District bill with GCash?

Yes, DCWD is commonly listed under water utilities in GCash and Maya. Enter your account number exactly as shown on your bill and keep the receipt.

### Where can I pay my DCWD bill in person?

You can pay at DCWD's main and satellite offices and at accredited payment centers. Check DCWD's official channels for current locations and hours.

### How do I compute my water consumption?

Subtract the previous meter reading from the present reading. The result is your consumption in cubic metres.

### What happens if I pay my DCWD bill late?

A penalty may be added, and unpaid bills can lead to disconnection. Pay before the due date and contact DCWD if you have a billing issue.
