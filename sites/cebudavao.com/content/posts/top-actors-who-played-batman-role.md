Who played Batman? In live-action films and serials, nine actors have played the Caped Crusader: Lewis Wilson, Robert Lowery, Adam West, Michael Keaton, Val Kilmer, George Clooney, Christian Bale, Ben Affleck and Robert Pattinson. Add voice actors like the late Kevin Conroy and the count grows much longer. Here is each one, what they starred in and how they rank.

## How Many Actors Played Batman?

Counting the theatrical serials of the 1940s, the 1960s TV series and the big-screen films, **nine actors** have played Batman as the lead in live action. That number leaves out actors who played a young Bruce Wayne, and it leaves out the many voice actors in animation and video games.

## Every Actor Who Played Batman, in Order

### Lewis Wilson (1943)

The first live-action Batman. Columbia Pictures' 15-chapter serial *Batman* was shown in cinemas before main features. Wilson's Batman is a curiosity today, with a baggy costume and wartime storylines, but he got there first.

### Robert Lowery (1949)

Lowery took over for the follow-up serial *Batman and Robin*. Like the first serial, it was low-budget and episodic, and it is mainly of interest to film historians and dedicated fans.

### Adam West (1966-1968)

For a whole generation, the late Adam West *was* Batman. The colourful, tongue-in-cheek TV series, with its "POW!" and "BAM!" on-screen sound effects, ran for three seasons, and West also starred in the 1966 feature film. He later returned to the role in voice work, and the show remains hugely beloved for its campy fun.

### Michael Keaton (1989, 1992)

Tim Burton cast Keaton in *Batman* (1989), a choice that drew fan doubts at first and then won them over. Keaton returned in *Batman Returns* (1992) and, decades later, in *The Flash* (2023). His quiet, slightly odd Bruce Wayne and dark Gothic Gotham changed how superhero films looked.

### Val Kilmer (1995)

Kilmer stepped in for *Batman Forever*, directed by Joel Schumacher, opposite Jim Carrey's Riddler and Tommy Lee Jones's Two-Face. The film was a box-office success, though Kilmer played the role only once.

### George Clooney (1997)

*Batman & Robin* is often called the low point of the franchise, and Clooney himself has joked about it in interviews over the years. Still, he is part of the list, and the film has gained an ironic cult following.

### Christian Bale (2005-2012)

Christopher Nolan's trilogy, *Batman Begins*, *The Dark Knight* and *The Dark Knight Rises*, gave the character a grounded, realistic tone. Bale's committed performance and Heath Ledger's Oscar-winning Joker in *The Dark Knight* make this run many fans' favourite.

### Ben Affleck (2016-2023)

Affleck played an older, battle-worn Batman in *Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice*, *Justice League* and *Zack Snyder's Justice League*, with brief appearances in *Suicide Squad* and *The Flash*. Opinions on the films vary, but many fans rate his physical, brawling Batman highly.

### Robert Pattinson (2022)

In Matt Reeves's *The Batman*, Pattinson plays a younger, brooding Batman in his second year of crime-fighting, with an emphasis on detective work. A sequel has been announced; check official studio news for current release plans.

## The Voice of Batman: Kevin Conroy and Others

For many fans, the definitive Batman never appeared on screen in the suit at all. **Kevin Conroy** voiced Batman in *Batman: The Animated Series* (from 1992) and went on to voice him across many animated films, series and the *Batman: Arkham* video games. His deep, calm delivery, with a softer voice for Bruce Wayne, became a benchmark. Conroy passed away in 2022.

Other notable voice Batmen include **Will Arnett** in *The LEGO Batman Movie* and the *LEGO Movie* films, and **Diedrich Bader** in *Batman: The Brave and the Bold*.

## Batman in the Philippines: The Parody Era

Batman has long been popular in the Philippines, and local cinema produced its own unofficial and comedic takes. *Batman Fights Dracula* (1967) is a well-known example of the era's low-budget Filipino superhero films, and *Alyas Batman en Robin* (1991) starring Joey de Leon was a comedy spoof. These films are part of Philippine pop-culture history and show how quickly Hollywood heroes became local favourites.

## Batman on Television and Beyond

The big-screen list doesn't tell the whole story. Batman and Bruce Wayne have also appeared in many live-action TV projects, often as younger or alternative versions of the character.

- **Gotham** (2014-2019) followed a teenage Bruce Wayne, played by David Mazouz, growing up in a crime-ridden city before he becomes Batman.
- **Titans** featured an older Bruce Wayne played by Iain Glen.
- **Kevin Conroy** appeared in live action as an older Bruce Wayne in the Arrowverse crossover event *Crisis on Infinite Earths*, a treat for animation fans.

Because these actors mostly played Bruce Wayne rather than headlining as Batman in a film, most "how many actors played Batman" lists leave them out. Counting them is a matter of taste, and it's a debate that keeps fan forums busy.

## What Makes a Great Batman?

Fans tend to judge Batman actors on a few things at once: whether they convince as the brooding hero in the cowl, whether they make Bruce Wayne believable as a public figure, and whether the film around them works. Keaton and Bale are often praised for the dual role, Pattinson for the detective side, and West for capturing the fun of the comics of his era. It's also why opinions shift over time, as older films get rewatched and new ones reframe the character.

Lewis Wilson was the first, in Columbia's 1943 film serial *Batman*.
