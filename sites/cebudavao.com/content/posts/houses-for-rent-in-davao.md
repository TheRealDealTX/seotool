Finding a **house for rent in Davao** City is easier than in Manila or Cebu, and rents are generally lower. Popular areas include Lanang, Matina, Ecoland, Buhangin and Catalunan Grande, each with different price points, commutes and flood risks. Here is how to choose an area, what to budget and what landlords will ask for.

## Why Rent in Davao City?

Davao City is huge, covering a large land area with urban districts, suburbs and rural highlands. It has a reputation for being orderly, with strict local ordinances on smoking, noise and curfews for minors. Many people also find the cost of living friendlier than in other major cities. Our comparison of the [cost of living in Cebu vs Davao](/lifestyle/cost-of-living-cebu-vs-davao/) breaks this down.

## Best Areas to Rent a House in Davao

### Lanang

Lanang is upscale and convenient, near the airport, SM Lanang and business hotels. It has gated subdivisions and newer condos. Expect higher rents, but shorter airport runs and good amenities.

### Matina

Matina is central and busy, close to malls, schools and hospitals. It has everything from boarding houses to family homes. Some low-lying parts of Matina have experienced flooding during heavy rain, so check the exact street carefully.

### Ecoland

Ecoland is a well-established residential area near the city centre with subdivisions, apartments and easy access to SM City Davao. It is popular with families and young professionals.

### Buhangin

Buhangin is a large, mixed district north of downtown with many affordable apartments and houses. Commutes are decent, and prices are often lower than in Lanang.

### Catalunan Grande

Catalunan Grande is a growing area west of the city with subdivisions and a cooler, quieter feel. It suits families who want more space and don't mind a longer drive.

### Mintal, Toril and Further Out

Mintal and Toril offer cheaper rents and larger lots, including house-and-lot options for sale. They suit people working nearby or who are comfortable with longer commutes.

## Cheap and Affordable Houses for Rent

To find an affordable house for rent in Davao City:

- **Look further out.** Toril, Mintal, Calinan and parts of Buhangin are cheaper than Lanang or central Matina.
- **Consider unfurnished units.** They often rent for noticeably less.
- **Walk the area.** Many good-value rentals are only advertised with a "For Rent" sign on the gate.
- **Ask locally.** Barangay offices, sari-sari store owners and neighbours often know who is renting.

## Houses with a Sari-Sari Store Space

Some renters look for a house with a sari-sari store space, usually a front room or window facing the street. These are common in residential barangays. If you plan to run a store, ask the landlord for written permission and check what your barangay and the city require for business permits.

## Typical Requirements to Rent

| Requirement | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Advance rent | Usually 1 month |
| Security deposit | Commonly 1 to 2 months, sometimes more |
| Valid ID | Government ID such as a [National ID](/news/philsys-national-id-guide/), passport or driver's licence |
| Proof of income | Payslips, employment certificate or bank statements |
| Contract | Lease agreement, often 6 to 12 months |
| Post-dated cheques | Sometimes requested for condos and executive homes |

> **Tip:** Read the lease carefully. Check who pays association dues, water, electricity, repairs and what deductions may be made from the deposit.

## Safety and Flood-Prone Areas

Davao City is generally considered safe, but due diligence matters:

1. **Check flood history.** Low-lying areas near rivers and creeks can flood during heavy rain. Ask neighbours and the barangay about past flooding before signing.
2. **Visit after rain.** If you can, inspect the street after a downpour to see how it drains.
3. **Check the gate and locks.** Gated subdivisions with guards add peace of mind.
4. **Look at access roads.** Some areas are reachable only through narrow or poorly lit roads.
5. **Confirm water supply.** Ask if the home is connected to the Davao City Water District and whether there is a storage tank. Our guide explains [how to pay the water bill in Davao](/services/where-to-pay-water-bill-in-davao-city/).

## Where to Find Rentals

- Online property portals and classified sites
- Facebook Marketplace and local rental groups (watch for scams)
- Licensed real estate brokers
- "For Rent" signs while walking or driving around

Never send a deposit before seeing the property and confirming the landlord's identity. Scammers sometimes post photos of homes they do not own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is a house for rent in Davao City?

A small 2 to 3 bedroom house typically rents for roughly PHP 12,000 to 30,000 a month, while executive homes in gated subdivisions can cost much more. Prices are approximate and vary by area.

### What is the best area to rent in Davao?

Lanang and Ecoland are popular for convenience and amenities, Matina is central, and Catalunan Grande and Buhangin offer more space for the price. The best choice depends on your workplace and budget.

### What do landlords in Davao usually require?

Most ask for one month's advance rent, a security deposit of one to two months, a valid ID and proof of income. Many also require a signed lease.

### Are there flood-prone areas in Davao City?

Yes, some low-lying areas near rivers and creeks can flood during heavy rain. Ask neighbours and the barangay about flooding history before renting.
