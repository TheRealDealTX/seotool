The main **DFA passport requirements** are a confirmed online appointment, a completed application form, a PSA-issued birth certificate (for first-time applicants) and at least one valid government ID, plus personal appearance at a DFA consular office. Renewals are simpler: you mainly need your current or old passport and an appointment. This guide explains the requirements for new applicants and renewal, the fees, and tips for applying in Cebu and Davao.

> **Tip:** Requirements and fees can change. Always confirm the latest rules on the official DFA passport appointment site, [passport.gov.ph](https://passport.gov.ph), or the [Department of Foreign Affairs](https://dfa.gov.ph) website before your appointment.

## DFA passport requirements at a glance

| Applicant | Key documents |
|---|---|
| New adult applicant (18+) | Appointment, application form, PSA birth certificate, valid government ID, supporting documents if needed |
| Adult renewal | Appointment, application form, current or old passport |
| Minor (below 18) | Appointment, PSA birth certificate, parent's passport or ID, personal appearance with a parent; extra documents in some cases |
| Married woman using married name | Add PSA marriage certificate |
| Lost or damaged passport | Additional documents such as an affidavit of loss; may take longer |

## Step 1: Book an online appointment

Almost all applicants must book a slot through the DFA online appointment system. You choose a consular office, date and time, fill in your details and pay the passport fee through the accredited payment channels shown on the site.

- Use only the official website. Avoid fixers and social media pages that offer "guaranteed slots" for a fee.
- Print or save your appointment confirmation and application form.
- Slots in busy offices, including those in Metro Manila malls, fill quickly. Check often, as new slots are released.

Certain groups, such as senior citizens, persons with disabilities, pregnant women, solo parents, minors below a certain age and overseas Filipino workers, have historically been allowed to use a courtesy lane. Courtesy lane rules can change, so check the DFA's latest announcement before going without an appointment.

## Step 2: DFA requirements for new applicants

First-time adult applicants should bring:

1. **Confirmed appointment** and the accomplished application form.
2. **PSA birth certificate** on PSA security paper, or a certified true copy from the local civil registrar if the PSA copy is unclear or unavailable.
3. **At least one valid government-issued ID** with photo, such as the PhilSys National ID, UMID, driver's licence, PRC ID, postal ID or voter's ID. Bring the original and a photocopy.
4. **Supporting documents** if your ID is limited or your records have issues, for example school records, a baptismal certificate or NBI clearance.
5. **PSA marriage certificate** if you are a married woman who will use your married name.

If you do not have an ID yet, the [PhilSys National ID](/news/philsys-national-id-guide/) is one of the most widely accepted. Bring originals and photocopies of all documents.

### Tips for first-time applicants

- Make sure the spelling of your name, birth date and birthplace are consistent across your birth certificate and IDs.
- If there are errors on your PSA birth certificate, have them corrected before applying.
- Dress neatly for your photo. Avoid coloured contact lenses and heavy accessories.

## Step 3: DFA passport renewal requirements

Adult renewal is faster because your records are already in the system.

- Confirmed appointment and application form.
- Current or most recent passport, plus a photocopy of the data page.
- If your old passport was issued many years ago, or if your name has changed, you may need your PSA birth certificate or marriage certificate.
- A valid ID may be requested.

### DFA passport renewal for senior citizens

Senior citizens follow the same basic renewal requirements but have often been accommodated through a courtesy lane. Bring your senior citizen ID or any proof of age, and consider bringing one companion if you need help. Verify current courtesy-lane rules before your visit.

## DFA passport fees

As of the latest DFA schedule, the commonly cited fees are:

| Processing | Approx. fee | Typical release |
|---|---|---|
| Regular | ₱950 | Longer processing; time varies by office |
| Expedited | ₱1,200 | Faster processing; time varies by office |

Fees and processing times can change, and delivery fees, if you choose courier delivery, are separate. Verify the current amounts on passport.gov.ph before paying.

## DFA consular offices in Cebu and Davao

Both Cebu and Davao have DFA consular offices that process passports. Office locations have moved over the years, so always select your office and check its address through the official appointment system rather than relying on old blog posts.

- **Cebu:** serves Cebu and nearby Visayas provinces. Expect high demand around holidays and the summer travel season.
- **Davao:** serves Davao City and much of Mindanao. Slots fill quickly before school breaks and holidays.

> **Tip:** On your appointment day, arrive early but not too early. Many offices only allow entry shortly before your appointment time.

## How long is a Philippine passport valid?

Adult passports for applicants aged 18 and above are generally valid for 10 years, while passports for minors are generally valid for 5 years. Many countries require at least 6 months of validity remaining on entry, so renew early if you have trips planned.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the DFA requirements for new applicants?

You need a confirmed online appointment, an accomplished application form, a PSA birth certificate and at least one valid government-issued photo ID. Married women using their married name also need a PSA marriage certificate.

### How much is the DFA passport fee?

As of the latest DFA schedule, regular processing is about ₱950 and expedited processing about ₱1,200. Verify the current fees on passport.gov.ph.

### Do I need an appointment to renew my passport?

Most applicants need an online appointment. Some groups, such as senior citizens and persons with disabilities, have been allowed to use courtesy lanes, but check the DFA's current rules first.

### Can someone else apply for my passport?

No. Passport applicants must appear in person for photo, fingerprints and signature. Minors must be accompanied by a parent or authorised adult.

### How long is a Philippine passport valid?

Adult passports are generally valid for 10 years and minors' passports for 5 years.
