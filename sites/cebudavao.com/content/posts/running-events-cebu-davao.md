The **Cebu Marathon** is the best-known road race in Cebu, traditionally held in January and drawing thousands of runners across full, half and shorter distances. Beyond it, Cebu and Davao both have busy running scenes, from charity fun runs and city road races to trail runs in the mountains. Here is what to know and how to train in tropical heat.

## The Cebu Marathon

The Cebu City Marathon has become one of the biggest running events in the Visayas, typically scheduled in January when Cebu's weather is relatively cooler and drier. It usually offers several distances, so beginners can join a short category while seasoned runners tackle the full marathon.

Races start before dawn to avoid the heat, and routes have passed through major city roads. Dates, routes and categories can change each year, so check official race announcements and registration pages before you plan your training.

| Detail | What to know |
|---|---|
| Usual month | January (confirm each year) |
| Typical distances | Full marathon, half marathon and shorter categories |
| Start time | Usually before sunrise |
| Registration | Online or at designated sites, often weeks in advance |
| Typical fee range | Roughly PHP 500 to 2,500, depending on distance and inclusions |

Fees are approximate and change by year and category. Always confirm with the organiser.

> **Tip:** January is also Sinulog season in Cebu. Book accommodation early if you are travelling for the race, and read our [Sinulog Festival guide](/festivals/what-makes-the-sinulog-2012-festival-the-number-one-festival-in-the-philippines/).

## Road Races and Fun Runs

Both Cebu and Davao host fun runs nearly every month, especially in the cooler, drier months. They are often organised by schools, companies, hospitals, local governments and charities. Common formats include:

- **3K and 5K fun runs**, great for beginners and families
- **10K and 21K road races** for regular runners
- **Themed runs**, such as colour runs, night runs and costume runs
- **Charity runs** raising funds for causes

In Davao, look out for running events tied to the [Kadayawan Festival](/festivals/whats-so-interesting-about-the-kadayawan-festival/) in August, as fun runs often appear on the festival programme. Local running clubs and Facebook groups are the best way to find upcoming races.

## Trail Running in Cebu

Cebu City's mountains are only a short ride from downtown, making trail running easy to fit into a week.

### Busay and the Transcentral Highway

The hills of **Busay**, above Cebu City, offer steep roads and trails with views of the city and Mactan. Runners often head up around the Tops area or the Transcentral Highway for hill training. Start early to avoid traffic and heat.

### South Cebu Peaks

For longer adventures, the highlands around **Osmeña Peak** in Dalaguete offer rugged, grassy ridges. These are better for experienced runners and hikers. See our list of [Cebu tourist spots](/travel/destinations/cebu-tourist-spots/) for more on the area.

## Trail Running in Davao

Davao's highlands are cooler than the city and full of scenic roads.

- **Toril and Eden Nature Park area:** Hilly, green and cooler.
- **Calinan and Malagos:** Rolling farm roads near the Philippine Eagle Center.
- **Marilog District:** High-altitude roads and trails with cool weather.
- **Mount Apo region:** The country's highest peak attracts serious mountain runners and hikers. Climbing requires permits and guides. Read our guide to [climbing Mount Apo](/travel/tips/how-to-reach-the-highest-peak-in-the-philippines/).

## Training in the Tropics

Heat and humidity are the biggest challenges for runners in Cebu and Davao.

### Beat the Heat

1. **Run early or late.** Aim for before 7 a.m. or after sunset.
2. **Slow down.** Expect your pace to be slower in humid conditions. Run by effort, not pace.
3. **Wear light, breathable clothing** and a cap or visor.
4. **Use sunscreen**, even on cloudy days.
5. **Check the heat index.** PAGASA publishes heat index forecasts. On very hot days, train indoors or take a rest day.

### Hydration

| Run length | Hydration guide |
|---|---|
| Under 45 minutes | Drink water before and after |
| 45 to 90 minutes | Carry water; sip regularly |
| Over 90 minutes | Water plus electrolytes; plan refill stops |

Sari-sari stores along most routes are handy for buying water. Bring small change.

### Know the Warning Signs

Stop and seek help if you feel dizzy, confused, nauseous, stop sweating or have a severe headache. These can be signs of heat exhaustion or heat stroke.

This article is for general information, not medical advice. See a doctor before starting a new training programme, especially if you have a health condition.

## Rainy Season Running

Rain can be welcome on hot days, but be careful with slippery roads, flooded streets and lightning. Avoid trails during heavy rain, and check [PAGASA typhoon signals](/news/pagasa-typhoon-signals-explained/) before long runs. Use our [weather page](/weather/) for daily conditions.

## Joining a Running Club

Running clubs are a great way to stay motivated, find safe routes and hear about races early. In both cities, groups meet for early-morning runs on weekends, often around parks, business districts and long, flat roads. Many share schedules on social media.

Clubs also help newcomers learn local know-how: which roads have wide shoulders, where to find water, and which races are well organised. If you are visiting, ask at sports shops or your hotel for current group runs.

## Race Day Checklist

- Race bib and safety pins
- Broken-in shoes and socks
- Phone with emergency contacts
- Small cash for transport and water
- Headlamp for pre-dawn starts
- Light breakfast eaten 1 to 2 hours before the gun

For official weather updates, visit [PAGASA](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the Cebu Marathon held?

The Cebu Marathon is traditionally held in January. Exact dates and categories change each year, so check official announcements.

### Can beginners join the Cebu Marathon?

Yes. The event usually includes shorter categories alongside the full and half marathon, making it suitable for beginners.

### Where can I go trail running in Cebu?

Busay and the hills above Cebu City are popular and close to downtown. For longer adventures, the highlands around Osmeña Peak in Dalaguete are a favourite.

### How do I stay safe running in tropical heat?

Run early or late, slow your pace, hydrate with water and electrolytes, and watch for signs of heat exhaustion. Check the heat index before long runs.
