## Destination Notes: The Places Behind the Questions

Whether you aced it or guessed your way through, here's a short tourism refresher on the destinations Filipino travel quizzes love to feature.

### Chocolate Hills, Bohol

More than a thousand rounded hills spread across the interior of Bohol. In the dry season their grass turns brown, which is how they got their name. Bohol is an easy trip from Cebu by fast ferry; see our [Bohol day trip guide](/travel/bohol-day-trip-from-cebu/). The island is also home to the tiny Philippine tarsier.

### Kawasan Falls and Moalboal, Cebu

In southwest Cebu, Kawasan Falls in Badian is famous for its turquoise pools and canyoneering. Nearby Moalboal is known for the sardine run, huge schools of sardines just off the shore. Read our guides to [Kawasan Falls](/travel/kawasan-falls-canyoneering-badian/) and [Moalboal](/travel/moalboal-sardine-run-guide/).

### Mount Apo, Davao

Mount Apo is the highest mountain in the Philippines, at about 2,954 metres, rising between Davao del Sur and Cotabato. It's a serious multi-day climb that needs permits and guides. Our [Mount Apo guide](/travel/tips/how-to-reach-the-highest-peak-in-the-philippines/) explains how.

### Boracay, Aklan

Boracay's White Beach is one of the country's most famous stretches of sand, known for powdery white sand and sunsets. It reopened to tourists in 2018 after a major rehabilitation.

### El Nido and Coron, Palawan

Palawan's limestone cliffs and lagoons make it a quiz favourite. El Nido is known for island-hopping tours through lagoons and beaches, while Coron is famous for its lakes and World War II shipwreck dives. See our [Coron travel guide](/travel/most-beautiful-island-ph-coron-palawan/).

### Banaue Rice Terraces, Ifugao

Carved into the mountains of the Cordillera by the Ifugao people over centuries, the rice terraces are often called a wonder of engineering. The Rice Terraces of the Philippine Cordilleras are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

### Vigan, Ilocos Sur

Vigan's cobblestone Calle Crisologo and Spanish-colonial houses make it one of the best-preserved historic towns in Asia, and it is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Ride a *kalesa* (horse-drawn carriage) through the old town.

### Intramuros, Manila

The "walled city" is Manila's Spanish-era heart, home to Fort Santiago and San Agustin Church. It's easily explored on foot or by bike.

### Siargao, Surigao del Norte

Known as a surfing destination, Siargao is famous for the Cloud 9 surf break, island-hopping and a laid-back island vibe. See our [Siargao travel guide](/travel/siargao-travel-guide/).

### Camiguin and Siquijor

Camiguin, off northern Mindanao, is known for volcanoes, hot and cold springs and a sunken cemetery. Siquijor, south of Cebu, is known for waterfalls, beaches and folk-healing traditions. Read our [Camiguin guide](/travel/camiguin-island-guide/).
