The best **tourist spots in Davao** mix city parks, mountain nature resorts, wildlife centres and island beaches, most within two hours of downtown. Top picks are People's Park, Eden Nature Park, the Philippine Eagle Center, Malagos Garden Resort, Samal Island and Jack's Ridge, with Mount Apo and Marilog for adventurous travellers. Here is a guide to the best places in Davao City to visit, with a table of approximate distances and travel times.

## Tourist spots in Davao at a glance

## 1. People's Park

People's Park is a landscaped public park in downtown Davao City, known for its sculptures inspired by the indigenous peoples of Mindanao, its shady trees and its walking paths. It is free to enter, family-friendly and a pleasant break from the city heat. Visit in the late afternoon when it is cooler, and check the park rules before bringing food in.

## 2. Eden Nature Park

Eden Nature Park sits in the highlands of Toril, where the air is noticeably cooler than downtown. It is known for pine trees, flower gardens, nature trails and adventure activities such as ziplines. Many visitors come for a day tour with a buffet lunch. Read our step-by-step guide on [how to get to Eden Nature Park](/travel/tips/how-to-get-to-eden-nature-park/).

## 3. Philippine Eagle Center

The Philippine Eagle Center in Malagos, Calinan, is run by the Philippine Eagle Foundation and is one of the best places to see the critically endangered Philippine eagle, the national bird. It is a conservation and breeding centre, so visits are educational and calm. See our guide on [how to go to the Philippine Eagle Center](/travel/tips/how-to-go-to-philippine-eagle-center/).

## 4. Malagos Garden Resort

Close to the Eagle Center, Malagos Garden Resort is a garden resort known for its flowers, bird shows, butterfly sanctuary and locally made chocolate. Many travellers combine it with the Eagle Center for a full day in Calinan.

## 5. Samal Island

Officially the Island Garden City of Samal, this island across the Davao Gulf is the city's beach escape. It has white-sand beaches, resorts for every budget, snorkelling spots and the nearby smaller island of Talikud. Most visitors cross by ferry from Davao City, a short ride. Our guide explains [how to reach Samal Island](/travel/tips/how-to-reach-the-samal-island-in-davao/) step by step.

### Davao tourist spots on Samal Island

- **Beach resorts** on the western coast facing Davao City.
- **Island hopping** to Talikud Island for clear water and snorkelling.
- **Pearl Farm Beach Resort**, a well-known upscale resort.

## 6. Jack's Ridge

Jack's Ridge is a hilltop dining and leisure spot on Shrine Hills in Matina. Its main draw is the panoramic view over Davao City and the gulf, especially at sunset and after dark when the city lights come on. It is a favourite for family dinners and visitors' first night out.

## 7. Crocodile Park

The Davao Crocodile Park in Ma-a is a family attraction with crocodiles, other reptiles, birds and animals, plus shows at set times. It is easy to reach and good for children. Confirm show schedules and entrance fees before you go, as they change.

## 8. Museo Dabawenyo

Museo Dabawenyo is a downtown museum that introduces the history, cultures and peoples of Davao, including indigenous and Moro communities. It is a good first stop to understand the city. Check opening days before you go, as museums often close on certain days of the week.

## 9. Roxas Night Market

The Roxas Night Market on Roxas Avenue is a popular evening food street with grilled meat, seafood, fruit shakes and local snacks. It is cheap, lively and a great introduction to Davao street food. Keep your bag in front of you and bring small bills.

## 10. Japanese Tunnel

The Japanese Tunnel in the Matina area is a historical site associated with the Japanese occupation during World War II. It is a short stop that works well combined with other attractions in the Matina area.

## 11. Marilog District

Marilog is a highland district in the northern part of Davao City, about two hours or more from downtown. It is known for cool weather, mountain views, farms and small resorts and campsites. It is a favourite weekend escape for locals. Bring a jacket if you plan to stay overnight.

## 12. Mount Apo

Mount Apo is the highest mountain in the Philippines and a protected area. Climbing it is a serious multi-day trek that requires permits, registered guides and good fitness. Trails start from several jump-off points in Davao City and neighbouring provinces, and access rules can change. Read our guide to [climbing Mount Apo](/travel/tips/how-to-reach-the-highest-peak-in-the-philippines/) before you plan.

## Best time to visit Davao

Davao City sits south of the main typhoon belt and has rain spread across the year rather than a long dry season. Many visitors still prefer the drier months for beaches. If you want to experience the city's biggest celebration, come in August for the Kadayawan Festival. Check the latest forecast on our [Davao weather page](/weather/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most famous tourist spot in Davao?

The Philippine Eagle Center, Samal Island and People's Park are among the most visited. Eden Nature Park and Jack's Ridge are also very popular.

### What are the best tourist spots in Davao City for families?

People's Park, Crocodile Park, Eden Nature Park and Malagos Garden Resort are all family-friendly. Samal Island resorts are good for beach days with children.

### How do I get to Samal Island from Davao City?

Most visitors take a short ferry ride from Davao City across the gulf. Check our Samal Island guide for current terminals and options.

### Can I visit the Philippine Eagle Center and Malagos in one day?

Yes. Both are in the Malagos area of Calinan, so many visitors combine them in a single day trip from downtown.

### Is Mount Apo a day trip from Davao?

No. Climbing Mount Apo usually takes two to four days and requires permits and guides.
