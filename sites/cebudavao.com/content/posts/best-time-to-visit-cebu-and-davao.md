The best time to visit Cebu is generally from January to May, when the weather is drier and seas are calmer for island-hopping and diving. Davao is more flexible: it sits south of the main typhoon belt and has rain spread fairly evenly through the year, so it can be visited almost any month. Plan around festivals like Sinulog in January and Kadayawan in August if you want the party, or avoid those weeks if you want lower prices.

## Cebu vs Davao Weather at a Glance

| | Cebu | Davao |
|---|---|---|
| Driest period | Roughly January to May | No pronounced dry season |
| Wettest period | Roughly June to December | Rain fairly spread across the year |
| Typhoon exposure | Moderate to high, mainly the second half of the year | Low; Davao is rarely hit directly |
| Hottest months | April and May | Warm all year |
| Big festival | Sinulog (January) | Kadayawan (August) |

These are general climate patterns, not forecasts. Weather varies year to year with patterns such as El Niño and La Niña. For current conditions, check our [weather page](/weather/) and official forecasts from [PAGASA](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/).

### Dry season in Cebu (January to May)

This is Cebu's peak season. Skies are clearer, seas calmer, and outdoor trips to [Kawasan Falls](/travel/kawasan-falls-canyoneering-badian/), Moalboal, Malapascua and Bantayan are at their best. February and March are often the sweet spot: dry, not yet scorching, and after the Sinulog rush.

### Wet season and typhoons (June to December)

Rain usually comes in heavy bursts rather than all-day downpours, and you can still have plenty of sunshine. The main concern is typhoons, which can bring strong winds, flooding and cancelled ferries and flights. Cebu has been hit by powerful late-year storms in the past, such as Typhoon Odette (Rai) in December 2021, so travel insurance and flexible plans are smart in these months. Learn the warning levels in our [PAGASA typhoon signals guide](/news/pagasa-typhoon-signals-explained/).

## Best Time to Visit Davao

Davao City's location in southeastern Mindanao keeps it outside the usual paths of most typhoons. Rain is fairly evenly distributed across the year, often as afternoon or evening showers, so there's no strict "wrong" month.

| Period | What to expect in Davao |
|---|---|
| January to April | Often slightly drier; good for Samal beaches and Mount Apo trips |
| May to July | Warm, with regular showers; fruit season begins |
| August | Kadayawan Festival; durian and fruit in abundance; busy hotels |
| September to December | Showers common; Christmas holiday crowds in December |

If you're planning to climb Mount Apo, check trail opening rules and permits in advance, as access can be restricted at times. See our [Mount Apo guide](/travel/tips/how-to-reach-the-highest-peak-in-the-philippines/), and check the Davao forecast on the [weather page](/weather/).

## Festivals Worth Planning Around

## Crowds and Prices

Hotel rates and airfares climb during Sinulog, Holy Week, Kadayawan, Christmas and long weekends. Popular resorts can sell out weeks or months ahead.

- **For the best deals**, travel in the wet-season months outside holidays, accepting some rain.
- **For the best weather in Cebu**, aim for February to April, avoiding Holy Week if you dislike crowds.
- **For festivals**, book flights and rooms as early as possible.

Use the [trip budget calculator](/tools/trip-budget-calculator/) to estimate costs for your dates, and see our [cheap flights guide](/travel/flights-travel/cheap-flights-in-the-philippines/).

## What to Pack for Each Season

| Season | Pack these |
|---|---|
| Dry season (Cebu, roughly January to May) | Sunscreen, hat, light cotton clothes, reef-safe products, refillable water bottle |
| Wet season (Cebu, roughly June to December) | Compact umbrella, light rain jacket, quick-dry clothes, waterproof phone pouch, sandals |
| Davao, any time | Light layers, umbrella, insect repellent, a warm top for highland trips to Marilog or Mount Apo |

Downtown streets can flood quickly in heavy rain, so waterproof sandals are more practical than sneakers during the wet months. If you're heading to the highlands outside Davao City, nights can be surprisingly cool, so bring a light jacket.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best month to visit Cebu?

February and March are often ideal, with dry weather, calm seas and smaller crowds than Sinulog week in January or Holy Week.

### When is typhoon season in Cebu?

Typhoon risk is higher in the second half of the year, roughly July to December. Late-year storms can be strong, so monitor PAGASA forecasts and keep plans flexible.

### Is Davao affected by typhoons?

Davao is rarely hit directly by typhoons, but it still gets regular rain and occasional heavy downpours. It can be visited year-round.

### When is the Sinulog Festival?

Sinulog is held in January, with the grand parade usually on the third Sunday of the month.

### Is it worth visiting Cebu in the rainy season?

Yes, if you're flexible. You'll find lower prices and fewer crowds, but check forecasts and have backup plans for boat trips.
