Finding an **apartment for rent in Cebu** is easiest if you first pick the right area: IT Park and Lahug for work and nightlife, Banilad and Talamban for quieter living, Mabolo and Mandaue for value, Mactan for the airport and beaches, and student areas near universities for the cheapest rooms. Expect to pay anything from a few thousand pesos a month for a bedspace to tens of thousands for a furnished condo unit. Below are neighbourhood comparisons, approximate prices, requirements and tips for renting safely.

## Cebu rental prices at a glance

The figures below are rough monthly ranges based on typical listings and can differ widely by building, size, furnishing and season. Treat them as a starting point and always compare current listings.

| Type | Typical areas | Approx. monthly rent |
|---|---|---|
| Bedspace (shared room) | Near universities, downtown | ₱2,000-₱4,500 |
| Room for rent with own CR | Sambag, Urgello, Labangon, Lahug | ₱4,000-₱9,000 |
| Studio apartment (non-condo) | Mandaue, Talamban, Labangon | ₱6,000-₱15,000 |
| Condo studio, furnished | IT Park, Lahug, Mabolo, Banilad | ₱14,000-₱30,000 |
| 1-bedroom condo, furnished | IT Park, Cebu Business Park | ₱20,000-₱45,000+ |
| House for rent | Labangon, Talamban, Mandaue, Mactan | ₱15,000-₱60,000+ |

Rents are approximate and change with the market. Electricity, water, internet and association dues may or may not be included. Our guide to [the cost of living in Cebu vs Davao](/lifestyle/cost-of-living-cebu-vs-davao/) shows how rent fits into a monthly budget.

## Best areas to find an apartment for rent in Cebu

### IT Park and Lahug

Cebu IT Park is a hub for BPO companies, tech offices, restaurants and nightlife. Lahug around it has condos, apartments and older houses. It suits professionals who want to walk to work, but rents are among the highest in the city. If you work in a call centre, see our [list of call centers in Cebu](/jobs/updated-list-of-call-centers-in-cebu/).

### Cebu Business Park and Mabolo

Cebu Business Park, near Ayala Center Cebu, has upscale condos close to offices and malls. Neighbouring Mabolo offers slightly cheaper apartments while staying central.

### Banilad and Talamban

These uptown areas are quieter, greener and popular with families and long-stay foreigners. Talamban is home to the University of San Carlos Talamban campus, so you will find both student rooms and family homes.

### Mandaue City

Mandaue sits between Cebu City and Mactan and often offers better value, especially for larger units and houses. Traffic on the main roads can be heavy, so check your commute before signing.

### Mactan (Lapu-Lapu City)

Mactan is home to the airport, beach resorts and export processing zones. It suits people who work there or who travel often. The [CCLEX bridge](/news/cclex-cebu-cordova-bridge-guide/) has also made the Cordova side of Mactan more accessible from Cebu City's South Road Properties.

### Labangon, Sambag and Urgello

These older, busy neighbourhoods in Cebu City are close to several universities and downtown. They have many boarding houses, rooms for rent and small apartments at lower prices. Expect narrower streets and more noise.

## Requirements to rent in Cebu

Most landlords and condo owners ask for:

- **Advance rent and deposit.** Commonly one month advance plus one or two months deposit. Some condo owners ask for more.
- **Valid ID.** A government-issued ID or passport for foreigners.
- **Proof of income.** A certificate of employment, payslips or bank statements, especially for condos.
- **Post-dated cheques.** Many condo units and some agents ask for these for long leases.
- **Lease contract.** Usually 6 or 12 months. Read the terms carefully before signing.

> **Tip:** Ask exactly what the deposit covers and how it will be returned. Take dated photos of the unit's condition on move-in day and keep copies of all receipts.

## Where to find listings safely

- **Walk the neighbourhood.** Many boarding houses and apartments still post "For Rent" signs with contact numbers.
- **Property portals and reputable brokers.** Use licensed real estate brokers for condos and houses.
- **Online groups and marketplaces.** Useful but riskier; verify everything.
- **Condo administration offices.** They can sometimes connect you with owners or confirm whether a unit is legitimately for rent.

## Rental scams to avoid

Rental scams are common online. Watch for these red flags:

- **Asking for a reservation fee before a viewing.** Never pay before seeing the unit in person or by live video with a trusted person on site.
- **Prices far below the market.** A furnished IT Park condo at a fraction of normal rent is almost certainly fake.
- **"Owner is abroad, send money and I'll mail the key."** A classic scam.
- **Pressure to pay quickly** because "many others are interested".
- **Payment to personal e-wallets of strangers.** Ask for official receipts and confirm the owner's identity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is an apartment for rent in Cebu?

Roughly ₱2,000 to ₱4,500 for a bedspace, ₱6,000 to ₱15,000 for a basic studio and ₱14,000 to ₱30,000 or more for a furnished condo studio. Prices are approximate and vary by area and building.

### What is the best area to rent in Cebu City?

IT Park and Lahug suit professionals, Banilad and Talamban suit families, and Labangon, Sambag and Urgello suit students on a budget. Mandaue and Mactan can offer better value.

### How much deposit do landlords ask for in Cebu?

Commonly one month advance plus one or two months deposit. Condo owners may ask for more and for post-dated cheques.

### Where can I find a room for rent near Southwestern University Cebu?

Look around Urgello Street, Sambag and Labangon, where many boarding houses and rooms are located. Start early before the semester.

### How do I avoid rental scams in Cebu?

Never pay before viewing the unit, be wary of prices far below market and confirm the owner's identity. Ask for receipts and a written contract.
