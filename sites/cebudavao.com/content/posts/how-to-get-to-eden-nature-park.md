**Eden Nature Park Davao** is a mountain resort in the Toril district of Davao City, and you can reach it by the park's shuttle service, by taxi, by jeepney plus habal-habal, or by private car in roughly an hour from downtown. Set high in the uplands, Eden is famous for pine trees, flower gardens, adventure rides and weather noticeably cooler than the city. This guide covers every way to get there, what to do, and how to plan your day.

## Eden Nature Park Davao at a Glance

| Detail | Info |

Opening hours, rates and shuttle schedules change, so confirm the latest details on Eden's official channels before your trip.

## How to Get to Eden Nature Park

### 1. Eden shuttle from the city

Eden Nature Park has run a shuttle service from a pick-up point in the city, historically from the Matina Town Square area. Shuttles are often bundled with day-tour packages that include the round-trip ride, entrance and lunch. This is the easiest choice if you don't have a car, but seats can sell out on weekends and holidays, so reserve early and confirm the current pick-up point.

### 2. Taxi in Davao City

A taxi is convenient for small groups. Davao taxis are metered and generally have a good reputation, but the drive up the mountain road is long, so agree on the plan with the driver before leaving. Many drivers are happy to wait or come back for an agreed fee, which saves you from searching for a ride down later, since taxis are not always available at the park.

Ride-hailing apps also operate in Davao City, but pick-ups from the mountain can be unreliable. Arrange your return trip in advance. For tips on Davao's taxi culture, see our [Davao airport taxi guide](/travel/taxi-procedure-davao-international-airport/).

### 3. Jeepney and habal-habal

For a budget trip:

1. Take a jeepney bound for Toril from downtown Davao.
2. From Toril, ride a jeepney or multicab heading toward the uplands, or hire a habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) to Eden.
3. Ask locals or drivers to confirm the right stop.

This is the cheapest option but takes longer and is less comfortable, especially with kids or luggage.

### 4. Private car

Driving gives you the most freedom. Head south toward Toril, then follow the signs uphill to Eden. The road climbs and winds, so drive carefully in the rain and fog. Use our [distance calculator](/travel/mileage-from-to-distance-between-cities/) to plan your route if you're combining Eden with other stops.

> **Tip:** Leave the city early in the morning. You'll enjoy the clearest views, avoid traffic, and have time for activities before the afternoon mist and rain roll in.

## Things to Do at Eden Nature Park

Eden is more than a garden. Typical activities include:

- **Walking trails** through pine forests and landscaped gardens.
- **Adventure rides** such as zip lines and sky-ride style attractions, usually paid separately.
- **Horseback riding** on gentle trails.
- **Fishing** at the park's ponds.
- **Playgrounds and open lawns** for kids and picnics.
- **Garden and farm tours** showing vegetables, herbs and flowers grown on site.

Not every attraction runs every day, and fees differ per activity, so check what's open when you arrive.

## Food at Eden

The park has restaurants, and day-tour packages commonly include a buffet lunch featuring vegetables and herbs from its own gardens. Bring snacks and water for the trails. If you want to explore more of the city's dining scene after your trip, our [Davao food guide](/food/the-delectable-food-in-davao/) has plenty of ideas.

## Weather: Why Eden Feels Cooler

Eden sits high in the mountains, so temperatures are lower than at sea level in downtown Davao. Mornings can be fresh and pleasant, and afternoons often bring fog or rain. Pack:

- A light jacket or long-sleeved shirt
- An umbrella or rain jacket
- Comfortable walking shoes with grip
- Insect repellent

## Practical Tips

- **Book ahead** on weekends, holidays and during the Kadayawan Festival in August.
- **Bring cash**, as signal for e-wallets and cards can be weak in the mountains.
- **Respect the rules** on smoking, trash and noise. Davao City is known for strict local ordinances, including a smoking ban in public places.
- **Plan your return ride** before going, especially if you came by taxi or habal-habal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get to Eden Nature Park from Davao City?

You can take Eden's shuttle from its city pick-up point, hire a taxi, drive, or take a jeepney to Toril and then a habal-habal uphill. The trip usually takes about 1 to 1.5 hours.

### Can I take a taxi to Eden Nature Park?

Yes. Metered taxis will take you there, but taxis are not always available for the return trip. Arrange with your driver to wait or pick you up later for an agreed fee.

### Is Eden Nature Park cold?

It is noticeably cooler than downtown Davao because of its high elevation. Mornings are fresh, and afternoons often bring mist or rain, so bring a light jacket.

### Is Eden Nature Park good for kids?

Yes. It has open lawns, gardens, playgrounds, horseback riding and easy trails. Some adventure rides have age or height limits, so check on arrival.

### Do I need to book in advance?

Booking is recommended for shuttle seats, buffet packages and overnight rooms, especially on weekends and holidays.
