The best area for **hotels near Davao airport** is Lanang, especially around Damosa and SM Lanang Premier, which is typically a 10 to 20 minute drive from Francisco Bangoy International Airport outside rush hour. Sasa and Buhangin are even closer but have fewer choices, while downtown Davao is roughly 25 to 45 minutes away depending on traffic. If you have an early flight or a late arrival, staying in Lanang gives you the best balance of short transfer time, restaurants and mall access.

## Davao Airport Location: The Basics

Francisco Bangoy International Airport (DVO) is in the northern part of Davao City, in the Buhangin district along the Sasa side of the city. It is Mindanao's busiest airport, with domestic flights across the Philippines and a handful of international routes.

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Official name | Francisco Bangoy International Airport |
| Airport code | DVO |
| Location | Buhangin district, north of downtown Davao City |
| Distance to downtown | Roughly 10 to 12 km by road |
| Main nearby areas | Sasa, Lanang, Damosa, Buhangin |
| Ground transport | Airport taxis, ride-hailing cars, hotel shuttles |

## Hotels Near Davao Airport: Best Areas to Stay

### Lanang and Damosa (best overall)

Lanang is the area most travellers mean when they search for a hotel near Davao airport. It sits between the airport and downtown along J.P. Laurel Avenue and has grown into a modern business and lifestyle district. Damosa Gateway, SM Lanang Premier and its SMX Convention Center are all here.

Why stay here:

- Short taxi ride to the terminal outside peak hours
- Business hotels, serviced apartments and mid-range options
- Malls, restaurants and coffee shops within a short ride
- Easy access to the Sasa wharf for ferries to Samal

### Sasa and Buhangin (closest)

These neighbourhoods are right beside the airport. Choices are more limited and are mostly smaller inns, pension houses and budget hotels. They suit very early flights or overnight transits when you just need a clean bed.

### Bajada and J.P. Laurel Avenue (middle ground)

Bajada, home to Abreeza and Gaisano Mall of Davao, is further from the airport than Lanang but closer than the old downtown. It offers a wide range of hotels and good restaurant choices.

### Downtown Davao (most to see)

Downtown, around San Pedro Street, People's Park and Roxas Avenue, is the city's historic heart. It is better for sightseeing and food but adds travel time to the airport, especially at rush hour.

> **Tip:** Davao traffic peaks on weekday mornings and late afternoons. For flights between 6 and 9 a.m. or 4 and 8 p.m., add a generous buffer even from Lanang.

## What to Look For When Booking

Hotel names and services change, so rather than relying on old lists, check these points on the booking page or with the hotel directly:

1. **Recent reviews.** Look at reviews from the last few months to confirm the hotel is operating and well maintained.
2. **Airport shuttle.** Some hotels offer free or paid airport transfers. Confirm the schedule, especially for early-morning flights.
3. **24-hour front desk.** Essential if you land late at night.
4. **Early check-in or late check-out.** Useful when your flight times do not match standard check-in hours.
5. **Soundproofing.** Hotels very close to the runway may hear aircraft noise.
6. **Rooms with blackout curtains** if you need daytime sleep between flights.
7. **Breakfast timing.** Ask if they offer early packed breakfasts for dawn departures.

## Getting to and From the Airport

Davao has a reputation for well-regulated taxis, and many drivers use the meter without being asked. At the airport, follow the signs to the official taxi queue. Our [Davao airport taxi guide](/travel/taxi-procedure-davao-international-airport/) explains the procedure and what to expect.

Ride-hailing cars are another option, and some hotels in Lanang run shuttles. If you are heading to Samal, the Sasa ferry area is close to the airport, which makes Lanang a smart base for a beach trip. See [how to reach Samal Island](/travel/tips/how-to-reach-the-samal-island-in-davao/).

## Davao Airport Map Tips

The terminal is compact and easy to navigate. Departures and arrivals are on separate levels, with check-in counters, security and gates clearly signed. Arrive at least two hours before domestic flights and three hours before international ones, or follow your airline's advice. Check the official [Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines website](https://caap.gov.ph) or your airline for any terminal updates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the nearest area to stay near Davao airport?

Sasa and Buhangin are right next to the airport, while Lanang and Damosa are a short drive away and offer far more hotel choices. Most travellers prefer Lanang for its balance of convenience and amenities.

### How far is Davao airport from the city center?

The airport is roughly 10 to 12 km from downtown Davao. The drive usually takes 25 to 45 minutes depending on traffic.

### Do hotels near Davao airport offer free shuttles?

Some do, but many charge a fee or only run at certain times. Confirm the shuttle service and schedule with your hotel when booking.

### Is it better to stay in Lanang or downtown Davao?

Lanang is better for short airport transfers and modern malls, while downtown is better for sightseeing, local food and heritage sites. For a short layover, choose Lanang.
