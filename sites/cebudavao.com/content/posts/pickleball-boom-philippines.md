**Pickleball in the Philippines** has grown from a niche pastime into one of the country's fastest-rising recreational sports, with new players filling courts in malls, clubs, condos and barangay gyms. It is easy to learn, social and cheap to start, which makes it ideal for barkadas, families and office groups in Cebu and Davao. This guide explains why it is trending, how to play, what equipment costs and how to find a game near you.

## Why pickleball in the Philippines is booming

Pickleball was invented in the United States in the 1960s and spread worldwide over the past decade. In the Philippines, interest jumped as people looked for active, social hobbies, and as clips of rallies and tournaments spread on social media. Several factors explain the boom:

- **Easy to learn.** Most beginners can rally within their first session.
- **Social.** It is mostly played as doubles, and open-play sessions mix players of different levels.
- **Fits Filipino courts.** Existing basketball, badminton and tennis courts can be marked for pickleball, sometimes with several pickleball courts on one surface.
- **All ages.** Teens, office workers and seniors can play together.
- **Low impact.** Shorter court and slower ball mean less running than tennis.
- **Affordable gear.** A basic paddle and a few balls are enough to start.

## Pickleball basics: how to play

Pickleball combines elements of tennis, badminton and table tennis. It is played with solid paddles and a lightweight plastic ball with holes, over a net slightly lower than a tennis net.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Court size | 20 x 44 feet (about 6.1 x 13.4 m), same as a badminton doubles court |
| Net height | 36 inches at the sidelines, 34 inches at the centre |
| Kitchen | Non-volley zone, 7 feet from the net on each side |
| Format | Singles or doubles; doubles most common |
| Scoring | Games usually to 11 points, win by 2 |
| Points | Scored only by the serving side (traditional scoring) |

### Key rules for beginners

1. **Serve underhand.** The serve is made with an underhand motion and must land diagonally in the opponent's service court, beyond the kitchen.
2. **Two-bounce rule.** The receiving team must let the serve bounce, and the serving team must let the return bounce, before anyone can volley (hit the ball in the air).
3. **Stay out of the kitchen when volleying.** You cannot volley while standing in the non-volley zone or touching its line. You may enter the kitchen to hit a ball that has bounced.
4. **Faults end the rally.** Hitting into the net, out of bounds, or volleying from the kitchen are faults.
5. **Doubles serving order.** In traditional doubles scoring, each partner serves before the serve passes to the other team, except at the start of the game. The score is called as three numbers: serving team's score, receiving team's score and server number.

Rules are updated from time to time by the sport's governing bodies, so club organisers may follow slightly different versions. Ask about local house rules at open play.

> **Tip:** The "dink", a soft shot that lands in the opponent's kitchen, is the heart of pickleball. Practise dinking with a partner and you will improve faster than by smashing every ball.

## Pickleball equipment costs in the Philippines

You need only a paddle, balls, comfortable court shoes and sportswear. Prices below are approximate ranges and vary by brand, shop and promotions.

| Item | Approx. price range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner paddle | ₱1,000-₱3,000 | Fine for learning |
| Mid-range paddle | ₱3,000-₱7,000 | Better feel and control |
| Premium paddle | ₱7,000-₱15,000+ | For regular competitive players |
| Balls (pack) | ₱300-₱1,200 | Outdoor and indoor balls differ |
| Court shoes | ₱2,000-₱7,000+ | Non-marking soles for indoor courts |
| Court rental | ₱200-₱1,000+ per hour | Varies widely by venue and time |

Many groups lend paddles to first-timers, so you can try the sport before buying. Watch out for counterfeit branded paddles sold online at suspiciously low prices.

## Where to play pickleball in Cebu and Davao

New venues open and close often, so the best approach is to search for active communities rather than relying on old lists.

### Types of venues

- **Sports clubs and private courts.** Many clubs have added pickleball lines to tennis or badminton courts and offer hourly rentals.
- **Malls and commercial complexes.** Some malls have rooftop or indoor sports areas with pickleball courts or pop-up events.
- **Condominium courts.** Residents in many Cebu and Davao condos play on building courts; check with your administration.
- **Barangay and school gyms.** Covered courts are sometimes rented to pickleball groups at off-peak times.
- **Hotels and resorts.** Some offer courts for guests or members.

### How to find a group

- Search social media for "pickleball Cebu" or "pickleball Davao" to find community groups and open-play schedules.
- Ask at sports shops that sell paddles; staff usually know where people play.
- Look for beginner clinics, which many clubs offer to new players.
- Join open-play sessions, where you rotate partners and meet the local community.

Davao's tropical weather means early mornings and evenings are the most comfortable outdoor times. Check the [Cebu and Davao weather](/weather/) before heading to outdoor courts.

## Beginner tips

- **Warm up and stretch.** Quick lateral moves can strain calves and shoulders.
- **Wear proper shoes.** Running shoes are not designed for side-to-side movement.
- **Stay hydrated.** Courts get hot, especially outdoors.
- **Learn the etiquette.** Call the score clearly, return balls to the next court politely and tap paddles after games.
- **Do not rush to buy expensive gear.** Borrow or start with a beginner paddle.
- **Be patient with the kitchen rule.** It feels strange at first but becomes natural.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is pickleball so popular in the Philippines?

It is easy to learn, social, suitable for all ages and can be played on existing courts. Social media has also helped spread the sport quickly.

### How much does it cost to start playing pickleball?

A beginner paddle costs roughly ₱1,000 to ₱3,000, plus balls and court fees. Prices are approximate and many groups lend paddles to beginners.

### Where can I play pickleball in Cebu or Davao?

Look for sports clubs, malls with sports areas, condo courts and barangay gyms. Searching social media for local pickleball groups is the fastest way to find open-play sessions.

### What is the kitchen in pickleball?

The kitchen is the non-volley zone, 7 feet from the net on each side. You cannot hit the ball out of the air while standing in it.

### Is pickleball good for seniors?

Many seniors enjoy pickleball because it is lower impact than tennis. Anyone with health concerns should check with a doctor before starting a new sport.
