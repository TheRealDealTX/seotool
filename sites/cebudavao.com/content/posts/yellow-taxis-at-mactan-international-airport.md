At Mactan-Cebu International Airport (MCIA), you will usually find two kinds of taxis: **yellow cabs**, the airport taxis with a higher flag-down rate, and **white metered taxis**, the regular city taxis that are a little cheaper. A yellow cab Cebu airport ride is generally more comfortable and easy to find at the arrivals bay, while white taxis, Grab and MyBus buses can save you money if you know where to look.

Here is how each option works, what it roughly costs and how to choose.

## Your Options at a Glance

| Option | Typical cost to Cebu City (approx.) | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow airport taxi | PHP 400-800 | Easy, dedicated queue, metered | Higher flag-down and per-km rate |
| White metered taxi | PHP 300-600 | Cheaper, metered | Queue can be longer |
| Grab (car) | PHP 350-900+ | Fixed price shown upfront, card or e-wallet | Surge pricing at peak times |
| MyBus / airport bus | Roughly PHP 50-100 | Cheapest, air-conditioned | Fixed routes, limited hours, luggage hassle |
| Hotel transfer | Varies | Door to door, pre-arranged | Usually the priciest |

These are approximate ranges for trips to central Cebu City (for example IT Park, Fuente or Colon) and depend heavily on traffic, route and tolls. Always check current rates.

## Yellow Cab Cebu: The Airport Taxis

Yellow airport taxis are accredited to serve the airport and wait at a dedicated bay outside arrivals. They use a meter, but the flag-down rate and per-kilometre charge are higher than regular white taxis. In exchange, the cars are often newer and roomier, and the queue is managed by airport staff.

Yellow cabs are a good choice when:

- You land late at night and want the quickest, simplest ride
- You have lots of luggage or are travelling as a family
- You prefer not to use an app or do not have mobile data yet

## White Metered Taxis

Regular white taxis also pick up passengers at the airport at a separate bay. They charge the standard Cebu meter rate, so the final fare is usually lower than a yellow cab for the same trip. Make sure the meter is switched on at the start of the ride.

> **Tip:** If a driver offers a fixed "contract" price instead of the meter, politely decline and ask for the meter, or walk to the next car. Tolls (such as the CCLEX bridge) are added to the fare.

## Grab and Ride-Hailing

Grab operates in Cebu and has designated pickup areas at the airport terminals. The fare is shown in the app before you book, so there are no surprises, and you can pay by card or e-wallet. During peak times, rainy evenings or holidays, surge pricing can make Grab more expensive than a taxi. Compare before booking.

Airport Wi-Fi is available, but having a local SIM or eSIM makes everything easier. If you need to pay by e-wallet, our guide to [GCash vs Maya](/tech/gcash-vs-maya-ewallet-guide/) explains the main options.

## MyBus and Airport Buses

MyBus is a bus service that has run routes connecting the airport with major malls and terminals in Metro Cebu, such as SM City Cebu. It is by far the cheapest option and is air-conditioned. Routes, stops and hours change, so check the current schedule at the airport or online before relying on it, especially for late flights.

The bus works best if you travel light and your hotel is near a mall or main road where you can catch a short taxi or Grab ride onward.

## Which Route Will Your Taxi Take?

From Mactan to Cebu City, drivers usually use one of these:

- **Marcelo Fernan Bridge or Mactan-Mandaue Bridge** — the older bridges to Mandaue, often congested at rush hour
- **CCLEX (Cebu-Cordova Link Expressway)** — the newer toll bridge, which can be faster for destinations in the south of the city; read our [CCLEX guide](/news/cclex-cebu-cordova-bridge-guide/)

Traffic is the biggest factor in both time and cost. A trip that takes 30 minutes at night can take more than an hour at rush hour.

## Tips for Taking a Taxi at Mactan Airport

1. **Use the official taxi bays.** Ignore people offering rides inside the terminal.
2. **Ask for the meter** and check that it starts at the correct flag-down rate.
3. **Have small bills.** Drivers may not have change for large notes.
4. **Note the taxi's plate number** or take a photo of it, in case you leave something behind.
5. **Keep the airport slip** if staff give one at the queue; it records your taxi details.
6. **Know your hotel's address and landmark.** Cebu drivers navigate by landmarks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between yellow and white taxis in Cebu?

Yellow taxis are accredited airport taxis with a higher flag-down and per-kilometre rate. White taxis are regular city cabs with the standard meter rate, so they are usually cheaper for the same trip.

### How much is a taxi from Mactan airport to Cebu City?

Roughly PHP 300-600 by white taxi and PHP 400-800 by yellow cab, depending on traffic, route and tolls. These ranges are approximate and change, so check the meter.

### Is Grab available at Mactan-Cebu International Airport?

Yes. Grab has designated pickup points at the airport. The fare is shown upfront, but surge pricing can apply during busy times.

### What is the Mactan-Cebu airport contact number?

Contact details change, so use the numbers and channels listed on the official airport website or contact your airline directly for flight-specific concerns.
