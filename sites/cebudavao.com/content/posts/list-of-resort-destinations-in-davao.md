The best **Samal Island resorts** range from the luxurious Pearl Farm Beach Resort to easy day-trip spots like Paradise Island Park & Beach Resort and the public Kaputian Beach Park, all a short boat ride from Davao City. If you prefer cool air over sand, Davao's highland resorts such as Eden Nature Park and Malagos Garden Resort are within driving distance of downtown. This guide groups the top options by budget and travel style so you can pick the right one quickly.

## Luxury: Pearl Farm Beach Resort

Pearl Farm is Samal's best-known upscale resort, built on the site of a former pearl farm on the island's coast. Its stilt cottages over the water, villas on neighbouring Malipano Island and quiet coves make it a favourite for honeymooners and special occasions. Guests usually travel by the resort's own boat from Davao City.

We cover rooms, activities and practical tips in our full [Pearl Farm Davao guide](/beach/pearl-farm-davao/).

> **Tip:** Even if you are not staying overnight, some upscale resorts have offered day-use packages. Ask in advance, as availability is limited and rules change.

## Mid-Range: Family-Friendly Samal Resorts

### Paradise Island Park & Beach Resort

Located in the Babak district, close to the ferry landing from Davao City, Paradise Island is one of the most popular day-trip choices. It has a long stretch of fine sand, picnic cottages and space for big family groups, plus overnight rooms. Read our [Paradise Resort guide](/beach/paradise-resort/) for details.

### Maxima Aqua Fun

Maxima Aqua Fun is known for water-based activities rather than quiet relaxation, making it a fun pick for barkadas (groups of friends) and teams. Expect activity fees on top of entrance, and check the current list of attractions before you go.

### Other mid-range resorts

Samal has dozens of smaller resorts, and travellers often search for names like Mahan Garden Resort, Costa Marina, Caliclic, Dayang, Precious, Holiday Beach and Bluewaters. We have not profiled them individually here because facilities, ownership and prices change. Before booking any of them, look at recent guest reviews and photos, and confirm rates, room inclusions and boat transfers directly with the resort.

## Budget: Public Beaches and Natural Spots

### Kaputian Beach Park

Kaputian Beach Park is a public beach on Samal with a modest entrance fee, sand, shade trees and cottages for rent. It is a great choice for families who want a low-cost beach day and don't need resort amenities.

### Canibad and the east coast

Samal's east side faces open water and has rockier, more rugged beaches, including the Canibad area. It feels less developed than the western coast facing Davao City. Bring your own food and water, as facilities can be basic.

### Hagimit Falls

For a change from salt water, Hagimit Falls in Peñaplata offers cool freshwater pools set among trees. It is usually reached by habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) or private vehicle from the Peñaplata area.

### Monfort Bat Sanctuary

Not a resort but a must-see, the Monfort Bat Sanctuary in Babak is home to a huge colony of Geoffroy's rousette fruit bats inside a cave system. Visit quietly and follow the guides' instructions.

## Talikud Island: The Quieter Neighbour

Talikud, south of Samal, is smaller and calmer. Isla Reta is its best-known beach stop, popular for its white sand and clear water. Boats leave from Samal and from Davao City; check current schedules before you go.

## Highland Resorts in Davao City

Not everyone wants a beach. Davao City has cooler upland escapes:

- **Eden Nature Park** in Toril sits high in the mountains, with pine trees, gardens and adventure rides. Read [how to get to Eden Nature Park](/travel/tips/how-to-get-to-eden-nature-park/).
- **Malagos Garden Resort** in Calinan is known for its gardens, bird shows and chocolate.

## How to Get to Samal Island Resorts

Most visitors cross from Davao City by ferry or barge. Passenger boats and RoRo (roll-on/roll-off) vessels run from wharves in the Sasa area to Babak, taking only about 10 to 20 minutes. Upscale resorts such as Pearl Farm typically arrange their own boat transfers.

For the full breakdown, read [how to reach Samal Island from Davao City](/travel/tips/how-to-reach-the-samal-island-in-davao/). A bridge linking Davao City and Samal has been under construction, which may change travel options in the future.

## How to Choose the Right Resort

1. **Day trip or overnight?** For a day trip, stay near Babak. For a romantic or quiet stay, look farther south toward Kaputian.
2. **Beach or pool?** Some resorts focus on pools and activities, while others have better natural beaches.
3. **Food rules.** Many resorts charge a corkage or food fee if you bring your own meals. Ask first.
4. **Transfers.** Check whether boat and land transfers are included or extra.
5. **Season.** Davao is outside the main typhoon belt, but rain can still affect sea conditions. Check the [Davao weather page](/weather/) before you go.

## Budget Planning

Plan for entrance fees, cottage rentals, ferry fares, tricycle or habal-habal rides and food. Day trips to public beaches are the cheapest, while overnight stays at upscale resorts cost several times more. Use our [trip budget calculator](/tools/trip-budget-calculator/) to estimate your total, and remember all prices are approximate and change often.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best resort on Samal Island?

Pearl Farm Beach Resort is the best-known luxury option. For families on a day trip, Paradise Island Park & Beach Resort is a popular, convenient choice near the Babak ferry landing.

### How do I get to Samal Island resorts from Davao City?

Take a ferry or barge from the Sasa area wharves to Babak, which takes roughly 10 to 20 minutes, then a tricycle or habal-habal to your resort. Luxury resorts often provide their own boat transfers.

### Are there cheap beaches on Samal Island?

Yes. Kaputian Beach Park is a public beach with a modest entrance fee, and Hagimit Falls offers inexpensive freshwater swimming.

### Can I bring food to Samal resorts?

Policies vary. Some resorts allow outside food with a fee, while others don't allow it at all. Always ask the resort before your trip.

### Are there resorts in Davao City itself?

Yes. Eden Nature Park in Toril and Malagos Garden Resort in Calinan are popular highland resorts within Davao City, offering cooler weather and nature activities.
