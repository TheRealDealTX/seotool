When people search for **internet providers Cebu** and Davao households can actually get, the main names are PLDT Home, Globe At Home and Converge for fiber, with Sky Fiber in some areas, DITO for 5G home wireless and Starlink for satellite internet in places fiber does not reach. For most homes in Metro Cebu and Davao City, fiber is the best choice, with entry plans typically starting at roughly ₱1,300 to ₱1,700 a month. Coverage varies street by street, so always check availability at your exact address before deciding.

## Fiber Internet in Cebu and Davao

Fiber-to-the-home is now widely available in Metro Cebu (Cebu City, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu, Talisay and nearby towns) and in much of Davao City. Fiber offers the fastest and most stable connection, with low latency for video calls and gaming.

### PLDT Home

PLDT is the country's oldest major telecom company and has an extensive fiber network. Its plans usually bundle a modem with WiFi, and some include landline or streaming add-ons.

### Globe At Home

Globe offers fiber plans in many areas and also sells prepaid home WiFi devices that run on its mobile network. Prepaid WiFi is popular with renters and students because there is no long contract, though speeds depend on signal.

### Converge ICT

Converge started as a fiber provider in Central Luzon and expanded across the country, including the Visayas and Mindanao. It focuses on fiber-only plans and has become a strong competitor in Cebu and Davao.

### Sky Fiber

Sky has offered fiber broadband in selected areas, though its broadband business has gone through changes in recent years. Check current availability, plans and who services your account before signing up.

## Wireless and Satellite Options

### DITO home WiFi

DITO Telecommunity, the newest of the major mobile networks, sells 5G and 4G home WiFi. It can be a good option if DITO's signal is strong where you live, but speeds can vary with network load and location. Test your area's signal first.

### Starlink

Starlink, the satellite internet service, launched in the Philippines in 2023. It is especially useful for rural homes, islands, farms and resorts where fiber is unavailable, such as parts of northern Cebu, the mountain barangays of Davao City, or small islands. It requires buying a kit and paying a monthly subscription, which typically costs more than urban fiber.

## How to Choose an Internet Provider

1. **Check coverage at your exact address.** Use each provider's online availability checker or ask their sales channels. Your neighbour's provider may not reach your building.
2. **Ask neighbours and building admins.** In condos, some providers have better in-building wiring. Ask which ones residents use and how reliable they are.
3. **Compare real-world reliability.** Speed matters, but outages and repair times matter more. Local Facebook groups often have candid feedback.
4. **Read the contract.** Note the lock-in period, termination fees and what happens if you move.
5. **Consider a backup.** Many remote workers keep a mobile data plan or prepaid WiFi as a backup during outages.
6. **Check upload speed** if you do video calls, streaming or upload large files.

> **Tip:** Fiber can still be affected by typhoons and flooding. A small UPS (uninterruptible power supply) for your modem keeps you online during short brownouts, and a mobile hotspot helps during longer outages.

## Mobile Data Options

Smart, Globe and DITO all offer prepaid and postpaid mobile data. 5G coverage has expanded in central parts of Cebu and Davao, though it remains patchy elsewhere. Mobile data works well as a backup or for light users, and many people use a pocket WiFi device on the go.

## From Smart Bro to Fiber: A Quick History

A little over a decade ago, many Cebu and Davao households relied on DSL lines or wireless services such as Smart Bro, often using a rooftop antenna or a USB stick. Speeds were slow by today's standards, and video calls were frustrating. The big shift came with the spread of fiber-to-the-home and competition from newer providers, which pushed speeds up and prices down in many areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best internet provider in Cebu?

It depends on your exact location. PLDT, Globe and Converge all offer fiber in Metro Cebu, so check coverage at your address and ask neighbours about reliability.

### How much is home internet in Davao?

Entry-level fiber plans typically start at roughly ₱1,300 to ₱1,700 a month, with faster plans costing more. Prices and promos change, so check providers' official sites.

### Is Starlink available in Cebu and Davao?

Yes, Starlink has been available in the Philippines since 2023 and can be used in Cebu and Davao, especially in rural or island areas without fiber.

### Is prepaid home WiFi good enough for working from home?

It can be for light use, but speeds depend on mobile signal and network load. For video calls and heavy work, fiber is more reliable.

### How do I check if fiber is available at my address?

Use each provider's online coverage checker or contact their official sales channels with your exact address. Building administrators can also tell you which providers are already installed.
