To find the **mileage between two cities**, start with the straight-line (as-the-crow-flies) distance, then adjust for how you will actually travel: roads wind around mountains, ferries follow sea lanes and flights add airport time. Use our distance tool below for the straight-line figure in kilometres and miles, then check the table for realistic travel times between popular Philippine cities. In an island country, the fastest route is often not the shortest one on the map.

## Straight-Line vs Road Distance

The figure most calculators show first is the great-circle distance, the shortest path over the Earth's surface. It is useful for comparing places and estimating flight distance, but it underestimates road trips.

| Distance type | What it measures | Best used for |
|---|---|---|
| Straight-line (great-circle) | Shortest path over the Earth's surface | Flights, quick comparisons |
| Road distance | Actual length of the roads you drive | Bus and car trips |
| Sea distance | Ferry route between ports | Boat trips between islands |
| Travel time | Door-to-door time including waits | Real planning |

In the Philippines, road distance is often 20 to 50 percent longer than the straight line, and in mountainous areas it can be more. Ferries add waiting time at ports, and flights add check-in and transfers. That is why travel time matters more than mileage when planning.

## How to Estimate Travel Time

### By land

Philippine highways mix fast stretches with town centres, tricycles, jeepneys and roadworks. A realistic rule of thumb for long-distance buses is an average of 40 to 60 km/h including stops. Cities like Cebu, Davao and Manila add heavy rush-hour delays, so add extra time if you travel between roughly 7 and 9 a.m. or 5 and 8 p.m.

### By sea

Fast craft are much quicker than conventional ferries and roll-on, roll-off (ro-ro) vessels, but they are more sensitive to rough seas. Always add time for check-in, terminal fees and boarding. During typhoons, the Coast Guard may suspend trips, so check the [PAGASA typhoon signals guide](/news/pagasa-typhoon-signals-explained/) before sailing.

### By air

Flight time is only part of the trip. Add travel to the airport, check-in (often 1.5 to 2 hours before domestic flights), boarding and baggage claim. A one-hour flight can easily become a four-hour door-to-door journey. Our guide to [cheap flights in the Philippines](/travel/flights-travel/cheap-flights-in-the-philippines/) helps you compare.

> **Tip:** For trips under about 150 km within one island, a bus or car is often just as fast as flying once airport time is included.

## World Distances Between Cities

The same method works internationally. Straight-line distance gives you a good idea of flight length, and airlines plan routes close to the great-circle path, though air-traffic rules and winds change it slightly. As a rough rule, jet aircraft cruise at around 800 to 900 km/h, so a 2,000 km straight-line distance usually means a flight of about 2.5 to 3 hours including climb and descent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I calculate the mileage between two cities?

Use a distance tool to get the straight-line distance, then add 20 to 50 percent for road trips to account for curves and detours. Convert kilometres to miles by multiplying by about 0.621.

### What is the distance between Cebu and Davao?

The straight-line distance is roughly 400 km (about 250 miles). Most travellers fly, which takes about an hour, though land-and-sea routes via Leyte and Surigao also exist.

### Why is road distance longer than straight-line distance?

Roads follow terrain, coastlines and town centres rather than a straight path. In the Philippines, mountains and islands make the difference especially large.

### How far is Manila from Cebu?

The straight-line distance is roughly 570 km (about 355 miles). A direct flight usually takes about an hour and a quarter to an hour and a half.
