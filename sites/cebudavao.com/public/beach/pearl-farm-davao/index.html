Pearl Farm Davao: Guide to Pearl Farm Beach Resort, Samal

Pearl Farm Beach Resort on Samal is one of Davao's best-known luxury stays. Here is its story, what to expect, how to get there and who will love it.

By Cebu-Davao Editorial Team · October 7, 2026 · 5 min read

Pearl Farm Davao usually refers to Pearl Farm Beach Resort, a luxury island resort on the western coast of Samal Island, across the gulf from Davao City. It is built on the site of a former pearl farm, is known for its stilt houses over the water and hilltop rooms inspired by indigenous Mandaya design, and is reached by the resort's own boat from Davao City.

If you are considering it for a honeymoon, family holiday or a special weekend, this guide covers the history, rooms, getting there and practical tips.

From pearl farm to beach resort

As its name suggests, the property was once a working pearl farm. Oysters were cultivated in the surrounding waters to produce pearls before the site was developed into a resort. The resort keeps that heritage in its name and much of its identity, and guests often enjoy learning a little of the story during their stay.

The architecture is a major draw. The resort was designed by the celebrated Filipino architect Francisco "Bobby" Mañosa, known for championing native materials and forms in modern buildings. Its look draws on the traditional houses of the Samal (Sama) people, raised on stilts over the water, and the hilltop homes of the Mandaya, an indigenous group of eastern Mindanao.

Rooms and accommodation types

Room categories and names can change, so always confirm on the resort's official booking channels. Generally, you can expect a mix of:

  • Stilt houses over the water, inspired by the Samal fishing villages, with direct sea views and access.
  • Hilltop rooms inspired by Mandaya houses, with views over the gulf and gardens.
  • Villas on Malipano Island, a smaller island next to the main resort, offering more space and privacy for larger groups or those wanting extra seclusion.

Interiors feature wood, woven materials and local textiles, so the rooms feel distinctly Mindanaoan rather than generic.

What to do at Pearl Farm

Pearl Farm is mainly a place to slow down. Typical activities include:

  • Swimming in the calm, clear water and lounging on the white-sand beach.
  • Snorkelling around the reefs and in the resort's waters.
  • Kayaking and other non-motorised water sports.
  • Relaxing by the pool.
  • Dining on seafood and Filipino dishes with a view of the gulf.
  • Watching the sunset over Davao Gulf with Mount Apo on the horizon on clear days.

Activities and facilities can change, and some carry extra charges. Ask the resort what is included in your package.

How to get to Pearl Farm Beach Resort

Most guests do not take public ferries to Pearl Farm. The usual route is the resort's own boat service from its marina in Davao City.

  1. Fly into Davao. Francisco Bangoy International Airport has direct flights from Manila, Cebu and other cities.
  2. Head to the resort's departure point. Pearl Farm will confirm the location of its Davao City marina and the boat schedule when you book. Allow extra time for city traffic.
  3. Take the resort boat. The crossing to the western coast of Samal usually takes under an hour, depending on sea conditions.

Boat transfers usually run on fixed schedules and may be included in some packages or charged separately, so confirm both when you reserve. If you are travelling independently around Samal, read our guide on how to reach Samal Island from Davao City, and see the Samal Island bridge update for news about the planned road link.

Tip: If you have an evening flight, ask about the last boat back to the city before you book your return. Missing it can mean an expensive extra night.

Who is Pearl Farm for?

Pearl Farm suits travellers who want comfort, privacy and scenery more than nightlife or budget travel.

  • Great for: honeymooners, anniversaries, families wanting a safe and calm beach, corporate retreats, and anyone wanting a short luxury break from Davao City.
  • Less ideal for: backpackers, party-seekers, and travellers on a tight budget. Samal has many more affordable resorts, which we list in our guide to resorts in Davao and Samal.

Pearl Farm reviews: what guests typically say

Reviews of Pearl Farm Beach Resort on travel sites tend to praise the setting, architecture, calm sea and friendly staff. Common points of criticism in reviews of any island resort, including this one, are the cost of food and extras, the need to plan around boat schedules, and older fixtures in some rooms. Read recent reviews from several sources before booking, since renovations and management changes can make older reviews outdated.

Tips before you book

  • Book early for holidays. Christmas, New Year, Holy Week and Davao's Kadayawan Festival in August are busy periods.
  • Check what your package includes. Meals, boat transfers and activities vary.
  • Pack reef-safe sunscreen and water shoes. Some areas have coral and rocky patches.
  • Plan your budget. Use our trip budget calculator to estimate the total cost of your stay.
  • Check the weather. Davao is south of the main typhoon belt, but rain can still affect sea conditions. See our weather page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Pearl Farm Davao located?

Pearl Farm Beach Resort is on the western coast of Samal Island, across Davao Gulf from Davao City. It is reached by the resort's boat from Davao City.

Was Pearl Farm really a pearl farm?

Yes. The site was once a working pearl farm before it was developed into a beach resort, which is where the name comes from.

Can I visit Pearl Farm on a day trip?

Day-use arrangements have been offered at times, but availability and rates change. Contact the resort directly to ask what is currently possible.

How long is the boat ride to Pearl Farm?

The resort boat from Davao City usually takes under an hour, depending on sea conditions and the departure point. Confirm the schedule when booking.

Is Pearl Farm good for families?

Yes. The calm water, beach and pool make it popular with families, though it is a premium-priced resort.
