Discover What’s New: Exploring the Latest Apartments in Webster, TX

Tucked between Houston’s hustle and Galveston’s laid-back vibe, Webster, TX, is more than a waypoint on the Gulf Freeway. It’s a growing hub of opportunity, convenience, and comfort. With major employers like NASA and the Texas Medical Center nearby, and retail meccas like Baybrook Mall in your backyard, it’s no wonder apartment living in Webster is heating up.

In fact, the demand for rentals in the Clear Lake area—including Webster—has increased steadily over the last few years, driven by its central location and access to top-tier amenities. So if you’re on the hunt for a fresh place to live in Webster, you’re in luck. The apartment scene here is evolving fast, and we’re here to help you explore the latest and greatest options.

Let’s dive into the top apartment communities redefining life in Webster, TX.

1. Barringer Square

623 Barringer Ln, Webster, TX 77598

Barringer Square keeps things simple—but stylish. This community strikes the perfect balance between affordability and comfort. Units come with washer and dryer connections, spacious closets, and private patios or balconies. But here’s what makes it stand out: the peaceful, park-like setting. Mature trees, wide green lawns, and a relaxed vibe make it feel more like a quiet suburban neighborhood than a rental complex.

Quick Highlights:

  • Private patios/balconies

  • On-site laundry center

  • Walkability to local shops

If you value serenity and simplicity, this is a strong contender.

2. Broadstone Baybrook

19101 Town Ridge Ln, Webster, TX 77598

Modern. Sleek. Polished. Broadstone Baybrook is luxury living with a bold touch. The interiors feature quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and spa-inspired bathrooms. You’ll also find smart home technology integrated throughout the units—a huge plus for tech-savvy renters.

The resort-style pool, 24-hour fitness center, and indoor club lounge make it feel like you’re living in a boutique hotel. It’s ideal for those who want style without sacrificing substance.

Notable Features:

  • Smart home integration

  • Covered parking and garages

  • Pet-friendly with a dog spa

3. Lenox Bayside

250 N Village Dr, Webster, TX 77598

Lenox Bayside is one of the newest additions to Webster’s apartment lineup—and it shows. Everything here feels clean, contemporary, and thoughtfully designed. The open-concept layouts, wood-style flooring, and expansive kitchen islands are perfect for hosting or relaxing solo.

Even better? You’re just minutes from both NASA Parkway and Baybrook Mall. It’s location meets luxury, with a splash of practicality.

Why You’ll Love It:

  • Yoga and spin studio

  • Resort-style pool with cabanas

  • Co-working lounge with private offices

A top choice for remote workers or young professionals.

4. The Brook on Bay Area

2702 W Bay Area Blvd, Webster, TX 77598

Looking for a more budget-conscious option without sacrificing lifestyle? The Brook on Bay Area delivers. It’s recently renovated, featuring upgraded interiors with black appliances, faux wood flooring, and updated cabinetry.

The location is one of its biggest assets—nestled right on Bay Area Blvd with easy access to major highways and a short drive to Clear Lake.

Key Amenities:

  • Two sparkling pools

  • Playground and picnic areas

  • Covered parking

Perfect for families or commuters looking for value and location.

5. Watersedge

1799 FM 528 Rd, Webster, TX 77598

Watersedge offers an intimate community feel with standout outdoor spaces. Picture this: lounging by a tree-lined pool or hosting friends in a scenic grilling area. The units feature plank flooring, brushed nickel fixtures, and walk-in closets.

And here’s a unique perk—there’s a nature-inspired walking trail wrapping around the community. Not every apartment offers that kind of everyday escape.

Unique Features:

  • Outdoor walking trails

  • Pet stations and green space

  • Energy-efficient appliances

It’s the right blend of comfort and nature.

6. McAlister

450 El Dorado Blvd, Webster, TX 77598

McAlister keeps it clean, crisp, and highly livable. Think modern finishes, bright open spaces, and contemporary architecture that stands out from older properties in the area. The community includes a fitness center, pool, and resident clubhouse.

This place is great for people who like new construction without an ultra-premium price tag.

Resident Perks:

  • Granite countertops

  • Outdoor kitchens with grilling stations

  • Detached garages available

And yes, it’s pet-friendly too!

7. The Landing at Clear Lake

240 El Dorado Blvd, Webster, TX 77598

Want it all without blowing your budget? The Landing at Clear Lake is spacious, pet-friendly, and well-situated. The apartments offer full-size washers and dryers, fireplaces in select units, and built-in bookshelves—a surprisingly charming touch.

The real gem is the massive pool area and well-maintained green spaces, giving you room to relax without feeling cramped.

What Stands Out:

  • Fireplaces in select units

  • Business center and clubhouse

  • Covered parking

A versatile choice for couples, students, or young families.

Honorable Mentions:

The Townhomes on Three

If you’re looking for more privacy and space, The Townhomes on Three offer two- and three-bedroom layouts with attached garages and multi-level floorplans. It’s like living in a house—but with all the perks of apartment maintenance.

The Haven Apartments

Sleek and modern, The Haven includes stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, and walk-in closets. Their saltwater pool and tech-friendly amenities give it a strong edge for those wanting upscale without going overboard.

Actionable Tips for Renters in Webster

Whether you’re relocating for work, upgrading your current living situation, or simply looking for a fresh start, here are a few quick tips:

✅ Ask About Move-In Specials

Many of these communities offer limited-time discounts or waived fees. Always ask about current incentives.

✅ Tour in Person or Virtually

Photos can only tell part of the story. Schedule a tour—either in person or virtual—to get a true feel for the space and surroundings.

✅ Compare Utility Packages

Some properties bundle utilities or offer flat-rate options for things like water, trash, and pest control. This can affect your total monthly cost.

✅ Check for Smart Home Features

If automation is important to you, Broadstone Baybrook and Lenox Bayside are leading the way with built-in tech like app-controlled thermostats and smart locks.

Final Thoughts

Webster, TX, may be small in size, but it’s big on modern living. From luxurious new builds to well-loved communities with recent upgrades, there’s a wide variety of apartments that cater to every lifestyle—and budget.

Whether you’re working at NASA, enjoying weekends at Clear Lake, or commuting into Houston, these apartments make daily living easier, more stylish, and more convenient.

Ready to make your move? Webster’s waiting.

“Webster is where life happens.” — City of Webster
