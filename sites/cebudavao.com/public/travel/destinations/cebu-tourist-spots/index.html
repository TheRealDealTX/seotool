Cebu Tourist Spots: 14 Places to Visit from City to Islands

Heritage sites, mountain views, waterfalls and white-sand islands. Our list of the top Cebu tourist spots, grouped by area so you can plan your days.

By Cebu-Davao Editorial Team · October 7, 2026 · 5 min read

The top Cebu tourist spots range from 16th-century landmarks in downtown Cebu City to mountain viewpoints in Busay, waterfalls in Badian and white-sand islands up north. The easiest way to plan is by area: spend a day in the city, a day in the hills, and the rest heading south or north. Here are 14 places worth your time.

Travel times are approximate and depend heavily on traffic. Allow extra time for traffic, especially on weekday mornings and evenings.

Downtown Cebu City

1. Magellan's Cross

Housed in a small chapel-like kiosk beside the basilica, Magellan's Cross marks the arrival of Christianity in the Philippines in 1521. The original cross is said to be encased inside the wooden one you see today. Look up at the painted ceiling showing the baptism of Cebu's rulers. Our guide explains how to get to Magellan's Cross.

2. Basilica Minore del Santo Niño

Steps from the cross, this basilica houses the image of the Santo Niño, the Child Jesus, believed to be the oldest Catholic relic in the country. It is the spiritual heart of the Sinulog Festival. Dress modestly and be quiet during Mass. Its history goes back to the arrival of the Spanish in 1521.

3. Fort San Pedro

This small triangular fort near the port is one of the oldest Spanish-era fortifications in the country. Walk the walls, explore the small museum and enjoy the garden courtyard. It is a short walk from Plaza Independencia.

Tip: Visit the three downtown spots in one morning. They are within walking distance, and early hours are cooler and less crowded.

Uptown and the Hills

4. Taoist Temple

Set in the Beverly Hills subdivision, this colourful temple is a reminder of Cebu's large Chinese-Filipino community. Climb the steps for city views. Respect worship areas and photo restrictions.

5. Temple of Leah

Built in Busay as a tribute from a husband to his late wife, the Temple of Leah is a Roman-style structure with columns, statues and sweeping views. It is popular at sunset and for photo shoots. An entrance fee applies.

6. Tops Lookout

High up in Busay, Tops offers one of the best panoramic views of Metro Cebu and Mactan, especially after dark when the city lights glow. Bring a jacket; it gets breezy.

7. Sirao Flower Garden

Often nicknamed Cebu's "Little Amsterdam", this hillside garden is known for bright celosia blooms and photo props. It is best combined with the Temple of Leah and Tops on a half-day hill tour.

South Cebu

8. Kawasan Falls

The turquoise falls in Badian are Cebu's most famous swimming spot and the end point of the popular canyoneering route. Expect cool water, bamboo rafts and crowds at peak times. See our Kawasan Falls and canyoneering guide.

9. Moalboal

Moalboal is famous for its sardine run, where huge schools of sardines swirl just off the shore, plus sea turtles and nearby Pescador Island. It is a laid-back base for snorkelers and divers. Read our Moalboal guide.

10. Oslob

Oslob is best known for whale shark watching, an activity that has drawn debate over feeding practices and animal welfare. Nearby, Tumalog Falls and the town's old church ruins are worth a stop. Our article on Oslob whale sharks covers the ethics so you can decide.

11. Osmeña Peak

In the mountains of Dalaguete, Osmeña Peak is widely considered the highest point in Cebu. A short hike from the jump-off leads to jagged, grassy hills that look surreal at sunrise. Bring warm clothes for an early start.

12. Simala Shrine

The castle-like Monastery of the Holy Eucharist in Sibonga draws pilgrims from all over the country. Strict dress codes apply, so cover your shoulders and knees. Buses heading south from the city can drop you near the access road.

North Cebu

13. Bantayan Island

Bantayan offers powdery white sand, calm water and a slow island pace, especially around Santa Fe. Reach it via a bus to Hagnaya port and a ferry. Our guide explains how to reach Bantayan Island.

14. Malapascua Island

This small island off the northern tip of Cebu is a diving favourite, known for the chance to see thresher sharks at dawn dives. Even non-divers love its beaches and sunsets. Take a bus to Maya port, then a boat across.

Practical Tips for Visiting Cebu Tourist Spots

  • Start early. Traffic in Metro Cebu can be heavy, and southern spots are best in the morning.
  • Carry cash. Many rural attractions charge small entrance or environmental fees and may not accept cards.
  • Respect sacred sites. Dress modestly at churches, temples and Simala.
  • Check the weather. Typhoons and heavy rain can close falls, hikes and boat trips. Check the forecast before you go.
  • Budget ahead. Small fees, boat rentals and transport add up across several days.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most famous tourist spot in Cebu?

Magellan's Cross and the Basilica del Santo Niño are the most iconic in the city. Outside the city, Kawasan Falls, Moalboal and Oslob draw the biggest crowds.

What Cebu tourist spots can I visit in one day?

In one day, you can do the downtown heritage spots in the morning and the Busay hills, including Temple of Leah and Tops Lookout, in the afternoon.

What is the highest point in Cebu?

Osmeña Peak in Dalaguete is widely considered the highest point on Cebu Island. It is a short and fairly easy hike from the jump-off point.

Are there entrance fees at Cebu tourist spots?

Many attractions charge small entrance or environmental fees, while churches are usually free. Fees change, so bring cash and confirm on arrival.
