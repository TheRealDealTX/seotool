Kawasan Falls Canyoneering Guide: Badian Costs, Safety, Tips

Jump, swim and scramble down a turquoise canyon to Kawasan Falls. Here's what Badian canyoneering involves, what it roughly costs, how to stay safe and how to get there.

Kawasan Falls canyoneering is a half-day adventure in southwest Cebu where you jump, swim, slide and walk down a turquoise river canyon, finishing at the famous Kawasan Falls in Badian. Most trips start upriver in the Alegria-Badian area and take roughly 3-5 hours with a licensed guide, helmet and life vest. Expect to get completely wet, and expect it to be one of the highlights of your Cebu trip.

What to expect on the river

After registration and a safety briefing, you'll put on a helmet and life vest and ride a motorbike or van to the jump-off point upriver. From there, the route follows the river through a narrow, jungle-covered canyon with cliffs on both sides and water that ranges from milky turquoise to deep blue.

Along the way you'll:

  • Swim and float through calm pools, often letting the current carry you.
  • Walk and scramble over rocks and along riverbanks.
  • Slide down natural rock chutes into pools.
  • Jump from rock ledges of different heights. Lower jumps are a few metres; the highest are much taller and always optional.
  • Rest and snack at stops along the river.

The trip ends at the multi-level Kawasan Falls, where you can swim in the main pool and have lunch before heading back.

Tip: You can skip any jump. Guides will show you a way to climb down or go around. Never let anyone pressure you into a jump you aren't comfortable with.

Safety first: helmets, life vests and accredited guides

Canyoneering is fun but it is an outdoor adventure sport. Choose operators carefully and follow your guide.

  • Use accredited or registered guides. Local governments and tourism offices have worked to regulate canyoneering guides. Ask whether your operator and guides are registered with the local tourism office.
  • Always wear a helmet and life vest. Even strong swimmers can be knocked by currents or slip on rocks.
  • Listen to the briefing. Guides explain where to jump, how to land, and which areas to avoid.
  • Check the weather. Heavy rain can make the river rise quickly and turn the water brown and fast. Trips may be cancelled after storms. Check our Cebu weather page and the official PAGASA website.
  • Know your limits. If you have heart, back or neck problems, are pregnant, or can't swim, talk to a doctor and your operator first. This article is general information, not medical advice.
  • Wear proper footwear. Water shoes or sandals with straps, not flip-flops.

How to get to Kawasan Falls and Badian

By bus from Cebu City

  1. Go to the Cebu South Bus Terminal in Cebu City.
  2. Take a bus going to Badian or Moalboal via Barili, or one bound for Bato via Barili, and ask to get off at Kawasan Falls in Matutinao, Badian, or at your operator's meeting point.
  3. The trip usually takes around 3-4 hours depending on traffic and stops. Fares are approximate and change; ask the conductor.

By private car or tour van

Driving gives more flexibility, especially for groups. The road goes through southern Cebu's coastal towns. Many tour operators include round-trip transfers from Cebu City or Mactan hotels.

From Moalboal

Many travellers base themselves in Moalboal, which is close to Badian, and combine canyoneering with the famous sardine run. Our Moalboal guide covers the area. A tricycle, habal-habal or hired van can get you to Badian.

Best time to go canyoneering in Badian

The best conditions are usually in the drier months, roughly December to May, when the water is clearer and calmer. Rainy-season trips can still run, but heavy rain can cloud the water and raise the river. Go early in the day to beat the crowds and the afternoon heat. Weekdays are quieter than weekends and holidays. Read more in our best time to visit Cebu and Davao guide.

What to bring

  • Swimwear and quick-dry clothes (rash guards help with sun and scrapes)
  • Water shoes or strapped sandals
  • Waterproof phone pouch or action camera
  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • Change of clothes and a towel
  • Cash for fees, food and tips
  • Drinking water and light snacks

Leave valuables in your hotel, as everything will get wet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need to know how to swim for Kawasan Falls canyoneering?

It helps a lot, but many non-swimmers do the trip because life vests are required and guides assist in deep sections. Tell your guide before you start.

How long is the Kawasan canyoneering trip?

The river section usually takes about 3-5 hours, depending on group size and pace. Add travel time from Cebu City or Moalboal.

Are the cliff jumps mandatory?

No. All high jumps should be optional, and guides can show you an alternative route down. Never jump without a guide's instructions.

When is the best time to go canyoneering in Badian?

The drier months, roughly December to May, usually give the clearest water. Avoid going right after heavy rain, when the river can rise and become dangerous.
