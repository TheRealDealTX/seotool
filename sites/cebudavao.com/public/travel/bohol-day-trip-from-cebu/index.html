Bohol Day Trip From Cebu: Chocolate Hills, Tarsiers and Loboc

Yes, you can see Bohol in a day from Cebu. Here's how: fast craft to Tagbilaran, Chocolate Hills, tarsiers, a Loboc River lunch cruise, and when to stay two days.

By Cebu-Davao Editorial Team · October 7, 2026 · 5 min read

A Bohol day trip from Cebu is very doable: take an early fast craft from Cebu City to Tagbilaran (about two hours), hire a car or join a countryside tour to see the Chocolate Hills, the tarsiers and a Loboc River lunch cruise, then catch an evening boat back. It makes for a long but rewarding day. If you want beach time in Panglao too, a two-day trip is more relaxed.

How to Get From Cebu to Bohol

Fast craft (recommended)

Fast crafts operated by companies such as OceanJet sail between Cebu City's Pier 1 and Tagbilaran City Port several times a day. The trip takes roughly two hours. For a day trip, take one of the first morning departures and book an evening return in advance, especially on weekends and holidays.

Fares vary by class (tourist, business and so on) and season. Expect an approximate range of several hundred to over a thousand pesos one way, plus a terminal fee. Always confirm current fares directly.

Slower ferries

Overnight and conventional ferries also run between Cebu and Bohol, sometimes to other Bohol ports, and can be cheaper. They take longer, so they suit two-day trips better.

Tip: Bring a jacket for the fast craft. The air-conditioning is often very cold, and the sea can be choppy in the afternoon.

Getting Around Bohol

From Tagbilaran port, you have three main options:

  1. Hire a van or car with driver. The most convenient for groups. Drivers know the classic "countryside tour" loop.
  2. Join a tour package. Many agencies in Cebu sell packages including boat tickets, transport and lunch.
  3. Rent a scooter. Cheapest for confident riders, but the round trip to the Chocolate Hills is long.

Agree on the route, duration and price before setting off, and ask what entrance fees are included.

What to See on a Bohol Day Trip

Chocolate Hills (Carmen)

The Chocolate Hills are more than a thousand cone-shaped grassy hills spread over several towns in central Bohol. The main viewing complex is in Carmen, where stairs lead to a viewpoint. The hills turn brown in the dry months, which gives them their name, and stay green in the rainy season.

Tarsier sanctuary

The Philippine tarsier is one of the world's smallest primates, with huge eyes and a body that fits in your palm. Visit a responsible sanctuary, such as the Philippine Tarsier Foundation's sanctuary in Corella, where tarsiers live in a natural forested area.

Rules to follow:

  • No flash photography.
  • Keep your voice low.
  • Never touch or feed the tarsiers.
  • Avoid roadside "tarsier attractions" that keep animals in small cages.

Loboc River lunch cruise

Floating restaurants cruise slowly along the green Loboc River while you eat a buffet lunch, often with live music. It's a relaxing break in a busy day.

Other stops along the way

  • Baclayon Church, one of the oldest stone churches in the Philippines
  • Blood Compact Shrine in Tagbilaran, marking the 1565 blood compact between Datu Sikatuna and Miguel López de Legazpi
  • Bilar man-made forest, a shady stretch of mahogany trees along the road
  • Hanging bridges over the river in the Loboc-Sevilla area

1 Day vs 2 Days: Which Is Better?

PlanProsCons
1-day tripCheaper, no hotel neededLong, rushed, no beach time
2-day tripPanglao beaches, slower pace, sunsetExtra cost for accommodation

With two days, spend day one on the countryside tour and day two in Panglao. Alona Beach is the best-known spot, and island hopping to Balicasag for snorkelling is popular. Bohol-Panglao International Airport also lets you fly out directly if you're continuing elsewhere.

Budget Tips

  • Travel with a group and split the cost of a private van.
  • Book fast craft tickets early for better availability.
  • Bring cash for entrance fees, which are usually collected at each site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you do Bohol as a day trip from Cebu?

Yes. Take an early fast craft to Tagbilaran, do the countryside tour with a hired driver, and catch an evening boat back. It's a long day, so plan carefully.

How long is the boat from Cebu to Bohol?

The fast craft from Cebu City to Tagbilaran takes about two hours. Slower conventional ferries take longer.

Where can I see tarsiers responsibly?

Visit a conservation sanctuary such as the Philippine Tarsier Foundation's site in Corella. Avoid places that keep tarsiers in cages, and never use flash.

How far are the Chocolate Hills from Tagbilaran?

The main viewing complex in Carmen is roughly a 1.5 to 2 hour drive from Tagbilaran, depending on stops and traffic.

Is two days better than one for Bohol?

If you can, yes. Two days lets you add Panglao's beaches and island hopping without rushing the countryside tour.
