Cheap Hotels in Davao: Can You Still Stay Under ₱1,000?

Budget rooms in Davao City still exist, but you need to know where to look. Here's what ₱1,000 a night gets you and the best areas for cheap stays.

By Cebu-Davao Editorial Team · October 7, 2026 · 5 min read

Yes, you can still find cheap hotels in Davao for around ₱1,000 a night or less, but they are mostly pension houses, hostel dorms, budget inns and simple apartelles rather than full-service hotels. Expect a clean, basic room with a fan or air-conditioning, often a private bathroom, and few extras. The best hunting grounds are downtown Poblacion, Bajada and Matina, and the key is booking smart.

Best Areas for Budget Stays in Davao

Poblacion (downtown)

The old downtown has the highest concentration of pension houses and budget inns. You're close to San Pedro Cathedral, People's Park, markets, jeepney routes and cheap eateries. It can be noisier, but it's the most convenient area for travellers on a budget.

Bajada

North of downtown, Bajada is a busy commercial area with hospitals, offices and restaurants, plus a good number of small hotels and inns. It's handy for both downtown and the Lanang side of the city.

Matina

Matina, on the south side, has malls, restaurants and residential neighbourhoods. Budget inns and apartelles here suit visitors who want to be near shopping and away from the busiest downtown streets.

Near the airport and Lanang

Staying near Francisco Bangoy International Airport makes sense for early flights, and Lanang has a mix of hotels. Budget choices are fewer here. See our guide to hotels near Davao airport.

Pension Houses, Hostels and Apartelles Explained

Pension houses are small, family-run guesthouses, common across the Philippines. Rooms are simple, often with a choice of fan or air-con. Hostels offer dorm beds and some private rooms, and are great for meeting other travellers. Apartelles are apartment-style rooms rented by the night, week or month, popular with workers and long-stay visitors. If you plan to stay longer, read our guide to renting in Davao.

What to Check Before You Book

A cheap room is only a bargain if it's clean, safe and accurately described. Check these:

  • Recent reviews and photos from guests, not only the listing photos.
  • Air-con or fan? Many "from" prices are for fan rooms.
  • Private or shared bathroom, and whether there's hot water.
  • Wi-Fi quality, if you need to work.
  • Windows. Some budget rooms are windowless; fine for sleeping, not for everyone.
  • Location on a map, relative to where you'll spend your time.
  • Cancellation and payment terms.

How to Book Cheap Hotels in Davao Safely

  • Book through established booking platforms or directly with the property using contact details listed on its official page or the platform.
  • Be wary of social-media "deals" that ask for full payment by e-wallet to a personal account before you've confirmed the property exists.
  • Get written confirmation of your rate, dates and room type.
  • Ask about extra charges, such as deposits, extra-person fees or early check-in.
  • Walk-in rates at pension houses can be competitive on quiet weekdays, but don't count on availability during festivals.

Davao City Rules Budget Travellers Should Know

Davao is known for strictly enforcing local rules, including a city-wide public smoking ban and restrictions on firecrackers. Hotels and inns also enforce these. Read our article on Davao safety and city rules before you arrive.

When to splurge a little

If you're arriving late at night, travelling with children or working remotely, paying a little more for a budget hotel with a 24-hour front desk, reliable Wi-Fi and a backup generator can be worth it. Brownouts are less common than in the past but still happen in parts of Mindanao, and a generator keeps the air-con and lights running.

Getting around from a budget base

Downtown Davao is easy to explore by jeepney, taxi and ride-hailing apps, and taxis in the city are generally metered. Staying central means shorter, cheaper rides to People's Park, the markets and the Roxas Avenue night market. For airport transfers, see our Davao airport taxi guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there hotels in Davao under ₱1,000 a night?

Yes. Pension houses, budget inns and hostel dorms often fall under ₱1,000 a night, especially on weekdays. Prices are approximate and change, so confirm when booking.

What is the cheapest area to stay in Davao City?

Downtown Poblacion has the most pension houses and budget inns, and Bajada and Matina also have affordable options.

Is it safe to stay in budget hotels in Davao?

Davao has a reputation as one of the safer Philippine cities, but always read recent reviews, check the location and book through reliable channels.

Are apartelles in Davao cheaper for long stays?

Usually, yes. Many apartelles offer weekly or monthly rates that work out cheaper per night than daily bookings.
