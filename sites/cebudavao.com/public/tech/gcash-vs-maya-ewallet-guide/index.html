GCash vs Maya: Which E-Wallet Is Better in the Philippines?

GCash or Maya? We compare features, fees, savings, transfers and QR payments, plus essential tips to protect your account from scams.

In the GCash vs Maya comparison, GCash wins on reach: it is the most widely used e-wallet in the Philippines and is accepted almost everywhere, from sari-sari stores to bills payment. Maya stands out for its digital bank features, especially its savings account and credit options. Many Filipinos simply use both, and since both support QR Ph and InstaPay, moving money between them is easy.

Here is how they compare, what to watch for in fees, and how to keep your account safe.

What is GCash?

GCash is the mobile wallet operated by G-Xchange, part of the Globe Group's fintech business. It started as a mobile money service and has grown into an everyday app for paying bills, buying load, scanning QR codes in stores, sending money and accessing savings, insurance, investments and credit. Its biggest advantage is that so many people and businesses use it; if someone asks "May GCash ka?" (Do you have GCash?), they usually mean they want to send or receive money quickly.

What is Maya?

Maya, formerly PayMaya, is an e-wallet that also operates alongside Maya Bank, a digital bank licensed by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas. That bank side lets Maya offer savings accounts, time deposits and credit products directly. Maya is popular with users who want to keep savings in a digital bank and earn interest, as well as for card and QR payments.

Fees: what to watch for

Fees change and depend on the transaction, so always check the app before confirming. In general:

  • Sending money between users of the same app is often free.
  • Bank transfers via InstaPay usually carry a small fee per transaction, though promos sometimes waive it.
  • Cash-in may be free through some channels and charged through others, such as certain over-the-counter partners.
  • Cash-out to banks or at partner outlets may have fees.
  • Bills payment may include a small convenience fee for some billers.

Tip: Before sending, look at the confirmation screen. Both apps show the fee, if any, before you finalise the transaction.

Savings and interest

Both apps let you save money and earn interest, but they work differently.

  • GCash offers savings products through partner banks inside the app. Your deposit is held by the partner bank.
  • Maya offers savings through Maya Bank, its own digital bank.

Deposits in banks, including digital banks, are generally insured by the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation (PDIC) up to the legal maximum per depositor per bank. Interest rates change and promotional rates may only apply to certain balances or periods, so compare current rates in each app. Note that money sitting in an e-wallet balance is not the same as a bank deposit; read each app's terms to understand the difference.

Sending money and QR Ph

Both apps support:

  • Sending to another user of the same app using a mobile number.
  • Bank transfers through InstaPay (real-time, usually for smaller amounts) and PESONet (batch, often used for larger amounts).
  • QR Ph, the national QR code standard promoted by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas. A QR Ph code from one bank or e-wallet can usually be scanned by another, so a GCash user can often pay a merchant's Maya QR code and vice versa.

This interoperability means you rarely need both apps just to pay someone, though it can still be handy for promos and backups.

Bills, load and everyday use

Both apps let you pay utilities, buy mobile load, book tickets and shop online. Many government agencies and utilities accept e-wallet payments. For example, residents of Davao often pay water bills by app; see our guide on where to pay your Davao City Water District bill. If you are a remote worker in the south, our digital nomad guide to Cebu and Davao explains how locals handle everyday payments.

Safety tips: how to avoid scams

E-wallet scams are common. Scammers pretend to be app staff, couriers, relatives or sellers. Protect yourself with these rules:

  • Never share your OTP or MPIN. No legitimate GCash or Maya employee will ever ask for them.
  • Ignore links in texts and emails claiming your account will be blocked or that you won a prize. Open the official app directly instead.
  • Use only official apps from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
  • Turn on extra security such as biometrics and device linking.
  • Be careful with "wrong send" requests. If someone says they sent money to you by mistake, contact official support rather than sending money back to a new number.
  • Check sellers before paying online, and prefer cash on delivery or protected checkout for unfamiliar shops.
  • Report quickly. If you suspect fraud, contact the app's official support channels and your bank immediately.

Learn to spot online hoaxes in our guide on how to identify fake news in the Philippines, and see the official consumer advice of the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas.

Which should you use?

  • Choose GCash if you mainly want to pay merchants, send money to family and friends, and pay bills with the widest acceptance.
  • Choose Maya if you want an app-based savings account from a digital bank and like its card and credit features.
  • Use both if you want to compare promos, separate spending money from savings, or have a backup when one app is under maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GCash or Maya better?

GCash is better for wide acceptance and sending money, while Maya stands out for digital bank savings and credit. Many people use both.

Can I send money from GCash to Maya?

Yes. You can transfer via InstaPay using the recipient's account details, or pay a Maya QR Ph code using GCash. A small fee may apply.

Are GCash and Maya safe?

Both are regulated by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas and offer security features, but you must protect your account. Never share your OTP or MPIN with anyone.

Is my money in Maya Savings insured?

Deposits in Maya Bank are generally covered by PDIC insurance up to the legal maximum per depositor. Check the app and the PDIC for current details.

What is QR Ph?

QR Ph is the national QR code standard for payments. It lets you pay a merchant's QR code from a different bank or e-wallet.
