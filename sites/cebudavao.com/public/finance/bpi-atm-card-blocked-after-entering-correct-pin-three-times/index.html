How to Activate BPI ATM Card and Fix a Blocked or Captured Card

New BPI debit card, or one that got blocked or swallowed by the ATM? Here's how activation generally works, why cards get blocked, and the safe way to get help from BPI.

By Cebu-Davao Editorial Team · October 7, 2026 · 6 min read

If you're wondering how to activate BPI ATM card, the usual first step is to use it at a BPI ATM with the PIN you received and follow the on-screen prompts, which may ask you to change your PIN; some cards may also be activated or managed through BPI's official app or customer service. If your card is blocked or captured, even after entering what you're sure is the correct PIN, the fix is to contact BPI through official channels so they can check your card status and help you unblock or replace it. Procedures change, so always confirm the exact steps with BPI.

Important: This is a general guide, not official BPI instructions. For your specific card and account, contact BPI through the numbers printed on the back of your card or the official BPI website. Never share your PIN, OTP or full card details with anyone.

How to activate BPI ATM card (general steps)

Banks update their onboarding, so the exact flow can differ. In general, new BPI debit or ATM cards are activated like this:

  1. Receive your card and PIN from the branch or by the delivery method BPI uses. Check that the envelope looks untampered.
  2. Go to a BPI ATM. Using BPI's own machines is usually recommended for the first transaction.
  3. Insert the card and enter the PIN you were given.
  4. Follow any prompts to change your PIN. Choose a PIN that isn't your birthday, 1234, or repeated digits.
  5. Do a simple first transaction, such as a balance inquiry, to confirm the card works.
  6. Enrol in digital banking. Register in the official BPI app so you can monitor your account and manage your card.

If activation doesn't work, don't keep retrying. Several failed attempts can lock the card. Contact BPI instead.

How to activate ATM card BPI through the app

Many banks now let customers manage cards in their mobile app, for example locking or unlocking a card or setting limits. If you have a BPI online account, check the cards section of the official app for activation or management options. Download the app only from the official App Store or Google Play listing.

Why your BPI card was blocked after entering the correct PIN

It's frustrating to be sure you typed the right PIN and still see your card blocked or swallowed. Possible reasons include:

  • Earlier wrong attempts. PIN attempt counters can add up across different days or machines, so a correct entry may come after the limit was already reached.
  • The PIN was never changed. Some new cards require a PIN change on first use.
  • Damaged chip or magnetic strip. The machine may misread the card even with the correct PIN.
  • Expired card. Check the expiry date on the front.
  • Security holds. Banks may block cards when they detect unusual activity, to protect you.
  • Machine or network error. ATMs sometimes keep cards during a glitch or if you don't take the card back in time.
  • Account issues. A dormant, closed or restricted account can stop the card working.

What to do if your BPI card is captured or blocked

  1. Stay at the machine briefly and note the ATM's location, the date and time, and any error message on screen.
  2. Don't accept help from strangers. A person "helping" at an ATM can be part of a scam. Never tell anyone your PIN.
  3. Contact BPI right away using the hotline printed on the back of your card or listed on the official BPI website. Ask them to block the card if you suspect fraud.
  4. Visit your branch with valid government ID if instructed. Captured cards are usually not handed back over the counter at the ATM site; BPI may advise a card replacement instead.
  5. Request a replacement card if needed. Replacement fees and timelines vary, so ask BPI.
  6. Check your account in the app for any transactions you don't recognise.

Where to find the account number on a BPI ATM card

A common confusion: your card number and your account number are not the same thing. The long number embossed or printed on the front of the card is the card number. Your bank account number is a separate number.

If someone asks for your "BPI Express Teller card account number" to send you money, give your account number, not the card number, and never share your PIN or OTP.

What is the BPI bank code?

For international transfers, banks use a SWIFT/BIC code. BPI's SWIFT code is commonly listed as BOPIPHMM, but always confirm it with BPI or the sender's bank before an international transfer. For local transfers through InstaPay or PESONet, you usually just select BPI from the bank list in your app.

Keeping your BPI card safe

  • Cover the keypad when entering your PIN.
  • Use ATMs inside bank branches or malls where possible.
  • Check for loose or bulky card slots, which could be skimming devices.
  • Turn on transaction alerts in the app.
  • Be careful with messages or calls claiming to be from BPI; banks don't ask for your PIN or OTP.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I activate my new BPI ATM card?

Generally, you use it at a BPI ATM with the PIN provided and follow any prompts, such as changing your PIN. If that doesn't work, contact BPI customer service or your branch.

Why did my BPI card get blocked even with the correct PIN?

Possible reasons include earlier wrong attempts, a damaged card, an expired card, a security hold, or a machine error. Contact BPI so they can check the exact cause.

Can I get my captured card back from the ATM?

In many cases banks replace captured cards rather than returning them. Contact BPI immediately and follow their instructions.

Is my account number printed on my BPI ATM card?

The number on the front is usually your card number, not your account number. Find your account number in the BPI app, on your passbook or statement, or by asking your branch.
