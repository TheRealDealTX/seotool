Call Centers in Cebu: Top BPO Employers, Pay and How to Apply

Cebu is one of the country's biggest BPO hubs. Here are the major employers, where they are, what they pay and how to get hired.

Call centers in Cebu are among the biggest employers in the province, with global BPO (business process outsourcing) companies running large sites in Cebu IT Park, Cebu Business Park, Mandaue and Mactan. Entry-level agents can often start with a high school or senior high diploma and good English, and experienced staff can move into team lead, quality, training or specialised roles.

This guide covers the major employers, where they operate, what they look for, approximate pay and how to land the job.

Why Cebu Is a BPO Hub

Cebu has a large, young, English-speaking workforce, many universities and good air links through Mactan-Cebu International Airport. Dedicated office zones such as Cebu IT Park in Lahug and Apas grew around the industry, and over the years Cebu has become one of the leading BPO locations in the Philippines outside Metro Manila.

Work today goes beyond voice calls. Many sites handle chat and email support, content moderation, technical support, healthcare and insurance processes, finance and accounting, and IT services.

Older company names you may still see

If you search for call centers in Cebu, you will run into names from older job listings and directories. Some of these companies have since merged, rebranded, changed focus or left Cebu. For example, Convergys became part of Concentrix. Treat old lists with caution and verify any company on its official website before applying.

Typical Requirements

Requirements vary by company and account, but most entry-level customer service roles look for:

  • Senior high school graduate, college undergraduate or college graduate (some accounts accept SHS graduates)
  • Good spoken and written English
  • Basic computer and typing skills
  • Willingness to work night shifts, weekends and holidays
  • Ability to handle pressure and stay polite with difficult customers

Documents to prepare

Once you get a job offer, you will usually be asked for government numbers and clearances. Getting these early speeds up onboarding.

  1. Valid government ID (the PhilSys National ID is widely accepted)
  2. SSS number
  3. PhilHealth number
  4. Pag-IBIG (HDMF) number
  5. TIN (Tax Identification Number)
  6. NBI clearance
  7. Birth certificate (PSA copy)
  8. Diploma or transcript, as applicable

Approximate Salary Ranges

Salaries depend on the company, account, skills and experience. The ranges below are approximate monthly basic pay figures and can change; confirm details during your interview.

RoleApproximate monthly basic pay
Entry-level agent (non-voice or voice)Around PHP 16,000-25,000
Experienced agent / specialised accountAround PHP 22,000-35,000
Team leader / supervisorAround PHP 30,000-50,000
Quality analyst / trainerAround PHP 28,000-45,000

On top of basic pay, many companies offer night differential pay, performance incentives, HMO health coverage and allowances. Ask for a breakdown so you can compare offers properly.

Tip: Compare the full package, not just basic pay. A slightly lower salary with HMO for dependents, a shuttle service or a site close to home can be worth more in practice.

How to Get Hired at Call Centers in Cebu

  1. Apply on official channels. Use company career sites and verified job portals. Be careful of anyone asking for payment to "guarantee" a job; legitimate employers do not charge applicants.
  2. Polish your English. Practise speaking clearly at a steady pace. Read aloud, record yourself and listen back.
  3. Prepare for the usual steps. Expect an initial interview, an English or communication assessment, a typing test, sometimes a computer-based test and a final interview with operations.
  4. Prepare your story. Have clear answers for "Tell me about yourself", "Why do you want this job?" and "How would you handle an angry customer?"
  5. Dress neatly for on-site interviews, even if the job is casual.
  6. Be patient and persistent. If you are not hired, ask for feedback and try again later or apply to other accounts.

Life as a Call Center Agent

Night shifts and long hours are common, so look after your health: sleep well, eat proper meals and stay hydrated. Many agents enjoy the steady income, training and career growth, and the industry has helped many Cebuanos support their families.

For general labour information and worker rights, visit the Department of Labor and Employment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a college degree to work in a Cebu call center?

Not always. Many accounts accept senior high school graduates or college undergraduates with good English. Specialised roles such as healthcare or technical support may require a degree or relevant experience.

How much do call center agents earn in Cebu?

Entry-level agents typically earn roughly PHP 16,000-25,000 a month in basic pay, plus night differential and incentives. Figures are approximate and vary by company and account.

Where are most call centers in Cebu located?

The largest cluster is in Cebu IT Park in Lahug/Apas, followed by Cebu Business Park, Mandaue and Mactan in Lapu-Lapu City.

Are there non-voice BPO jobs in Cebu?

Yes. Many companies hire for chat and email support, back-office processing, content moderation and other non-voice roles.
