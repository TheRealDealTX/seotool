SunStar Davao and Other Davao News Sources: A Reader's Guide

A practical guide to SunStar Davao and the other news outlets that cover Davao and Mindanao, plus SunStar's sister editions in Cebu and Cagayan de Oro.

SunStar Davao is the Davao edition of the SunStar network, a group of regional news outlets in the Philippines that started in Cebu. It covers city hall, business, crime, weather, sports and community stories in Davao City and the wider region, and its articles are published online at sunstar.com.ph. This guide explains how SunStar is organised and lists other reliable Davao news sources so you can follow local news from more than one angle.

What is SunStar?

SunStar is one of the best-known regional media groups in the Philippines. Its flagship is SunStar Cebu, a daily newspaper with a long history in Cebu City, and the group expanded into other cities with local editions. Today most readers follow SunStar through its website and social media, where stories from all editions appear under one roof.

How to read SunStar Davao online

  • Website: go to sunstar.com.ph and choose the Davao section.
  • Social media: SunStar's local editions keep official pages where they share breaking news and live updates. Look for verified or long-established pages to avoid copycats.
  • Search: add "SunStar Davao" to your search terms when looking for a specific local story.

Tip: Copycat pages sometimes use the names of real newspapers. Before sharing a story, check that it comes from the outlet's official website or verified page. Our guide on how to spot fake news in the Philippines explains what to look for.

Other Davao news sources

Following more than one outlet gives you a fuller picture, especially on politics and big issues. These are well-established sources for Davao and Mindanao news.

Mindanao Times

Mindanao Times is a long-running Davao City newspaper that covers local government, business, community events and regional issues. It is a useful source for city-level news.

Edge Davao

Edge Davao is a Davao-based newspaper that covers local news, business and lifestyle, with an online presence for readers outside the city.

MindaNews

MindaNews is a Davao-based news organisation focused on Mindanao as a whole. It is known for in-depth reporting on peace and development, indigenous peoples, the environment and the Bangsamoro region, making it valuable for context beyond Davao City.

GMA Regional TV One Mindanao

GMA Network's regional operation for Mindanao produces local newscasts in Bisaya from Davao, covering news across the island. Its reports are also shared online.

National outlets and official sources

  • Philippine News Agency (PNA): the government's news agency, often carrying official announcements from Davao offices.
  • National newspapers and TV networks: they cover major Davao stories, especially those with national impact.
  • Davao City government channels: for official advisories, schedules and public announcements.
  • PAGASA: for weather and typhoon information. Visit pagasa.dost.gov.ph or see our Davao weather page.

SunStar Cebu: Cebu news from the same group

If you also follow Cebu, SunStar Cebu is one of the main sources for Cebu news, alongside other Cebu newspapers, radio stations and TV newsrooms. Cebu and Davao are often compared, and reading both editions shows how the two cities tackle similar issues such as traffic, water, housing and tourism. For a side-by-side look, read our guide to Cebu vs Davao.

SunStar Cagayan de Oro and Zamboanga

Readers in Northern and Western Mindanao can follow SunStar's Cagayan de Oro and Zamboanga editions for local news. If you are travelling around Mindanao, these editions are handy for checking road conditions, weather disruptions and local events. Our Camiguin island guide is a good companion if you are heading north via Cagayan de Oro.

Tips for following local news responsibly

  • Check the date. Old stories are often reshared as if they were new.
  • Read beyond the headline. Headlines are short; the details matter.
  • Compare outlets. If only one unknown page carries a dramatic claim, wait for confirmation.
  • Be careful with screenshots. They can be edited easily. Look for the original link.
  • Follow official advisories for emergencies. In storms or earthquakes, rely on PAGASA, PHIVOLCS and local government announcements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SunStar Davao still available?

SunStar Davao stories are published on SunStar's official website, sunstar.com.ph, and through its official social media pages. Check the site for the current format of the edition.

Is SunStar a Cebu newspaper?

SunStar began in Cebu, where SunStar Cebu is its flagship edition, and it later expanded to other cities including Davao, Cagayan de Oro and Zamboanga.

What are the best news sources for Davao City?

Good sources include SunStar Davao, Mindanao Times, Edge Davao, MindaNews and GMA Regional TV One Mindanao. Official city government channels and PAGASA are best for advisories.

Where can I read Cebu news in Cebuano?

SunStar Superbalita Cebu publishes popular news in Cebuano. Several Cebu radio and TV newsrooms also report in the local language.

How can I tell if a Davao news page is fake?

Check whether it links to an established outlet's official website, look for real reporter names and dates, and compare the story with other trusted sources.
