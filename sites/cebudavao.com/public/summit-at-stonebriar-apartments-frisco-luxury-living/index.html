Summit at Stonebriar Apartments: Frisco Luxury Living

Summit at Stonebriar Apartments in Frisco, TX offers the ideal blend of modern amenities, comfort, and luxury living. Designed to cater to all residents, this apartment community promises a unique lifestyle. With one and two-bedroom floor plans, Summit at Stonebriar caters to everyone from young professionals to growing families.

Located at 3033 Ohio Drive, the community is conveniently situated for easy access to both local businesses and Downtown Dallas. It’s the perfect retreat after a long day, where every aspect of luxury is reflected in the details of your new home.

Frisco Luxury Homes that Welcome You

Living at Summit at Stonebriar means stepping into a Frisco luxury apartment that feels like home from day one. Each apartment features modern designs with high ceilings and spacious layouts, giving every unit a luxurious and open feel. The sleek design of the modern kitchens includes granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and hardwood floors that make meal prep a pleasure.

Whether you’re enjoying the breeze from your private balcony or the modern lighting throughout the home, the community ensures comfort in every corner. Select homes even offer glass-enclosed showers and fenced yards for added convenience. Summit at Stonebriar is more than just an apartment—it’s a Frisco luxury home that welcomes you to relax, unwind, and live your best life.

A Community Designed for Every Resident

Summit at Stonebriar Apartments provides all the amenities you need to enjoy the best in modern living. The spectacular clubhouse is the heart of the community, where residents can connect with friends and neighbors or participate in events hosted by the property manager. The resort-style pool is perfect for those hot Texas summers, offering a place to cool off and relax. Staying fit is easy with the on-site fitness center, designed to help you maintain your health without leaving the community.

This luxury community also caters to your furry friends, with a pet-friendly neighborhood and fenced yards available in select homes. The property offers a true sense of community, where residents feel connected and supported. The neighborhood itself is a perfect balance between the calm of suburban life and the convenience of city living, giving residents the best of both worlds.

Why Choose Summit at Stonebriar?

One of the top reasons to choose Summit at Stonebriar is the wide range of modern features each home offers. From high ceilings to private balconies, these homes are designed to maximize comfort and style. The open floorplans provide ample space for entertaining or simply enjoying quiet evenings at home. Stainless appliances, granite countertops, and modern ceiling fans are just a few examples of the high-end finishes that set these apartments apart.

Residents love the convenience of living in such a central location, with easy access to major highways, Downtown Dallas, and nearby businesses. Collin County Community College (CC) is also close, making it an ideal location for students and professionals alike. Whether you’re looking for a home near Dallas Love Field or want to be part of a growing neighborhood, Summit at Stonebriar offers it all.

Amenities that Make a Difference

Summit at Stonebriar goes beyond the basics to provide all the amenities you need for a comfortable life. The resort-style pool offers the perfect way to relax after a long day, while the fitness center helps you stay fit without needing a gym membership. Residents also enjoy the luxury of hardwood floors, modern kitchens, and select homes with glass showers and fenced yards.

Private balconies offer a peaceful retreat where you can unwind and take in the views. Meanwhile, modern features like stainless appliances and granite countertops elevate everyday living. With a friendly and responsive property manager, you can rest easy knowing that help is always just a phone call away.

Why Live in Frisco?

Frisco, TX, is known for its rapidly growing community and convenient location near major businesses and entertainment hubs. Summit at Stonebriar Apartments gives residents quick access to shopping, dining, and entertainment in the nearby Stonebriar area. With Downtown Dallas just a short drive away, residents have the best of both worlds—suburban living with city amenities just a step forward.

Frisco is home to top-rated schools, making it an ideal choice for families. The area also offers a vibrant social scene, with plenty of opportunities to connect with friends, neighbors, and the local community. With such a dynamic and friendly atmosphere, it’s easy to see why Frisco is one of the most desirable places to live in Texas.

Pros of Living at Summit at Stonebriar

There are many reasons to love life at Summit at Stonebriar. The luxury amenities, such as the resort-style pool and modern kitchens, provide a level of comfort and convenience unmatched by other apartment communities. Private balconies and hardwood floors add a touch of elegance, while open floorplans make every home feel spacious and inviting.

Frisco’s convenient location ensures that residents have easy access to everything they need, whether they’re commuting to Dallas or exploring the local community. Summit at Stonebriar offers more than just a place to live—it’s a lifestyle that offers endless possibilities.

With all these features and more, Summit at Stonebriar Apartments is the perfect place to start your next chapter. Whether you’re looking for a luxury apartment community with all the amenities or a unique lifestyle in one of Texas’s fastest-growing areas, Summit at Stonebriar is ready to welcome you home today.

Availability and Fees

Summit at Stonebriar offers a range of floor plans, including one and two-bedroom options. Availability is subject to change, so be sure to reserve your next home before your preferred unit becomes unavailable. Rent prices vary based on the size and features of each home, and fees such as deposits and pet fees may apply.

Residents can earn points and take advantage of various community perks throughout their lease term. The apartment community regularly updates its offerings, so contact the property manager for the most current availability, pricing, and fees.

Conclusion

Summit at Stonebriar Apartments in Frisco, TX, is a luxury community offering all the amenities you need for modern living. With spacious open floorplans, private balconies, and a host of other features, it’s easy to see why so many residents call this Frisco luxury home. Whether you’re enjoying the resort-style pool or taking in the local area, Summit at Stonebriar provides the perfect retreat for your next chapter.
