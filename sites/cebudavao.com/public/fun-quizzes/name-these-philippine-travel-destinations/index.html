Destination Notes: The Places Behind the Questions

Whether you aced it or guessed your way through, here's a short tourism refresher on the destinations Filipino travel quizzes love to feature.

Chocolate Hills, Bohol

More than a thousand rounded hills spread across the interior of Bohol. In the dry season their grass turns brown, which is how they got their name. Bohol is an easy trip from Cebu by fast ferry; see our Bohol day trip guide. The island is also home to the tiny Philippine tarsier.

Kawasan Falls and Moalboal, Cebu

In southwest Cebu, Kawasan Falls in Badian is famous for its turquoise pools and canyoneering. Nearby Moalboal is known for the sardine run, huge schools of sardines just off the shore. Read our guides to Kawasan Falls and Moalboal.

Mount Apo, Davao

Mount Apo is the highest mountain in the Philippines, at about 2,954 metres, rising between Davao del Sur and Cotabato. It's a serious multi-day climb that needs permits and guides. Our Mount Apo guide explains how.

Boracay, Aklan

Boracay's White Beach is one of the country's most famous stretches of sand, known for powdery white sand and sunsets. It reopened to tourists in 2018 after a major rehabilitation.

El Nido and Coron, Palawan

Palawan's limestone cliffs and lagoons make it a quiz favourite. El Nido is known for island-hopping tours through lagoons and beaches, while Coron is famous for its lakes and World War II shipwreck dives. See our Coron travel guide.

Banaue Rice Terraces, Ifugao

Carved into the mountains of the Cordillera by the Ifugao people over centuries, the rice terraces are often called a wonder of engineering. The Rice Terraces of the Philippine Cordilleras are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Vigan, Ilocos Sur

Vigan's cobblestone Calle Crisologo and Spanish-colonial houses make it one of the best-preserved historic towns in Asia, and it is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Ride a kalesa (horse-drawn carriage) through the old town.

Intramuros, Manila

The "walled city" is Manila's Spanish-era heart, home to Fort Santiago and San Agustin Church. It's easily explored on foot or by bike.

Siargao, Surigao del Norte

Known as a surfing destination, Siargao is famous for the Cloud 9 surf break, island-hopping and a laid-back island vibe. See our Siargao travel guide.

Camiguin and Siquijor

Camiguin, off northern Mindanao, is known for volcanoes, hot and cold springs and a sunken cemetery. Siquijor, south of Cebu, is known for waterfalls, beaches and folk-healing traditions. Read our Camiguin guide.

More Tourism Quiz Questions and Answers

Want a few bonus questions? Try these on your friends:

  1. What is the highest mountain in the Philippines? Mount Apo.
  2. Which island is home to the Chocolate Hills? Bohol.
  3. Where did Magellan's expedition plant a cross in 1521? Cebu.
  4. Which festival in Cebu honours the Santo Niño every January? Sinulog.
  5. Which Davao festival in August celebrates thanksgiving for the harvest? Kadayawan.
  6. What town in Ilocos Sur is famous for Calle Crisologo? Vigan.

Tips for Playing and Learning

Quizzes are a fun way to plan your next trip. A few ideas for getting more out of this one:

  • Play with family or friends and keep score; the loser plans the next weekend trip.
  • Look up the destinations you missed using the links above, and add them to your bucket list.
  • Teachers can use it as a warm-up for geography or Araling Panlipunan (social studies) lessons.
  • Check the distance from your home city with our distance calculator before you dream too big.

Many of these destinations are only a short flight or ferry from Cebu or Davao, which makes either city a great base for exploring the country.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Philippines travel quiz work?

Each question shows a clue about a Philippine destination with multiple-choice answers. Pick one, and your score is shown at the end. You can replay as often as you like.

Is the quiz suitable for students?

Yes. It's a light, family-friendly way to learn Philippine geography and tourism, and works well as a classroom warm-up.

What are the most famous tourist destinations in the Philippines?

Commonly cited favourites include Boracay, Palawan (El Nido and Coron), Bohol's Chocolate Hills, Siargao, Cebu's Kawasan Falls and Moalboal, and the Banaue Rice Terraces.
