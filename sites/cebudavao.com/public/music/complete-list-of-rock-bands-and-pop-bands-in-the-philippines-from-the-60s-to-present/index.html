List of Filipino Bands: OPM Rock and Pop From the 70s to Now

From Pinoy rock pioneers and Manila Sound to the 90s band boom, 2000s alt-rock and today's indie-folk and P-pop: the OPM bands every Filipino music fan should know.

This list of Filipino bands walks through OPM (Original Pilipino Music) decade by decade: the Pinoy rock pioneers and Manila Sound of the 70s, the new wave and rock of the 80s, the huge 90s band boom, the 2000s alternative wave, and today's indie-folk and P-pop groups. Each table gives the act, the style and why it matters. It is not every band ever formed, but it covers the names that shaped Filipino pop and rock.

60s and 70s: Pinoy rock pioneers and Manila Sound

The late 60s and 70s are when Filipino musicians began writing rock and pop in their own languages and with their own stories. Radio and record labels helped Tagalog and Taglish songs reach a mass audience.

Apo Hiking SocietyFolk-popFormed as students, became one of OPM's most loved trios

Who is Sampaguita?

People often search "Sampaguita singer" when looking up 70s Pinoy rock. Sampaguita is a solo rock singer rather than a band, and she was one of the most recognisable female rock voices of that era, known for the hit "Bonggahan". She is a must-mention alongside the bands of the time.

Rock bands that start with Y

Trivia hunters often look for rock or metal bands that start with Y. In OPM, the obvious answer is Yano, the folk-punk band behind some of the most biting songs of the 90s.

Tip: Want to see these acts live? Big concerts in Cebu and Davao are usually held at arenas, malls and convention centres. Follow official band pages and authorised ticket sellers, and be wary of resold tickets on social media.

How to explore OPM bands today

  • Start with greatest hits. Most of the bands above have compilation playlists on streaming platforms.
  • Listen by decade. Hearing the 70s, 90s and 2010s back to back shows how much OPM has changed.
  • Go local. Bars and music events in Cebu and Davao still feature original bands; check local listings and our news section.
  • Watch the films and documentaries. Several OPM bands have been the subject of concerts, reunions and documentaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most famous Filipino band?

The Eraserheads are often named the most influential Filipino band, especially for 90s OPM. Other names that come up often include Rivermaya, Parokya ni Edgar, APO Hiking Society and Ben&Ben.

What does OPM band mean?

OPM stands for Original Pilipino Music, so an OPM band is a Filipino band that writes and performs its own songs, in Filipino, English or regional languages.

Which famous Filipino bands come from Cebu?

Urbandub and Cueshé are two of the best-known bands from Cebu. Cebu also has a large Bisrock scene of bands singing in Cebuano.

Who were the Filipino rock bands of the 80s?

The Dawn is the best-known Pinoy rock band of the 80s, with Introvoys rising late in the decade. Apo Hiking Society dominated folk-pop during the same years.
