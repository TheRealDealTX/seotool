SM Cebu Cinema Showing Today: How to Check Cebu Movie Schedules

Where to watch movies in Cebu, how to check SM, Ayala and Robinsons showtimes online, approximate ticket prices, and tips for a better night at the cinema.

To see the SM Cebu cinema showing today, the fastest and most reliable way is to check the official SM Cinema website or app, where you can pick SM City Cebu or SM Seaside City Cebu and view every screening for the day. Ayala Center Cebu and Robinsons Galleria Cebu publish their schedules through their own cinema websites, apps and mall social media pages.

We don't post daily schedules here because showtimes change often, sometimes on the day itself. Instead, this guide shows you where to watch, how to check schedules in under a minute, and what to expect on price.

Most cinemas in Metro Cebu are inside large shopping malls, so you can easily combine a movie with dinner or shopping.

Other malls in Metro Cebu may also have cinemas. Check each mall's official website or Facebook page for current information, as cinemas sometimes close for renovation or open new screens.

SM Cebu cinema showing today: SM City Cebu

SM City Cebu, one of the oldest big malls in the city, has several cinema halls showing Hollywood releases, Filipino films and occasional Asian titles. It is easy to reach by jeepney, taxi or ride-hailing from most parts of the city and is close to the North Reclamation Area and the port area.

How to check the SM movie schedule in Cebu

  1. Go to the official SM Cinema website or open the SM Cinema app.
  2. Choose your branch: SM City Cebu or SM Seaside City Cebu.
  3. Select the date. Today's screenings appear first.
  4. Tap a movie to see times, cinema type and seat availability.
  5. Book and pay online if you want to reserve a seat, or buy at the ticket counter.

Tip: New movies usually open on Wednesdays in the Philippines, and schedules for the coming days are often posted only a day or two in advance. Check again on the day if you are planning ahead.

SM Seaside City Cebu

SM Seaside City Cebu is a large mall on the South Road Properties with sea views and plenty of dining. Its cinema complex includes regular halls and, at the time of writing, premium formats. Premium halls have different seating and prices, so check the cinema type when booking. Getting there is easiest by car, taxi or ride-hailing; traffic on weekends and holidays can be heavy.

Ayala Center Cebu cinema schedule

Ayala Center Cebu in Cebu Business Park is popular with families and office workers. Its cinemas are run under Ayala Malls Cinemas, which has its own website and app for showtimes and online booking. To check the Ayala cinema schedule:

  • Visit the official Ayala Malls Cinemas website or app and choose Ayala Center Cebu.
  • Look at the mall's official Facebook page, which often posts the week's lineup.
  • Call or visit the cinema ticket counter.

Robinsons Galleria Cebu

Robinsons Galleria Cebu, near the port area and the city's northern districts, runs its cinemas under Robinsons Movieworld. It is a good alternative when SM City Cebu is crowded, and schedules are posted on Robinsons' official cinema channels and the mall's social pages.

Ticket prices in Cebu cinemas

Prices depend on the cinema, the format and the day. The ranges below are approximate and change often, so always confirm on the cinema's official app or at the counter.

FormatApproximate price per ticket
Regular 2Daround ₱250 to ₱400

Senior citizens and persons with disabilities are entitled to a 20% discount on movie tickets with a valid ID under Philippine law. Some local governments offer extra perks for seniors, so ask at the counter. Convert prices for visiting friends with our currency converter.

Tips for watching movies in Cebu

  • Book online for blockbusters. Opening weekends of big releases sell out, especially evening shows.
  • Bring a jacket. Philippine cinemas are famously cold.
  • Watch the clock on Fridays and payday weekends. Traffic to the malls, especially SM Seaside and Ayala, can add a lot of time.
  • Check the language. Some foreign films are shown with subtitles; Filipino films may have English subtitles at some screenings.
  • December is MMFF season. During the Metro Manila Film Festival, which runs from Christmas Day into early January, cinemas nationwide, including Cebu, show mainly the official festival entries.

Planning a night out

Make a full evening of it. Many Cebu malls have food courts and restaurants right next to the cinemas, and if you want something more local afterwards, try our guide to Cebu street food. If you are heading to Davao, we have a separate Davao cinemas guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check the SM City Cebu cinema showing today?

Open the official SM Cinema website or app, choose SM City Cebu, and select today's date. You can see showtimes and book seats there.

Where can I find the Ayala cinema schedule in Cebu?

Check the Ayala Malls Cinemas website or app and choose Ayala Center Cebu. The mall's official Facebook page also posts weekly lineups.

How much is a movie ticket in Cebu?

Regular 2D tickets are roughly ₱250 to ₱400, with premium formats costing more. Prices change, so confirm with the cinema.

What day do new movies open in Cebu cinemas?

New releases in the Philippines usually open on Wednesdays, though big titles sometimes have special schedules.

Do seniors get discounts at Cebu cinemas?

Yes. Senior citizens and persons with disabilities get a 20% discount with a valid ID. Some LGUs offer extra benefits, so ask at the ticket counter.
