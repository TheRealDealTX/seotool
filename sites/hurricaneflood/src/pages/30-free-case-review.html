What the firm reviews

{{FIRM_LEGAL}} handles Florida residential and commercial property insurance claims. Its practice areas include:

  • Hurricane and windstorm claims, including wind damage an insurer attributes to flood or storm surge
  • Roof damage and roof leak claims, including wind-driven rain through storm openings
  • Water damage claims
  • Commercial property claims, including hurricane damage and business interruption
  • Denied, delayed and underpaid claims under these policies

The firm's listed practice areas do not include National Flood Insurance Program claims. If part of your loss is under a flood policy, mention it in the form; the firm can tell you whether it can help with that part or where to look.

How fees work

The case review is free and there are no upfront fees. There is no attorney fee unless there is a recovery. Ask how case costs are handled under the written fee agreement before you sign anything.
