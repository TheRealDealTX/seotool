About the firm

HurricaneFlood.com is an information site sponsored by {{FIRM_LEGAL}}, a Florida law firm that represents property owners in disputes with their property insurers.

What the firm does

{{FIRM_LEGAL}} focuses on property insurance claims for Florida homeowners and commercial property owners. According to the firm's own website, its residential practice areas include hurricane and windstorm, water damage, roof damage, roof leaks, plumbing leaks, mold, fire, hail, sinkholes, theft and vandalism, and construction defect claims, and it also handles commercial property claims for owners and community associations, including hurricane damage and business interruption.

On this site, that means the firm can review hurricane claims where an insurer has denied, delayed or underpaid the wind, roof or water-damage side of a loss, including cases where the insurer attributes wind damage to flood or storm surge. The firm's listed practice areas do not include National Flood Insurance Program claims.

The firm's website describes its founding attorney, David Dube, as an attorney who also has experience as a licensed adjuster, with a background in construction, adjusting and appraising.

Learn more at thelawgicalfirm.com or on its hurricane and windstorm claims page.
