Valentine Mason Jar Gift: Sugar Scrub & Love Notes

Two easy ways to fill a mason jar for someone you love: a fragrant homemade sugar scrub or a jar stuffed with rolled-up love notes.

A Valentine mason jar gift is personal, affordable and fast. This tutorial covers two versions: a homemade sugar scrub that feels like a little spa treat, and a love notes jar filled with folded messages to open one at a time. Make one, or tuck a few notes into the tag of a scrub jar to combine both.

The sugar scrub uses pantry staples and takes about 15 minutes. The love notes jar takes a bit longer because you’re writing the notes, but that’s exactly what makes it meaningful.

Either way, finish with a ribbon, a fabric topper and a hand-written tag, and you have a gift that looks thoughtful on any budget.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. 1

    Clean and dry the jars

    Wash the jars and lids in hot soapy water and let them dry completely. Any water left in a scrub jar can shorten its shelf life. Remove any old labels with warm water and a bit of oil.

  2. 2

    Mix the sugar scrub

    Stir the sugar and melted coconut oil together until it looks like wet sand. Add the vanilla and, if using, 3-5 drops of essential oil and the rose petals. If it’s too dry, add oil a teaspoon at a time; if it’s too oily, add more sugar.

  3. 3

    Fill the scrub jar

    Spoon the scrub into the 8 oz. jar and press it down lightly. Leave about 1/2 in. of headspace below the rim. Wipe the rim clean before closing the lid.

  4. 4

    Write the love notes

    On each paper slip, write a short note: a favorite memory, something you admire, an inside joke or a small promise like “one breakfast in bed.” Aim for at least 30 so they can open one a day for a month.

  5. 5

    Fold and fill the notes jar

    Roll each slip into a tight scroll and tie it with a short piece of thread, or fold it into a small square. Drop them into the 16 oz. jar, shaking gently now and then so they settle and you fit more.

  6. 6

    Dress the jars

    Center the fabric square over the lid and screw the band on over it, or tie it down with ribbon. Add a few wraps of ribbon around the neck and a bow.

  7. 7

    Add the tags

    For the scrub, write the ingredients and simple directions: “Massage onto damp skin, rinse well. Use within 3 months.” For the notes jar, write something like “Open one whenever you need a smile.” Punch and tie the tags to the ribbon.

  8. 8

    Package and deliver

    Nestle the jar in a small gift bag with crinkle paper, or set it in a shallow basket with a washcloth and a candle for a fuller spa gift. Keep the scrub out of hot cars and direct sun, which can melt coconut oil and make the scrub separate. Give it a stir if that happens.

Tips for success

  • Oil makes shower and tub floors slippery; include a note to rinse the floor after using the scrub.
  • Essential oils can irritate sensitive skin. Keep them to a few drops, avoid citrus oils before sun exposure, and skip them entirely if the recipient has allergies.
  • Don’t use a sugar scrub on broken skin or the face; it’s meant for hands, feet and body.
  • Keep scrub jars dry inside; use a clean spoon rather than wet fingers to scoop it out.

Make it your own

Pink peppermint scrub

Add 1/4 tsp peppermint extract and a drop of red food coloring for a festive pink scrub. Test a small amount on your skin first.

Reasons I love you jar

Number each note and have them all start with “I love you because...” for a themed version that reads like a countdown.

Date night jar

Fill the jar with date ideas instead of notes, color-coded by cost: free, cheap and splurge, so you can draw one each weekend.

Brown sugar vanilla scrub

Swap half the white sugar for brown sugar and add a pinch of cinnamon. Brown sugar is softer and gentler, which makes it a nice choice for hands and feet.

Frequently asked questions

How long does homemade sugar scrub last?

Kept closed and free of water, a simple sugar and oil scrub generally stays good for about 3 months. If it smells off or changes color, throw it out.

Why did my coconut oil scrub harden?

Coconut oil solidifies below about 76°F. It’s still usable; just break it up with a spoon, or use liquid oil like sweet almond or fractionated coconut oil for a softer scrub.

Can kids make this gift?

Yes. Kids can mix the scrub with supervision and write or decorate love notes. An adult should handle melting the coconut oil.
