About The Lawgical Firm, P.A.

Claim Roof Leak is an information site for Florida homeowners, sponsored by {{LEGAL}}, a Florida law firm that represents property owners in insurance claims against their own insurers.

What the firm handles

According to the firm’s own website, its residential property insurance practice includes roof leak and roof damage claims, water damage, plumbing leaks, mold, hurricane and windstorm, hail, fire, sinkhole, theft and vandalism, and construction defect claims, along with commercial property claims. The firm helps policyholders when a claim has been denied, underpaid or delayed.

For roof leak claims, that work typically starts with reviewing your policy and the insurer’s explanation, looking at the cause of the damage and the roof’s age and condition, and evaluating whether the insurer’s estimate covers what the policy and Florida law require.
