What we collect

When you submit the case review form, we collect what you enter – name, phone, email, property city or ZIP, claim type and description – plus the page you submitted from, the time, and your IP address for spam prevention. The “covered or not?” helper runs entirely in your browser; your answers are not sent to us.

This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.

How we use it

Form submissions go to {{LEGAL}} so the firm can respond to your inquiry and evaluate your claim. If you checked the consent box, the firm may contact you by phone, text or email about your inquiry. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with the firm and the service providers that host the website and deliver email, or as required by law.

Retention and security

Submissions are stored on the web server in a location that is not publicly accessible and are kept as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and meet the firm’s recordkeeping obligations. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please do not send confidential documents through the form.
