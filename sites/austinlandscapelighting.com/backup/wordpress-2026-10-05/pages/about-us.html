About Us

Bringing Austin Properties to Life After Dark

At Austin Landscape Lighting, we believe outdoor lighting should do more than brighten a property—it should transform it.

Every home, landscape, and commercial property has unique architectural details, mature trees, gardens, pathways, and outdoor living spaces that deserve to be appreciated long after the sun goes down. Our mission is to create custom landscape lighting systems that highlight those features while improving safety, security, and curb appeal.

As a locally focused Austin landscape lighting company, we specialize in designing, installing, maintaining, and upgrading premium outdoor lighting systems for residential and commercial properties throughout Austin and the surrounding communities.

Whether you’re looking to create a welcoming front entrance, illuminate a backyard entertainment area, showcase architectural details, or increase nighttime visibility, our experienced team delivers lighting solutions built specifically for your property.

Our Passion for Outdoor Lighting

Landscape lighting is both an art and a science.

It requires technical expertise, thoughtful design, and an understanding of how light interacts with architecture, trees, stonework, water features, and outdoor living spaces.

Rather than relying on generic layouts or one-size-fits-all installations, we create lighting designs that complement the natural beauty of your property.

Every project begins with listening.

We take the time to understand your goals, evaluate your landscape, identify focal points, and recommend lighting techniques that produce dramatic yet natural-looking results.

The goal is simple:

Create an outdoor environment that looks just as impressive at night as it does during the day.
