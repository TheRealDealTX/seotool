How to use this

The first five are causes of loss. The last four are stages.

Most people arrive knowing what happened to their building, so the first five pages are organised by peril: wind, hail, fire, water and the general case. Each sets out the coverage question that peril raises and the evidence that answers it.

The last four are for claims that are already in trouble — deadlocked on amount, denied outright, or short-paid and closed — plus the one service that works best before there is a claim at all.
