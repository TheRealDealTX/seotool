Who we serve

Property types

  • 01Churches & Faith Organizations
    Sanctuaries, family life centers, schools and parsonages — usually insured on values nobody has revisited in a decade.    →
  • 02School Districts & ISDs
    Multi-campus property programs, risk-pool coverage, bond-funded buildings and a repair window that closes in August.    →
  • 03Cities & Municipalities
    City halls, public safety, utilities and public works — with FEMA, a council and the open-records file all watching.    →
  • 04Universities & Colleges
    Campus-wide schedules, research exposure, auxiliary revenue and blanket limits nobody has stress-tested.    →
  • 05Hospitals & Healthcare
    Where remediation standards, licensure and patient volume all constrain what a repair is allowed to be.    →
  • 06Multifamily & Apartments
    Per-building deductibles, loss of rents, and a unit-by-unit scope nobody on the carrier’s side wants to walk.    →
  • 07Hotels & Hospitality
    Where the room-nights lost are worth more than the drywall, and the brand standard dictates the scope.    →
  • 08Industrial & Manufacturing
    Plants, warehouses and distribution centers, where the equipment and the downtime dwarf the building.    →
  • 09Retail & Shopping Centers
    Landlord and tenant obligations, loss of rents, co-tenancy exposure and a roof nobody has walked in four years.    →

Services

What we are retained to do

  • 01Expert Witness
    Reports, rebuttals, deposition and trial testimony on damage, scope, causation and cost — for either side.    →
  • 02Causation Determinations
    What actually caused the damage, and when — hail or wear, wind or water, freeze or long-term seepage.    →
  • 03Cost Estimating
    Line-item scopes in the industry-standard platforms, and forensic critique of somebody else’s.    →
  • 04Appraisal & Umpire
    Party appraiser for either side, or umpire — the policy’s own mechanism for settling the amount of loss.    →
  • 05Soot, Smoke & Mold Testing
    Sampling, laboratory analysis and written findings on combustion residue and microbial contamination — and on what remediation is actually required.    →
  • 06Cabinet Repairability
    Whether damaged casework can actually be restored — substrate, finish, hardware and whether the profile can still be matched.    →
  • 07Contents & Pricing Disputes
    Room-by-room inventory, like-kind-and-quality research and the depreciation arguments that decide a contents claim.    →
  • 08Public Adjusting
    Where the engagement is to present and negotiate the claim as the policyholder’s representative, under chapter 4102.    →
  • 09Policy Review & Pre-Loss
    The cheapest hour in insurance: reading the wording before there is a claim, while it can still be changed.    →

Claims Consultant provides expert witness and damage consulting services: causation analysis, damage assessment, construction cost estimating, testing and reporting, contents valuation, appraisal and testimony. We hold Texas adjuster and public insurance adjuster licences, which is what allows us to be retained by either party; on any individual matter we act in one capacity only, stated in the engagement letter, and we never act for both parties to the same loss. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice. Calculators, timelines and figures published on this site are planning aids built from published industry cost conventions and published standards. They are not appraisals, not coverage opinions, and not a substitute for reading the policy. Coverage is determined by the policy or coverage document in force at the date of loss. Serving Houston · Austin · Dallas–Fort Worth · San Antonio · Corpus Christi · Rio Grande Valley · statewide Texas.
