    Good to know

    What’s always included

    • Guaranteed image counts. Smaller weddings (4–6 hours) receive at least 300 edited images; full days (7+ hours) at least 500.
    • Turnaround. Sessions are delivered within a week or two. Weddings take 8–10 weeks, with sneak peeks much sooner.
    • Overtime. Running 15–30 minutes late is no big deal. Beyond an hour, additional time is $200/hour.
    • Booking. A 50% non-refundable retainer holds your date; the remainder is due one month before your wedding.
