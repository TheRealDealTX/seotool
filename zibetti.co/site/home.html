Hello, we’re Zibetti

Photo + film for couples who’d rather be outside.

We’re a small photo-and-film studio based in Provo, Utah. We love traveling, and we love couples who want their wedding to feel like them — not like a template.

Since we offer both photography and film, you get one calm, coordinated crew on your wedding day instead of two vendors working around each other. We guide you through every pose that doesn’t look like a pose, plan around the best light, and edit every image by hand.
