How Much Does a Destination Wedding Photographer Cost?

July 16, 20267 min readBy the Zibetti team

Destination pricing can feel like a black box. Here’s how it actually works, from a team that prices it every week.

Typical price ranges

Every market is different, but for experienced photographers you’ll commonly see:

Type of dayCommon rangeZibetti starts at
Local elopement (up to 4 hrs)$1,800–$4,000$2,400
Local full wedding (8 hrs)$3,000–$6,000$3,600
Stateside destination$4,500–$9,000$5,200
International destination$6,000–$12,000+$6,500

Ranges are general market observations, not quotes. Build your own estimate in our pricing estimator.

What drives the price

  • Travel days. A destination wedding often means two or three days away from home, not one.
  • Flights, lodging and rental cars. Some photographers bill these separately; others (like us) include them.
  • Hours of coverage and whether a second photographer is needed.
  • Film, which can be bundled at a discount with a single team.
  • Albums and prints.

What should be included

Ask every photographer for a written list. A good destination collection should include coverage hours, edited image count (we guarantee 300+ for short days and 500+ for full days), an online gallery, travel and lodging (or a clear estimate), and a turnaround time.

Comparing quotes fairly

  1. Convert every quote to an all-in number, including travel.
  2. Compare hours and image counts side by side.
  3. Ask about overtime rates — ours is $200/hour beyond the first hour of grace.
  4. Check the retainer and payment schedule (we take 50% to book, the rest a month before).
  5. Look at full galleries, not just highlights.

Ways to save

  • Choose an off-peak month or a weekday.
  • Combine your welcome party and wedding into fewer coverage hours.
  • Book photo and film from the same studio.
  • Skip the second shooter for guest counts under about 75.
