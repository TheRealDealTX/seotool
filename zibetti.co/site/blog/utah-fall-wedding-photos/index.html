Fall Wedding Photos in Utah: When and Where to Find Peak Color

June 18, 20265 min readBy the Zibetti team

Fall in Utah is short and spectacular. Mountains turn gold, red and orange, then winter arrives almost overnight. If you want fall color in your photos, timing is everything.

When does fall color peak in Utah?

Color moves downhill as the weeks go by. In a typical year:

  • High elevations (Uintas, upper Alpine Loop): mid to late September.
  • Wasatch canyons near Provo, Salt Lake and Park City: late September to early October.
  • Valley floors: mid to late October.
  • Zion’s cottonwoods: late October to mid-November.

Every year shifts by a week or more depending on temperature and moisture, so build flexibility into session dates.

Where to go

  • The Alpine Loop between American Fork and Provo canyons — famous aspen groves.
  • Guardsman Pass above Park City.
  • Mirror Lake Highway for early-season color at the lakes.
  • Sundance for an intimate fall wedding with lodging.
  • Zion for late-season gold against red cliffs.

Fall bridal and engagement sessions

A bridal session — wearing your dress before the wedding — is a Utah favorite, and fall is the perfect time for it. Warm colors in the mountains and your bouquet can look like they were made for each other. Choose florals in burgundy, rust and cream, and plan the session for golden hour so the backlit leaves glow.

Fall planning tips

  • Golden hour arrives earlier every week — check your date with the golden hour calculator.
  • Mountain evenings get cold fast. Bring a coat for between shots.
  • Weekends in peak color are busy; weekday sessions are calmer.
  • Some mountain roads close for the season, typically in late fall. Check before you go.

Looking for the right spot? Try the location finder with the season set to “Fall”.
