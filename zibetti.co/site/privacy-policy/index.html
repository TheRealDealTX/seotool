Privacy policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Zibetti Photo + Film (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through zibetti.co and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry, we receive the details you choose to share — typically your names, email address, event date, location and message. Our planning tools (golden hour calculator, timeline builder, location finder and pricing estimator) run entirely in your browser; the information you enter into them is not sent to us. If you use “Use my location”, your coordinates stay on your device.

How we use it

We use inquiry details only to reply to you, prepare a proposal and, if you book, deliver our services. We never sell your information.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log standard request data such as your IP address.

Your photos

We may share images from your session or wedding in our portfolio or on social media. If you’d prefer we don’t, just tell us — we’ll respect that.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: info@zibetti.co.
