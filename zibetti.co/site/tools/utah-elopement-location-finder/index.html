Choosing your Utah elopement location

Utah packs five national parks, dozens of state parks, alpine lakes, salt flats and slot canyons into one state. The right spot depends on three things: the season, how far you want to walk in your wedding clothes, and how many people you’re bringing. National parks cap group sizes and require a special use permit, state parks have their own process, and national forest and BLM land can be more flexible.
