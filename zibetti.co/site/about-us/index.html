About us

The people behind the cameras.

A small photo + film studio from Provo, Utah, chasing light and love stories.

Our story

One team. Two crafts. Zero stiff poses.

Zibetti Photo + Film is a small photography and filmmaking studio based in Provo, Utah, built for couples who want their wedding to feel like them — not like a template.

We started with a simple idea: couples shouldn’t have to juggle two strangers with cameras on the most important day of their lives. So we built a studio where photo and film are planned together, shot together and delivered together. One timeline, one crew, every angle covered.

Home base is the Wasatch Front, which means red-rock deserts, alpine lakes and salt flats are all a day trip away. But we pack light and travel often — across the United States and abroad — anywhere love takes you.

What we believe

The way we work.

01

Direction, not posing

Almost every couple tells us they’re awkward on camera. We give lots of gentle direction and prompts, so your photos feel candid and natural — never stiff.

02

Light is everything

We plan your timeline around the sun. Golden hour, blue hour, canyon shadows — we check it all before your day so the light works for you.

03

Every image, by hand

No bulk presets. We edit and deliver all the good, non-duplicate images, so you usually end up with more than the guaranteed count.

We’re here to help capture your big, special moments and all the tiny details for you to remember forever.Why we do this
