Frequently asked questions

Good questions.

Everything couples usually ask us — delivery, travel, film, booking and what to do if you’re camera-shy.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes! We love traveling for weddings and have photographed them all over the United States and abroad. We have special collections built just for destination weddings — travel and lodging are already included. Contact us with your location for details.

Do you offer both photo and video?

Yes. Because we work as a photo + film team, we can cover both on your wedding day. Adding a wedding film to a photo collection is a $2,000 add-on and covers the same hours as your photography. Photo-only and film-only collections are available too, but hiring one coordinated team for both is just so convenient. Read more in Photo and video: should you hire both?

How many images will we receive? Do you deliver RAW files?

It depends on the length of your day. For smaller weddings (4–6 hours) we guarantee 300 edited images; for longer weddings (7+ hours) at least 500. That isn’t a cap — we deliver every good, non-duplicate image, so most couples get more. Unedited files are available on a hard drive for a small fee; we ask that you don’t edit or post them, since they don’t represent our finished style.

What if our wedding runs late?

No problem. Running 15–30 minutes over usually isn’t a big deal. If your day goes over by more than an hour, additional time is billed at $200/hour.

How long until we get our photos?

Engagement and bridal sessions are usually delivered within a week — sooner if you need them for announcements. Wedding galleries take 8–10 weeks depending on the season. We send sneak peeks as soon as possible, but we never rush the edit.

Do we need a second shooter?

It depends. Smaller weddings rarely need one. For larger weddings, or if you book photo + film and want an extra photographer, a second shooter is a $600 add-on.

How do we book our date?

A signed contract and a 50% non-refundable retainer hold your date. The remaining balance is due one month before your wedding.

What happens if you get sick?

Only an extreme illness, a death in the family or a true emergency would keep us from your wedding — and we’ve worked through plenty of colds. In the rare case something happens, we have a list of trusted photographers and filmmakers we can call on.

Can we combine our wedding with a second location or day?

Yes. Many couples add a day-after adventure session, a welcome party or a second location. We’ll build it into your collection and timeline.

How will our photos be delivered?

Through a beautiful online gallery you can share with family. You can download everything in high resolution and order prints and albums directly from the gallery.

Can we see your full price list?

Our price guide is packed with information, so it’s easiest to email us and we’ll send the complete version. You can also get a ballpark instantly with our pricing estimator.

We’re awkward in front of the camera. Help?

This is the thing we’re proudest of. Almost every couple says they’re nervous on camera, and our answer is always: don’t worry. We give lots of direction (not stiff posing) so your photos feel candid and natural, and we take time to get to know you. By the end of the shoot you’ll realize there was never anything to worry about.

Do you use shot lists?

Honestly, we don’t love them — we’d rather you trust us to document your day the way we know how, and we won’t miss a big moment. Just give us a heads-up about anything non-traditional, plus a list of the family group photos you want after the ceremony.

Do you help with permits and locations for elopements?

Absolutely. We help you choose a location, understand the permit you’ll need and plan around the light. Try our Utah elopement location finder and read how to elope in Utah.
