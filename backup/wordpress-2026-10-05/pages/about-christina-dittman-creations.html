About Christina Dittman Creations

Every celebration deserves something personal. Something that feels like it was made just for that moment.

That’s exactly where Christina Dittman Creations began.

What started as a love for art, lettering, and meaningful details grew into a passion for creating custom hand-painted banners that turn ordinary events into unforgettable experiences. Whether it’s a wedding, birthday, baby shower, engagement, graduation, or a once-in-a-lifetime celebration, each banner is designed to reflect your story.

No templates. No shortcuts. Just thoughtful, handcrafted work.

The Heart Behind the Craft

At the core of Christina Dittman Creations is a simple idea: the small details matter.

A banner isn’t just decoration. It’s the backdrop to your photos. The focal point of your event. The piece people remember long after the celebration ends.

That’s why every banner is:

  • Hand-painted with care
  • Designed specifically for your occasion
  • Created with attention to color, style, and theme
  • Made to feel personal, not mass-produced

Each order is treated like it’s for a close friend or family member—because that level of care shows in the final result.

Custom Made, Start to Finish

Every banner begins with your vision.

From the wording and colors to the overall style, each detail is carefully planned and brought to life by hand. Whether you want something elegant and minimal or bold and eye-catching, the goal is always the same: to create a piece that feels uniquely yours.

You’re not just ordering a banner—you’re collaborating on something meaningful.

Designed for Life’s Biggest Moments

Christina Dittman Creations proudly creates banners for:

  • Weddings
  • Birthdays
  • Baby Showers
  • Engagement Celebrations
  • Graduations
  • And all of life’s special occasions

If it matters to you, it matters here.

Made With Care, Meant to Last

There’s something special about handmade work. You can see it. You can feel it.

Every brushstroke, every letter, every detail is created with intention. The result is more than just décor—it’s a keepsake you’ll want to hold onto long after the event is over.
