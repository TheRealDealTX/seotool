Texas Outdoor Lighting Designed for Your Property

No two Texas properties are exactly alike. A Hill Country home, a Houston garden, a North Texas estate, and a Gulf Coast business each present different architecture, landscapes, weather conditions, and nighttime needs.

Our lighting designers build each system around the property itself. We combine professional-grade LED fixtures, low-voltage installation, careful beam placement, and smart-control options to create outdoor lighting that looks refined and performs reliably in Texas conditions.

Built for the Environment

Landscape Lighting Made for Texas Conditions

Texas outdoor lighting must perform through heat, humidity, heavy rain, shifting soil, irrigation, wind, and long operating seasons. Our design and installation approach accounts for the conditions surrounding your property—not just how it looks on installation night.

  • Weather-resistant, professional-grade LED fixtures
  • Low-voltage systems planned for efficiency and expansion
  • Careful wire routing around roots, lawns, and hardscapes
  • Fixture placement that limits glare and unnecessary spill
  • Nighttime aiming and system balancing
  • Five-year workmanship warranty
