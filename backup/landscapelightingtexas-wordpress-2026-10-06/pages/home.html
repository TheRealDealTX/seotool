How It Works

From Vision to Illumination

Our proven four-step process ensures your landscape lighting project is flawless — from the first conversation to the final reveal.

I

Free Consultation

We visit your property at dusk to truly understand how light interacts with your landscape. No pressure, just a thoughtful conversation about your vision.

II

Custom Design

Our designers create a detailed lighting plan with fixture placements, beam angles, and color temperatures selected specifically for your property's architecture and plantings.

III

Expert Installation

Our licensed Texas technicians handle everything — from trenching low-voltage wiring to precisely aiming each fixture — with zero disruption to your landscaping.

IV

The Reveal & Tune

We walk your property with you after dark, fine-tuning every angle and intensity until the result is exactly what you imagined — and often better.

FAQ

Common Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about landscape lighting in Texas — from energy costs to installation timelines.

Ask Us Anything
Most residential landscape lighting projects in Texas range from $2,500 to $12,000 depending on property size, number of fixtures, and complexity. A starter package for a typical home runs $2,500–$5,000, while comprehensive estate lighting can exceed $20,000. We provide detailed, itemized quotes with zero obligation so you know exactly what you're getting before any decision is made.
Absolutely. Modern LED landscape lighting uses 75–80% less energy than traditional halogen systems. A typical whole-property lighting system running 6 hours per night costs approximately $10–$25 per month on your electricity bill. Most homeowners recoup the energy savings within 3–4 years compared to older systems.
Most residential installations are completed in one day. Larger estate projects or commercial properties may take two to three days. We schedule installations with your convenience in mind and ensure your property is fully functional — and spectacular — by nightfall on the final day.
We take exceptional care of your property. Low-voltage wiring is buried using narrow-blade trenching tools that minimize disruption, and we carefully hand-trench around roots and existing plantings. We re-seed or replace any turf affected and leave your property looking pristine.
We back all our installations with a 5-year workmanship warranty — one of the strongest in the Texas landscape lighting industry. Additionally, all fixtures carry manufacturer warranties ranging from 3 to 10 years. If anything fails due to installation defects, we fix it at no charge.
Yes — we design all systems with expansion in mind. Low-voltage systems are highly flexible, making it easy to add fixtures, upgrade to smart controls, or adapt your lighting as your landscaping matures and changes. Many of our clients add to their system as they see how beautiful the results can be.
