Our Story

Built Around Better Outdoor Lighting

For more than 5 years, Landscape Lighting Texas has helped property owners see their outdoor spaces in an entirely new light. What began with a simple belief—that thoughtful lighting should be as functional as it is beautiful—has guided more than 1,400 residential and commercial projects.

Our team approaches every property as its own design challenge. We study the architecture, mature trees, pathways, gathering spaces, and natural sightlines before recommending a fixture. The result is a custom low-voltage lighting system that feels intentional, balanced, and distinctly yours.

From architectural uplighting and garden illumination to smart controls and security lighting, we combine creative design with careful installation and dependable long-term support.

Our Approach

Thoughtful Design. Careful Installation. Lasting Support.

Excellent landscape lighting is not a collection of fixtures. It is a complete system designed around your property, your priorities, and how you use the space after dark.

Talk with a Texas lighting designer →
  1. 01

    Listen First

    We begin with your goals, style, budget, safety concerns, and the parts of your property you want to experience differently.

  2. 02

    Design for the Property

    We select techniques, beam spreads, color temperatures, and fixture locations that complement the architecture and landscape.

  3. 03

    Install With Care

    Our team protects lawns, planting beds, roots, hardscapes, and existing systems while placing and concealing low-voltage wiring.

  4. 04

    Fine-Tune at Night

    We aim, balance, and adjust the system after dark so the finished effect feels cohesive—not overly bright or uneven.
