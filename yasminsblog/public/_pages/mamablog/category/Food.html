Food

Delis, noodle shops, coffee roasters, candy stores and the dollar slice: the places that make New York taste like New York.

New York's food story is told best by its institutions: the delis, coffee merchants, candy stores and slice shops that have fed the same blocks for generations. This section covers them, from the hand-carved pastrami at Katz's Delicatessen and the bean bins at Porto Rico Importing Co. to the late-night dollar slice on the Lower East Side.

We also write about the newer arrivals that changed how the city eats, like Yunnan mixian at Little Tong Noodle Shop, Philippine coffee at Mountain Province, and the immigrant families behind the city's Turkish restaurants. Every guide is practical: what to order, when to go and what makes the place worth the trip.
