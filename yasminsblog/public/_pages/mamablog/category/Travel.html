Short trips out of the city, from a day in New Orleans to the Italian coast.

Short trips out of the city, planned around food, music and a good walk. Start with 24 hours in New Orleans during Carnival season, with beignets, parades, the St. Charles streetcar and live music on Frenchmen Street. Or head to Italy's Salerno, the port city at the start of the Amalfi Coast.

Each guide is built for a short stay: what to see first, where to eat and how to get around without a car.
