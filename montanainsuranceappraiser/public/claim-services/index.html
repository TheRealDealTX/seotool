Not sure where to start?

Most files begin with an estimate review

Before anyone demands appraisal, it helps to know exactly where the carrier’s estimate falls short and what that difference is worth. A review answers that quickly, and some disputes close right there with a documented supplement.

About estimate reviews
Can I hire you for just one stage?

Yes. Many clients start with a review and decide later whether to demand appraisal.

Do you handle homes, ranches and commercial buildings?

Yes: houses, cabins, condos, HOA communities, apartments, shops, churches, farm and ranch outbuildings and commercial buildings.

Do you handle hurricane claims?

Montana doesn’t get hurricanes. Our work centers on hail, wind, wildfire, winter, water and fire losses.

Think the carrier’s number is short?

Send the estimate and a few photos. We’ll tell you what we see, free.
