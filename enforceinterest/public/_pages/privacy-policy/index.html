Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-04.

Who we are

enforceinterest.com is operated by Rise Public Adjusting (“we,” “us”), a Texas public adjusting firm. Questions about this policy: info@enforceinterest.com.

What we collect

  • Information you send us through the claim review form or by email: name, contact details, insurer, claim details, and any documents you upload.
  • Technical information our web host records automatically in server logs, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of request.

Calculators and tools

Our calculators run entirely in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent to us. The claim timeline builder stores your entries in your own browser’s local storage so they are there when you return; you can clear them at any time with the “Clear all” button or your browser settings.

How we use information

  • To review your claim and respond to you.
  • To provide public adjusting services if you engage us.
  • To keep our website secure and working.
  • To comply with legal and licensing obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Cookies

The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address when delivering them.

Retention and security

We keep claim information as long as needed to evaluate and handle the claim and to meet record-keeping requirements, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent us by emailing info@enforceinterest.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
