Percentage deductibles on Texas storm claims

Many Texas homeowners policies use a separate deductible for wind and hail — and on the coast, for named storms — written as a percentage of the dwelling (Coverage A) limit rather than a flat amount. A 2% deductible on a home insured for $350,000 is $7,000, regardless of the size of the loss. That is why storm claims that look large can pay surprisingly little, and why some small roof claims are not worth filing.

  • Check the declarations page. It lists the all-other-perils deductible and any separate wind/hail or named-storm deductible.
  • The deductible comes off once per occurrence, typically from the replacement-cost total.
  • TWIA policies on the coast carry their own windstorm deductible options.
  • Interest is calculated on the amount owed after the deductible. Use the result here as the “amount owed” in the statutory interest calculator.
