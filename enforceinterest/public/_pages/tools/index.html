Which tool should I start with?

  • Not sure anything is owed? Start with Does my claim qualify?
  • Know your dates but not your deadlines? Use the claim deadline calculator.
  • Know the deadline and the payments? Go straight to the statutory interest calculator.
  • Went through appraisal? Use the appraisal interest calculator.
  • Claim still open? Keep a log in the claim timeline builder.

The tools give estimates from the information you enter. They are not legal advice. For a review of your actual claim file, send it to Enforce Interest.
