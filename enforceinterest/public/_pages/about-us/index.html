About Enforce Interest

A Texas-licensed public adjusting service built around one part of the Insurance Code: the deadlines insurers must meet, and the interest they owe when they don’t.

Our focus

The work most policyholders never realize they’re owed.

Insurance claims don’t just get delayed. They get underpaid, re-inspected, supplemented and dragged out — sometimes for months, sometimes for years.

What most policyholders never hear is that in Texas, delay isn’t only an inconvenience. Under Chapter 542 of the Insurance Code it creates a financial liability for the carrier. Enforce Interest exists to identify that liability and document it so it can be recovered.

We are not a general-purpose claims shop. Enforce Interest is a service of Rise Public Adjusting, a Texas Department of Insurance–licensed public adjusting firm (license #3356839) based in Austin, and it concentrates on one question: when should the carrier have paid, and when did it actually pay?

What we do

Timeline-driven claim analysis.

Every Enforce Interest engagement follows the same disciplined process.

Review the history

Policy, claim letters, inspections, estimates, payments, supplements and appraisal — everything with a date on it.

Find the triggers

When notice was given, what was requested, when each item arrived and when each payment was due.

Calculate

Interest on each late amount at the right rate, period by period, with every figure traceable.

Present & recover

A clear written presentation to the carrier, and follow-through until the claim is resolved.

How interest builds

Each phase of a claim can carry its own consequences.

ScenarioWhat happensWhy it matters
Late initial paymentCarrier pays after its §542.057 or §542.058 deadlineInterest on the late amount for each day of delay
Underpayment corrected laterMore money is paid months afterwardsInterest on the difference from the original deadline (Hinojos)
Supplement delayAdditional damage submitted; payment stallsThe supplement is evaluated on its own timeline
Appraisal awardCarrier pays the award after the disputePaying the award doesn’t by itself end prompt-payment liability
Who we help

Homeowners and businesses with claims that took too long.

  • Homeowners with delayed or underpaid claims
  • Property owners dealing with supplements
  • Policyholders who went through appraisal
  • Commercial property claimants with complex, multi-payment timelines

What we hear most

“My insurance paid — but it took months.”

“They sent more money later and never explained why.”

“We had to file supplement after supplement.”

“We won appraisal, and that was it.”

If that sounds familiar, the interest was probably never addressed.

Built for Texas law

Our work is grounded in Chapter 542, Chapter 542A, the Texas Supreme Court’s appraisal decisions and current OCCC rates — not generic claim advice.

Licensed and accountable

Public adjusters in Texas are licensed and regulated by TDI under Chapter 4102. Our fees are contingent and within the 10% statutory cap.

Honest about limits

We are not a law firm and don’t give legal advice. If a claim needs litigation, we’ll say so plainly.

Our perspective

Claims are treated like transactions. They’re really timelines.

And timelines create obligations. Every day past a statutory deadline has a value set by Texas law. Most people never calculate it. Enforce Interest does.
