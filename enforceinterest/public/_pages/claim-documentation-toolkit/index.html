Claim documentation toolkit

The tools that make a Texas property claim easier to prove: moisture meters, thermal cameras, scanners, storm and freeze protection. Picked by a public adjusting team for what they add to the claim file.

Why gear matters to statutory interest

The Chapter 542 clock starts when the insurer has the items it reasonably requested. Clear photos, moisture readings and organized, dated documents get those items to the carrier faster and leave less room for repeat requests, so the deadline is easier to prove.

FAQ

Toolkit questions

Do I need special equipment to file an insurance claim?

No. A phone camera and a written record of dates are the essentials. The tools here make documentation faster and more convincing, especially for water damage, where moisture readings show how far water spread.

Will my insurer reimburse these purchases?

Sometimes. Reasonable costs to protect property from further damage (a tarp, water extraction, drying equipment) are often covered as part of the claim. Keep receipts and submit them. Documentation gear you keep, like a scanner, usually isn’t reimbursed.

How were these products chosen?

For what they add to a claim file: dated readings, clear images, organized records, or protection that satisfies your policy’s duty to prevent further damage. We favor established brands with long review histories. Check the current price and availability on Amazon before you buy.
