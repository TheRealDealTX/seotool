About Kotric

Kotric is an independent craft blog about fabric crafts, DIY projects and sewing. We believe anyone can make something useful and beautiful with fabric — with or without a sewing machine.

What we cover

  • No-sew fabric crafts — projects made with knots, fabric glue and fusible web, like our 41 no-sew DIY fabric projects.
  • DIY projects for the home, kids and gifts.
  • Sewing tutorials and hacks for beginners.
  • Organization — taming the fabric stash and setting up a craft space that works.

How we write our guides

Every tutorial is written to be followed, not just admired. We list materials and realistic times, explain why a step matters, and point out the mistakes we've made ourselves. When we recommend a technique — like using a denim needle or prewashing flannel — it's because it works.

Free tools

We built a set of free craft tools to answer the questions we kept asking ourselves: how much fabric do I need, how big should this fleece blanket be, and what is 1.5 cm in inches?

Say hello

Questions, corrections, project photos or collaboration ideas are always welcome. Email us at info@kotric.com or use the contact form.
