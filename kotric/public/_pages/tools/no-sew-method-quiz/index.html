Your best match

Permanent fabric glue

Flexible and washable once cured. Apply a thin bead, press and weigh down, and wait 24–72 hours before washing.

Your best match

Fusible web

Paper-backed fusible web gives crisp, flat appliqué and bonded layers. Press — don't slide — the iron, using a pressing cloth.

Your best match

Iron-on hem tape

The fastest way to hem curtains, trousers and straight edges. Choose a heavy-duty tape for thick fabrics.

Your best match

Hot glue

Instant and strong on décor that never gets washed: wreaths, frames, wrapped jars and headbands.

Your best match

Knots and ties

Fleece and jersey don't fray, so cut fringe and knot the layers. Soft, washable and endlessly sturdy.
