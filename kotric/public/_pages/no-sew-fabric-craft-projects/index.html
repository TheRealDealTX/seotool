41 Amazing No-Sew DIY Fabric Projects

No sewing machine? No problem. These no-sew fabric craft projects use knots, fabric glue, fusible web and a little creativity to make blankets, pillow covers, bags, gifts, kids' crafts and home décor. Filter the list, search it, and tick off the projects you've made.

By the Kotric TeamUpdated Oct 19, 202612 min read

Before you start: the no-sew toolkit

Almost every project below uses some combination of these supplies. Pick up the basics once and you'll be ready for dozens of makes:

  • Sharp fabric scissors & pinking shears
  • Permanent fabric glue
  • Fusible web & iron-on hem tape
  • Hot glue gun (décor only)
  • Mod Podge & a foam brush
  • An iron and pressing cloth

Not sure which bond to use? Read fabric glue vs fusible web vs hem tape or take the no-sew method quiz. For step-by-step photos of four of these projects, see our weekend no-sew tutorials.

Tips for no-sew success

  • Choose non-fraying fabrics — fleece, felt and jersey — when edges will show.
  • Let glue cure fully before washing: usually 24–72 hours.
  • Press, don't slide the iron when using fusible web.
  • Prewash cottons for anything washable, so they don't shrink away from the bond later. See should you prewash fabric?
  • Save your scraps — half of these projects need only small pieces. Our fabric scrap projects list has 25 more ideas.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest no-sew fabric project?

A knotted pillow cover or furoshiki gift wrap — both take minutes and need only a square of fabric. For a bigger project, the fleece tie blanket is the classic first make.

What can I use instead of sewing fabric?

Permanent fabric glue, fusible web, iron-on hem tape, hot glue (for décor only) and knots. Fleece, felt and T-shirt jersey don't fray, which makes them ideal for no-sew projects.

Are no-sew projects washable?

Projects made with knots, permanent fabric glue or fusible web can be washed once the adhesive has cured. Hot glue is not washable, so keep it for décor.

What fabric is best for no-sew crafts?

Fleece, felt, T-shirt jersey and medium-weight cotton. Non-fraying fabrics are easiest because their cut edges don't need finishing.
