20 Sewing Hacks That Make Every Project Easier

SewingBy the Kotric TeamUpdated Oct 17, 20267 min read

Small tricks add up. These are the hacks we wish someone had told us in our first year of sewing — no special gadgets required.

Machine hacks

  1. Masking-tape seam guide. Measure from the needle and lay a strip of tape (or a stack of sticky notes) on the machine bed as a guide.
  2. Hold the threads for the first stitches. Holding the top and bobbin threads behind the foot stops the dreaded "bird's nest".
  3. Change your needle every 8 hours of sewing. Skipped stitches are usually a dull needle, not a broken machine.
  4. Use a scrap "leader". Start sewing on a folded scrap and chain straight onto your project — no more fabric getting sucked into the plate.
  5. Rethread with the presser foot up. That opens the tension discs so the thread seats properly.
  6. Clean out lint under the throat plate every bobbin or two.

Hand sewing hacks

  1. Cut thread at an angle — the sharp tip slides through the eye more easily.
  2. Run thread through beeswax or across a dryer sheet to stop tangles.
  3. Thread the needle, not the other way round: hold the thread still and push the eye onto it.
  4. Use a thimble on your middle finger for thick fabric — it really does help.

Cutting and marking hacks

  1. Tin cans or pattern weights instead of pins for faster, more accurate cutting.
  2. Fold the fabric on grain by matching selvedges and letting it hang straight before you lay it down.
  3. Bar of soap as a free marking tool for dark fabrics — it washes out.
  4. Freezer paper templates. Iron the shiny side to fabric, cut around it, peel off and reuse.

Finishing hacks

  1. Sharp corners: trim diagonally across the seam allowance, then push the corner out with a chopstick, not scissors.
  2. Curved seams: clip into the seam allowance so it lies flat when turned.
  3. Press, don't iron: lift and lower the iron instead of sliding it to avoid stretching.
  4. Hem with a cardboard template: cut a strip to the hem depth and press the fold over it.
  5. Safety pin + elastic: pin the end of elastic to thread it through a casing quickly.
  6. No time to sew a hem? Use fusible hem tape — see fabric glue vs fusible web.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my sewing machine keep skipping stitches?

The most common cause is a dull, bent or wrong-type needle. Replace it with a fresh needle suited to your fabric, then rethread with the presser foot up.

How do I stop thread tangling when hand sewing?

Use a shorter length of thread (18–24"), run it through beeswax or a dryer sheet, and let the needle dangle occasionally to untwist.

How do you get sharp corners when sewing?

Trim the seam allowance diagonally at the corner, turn right side out, and gently push the point out with a blunt tool like a chopstick.
