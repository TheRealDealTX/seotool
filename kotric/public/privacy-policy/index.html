Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 17, 2026

This policy explains what information kotric.com ("Kotric", "we") collects and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Messages you send us. If you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address and message, and use them only to reply.
  • Server logs. Our host automatically records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and performance.
  • Data stored on your device. Our interactive features (for example, the "I made this" checkboxes and tutorial step checklists) save your progress in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Cookies

Kotric does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address as described in Google's privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only where required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you've sent us by emailing info@kotric.com.

Children

Kotric is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above shows the latest revision.
