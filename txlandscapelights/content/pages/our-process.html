Six steps from idea to glowing yard

Landscape lighting is a design project, so we treat it like one. Each step builds on the last, and nothing is installed until you have seen the effect and approved a written proposal.

The process is the same whether you are lighting a small front yard or a full property with a pool, long driveway and mature trees. Bigger projects simply have more to plan. If you already have a system that needs work, we follow a shorter version focused on diagnosis, repair and upgrades.

  1. Consultation call

    We start with a short conversation about your home, how you use the yard at night and what you want to change. We ask about trees, entertaining areas, safety concerns, HOA rules and budget so the visit is productive. Call (281) 704-7210 or request a quote to get started.

  2. On-site visit and night demo

    We walk the property, note power sources, soil and existing wiring, then return after dark with sample fixtures. You see uplighting, path lights and wash effects on your actual house and trees, in different color temperatures, before you commit to anything.

  3. Custom lighting plan

    We map every fixture, beam angle and wire run, size the transformer and choose controls. The plan balances architecture, trees and gardens, and safe pathways so the property reads as one design.

  4. Written proposal

    You receive a clear proposal listing fixtures, quantities, controls and scope of work. If a new outdoor outlet or circuit is needed, we note it, since that line-voltage work is handled by a licensed electrician. Price figures are spelled out in writing, so there are no surprises, and you can approve the full design or start with a first phase.

  5. Clean installation

    Our crew installs fixtures, direct-burial wire, waterproof connections and the transformer. We route around root zones and irrigation, keep trenches narrow and clean up each day. Before we leave, we test every fixture and walk you through the transformer, timer and how to make simple adjustments.

  6. After-dark fine-tuning and follow-up

    We come back at night to adjust every fixture's aim and brightness and set your timer or smart controls. After that, we check in to make sure all is well, and offer maintenance visits to keep the system looking its best.

What to expect

  • A free consultation and night demo with no obligation
  • A written proposal before any work begins
  • 12V low-voltage LED fixtures chosen for Texas heat, storms and soil
  • Minimal disruption to lawns, beds and irrigation
  • A daily cleanup and a tidy finished site
  • A final adjustment visit after dark
  • Straight answers about timing, scope and what an electrician needs to handle

Tip: Have photos of your yard, your HOA's exterior lighting rules and the location of any outdoor outlets handy before the first call. It speeds up planning.

Why we finish after dark

Light behaves differently on site than on paper. A beam that looks right during installation can land too hot on a column or miss the canopy of a tree once it is truly dark. That is why the final visit happens at night, when we can see exactly what you will see from the street, the driveway and the patio. It is also when we set run times that match Texas sunsets through the seasons.
