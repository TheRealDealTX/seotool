What happens after you submit

Once your request comes in, we reach out within one business day to talk through your goals and schedule a visit. Prefer to talk now? Call (281) 704-7210.

  1. A short call to learn about your home, how you use the yard and what you want to change.
  2. A site visit and night demo where we test fixtures on your house and trees so you can see real effects.
  3. A written proposal listing fixtures, controls and scope of work, so you know exactly what you are getting.

You can read the full step-by-step on our process page.

What the consultation includes

We walk the property, note power sources and existing wiring, and discuss architectural, tree and garden, pathway and patio and pool lighting options. We also cover controls, color temperature and phasing if you want to build the system over time. Everything we design is 12V low-voltage LED. The visit is relaxed and there is no pressure. Ask as many questions as you like, and feel free to have everyone who shares the decision there for the night demo.

What to have ready

  • A few photos of the front and back of your property, day or night
  • Your HOA's exterior lighting rules, if you have an HOA
  • The location of existing outdoor outlets, ideally a GFCI outlet near where a transformer could go
  • Details on any existing lighting system and what is not working
  • Your rough budget and timing

Tip: No outdoor outlet nearby? That is common. A licensed electrician can add one, and we will note it in your proposal. Want a rough idea of cost first? Read our Texas landscape lighting cost guide.
