Texas Landscape Lights ("we," "us" or "our") operates txlandscapelights.com. We respect your privacy and collect only what we need to respond to you. This policy applies to this website and the forms on it.

Information we collect

When you submit a contact or quote form, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • City or area where your property is located
  • Project details, such as what you would like lit and any notes you add

You do not need to create an account to use this website, and we do not ask for payment information online.

How we use your information

We use the information you send only to respond to your inquiry, schedule a consultation, prepare a proposal and communicate with you about your project. We do not use it for unrelated purposes.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How form submissions are delivered

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through our website hosting provider. That provider processes the data only to deliver it to us and operate the website. We may also share information when required by law or to protect our rights.

Analytics

We may add basic website analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed most. If we do, analytics data is used in aggregate to improve the website, not to identify you personally. We will update this policy if we add analytics.

Cookies and local storage

This website uses your browser's local storage, or a similar cookie, only to remember whether you have dismissed the quote popup, so it does not keep appearing. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and project information for as long as needed to respond to you, complete any work and maintain normal business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have provided. Email info@txlandscapelights.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Texas residents

Our services are offered to homeowners and properties in Texas. We handle personal information in line with applicable Texas and United States law.
