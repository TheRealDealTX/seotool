Landscape lighting for a fast-growing city

Frisco has grown from a small town into one of the busiest cities in North Texas, and much of its housing is new. That creates a specific set of lighting needs: crisp new facades, young landscapes still filling in, and master-planned communities with clear design standards.

Texas Landscape Lights designs and installs custom 12V LED systems for Frisco homeowners in both Collin and Denton County. We serve Starwood, Stonebriar, Newman Village, Phillips Creek Ranch, Richwoods, Hollyhock, Frisco Lakes, Panther Creek and the newer neighborhoods north toward the Fields development. Each project starts with a free consultation and night demo, then a written proposal.

Stone, brick and modern farmhouse

Frisco homes are mostly masonry, with builders using a mix of brick, cast stone and the cream or white limestone-look stone popular across North Texas. Older sections near Stonebriar and Starwood include large traditional and French-inspired homes. Newman Village is known for its traditional, front-porch neighborhood design. Newer streets lean heavily toward modern farmhouse and transitional styles with white or painted brick, board and batten siding, black windows and gabled porches.

Clean modern elevations reward precise, symmetrical lighting: matched fixtures on each side of the entry, soft light on gables and porch columns, and careful aiming so light does not spill onto windows. Stone sections can be grazed to show texture, while painted brick needs a wider, gentler wash.

Designing around young trees

Many Frisco yards are only a few years old. Builder trees, often live oaks, cedar elms, red oaks or Chinese pistache, may still be small, and beds are filled with young boxwood, dwarf yaupon, Indian hawthorn, liriope and ornamental grasses. Crape myrtles and Eastern red cedars are common accents.

We design these yards in two stages. Today, the facade, entry and paths carry the look, with small accents on young trees. As the canopy grows, fixtures can be moved back from the trunk, switched to wider beams, or added to light the upper canopy. Because the system is low voltage, these changes are simple. New backyards, pools and outdoor kitchens

Frisco backyards are often where homeowners invest next, adding a pool, covered patio, outdoor kitchen or fire pit a year or two after moving in. If that is on your list, it pays to plan lighting at the same time. We can run cable and set transformer capacity for future zones so the backyard can be added later without tearing up new sod or hardscape.

Once the space is built, we focus on comfortable, glare-free light: soft downlights from pergolas and eaves, step lights on level changes, gentle accents on trees and fences, and pool lighting balanced with the surrounding landscape. On smaller lots with close neighbors, careful aiming matters more than fixture count.

Master-planned communities and HOAs

Most Frisco neighborhoods have HOAs, and master-planned communities like Phillips Creek Ranch, Starwood, Newman Village and Richwoods typically have architectural guidelines for exterior changes. Landscape lighting is usually welcome, but some associations want a quick review. We provide a simple plan and fixture details, aim every light onto your property, and use warm 2700K to 3000K light that fits most community standards.

Gated communities such as Starwood and parts of Stonebriar often have tighter rules about visible equipment. We mount transformers discreetly near the house and keep fixtures low and tucked into beds.

North Texas weather

Frisco shares the same storm patterns as the rest of the northern Metroplex: spring hail, occasional ice storms and long, hot summers on clay soil. Quality fixtures and sealed connections make the difference. Read our tips on protecting landscape lighting from Texas storms for what to check after severe weather.

Sunset and scheduling

Sunset in Frisco runs from roughly 5:20 pm in early December to about 8:40 pm in late June. With busy family schedules, an app-based controller is the easy choice: lights follow sunset, and you can change zones for parties, holidays or travel.
