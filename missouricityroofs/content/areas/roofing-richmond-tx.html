Richmond has two very different personalities. Near the Brazos River and the Fort Bend County Courthouse, it is a small historic town with homes that predate air conditioning. To the north and west, it is some of the fastest-growing suburban land in the Houston area. Older Homes Near Downtown Richmond

The streets around downtown hold early 20th century homes, mid-century ranches and later infill. Roofs here can hide surprises: plank decking with gaps, two or three old layers of shingles, low-slope porch roofs and chimneys with tired flashing. A careful tear-off is often the only way to know what is underneath. Older homes also tend to have smaller attics and poor ventilation, which bakes shingles from below in a Texas summer. We photograph the decking as soon as the old layers come off, so you see exactly what was replaced and why. If the home has historic character worth preserving, we talk through shingle profiles and colors that suit it before ordering anything.

Roofing Richmond TX: Pecan Grove and Established Suburbs

Pecan Grove, built largely in the 1980s and 1990s around its golf course, is one of the area's best-known established communities. Many of its brick homes are on their second roof, and big trees mean leaves, shade and algae. Typical issues include worn valleys, rotted fascia and soffit behind overflowing gutters, and pipe boots that have split in the sun. A targeted repair may carry a sound roof several more years; widespread granule loss usually points to replacement.

Newer Master-Planned Communities

Harvest Green, Veranda, Long Meadow Farms and Aliana are newer communities with mostly two-story homes, complex rooflines and active HOAs. Roofs are younger, so the issues are more often installation details than age:

  • Missing kick-out flashing at roof-to-wall joints
  • Undersized ridge vents on long hips
  • Shingles nailed high, making them prone to wind lift

When replacement time comes, expect HOA review of color and material. For roofing Richmond TX projects in communities like these, the HOA often requires matching the original color family, so we bring product details and samples to make approval easy.

New owners: If your builder warranty has not expired, get a roof inspection now. Documented defects are easier to address while coverage is active.

The Brazos River and Storm Exposure

Richmond sits right on the Brazos, and the river's flooding after Hurricane Harvey in 2017 is still fresh in local memory. The same tropical systems bring long bands of rain that test flashing, valleys and gutters. Spring hail and straight-line winds move across the open land west of town with little to slow them, and Hurricane Beryl in 2024 brought down limbs and knocked out power across Fort Bend County. Our storm and hail damage service documents damage with dated photos for your insurance claim.

Metal Roofs on Rural Richmond Properties

Outside the subdivisions, Richmond still has acreage, barns and shops, and metal is common there.
