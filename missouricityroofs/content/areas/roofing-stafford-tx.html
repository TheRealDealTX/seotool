Stafford's Housing Stock and Roof Ages

Most Stafford homes were built between the late 1970s and early 2000s. Kingsway and Quail Ridge date to the late 1970s and early 1980s, while Stafford Run, Stafford Colony and the Enclave at Stafford were built mostly in the 1990s and around 2000. Nearly every one of these houses has been reroofed at least once, often after a hailstorm, and many of those replacement roofs are now 15 to 20 years old themselves.

Typical problems we find on Stafford homes:

  • Brittle three-tab shingles from an earlier replacement, curling at the edges
  • Old turtle vents and power vents with cracked flashing
  • Rotted decking along the eaves where gutters were missing or undersized
  • Patchwork repairs with mismatched shingles from past storms

When the decking is sound, a targeted roof repair can buy several years. When it is soft in many places, replacement is usually the better use of money.

Townhomes and Shared Roofs

Stafford has a fair number of attached homes. Shared roofs complicate things: a leak above one unit may start at a flashing detail over the neighbor's wall. Before any work, we look at who is responsible under the association's documents and photograph both sides of the shared area so everyone sees the same evidence.

Before you call: Find your HOA or townhome association documents and look for the section on exterior maintenance. It often says whether the roof is your cost or a shared one.

Commercial Roofing in Stafford

Stafford's business parks and retail strips sit on low-slope roofs: modified bitumen, TPO and older built-up systems. Ponding water, split seams and failed flashing around rooftop units are the usual culprits. Our commercial roofing service covers inspections, repairs and recoat or replacement planning for owners and property managers who want to stay ahead of leaks.

Storm Exposure in a Dense Corridor

Stafford has fewer big trees than older parts of Missouri City, but more hard surfaces, and wind behaves differently between buildings. The May 2024 derecho and Hurricane Beryl later that summer both tore shingles and dropped debris across this part of the county. Hail tends to show up on soft metals first, such as vents, gutters and AC fins. After any major storm, a roofer Stafford TX homeowners trust should check those before anyone climbs up to judge the shingles.
