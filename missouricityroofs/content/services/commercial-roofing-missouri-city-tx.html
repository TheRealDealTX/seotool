Roof Systems We Work With

TPO single-ply membranes

White TPO reflects sunlight, which helps keep interior spaces cooler during long Texas summers. Seams are heat-welded, so a properly installed TPO roof resists leaks at the joints that commonly fail on older systems.

Modified bitumen

Multi-ply modified bitumen holds up well to foot traffic and is common on older buildings around Missouri City. It is often a good fit where rooftop equipment needs frequent service.

Commercial metal roofs

Many warehouses and newer retail buildings use metal panels. We repair loose fasteners, failed seams and rusted panels, and replace sections or whole roofs when needed. Our metal roofing page covers more of the details.

Roof coatings

On a roof that is still watertight with dry insulation, a coating can extend service life and improve reflectivity. We test before recommending one, because coating over wet insulation only hides a problem.

The Problem With Standing Water

Ponding is the most common issue we find on low-slope roofs in this area. Heavy rain from tropical systems and spring thunderstorms can drop more water than clogged drains and undersized scuppers can handle. Water that sits for days breaks down membranes, adds weight to the structure and finds every weak seam. Fixes range from clearing and enlarging drains to adding tapered insulation that redirects water. Debris from nearby trees, along with the trash and leaves that blow onto roofs along busy roads, makes regular drain cleaning especially important.

Commercial Roofing Missouri City TX Maintenance Plans

Most commercial roof failures start as small problems: a split seam, a loose pitch pan, a cracked boot around an HVAC line. Scheduled maintenance catches those early. A typical visit includes:

  • Walking the full roof surface and photographing seams, flashings and penetrations
  • Clearing drains, scuppers and gutters
  • Checking rooftop equipment curbs and condensate lines
  • Sealing minor openings before they leak
  • A written report with prioritized recommendations and photos

Spring and fall visits, plus a check after every major storm, give most buildings a solid baseline.

Keeping Your Business Running During Work

A roof project should not shut your doors. Before work begins we agree on staging areas, crane or material delivery times, dumpster placement and access routes away from customer entrances. Crews keep the roof watertight at the end of each day, which matters when afternoon storms can roll in quickly off the Gulf. We keep property managers updated with photos so you are never guessing where the project stands.

Replacement Planning and Budgeting

For owners managing several properties or an HOA reserve fund, knowing when a roof will need replacement is as important as fixing today's leak. Our inspection reports estimate remaining service life and flag the items that will drive cost, such as wet insulation, deck damage or drainage changes. That gives you time to budget instead of reacting to an emergency.

When replacement does come due, we compare systems side by side for your building: expected service life, reflectivity, how well each handles foot traffic from HVAC technicians, and what tear-off involves if there are already two roofs on the deck.
