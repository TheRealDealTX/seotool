Signs Your Roof Is Ready to Be Replaced

A single leak does not mean you need a new roof. A pattern of wear across every slope usually does. These are the signs we look for during an inspection:

  • Granules collecting in the gutters or at the bottom of downspouts, leaving bare, dark patches on the shingles.
  • Curling, cupping or cracked tabs, most often on the south and west slopes that bake all afternoon.
  • Soft or spongy spots when walking the roof, which point to decking that has absorbed water.
  • Repeated repairs in different areas over the past few years.
  • Daylight or water stains on the attic decking, especially around valleys and chimneys.
  • Widespread hail bruising after a spring storm, which may also mean an insurance claim is worth exploring.

What a Roof Replacement Missouri City TX Project Includes

A new roof is a system, not just a layer of shingles. Cutting corners on any piece of it shortens the life of the whole thing. On a typical job we:

  1. Protect landscaping, AC units and pool areas, then tear the roof down to the bare deck.
  2. Inspect every sheet of decking and replace boards that are rotted, delaminated or soft.
  3. Install synthetic underlayment across the deck and ice-and-water shield in valleys and around penetrations, where wind-driven Gulf rain tends to find its way in.
  4. Replace drip edge, step flashing, pipe boots and vent flashing instead of reusing worn metal.
  5. Balance intake and exhaust ventilation so the attic does not cook the new shingles from below.
  6. Install the shingles to the manufacturer's nailing pattern, then run a magnetic sweep across the yard and driveway.

Choosing Materials for Gulf Coast Weather

Missouri City roofs deal with heat, moisture and hail, often in the same month. Architectural asphalt shingles remain the most common choice because they balance price, appearance and wind resistance. Many homeowners now ask about Class 4 impact-resistant shingles, which some Texas insurers reward with a premium discount; check with your agent to see what your policy offers. Algae-resistant granules are worth considering on homes shaded by big live oaks and pines, where black streaks show up quickly in our humidity. For a longer-term option, standing-seam metal roofing handles wind and heat well and suits some homes and HOAs.

HOA rules in Sienna, Riverstone and other communities

Master-planned neighborhoods often limit roof colors and materials, and some require approval before the old roof comes off. Planning Ranges for Cost

Every roof is different, so treat these as planning numbers rather than a quote. In the Houston area, architectural asphalt shingle replacement runs roughly $4.50 to $7.50 per square foot of roof area installed, and standing-seam metal runs roughly $10 to $16. A typical Missouri City home has 2,200 to 3,200 square feet of roof area once you account for pitch, overhangs and garage roofs.
