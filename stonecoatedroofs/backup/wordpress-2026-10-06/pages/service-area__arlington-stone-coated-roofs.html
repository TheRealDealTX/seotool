What Are Stone Coated Roofs?

Stone coated roofing combines industrial-grade steel panels with textured stone granules designed to resemble traditional roofing materials.

At first glance, many people assume the roof is tile or architectural shingles. It is not.

Underneath the decorative surface sits a highly durable steel structure built for long-term weather resistance.

How Stone Coated Steel Roofing Works

Most systems include multiple layers:

  1. Steel core for structural strength
  2. Protective metallic coating for corrosion resistance
  3. Base coating for additional protection
  4. Stone granule surface for appearance and durability
  5. Protective overglaze coating

This layered construction helps improve resistance against weather exposure, impact damage, and UV deterioration.

Stone Coated Roofs vs Asphalt Shingles

FAQs

1. How long do stone coated roofs typically last?

Stone coated roofs can often last 40 to 70 years with proper installation and maintenance, making them a long-term roofing solution.

2. Are stone coated roofs good for Arlington weather?

Yes. Arlington stone coated roofs are designed to handle heat, hail, heavy rain, and strong winds better than many traditional roofing materials.

3. Do stone coated roofs look like metal roofs?

Not always. Many stone coated roofing systems are designed to resemble tile, shake, or architectural shingles rather than traditional exposed metal roofing.

4. Are stone coated roofs energy efficient?

Many stone coated roofing systems help reduce heat absorption, which may improve indoor comfort and reduce cooling strain during Arlington summers.

5. Can stone coated roofs resist hail damage?

Stone coated steel roofing is known for strong impact resistance, which can help improve roof performance during Texas hailstorms.

6. Are stone coated roofs noisy during rain?

No. With proper underlayment and attic insulation, stone coated roofs are typically no louder than other roofing systems during rainstorms.

7. Do stone coated roofs require a lot of maintenance?

Stone coated roofs generally require less maintenance than traditional asphalt roofing because they resist cracking, curling, and granule loss.

8. Can stone coated roofing be installed on residential homes?

Yes. Stone coated roofing is commonly installed on homes, luxury properties, commercial buildings, and new construction projects.

9. Is stone coated roofing heavier than shingles?

No. Most stone coated steel roofing systems are relatively lightweight compared to materials like clay tile or concrete roofing.

10. Why are more homeowners choosing Arlington stone coated roofs?

Many homeowners choose Arlington stone coated roofs for their durability, long lifespan, storm resistance, and attractive appearance.
