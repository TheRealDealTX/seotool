Stone coated metal roofing Irving TX property owners can rely on

When you start comparing stone coated metal roofing Irving TX installers offer, it helps to start with where Irving sits. The city covers a large stretch of western Dallas County, bordered by DFW International Airport on the west and Dallas on the east, and crossed by SH 114, SH 183, SH 161 and Loop 12. That puts it squarely in the corridor where spring storms from the west reach their full strength over the Metroplex. Hail, high winds and summer heat all take a toll, and asphalt shingles tend to be the first thing to give.

Stone coated steel roofing is built to take more of that punishment. Each panel is steel, protected with a metallic coating against rust, surfaced with bonded stone granules and sealed with a clear overglaze. The system carries a Class 4 impact rating under UL 2218, a Class A fire rating, and many products are wind-rated to 120 mph. Formed into shingle, shake, tile or slate profiles, it looks at home on a 1960s ranch or a Las Colinas townhome.

Irving's storm climate

The Dallas–Fort Worth area consistently ranks among the most hail-prone metro areas in the country. Irving's main severe weather season runs from March through June, with April and May the most active. Storm systems often form west of Fort Worth and intensify as they track east, crossing the airport and Irving on their way toward Dallas. A second, smaller window of severe storms can show up in the fall.

The rest of the year adds steady wear. Summer brings long runs of 100-degree afternoons and intense UV that dry and degrade asphalt. Sudden temperature swings in spring and fall work fasteners loose. Occasional ice storms and hard freezes stress valleys, flashing and gutters. That is why asphalt shingle roofs in Texas typically last only 12 to 18 years, while stone coated steel is expected to last 40 to 70. Irving neighborhoods and the right roof for each

Irving's housing reflects decades of growth, and the best approach to a roof depends on which part of town you are in.

South and central Irving

Many homes south of SH 183 and around the older parts of the city were built in the 1950s through the 1970s. They tend to be single-story brick with moderate pitches and original decking. A shingle-profile stone coated steel roof keeps that classic look while giving the house a much longer-lasting, Class 4 roof. Because stone coated steel weighs about 2.4 pounds per square foot, it does not require the structural work that concrete tile at 9 to 10 pounds, or clay at 8 to 12, would.

Valley Ranch and north Irving

Neighborhoods in north Irving and Valley Ranch were mostly built in the 1980s and 1990s, and many are now on their second or third roof. HOAs are common. Shingle and shake profiles match existing streetscapes, and we provide the samples and product data most architectural committees request.

Las Colinas

Las Colinas mixes custom homes, townhomes and condominiums around Lake Carolyn and the canals with office towers and hotels. Tile and slate profiles suit the area's Mediterranean and contemporary homes, and stone coated steel gives that look without tile's weight or tendency to crack under hail. Cost planning

For budgeting, stone coated steel installed in Texas runs about $10 to $18 per square foot. That is a planning range, not a quote. Simple single-story roofs with one layer to remove usually fall toward the lower end. Complex rooflines, steep pitches, tile and slate profiles, multiple tear-off layers and decking replacement push the cost higher.

It also helps to account for what you avoid. Each hail claim on an asphalt roof brings a deductible, and many North Texas policies now use percentage-based wind and hail deductibles. Over twenty or thirty years of ownership, those costs add up. Use the stone coated roof cost calculator for a ballpark on your roof.

Insurance and Class 4 credits

Many Texas carriers offer premium credits for Class 4 impact-resistant roofs, commonly in the range of 5% to 25% depending on the carrier and policy. Every insurer handles this differently, so talk with your agent before you commit and ask what documentation they need. After installation, we provide the product certification and project records. If your current roof is part of an open hail claim, ask your adjuster how upgrades are handled; often the claim covers like-kind replacement and you pay the difference to move up to stone coated steel.

After a storm passes

If you still have an asphalt roof and a storm just moved through Irving, start by walking the property. Dented gutters and downspouts, dinged AC condenser fins, granules washing out of downspouts and spatter marks on fences or patio furniture all point to hail impacts on the roof. Our hail damage roof checklist helps you document what you find before you call your insurer. Take your time choosing a contractor, read your deductible, and get a written scope before signing anything.

With stone coated steel, routine care is light: clear leaves from valleys and gutters, look the roof over each spring and after major storms, and have flashing checked every few years. The roof maintenance checklist and log makes it easy to keep a record, which can also help if you ever need to file a claim.
