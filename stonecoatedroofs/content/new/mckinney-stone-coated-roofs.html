Collin County Hail and McKinney Roofs

Collin County is one of the most hail-prone parts of Texas. Spring storms, usually between March and June, form to the west and push east across the northern DFW suburbs. The April 2016 hailstorm that hammered Wylie and neighboring parts of southern Collin County is a well-known example of how destructive these storms can be, but even ordinary hail seasons do steady damage.

On a typical asphalt roof, that damage looks like this:

  • Granule loss that leaves the asphalt exposed to sun and speeds up aging.
  • Bruising and fractured mats that may not leak right away but fail years sooner.
  • Lifted or creased tabs from thunderstorm winds, which break the seal and invite water in.
  • Cracking and curling as summer heat dries out shingles already weakened by hail.

Asphalt shingle roofs in Texas typically last 12 to 18 years. Stone coated steel is generally expected to last 40 to 70 years and is backed by 50-year to lifetime manufacturer warranties. Our article on how stone coated roofs and asphalt compare during hail explains the difference in detail.

Historic Downtown McKinney Homes

The neighborhoods around the downtown square include some of the oldest homes in Collin County, many with steep roofs, turrets, dormers and decorative gables. These homes deserve a roof that looks right and protects them well. Stone coated shingle and shake profiles can provide a traditional look while removing the short replacement cycle of asphalt and the upkeep of real wood.

A few things are different on older homes:

  • Design review. Properties in McKinney's historic districts may need approval before exterior changes. Check with the city's historic preservation office before you choose a material or color.
  • Decking. Older homes may have plank decking or several layers of past roofing. We inspect during tear-off and repair or overlay the deck as needed.
  • Weight. At about 2.4 pounds per square foot, stone coated steel adds very little load to older framing.

Brands and Installation

Stone Coated Roofs installs every leading brand: Decra, Roser, TEK, Tilcor, Boral (Westlake Royal), Gerard, Metro, Allmet and Unified Steel. Working across brands gives you more color and profile choices, which helps when an HOA has a specific palette in mind. Our TEK stone coated steel roof review is one example of how brands differ.

We install using two main methods:

  • Batten installation, with panels fastened to wood battens over the deck. The air space under the panels reduces heat transfer into the attic, and this method is standard for tile and shake profiles.
  • Direct-to-deck installation, with panels fastened to the deck over underlayment. This keeps a low profile and is common with shingle-style panels.

Either way, the details matter: valleys, chimneys, skylights, wall transitions and ridge ventilation are all flashed and trimmed with components made for the system.

Is Stone Coated Steel the Right Call for Your Home?

It is usually a strong choice if you plan to stay in your McKinney home for many years, if your roof has already been replaced after hail, or if your insurance costs have climbed after past claims. If you plan to sell soon and your current roof is in good condition, a replacement may not make sense yet, and we will tell you that during the inspection.

After a Hailstorm in McKinney

When a storm moves through Collin County, door-to-door roofing crews follow within days. Slow down before signing anything. Take photos, call your insurer, and get an inspection from a contractor you have checked out. If your claim covers a roof replacement, you may be able to pay the difference to upgrade to stone coated steel, which makes the next storm far less likely to send you through the claims process again.
