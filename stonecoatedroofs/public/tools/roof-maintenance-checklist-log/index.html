A roof maintenance schedule that actually gets done

Whether your roof is stone coated steel, a coated low-slope system or asphalt shingle, most problems start small — a clogged valley, a cracked pipe boot, a lifted ridge cap. Two seasonal walk-arounds and a check after every major storm catch nearly all of them.

The log matters as much as the checklist. Manufacturers and insurers both ask for maintenance records when you file a warranty or storm claim, and a dated log with photos makes those conversations short.

For stone coated specifics, see our stone coated roof maintenance checklist and maintenance guide. Never walk a roof you aren't trained to — Stone Coated Roofs offers free inspections.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often should a roof be inspected?

Twice a year — spring and fall — and after any storm with hail an inch or larger or winds over about 60 mph.

What should a roof coating maintenance log include?

The date, what was inspected or cleaned, any repairs, who did the work, and photos. Keep it with your warranty documents.
