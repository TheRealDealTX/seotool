How the stone coated steel roofing cost estimate works

Roofers price by the square — 100 sq ft of roof surface. The calculator converts your home's footprint into roof area using a pitch factor (a steeper roof has more surface than the floor beneath it) plus about 10% for overhangs, then applies Texas installed pricing of roughly $10–$18 per sq ft for stone coated steel.

What moves the price

  • Profile: shingle is the most economical; tile and slate profiles take more trim and labor.
  • Complexity: valleys, dormers, turrets and skylights add flashing work.
  • Tear-off: removing one or two layers of old roofing and hauling it away.
  • Install method: foam-set systems for high-wind coastal homes cost more than batten or direct-to-deck.
  • Brand: value tiers like Allmet sit at the low end; Tilcor and TEK at the high end.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a stone coated steel roof cost in Texas?

Most Texas homes land between $10 and $18 per sq ft installed — roughly $25,000 to $45,000 for a typical 2,500 sq ft roof — depending on profile, pitch, complexity and tear-off.

Is the calculator a quote?

No. It's a planning range. A free inspection measures every roof plane and checks decking, which is what a firm quote requires.
