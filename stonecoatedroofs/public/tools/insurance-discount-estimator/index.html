How Class 4 roof discounts work in Texas

Many Texas insurers file premium credits for roofs rated UL 2218 Class 4 — the highest impact-resistance rating. Stone coated steel, Class 4 shingles and some metal roofs qualify. Discounts commonly fall between 5% and 25%, but each carrier sets its own figure, and some apply it only to the wind/hail portion of the premium.

What your carrier will ask for

  • Proof of the product's Class 4 rating (manufacturer documentation)
  • An invoice or certificate showing installation date and product
  • Sometimes photos or an inspection

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does a metal roof lower your insurance in Texas?

A metal roof with a UL 2218 Class 4 rating — including stone coated steel — often qualifies for an impact-resistance discount. The amount depends on your carrier.

What is a Class 4 roof certificate?

It's the documentation showing your installed roof carries a Class 4 impact rating — usually the manufacturer's product approval plus the contractor's invoice or certificate of installation.
