Stone Coated Steel Roofing Westlake TX Estates Are Choosing

Westlake is a small town with very large homes. Gated communities like Vaquero, Glenwyck Farms and Terra Bella, along with custom estates on acreage across the town, are built with long, steep, complicated rooflines and premium materials like slate, clay tile and concrete tile. When owners look at stone coated steel roofing, Westlake, TX's estate homes raise a specific question: can a roof deliver the look of those premium materials while standing up to North Texas hail and cutting the weight? Stone Coated Roofs installs stone coated steel across Westlake for owners who want exactly that.

A Premium Roof Problem

Westlake sits along SH 114 and SH 170 in far northeast Tarrant County, with a portion in Denton County, between Southlake, Trophy Club, Roanoke and Keller. It is a quiet, wooded, residential town, but it is not sheltered from weather. Spring storms, generally from March through June, sweep across Tarrant and Denton Counties most years, bringing hail and strong straight-line winds.

On a large estate home, that weather creates a particular set of problems:

  • Tile breaks. Concrete and clay tile can crack under hail, and broken tiles are often discovered only when a leak appears.
  • Repairs are slow and costly. Matching discontinued tile colors, working on steep pitches and detailing around turrets and dormers all take time.
  • Weight stays on the structure. Like-for-like tile replacement keeps a heavy load on the framing for decades more.
  • Insurance attention. High-value homes with repeated roof claims can see premiums rise.

Stone coated steel addresses each of these. It carries a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating on the major brands, a Class A fire rating, wind ratings up to 120 mph on many systems, and weighs a fraction of tile. It is generally expected to last 40 to 70 years, with 50-year to lifetime manufacturer warranties.

Taking Weight Off the Structure

One of the biggest advantages for Westlake homes is weight. Stone coated steel weighs about 2.4 pounds per square foot. Concrete tile runs 9 to 10 pounds per square foot and clay tile 8 to 12. On a large estate roof, the difference can amount to many tons removed from the rafters, trusses and walls. That reduces long-term stress on the structure and can make future renovations simpler. Westlake Communities and Architectural Review

Vaquero, Glenwyck Farms, Terra Bella and other Westlake neighborhoods have architectural standards meant to protect the look and value of the community. Most require review before a roof replacement, especially one that changes material.

Stone coated steel is often a strong candidate for approval because it replicates premium materials rather than looking like metal. To make the review go smoothly, we:

  1. Review your community's guidelines for materials, colors and profiles.
  2. Recommend products that fit those guidelines and your home's architecture.
  3. Provide full-size samples, color charts, spec sheets and impact, fire and wind ratings.
  4. Help you prepare the application, then schedule installation after written approval.

Brands and Installation on Estate Roofs

Stone Coated Roofs installs every leading brand: Decra, Roser, TEK, Tilcor, Boral (Westlake Royal), Gerard, Metro, Allmet and Unified Steel. Despite the name, the Westlake Royal brand has no connection to the town; it is simply one of the manufacturers we work with. Being able to install across brands means we can choose the profile and color that suit your home best rather than the one a single manufacturer happens to make.

On Westlake estates, installation detail is everything. Large roofs often include:

  • Turrets, curved sections and eyebrow dormers that need custom-formed trim.
  • Multiple chimneys, skylights and wall transitions that need careful flashing.
  • Long valleys and high ridges that need proper ventilation.
  • Copper or decorative accents that the new roof must tie into cleanly.

Most tile, slate and shake profiles are installed on battens, which creates a ventilated air space under the panels and reduces heat transfer into the attic. Shingle profiles are often installed direct-to-deck. We document every transition and penetration before work starts and put the plan in writing.

Planning the Project on an Estate Property

A Westlake roof replacement takes more planning than a typical suburban job. Large properties have long driveways, mature landscaping, pools, outdoor living areas and sometimes guest houses or garages that need roofing too. Before work begins, we map out where materials will be delivered and staged, how tear-off debris will be removed, and how landscaping, hardscape and pool areas will be protected. If the property has multiple structures, roofing them together keeps the profile and color consistent across the estate.

Timing also matters. Full-size samples, community review and product lead times can add weeks before installation starts, so it pays to begin the process well before you want the work done, ideally outside the peak of spring storm season.

Wooded Lots and Ongoing Care

Many Westlake homes sit among mature post oaks and other native trees. Steel does not rot, grow moss or absorb moisture from leaf litter the way wood shake can, but valleys and gutters under heavy tree cover still need to be kept clear so water drains freely. A yearly inspection of flashing, sealant at penetrations, gutters and valleys, plus a check after any significant storm, keeps the roof performing as designed. Our stone coated roof maintenance checklist lists what to look for.

Cost, Insurance and Long-Term Value

As a planning range, stone coated steel installed in Texas runs $10 to $18 per square foot. Westlake roofs, with their size, pitch and complexity, typically fall in the upper part of that range, and removing tile or slate takes more labor than removing shingles. Our stone coated roof cost calculator provides a starting estimate.

Value comes from durability and insurance. A steel roof expected to last 40 to 70 years avoids the repeated tile repairs and replacements that follow hail seasons. Many Texas carriers offer discounts for Class 4 roofs, commonly 5% to 25% depending on the carrier, and on a high-value home even a modest percentage can be significant. Ask your agent what applies to your policy. Our guide to Class 4 roofing insurance discounts covers how to approach that conversation.

After a Storm in Westlake

When hail hits northeast Tarrant County, the volume of roofing traffic jumps. On an estate home, a rushed replacement or poorly detailed flashing is an expensive mistake. Document the damage, contact your insurer, and get a thorough inspection. If your claim covers replacement, ask about paying the difference to upgrade to stone coated steel so the next storm is much less likely to repeat the process.
