Stone Coated Steel Roofing Across Texas

Stone Coated Roofs proudly serves homeowners and commercial property owners throughout Texas. From major metro areas to smaller communities, we install premium stone coated steel roofing systems designed to handle the state’s toughest weather conditions.

Texas roofs face constant exposure to hailstorms, hurricane-force winds, intense UV heat, heavy rain, and rapid temperature changes. That is why more property owners are choosing durable roofing systems built for long-term performance instead of short replacement cycles.

Our crews work across the state installing high-performance roofing systems for residential, commercial, multifamily, and specialty properties.

Local Knowledge Matters

Roofing requirements vary across Texas.

Coastal regions face hurricane exposure and salt-air conditions. North Texas experiences frequent hailstorms and tornado activity. Central Texas sees intense heat and severe seasonal storms.

Our team understands the challenges specific to different Texas regions, including:

  • Local building requirements
  • Wind zone considerations
  • Storm exposure risks
  • Insurance claim environments
  • HOA roofing restrictions
  • Ventilation needs for Texas heat

That local experience helps us recommend the right roofing system for each property.
