Stone coated steel roofing Lewisville TX homeowners choose for the long haul

Most people researching stone coated steel roofing Lewisville TX contractors install have already lived through a hail claim or two. Lewisville sits mostly in southern Denton County, wrapped around the south end of Lewisville Lake and split by I-35E, and it shares the same severe spring weather as the rest of Dallas–Fort Worth. Hail, straight-line winds and long summers of intense heat wear down asphalt shingles fast, and a roof that was supposed to last twenty-plus years often needs replacing far sooner.

Stone coated steel was designed for conditions like these. Each panel is a steel core with a corrosion-resistant metallic coating, a layer of bonded stone granules and a clear protective overglaze. Installed, it carries a Class 4 impact rating under UL 2218, a Class A fire rating, and many systems are wind-rated to 120 mph. The panels are formed into shingle, shake, tile or slate profiles, so the finished roof looks traditional from the street.

Lake country weather in Lewisville

North Texas is one of the most hail-prone regions in the United States. Lewisville's main severe weather season runs from about March through June, with April and May typically the busiest. Storms often form along the dryline to the west and strengthen as they cross Denton and Tarrant counties. Hail of quarter to golf-ball size, and sometimes larger, is reported somewhere in the Metroplex most years.

Lewisville adds a local twist: the lake. Neighborhoods along the shoreline, near Lake Park and the Lewisville Lake Dam, and on the peninsulas that reach into the water face wind that crosses miles of open surface with nothing to slow it down. Shingle tabs that hold fine in a sheltered subdivision can lift and crease in those gusts. Stone coated steel panels interlock and are mechanically fastened, which gives them a real advantage in exposed locations.

The rest of the year is hard on roofs, too. Summer heat regularly passes 100 degrees, UV breaks down asphalt, and winter ice storms and freezes stress valleys, flashing and gutters. In Texas, asphalt shingles typically last 12 to 18 years; stone coated steel is expected to last 40 to 70. Our guide on hail-resistant roofing in Texas explains how impact ratings are tested.

Lewisville stone coated steel roofing vs. asphalt

The most common comparison we discuss is simple: another asphalt roof, or Lewisville stone coated steel roofing that should outlast several asphalt roofs. The short version:

  • Lifespan. 12 to 18 years for asphalt in Texas versus 40 to 70 for stone coated steel.
  • Hail. Standard shingles bruise, crack and shed granules. Stone coated steel carries a Class 4 rating, the highest impact class.
  • Wind. Shingles rely on adhesive strips; stone coated steel panels interlock and are fastened, with many systems rated to 120 mph.
  • Warranty. Stone coated steel manufacturers offer 50-year to lifetime warranties.
  • Upfront cost. Asphalt is cheaper today. Over the full time you own the home, the difference narrows or reverses.

Stone coated metal roofing Lewisville TX: cost and value

For planning, stone coated metal roofing Lewisville TX homeowners install runs about $10 to $18 per square foot in Texas. That is a planning range, not a quote. Simple, moderate-pitch roofs with one layer to remove land toward the lower end. Steep, complex roofs, tile or slate profiles, multiple tear-off layers and decking replacement move it higher.

To judge value, look at the full ownership period. A homeowner who replaces asphalt every 12 to 18 years, often after a hail claim, pays the deductible each time. Many North Texas policies now use percentage-based wind and hail deductibles, so those out-of-pocket costs are larger than they used to be. Run your own numbers with the lifetime roof cost calculator.

Insurance and Class 4 roofs

Many Texas carriers offer premium credits for Class 4 impact-resistant roofs. Statewide, those credits commonly range from 5% to 25% depending on the carrier and policy. Because the details vary, call your agent before you commit, ask whether a Class 4 credit applies, and find out what paperwork they need. Stone Coated Roofs provides the product certification and installation documentation when the job is complete. Our page on insurance discounts for Class 4 roofing lists the questions worth asking.

If you are replacing a hail-damaged roof through a claim, ask your adjuster how upgrades are handled. Often the claim covers like-kind replacement and you pay the difference to move to stone coated steel, which makes it one of the most cost-effective times to switch.

Brands and installation

We install every leading brand, including Decra, Roser, TEK, Tilcor, Boral (Westlake Royal), Gerard, Metro, Allmet and Unified Steel, in shingle, shake, tile and slate profiles. Depending on the roof and profile, panels go on a batten system, which leaves an air gap above the deck that can help keep the attic cooler, or directly to the deck for a lower profile.

A typical Lewisville project follows these steps:

  1. Inspection. Roof, attic, ventilation and decking are checked, and every plane is measured.
  2. Written proposal. Brand, profile, color, install method, underlayment and decking allowance are spelled out.
  3. Permit and HOA. We pull the City of Lewisville permit and supply samples and product data for HOA review.
  4. Tear-off and deck repair. Old roofing is removed and damaged decking replaced.
  5. Panels, flashing and trim. Panels are installed and fastened, with custom flashing at walls, chimneys, vents and skylights.
  6. Close-out. Cleanup, a final walk-through, warranty registration and Class 4 paperwork.

After a storm, and the years in between

If a storm just moved through and you still have shingles, walk the property before anyone climbs a ladder. Dents in gutters, downspouts and roof vents, dinged AC condenser fins, granules piling up where downspouts drain, and spatter marks on fences or decks are all signs the roof took hail. Our hail damage roof checklist walks you through what to photograph and note. Read your deductible, get a written scope, and do not feel pressured to sign with the first crew that knocks on the door.

Once a stone coated steel roof is in place, upkeep is light. Clear leaves and seed pods from valleys and gutters, especially under the mature trees common in older Lewisville neighborhoods. Look the roof over from the ground each spring and after major storms. Every few years, have the flashing around chimneys, skylights and walls checked, since sealants age faster than panels. Stone Coated Roofs can handle those inspections, and our stone coated roof maintenance checklist lays out a simple yearly routine if you prefer to keep track yourself.
