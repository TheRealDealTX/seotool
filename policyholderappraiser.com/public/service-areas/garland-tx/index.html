Why Garland claims end up in appraisal

Garland's storm exposure is a combination of tornado-track geography and ordinary Dallas County hail. After the 2015 tornado, carriers paid many homes as repairable that were later found to have racked framing, shifted foundations and compromised roof structures, and those scope disputes taught Garland policyholders what appraisal is for. In the hail seasons since, the pattern is familiar: partial slope approvals on 1970s roofs, no decking or code items, and depreciation that leaves a replacement-cost policy paying actual-cash-value money. The 2021 freeze layered interior water claims on the same homes.

Garland housing stock and the scope fights it creates

Central and south Garland carry 1950s through 1970s ranch homes with low-slope hips, plank decking, original single-pane windows and aging HVAC, where a hail replacement scope grows significantly once the attic is inspected and the window screens, gutters and condensers are added. North Garland and Firewheel carry 1990s and 2000s laminated roofs on steeper, more complex geometries where discontinued colors and steep-slope labor drive the argument. Garland's older apartment complexes along Garland Road and Jupiter Road produce multi-building hail claims with underscoped interiors.

Umpire appointment in Dallas County

Garland losses fall under Dallas County's district courts and county courts at law for umpire appointment when the appraisers cannot agree. We give the required written notice and file the request with a copy to the carrier. We also serve as umpire on Garland losses where we have no ties to either side.

Questions from Garland policyholders

My Garland house was paid as repairable after a tornado, but my contractor found racked framing. Is that an appraisal issue?

If the tornado is an accepted covered loss and the dispute is how much of the structure must be repaired or replaced, that is an amount-of-loss question appraisal can decide. Structural scope disputes benefit from an engineer's report, which we can help arrange and include in the submission.

Can appraisal include my detached garage and fence in Garland?

Yes, if they are covered under your policy's other-structures coverage and the carrier accepted the storm. Other-structures items are among the most commonly omitted lines on carrier estimates, and we scope them in the same inspection.
