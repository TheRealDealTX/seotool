Austin Insurance Appraiser for Policyholders

Austin's defining claim event is still the February 2021 freeze: days without power, burst pipes in tens of thousands of homes and apartments, and carrier scopes written from phone photos because adjusters could not get into the city. Hail seasons since have added roof claims on a housing stock that ranges from 1930s bungalows to homes finished last year.

Why Austin claims end up in appraisal

Winter Storm Uri produced the largest wave of accepted-but-underpaid water claims Austin has ever seen. Carriers paid for the obvious wet drywall and little else: no flooring continuity, minimal drying, no cabinet replacement, no plaster or custom trim. Many of those claims were never fully resolved and remain within the limitations period. Spring hail follows a northwest-to-southeast track across Travis and Williamson counties, so Cedar Park, Northwest Hills, Allandale and Pflugerville take frequent hits, and flash flooding along Onion Creek, Shoal Creek and Walnut Creek produces interior water claims where carriers argue over what was wind-driven rain and what was surface water.

Austin housing stock and the scope fights it creates

Central Austin, Hyde Park, Clarksville, Travis Heights, Bouldin Creek and Zilker, carries pre-war bungalows and mid-century ranches with plank decking, original hardwoods and plaster, and increasingly, high-end remodels with custom finishes that carrier pricing databases do not capture. Matching discontinued hardwood species and custom tile is the recurring dispute. West Austin and the lake communities, Westlake, Lakeway, Steiner Ranch and Bee Cave, carry tile, standing-seam metal and architectural shingles on large, complex roofs where steep-slope labor and matching drive the gap. The fast-built subdivisions of Pflugerville, Manor, Kyle and Buda carry laminated shingles where the fight is discontinued colors and code upgrades.

Austin's multifamily inventory, especially the garden apartments along Riverside Drive and in North Austin, generated enormous freeze claims with unit-by-unit scope disputes that are well suited to appraisal.

Umpire appointment in Travis County

If the appraisers cannot agree on an umpire, either party may apply to a Travis County district court or county court at law for appointment after the required ten days' written notice. We handle the notice and the filing. We also serve as neutral umpire on Austin losses where we have no relationship with either party or appraiser.

When an Austin homeowner needs a Austin insurance appraiser

Austin's underpaid claims come in two shapes. The first is the freeze claim written from photographs during Winter Storm Uri, where the carrier paid for wet drywall and nothing else. The second is the hail claim on a Northwest Hills or Cedar Park roof where two slopes were approved and the rest denied. In both, the carrier accepted the loss and underpaid the scope, which is exactly the dispute a Austin insurance appraiser is appointed to settle. A Austin insurance appraiser moisture-maps every room on a freeze claim and walks every slope on a hail claim, then writes the estimate the carrier should have written. For Travis County homeowners, a Austin insurance appraiser who worked Uri claims is a different thing from an adjuster who read about them.

Central Austin adds a third shape: the remodeled Hyde Park or Clarksville home with custom finishes the carrier's pricing database does not contain. A Austin insurance appraiser documents those finishes and obtains real supplier pricing, so the award restores the home with like kind and quality rather than builder-grade substitutes.

Questions for a Austin insurance appraiser

My Austin freeze claim from 2021 was underpaid. Is appraisal still available?

Possibly. The deadline is set by your policy's suit-limitation clause and appraisal clause, and some carriers reopened or supplemented Uri claims, which can affect timing. Send us the policy and the last carrier letter and we will tell you whether a demand is still viable.

Can appraisal price custom finishes in a remodeled Hyde Park home?

Yes. Appraisers are not limited to a carrier's pricing database. Where a finish is custom, we document it, obtain supplier or contractor pricing, and present it to the carrier's appraiser and the umpire as the actual cost to repair or replace with like kind and quality.
