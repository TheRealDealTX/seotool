San Antonio Insurance Appraiser for Policyholders

San Antonio sits squarely in the Texas hail belt, and the April 2016 storm that dropped softball-sized hail across the north side remains one of the costliest hail events in state history. A decade later, Bexar County roofs are still the most common subject of the appraisals we handle here.

Why San Antonio claims end up in appraisal

San Antonio hail comes from the northwest, which means Stone Oak, Shavano Park, Helotes, Leon Valley and the Medical Center area take the first and hardest hits. The pattern on carrier estimates after a Bexar County hail event is consistent: front and west-facing slopes approved, rear slopes called "no damage" or "mechanical," and collateral items like window screens, gutters, HVAC fins and painted fascia left off entirely. The storm is accepted; the scope is not. Appraisal resolves that gap without a lawsuit.

The February 2021 freeze also hit San Antonio hard. Homes built on pier-and-beam with exposed supply lines, and newer homes with PEX run through unconditioned attics, both burst. Carriers accepted those losses but frequently disputed drying duration, flooring continuity and the cost of opening ceilings to replace lines.

San Antonio housing stock and the scope fights it creates

San Antonio's older neighborhoods, Alamo Heights, Monte Vista, Olmos Park, Monticello Park and the King William district, carry clay-tile, slate and standing-seam metal roofs, some original to the house. Hail damage on tile is a matching and repairability argument: individual tiles may be replaceable, but matching discontinued profiles and colors frequently is not. In the post-1980 subdivisions on the north and west sides, the argument is more often brittleness on aging three-tab shingles and whether code-required decking and underlayment upgrades are owed.

Commercial losses in San Antonio skew toward restaurants, car dealerships along Loop 410 and I-10, and church campuses with large low-slope roofs and rooftop units. Hail on condenser coils and TPO membranes is where carrier and policyholder estimates diverge most.

Umpire appointment in Bexar County

When appraisers cannot agree on an umpire, a Bexar County district court or county court at law can appoint one on a short motion. We have found Bexar County appointments to be routine and fast. We also accept umpire appointments on San Antonio losses where neither party nor either appraiser has any connection to us.

What a San Antonio insurance appraiser brings to a Bexar County roof dispute

The typical San Antonio hail file arrives with two slopes approved and the rest marked "no damage." A San Antonio insurance appraiser re-walks the whole roof, marks test squares on every slope, photographs the soft metals and screens that corroborate the hail event, and performs a brittleness test so the repair-versus-replace question is decided on evidence rather than on the carrier adjuster's judgment. On the tile and slate roofs of Alamo Heights and Monte Vista, a San Antonio insurance appraiser also documents breakage patterns and researches whether a matching tile still exists, because that research is what turns a repair estimate into a replacement award.

San Antonio's freeze claims call for the same approach indoors: moisture mapping room by room, documentation of pier-and-beam plumbing access, and a scope that carries the repair to a finished, matched result. A San Antonio insurance appraiser who has written estimates on both sides of the table knows which of those items the carrier's appraiser will concede quickly and which need an umpire. A San Antonio insurance appraiser who has walked Stone Oak roofs after every storm since 2016 knows those items in advance.

Working with a San Antonio insurance appraiser from demand to award

We prepare the appraisal demand for your signature, serve it on the carrier, and once both appraisers are named, schedule the inspection within days. As your San Antonio insurance appraiser we exchange estimates with the carrier's appraiser, agree what can be agreed and narrow the dispute to a short list of items before anyone involves an umpire. Bexar County umpire appointments, when needed, are handled with the written notice the Texas rules require.

A San Antonio insurance appraiser is appointed by you but must remain independent, so our fee is flat or hourly and quoted in writing, and we decline any claim where Rise Public Adjusting acted as public adjuster. Questions for a San Antonio insurance appraiser

The carrier approved two slopes of my San Antonio roof. Can appraisal get the whole roof?

Appraisal can award a full replacement if the evidence supports it: documented hail hits on every slope, brittleness that makes repairs impractical, or a matching problem the policy covers. We inspect every slope and test repairability before we tell you what to expect.

Does appraisal work for clay tile roofs in Alamo Heights?

Yes, and tile is where an independent appraiser matters most. Tile claims turn on breakage counts, underlayment condition, and whether a matching tile is still manufactured. Those are factual questions an appraisal panel resolves far better than a desk adjuster working from photos.
