Corpus Christi Insurance Appraiser for Policyholders

Hurricane Harvey made landfall just up the coast at Rockport in August 2017, and the Coastal Bend has been adjusting its claims ever since. Corpus Christi policyholders also live with a split insurance market: a standard homeowners policy for most perils and a Texas Windstorm Insurance Association policy for wind and hail, each with its own appraisal process.

Why Corpus Christi claims end up in appraisal

Coastal wind claims are underpaid in predictable ways. Carriers approve the roof slopes that face the storm, call leeward damage "wear," and leave wind-driven rain intrusion through windows, doors and soffits out of the interior scope. Salt air shortens the life of every fastener, flashing and HVAC coil on the island and along Ocean Drive, and carriers use that corrosion to argue pre-existing damage on items a storm actually destroyed. After Harvey, many Rockport, Port Aransas and Aransas Pass claims were paid as partial losses on homes that were later condemned, and the scope gap on those files was enormous.

Corpus Christi also sees a steady flow of plumbing and cast-iron drain failures in the mid-century homes south of downtown, where slab-foundation access costs are disputed, and roof-leak claims on the flat and low-slope roofs common in Flour Bluff and the Southside.

TWIA policies have their own appraisal process

If your wind coverage is through TWIA, the appraisal process is governed by 28 TAC §5.4201 and following rather than by the general 2027 residential rules. TWIA appraisal has its own deadlines, its own appraiser and umpire qualification and conflict rules, and a TDI-maintained umpire roster that the Department selects from when the appraisers cannot agree. We handle TWIA appraisals under that process and can explain the differences on the first call. Standard homeowners policies for non-wind perils follow the policy's own appraisal clause.

Corpus Christi housing stock and the scope fights it creates

Ocean Drive, Del Mar and the older bayfront neighborhoods carry tile, slate and metal roofs on homes where matching and corrosion arguments dominate. Padre Island and Port Aransas carry elevated coastal construction with metal roofs, impact windows and exposed decks where carriers dispute whether damage to specialty components is storm-related. The Southside and Calallen subdivisions carry composition roofs where the dispute is slope-by-slope wind damage and the cost of code-required coastal fastening schedules on replacement.

Umpire appointment in Nueces County

For non-TWIA appraisals, a Nueces County district court or county court at law can appoint an umpire when the appraisers cannot agree, after ten days' written notice. For TWIA appraisals, umpire selection runs through the Department's roster process. We handle both. We also serve as a neutral umpire on Coastal Bend losses where we have no connection to either side.

Why coastal claims need a Corpus Christi insurance appraiser

Coastal wind claims are underpaid in ways a Dallas adjuster never sees: leeward slopes called "wear," salt-air corrosion blamed for storm-torn flashing, and wind-driven rain through soffits and windows left out of the interior scope entirely. A Corpus Christi insurance appraiser who has worked Harvey and Hanna claims documents the storm's path across the whole structure, photographs the corrosion separately from the impact and uplift damage, and moisture-maps the interior so the covered wind damage is fully priced. A Corpus Christi insurance appraiser who has worked both Harvey and Hanna files knows the coastal carriers' habits before the inspection.

A Corpus Christi insurance appraiser also has to know which process applies. TWIA policies follow TWIA's own appraisal rules and umpire roster; standard carriers follow their policy clause. Getting that wrong costs months. We confirm the process before the demand is written. Every Corpus Christi insurance appraiser engagement we accept begins with that policy check.

What your Corpus Christi insurance appraiser does once appointed

We serve the demand, inspect every elevation and slope plus the interior, and write a line-item estimate priced to Coastal Bend labor and the fastening schedules the windstorm code requires on replacement. At the appraiser conference your Corpus Christi insurance appraiser agrees what can be agreed and prepares the umpire submission for the rest. Nueces County court appointments or TWIA roster selection are handled as the policy requires.

Because a Corpus Christi insurance appraiser must be disinterested in the outcome, the fee is flat or hourly and quoted in writing before you appoint us. Celia, Allen, Harvey, Hanna and the freeze of 2021 are covered in our Corpus Christi storm history.

Questions for a Corpus Christi insurance appraiser

My Corpus Christi wind claim is with TWIA. Do the 2027 Texas appraisal rules apply?

No. TWIA policies are expressly excluded from the 2027 rules and follow TWIA's own appraisal process under 28 TAC §5.4201 and following, including TDI's umpire roster. We work within that process, and we will walk you through its deadlines before you demand appraisal.

The carrier blames salt-air corrosion for my damaged HVAC and flashing. Can appraisal address that?

Yes. Whether the flashing and coils were torn by the storm or eaten by salt air is a causation question, and in Texas the appraisal panel decides causation as part of the amount of loss. We document impact patterns, bent fins, torn flashing and the condition before and after the storm so the panel can award the storm-caused portion.
