Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Policyholder Appraiser, a service of Rise Public Adjusting LLC ("we," "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through policyholderappraiser.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you provide

When you submit a contact form, call, text or email us, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, property ZIP code or address, insurance carrier, claim details and any other information you choose to share. Please do not send policy numbers, Social Security numbers or financial account numbers through the website form.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider and analytics tools may record your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring site and the date and time of your visit. We use this information to operate and improve the website and to protect it against abuse.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and evaluate whether appraisal fits your dispute
  • To prepare an engagement letter and perform appraisal or umpire services you request
  • To communicate with you by phone, text or email about your claim, with your consent
  • To maintain business records and comply with legal obligations
  • To improve the website

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging

If you provide a mobile number and consent to be contacted, we may call or text you about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the website and our business (hosting, email, forms, analytics), with professionals involved in your appraisal at your direction (for example, the carrier's appraiser or an umpire), and where required by law. Service providers may use your information only to perform services for us.

Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies and third-party analytics to understand traffic and performance. You can disable cookies in your browser; some features may not work as intended.

Data retention and security

We keep inquiry and engagement records as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal and professional purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete information you provided, or to stop contacting you, by emailing josephdittmanappraiser@gmail.com or calling (832) 503-5866. We will honor requests as the law allows.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
