Bio

Joseph Dittman, Certified Insurance Appraiser & Umpire

Texas-certified insurance appraiser and umpire with three years of appraisal and umpire work, eight years of independent adjusting on the carrier side, and hands-on general contracting. Joseph has served in appraisal proceedings on engagements from both policyholders and carriers across residential, commercial, liability and catastrophe losses, which is exactly the balanced perspective an appraisal panel needs on scope, causation, pricing and policy application.

Appraisal and umpire experience (2022 to present)

463 appraisals closed and settled in 2025. Loss types handled: fire, hail, wind, tornado, hurricane, water damage, sewage backup, pipe burst, explosion, flood and large mitigation losses. Engagements have been balanced between policyholder-appointed and carrier-appointed appraiser roles, which supports impartial umpire selection and award drafting that both sides can accept.

Issues regularly evaluated: scope of damage, repair methodology, replacement cost and actual cash value, overhead and profit, depreciation, code and ordinance application, matching, and the scope of coverage under the appraisal clause.

Carriers appraised for or against include Allstate, Liberty Mutual, Mercury, Farmers, Texas Farm Bureau, State Farm, Germania, TWIA, TFPA, RVOS and MSI.

Sworn testimony

  • Expert witness deposition
  • Fact witness depositions in seven cases for Cypress, Kin and FPI/EDI, primarily tile roof losses and large mitigation claims

Carrier-side independent adjusting (2018 to 2025)

Commercial: Church Mutual, Hallmark, Tower Hill, Millennial Specialty Insurance (TRVCA), Travelers Farm & Ranch, Pharmacy Mutual and TML (Texas Municipalities), 2022 to 2025.

E&O and liability: National Interstate, HCC Casualty and Golden State Claims Adjusters, 2022 to 2025.

Residential: MSI, SageSure, FPI, EDI, Kin, Cypress, Slide, Blair, Allied, Kentucky Farm Bureau, Countryway, Progressive, Travelers, Conifer, Trident, Global Indemnity Group Services, American Reliable, K2 Insurance, American Risk, Catalytic Claim Services, Home First, Wellington, American Modern and Hastings, 2018 to 2025.

Loss types: wind, hail, sewage backups, pipe bursts, fire, tornado, hurricane, explosions, mitigation and complex multi-trade losses. Eight years of writing carrier estimates is why the carrier's appraiser cannot surprise us with a line item.

Policyholder-side public adjusting

Public adjusting on the policyholder side since 2025, including two commercial fire losses, commercial wind and hail claims and a commercial flood claim, with Rise Public Adjusting LLC. Policyholder Appraiser is kept separate from that work: we do not serve as appraiser on any claim where Rise Public Adjusting acted as public adjuster.

Construction and trades background

Construction foreman at Legend Landscape & Construction, 2013 to 2018: two years full-time as foreman and six years of hands-on exposure. Trades performed include foundation repair, deck construction, drywall, painting, carpentry, roofing and roof repair, pump and fountain systems, drainage, hardscape and rock features, mulching and landscape design. That background provides direct working knowledge of repair methods, sequencing and trade pricing used in appraisal scope review.
