Storm history · Fort Worth, Texas

Fort Worth Storm History: Mayfest, the Downtown Tornado and Since

Fort Worth's storms arrive a half hour before Dallas's and hit a city with century-old neighborhoods and brand-new subdivisions in the same path. Here is the timeline behind Tarrant County claims.

The Mayfest hailstorm, May 5, 1995

Softball-sized hail fell on the Mayfest festival crowd in Trinity Park and across Fort Worth, injuring hundreds and producing what was then the costliest hailstorm in United States history. It remains the event every Tarrant County roofer and adjuster measures others against.

The downtown tornado, March 28, 2000

An F3 tornado struck downtown Fort Worth, blowing out windows in the Bank One Tower and damaging the Cultural District and Linwood. Commercial glass, roof and interior claims from that night defined large-loss appraisal practice in the county.

Hail seasons through the 2000s and 2010s

Storms building over Parker County reach Benbrook, Arlington Heights and the Cultural District first, then Haltom City and Keller. Carriers assign out-of-state catastrophe adjusters after large events, and partial scopes follow: a slope or two approved, no decking, no drip edge, gutters and screens omitted.

Winter Storm Uri, February 2021

Fort Worth's pier-and-beam housing in Fairmount, Ryan Place and the Near Southside burst lines across the city. Claims were scoped from photographs and paid short on drying, flooring and plaster.

The May 28, 2024 wind event

The same derecho that hit Dallas crossed Tarrant County with gusts near hurricane strength, stripping roofing and fences across north Fort Worth's newer subdivisions.

What this means for a Fort Worth claim today

Accepted, underpaid hail, wind and freeze claims are what the appraisal clause resolves. A Fort Worth insurance appraiser can be appointed to settle the amount of loss on any of these events. Roof disputes run through hail and roof claim appraisal, freeze losses through water damage appraisal, and the Alliance-area warehouse roofs through commercial property appraisal.
