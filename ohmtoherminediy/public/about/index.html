About Oh, Mother Mine DIY

We're a small team of mom makers sharing pretty, practical, budget-friendly DIY — tested at our own kitchen tables, with kids, pets and real life happening all around.

since
day one

How it started

Oh, Mother Mine DIY began the way so many good projects do: with a tired thrift-store dresser, a can of leftover paint and a very small helper who insisted on holding the brush. That dresser still lives in a kid's bedroom today — a little chippy, a lot loved.

Since then we've painted, glued, stitched and upcycled our way through countless projects, and we share the ones that actually work for busy families. You'll find furniture makeovers, budget home decor, seasonal wreaths and porches, kids' crafts, handmade gifts and simple organizing ideas.

What you can expect

Every tutorial lists the time, cost and difficulty up front, includes a supply checklist, and explains the steps in plain English. We include safety notes where they matter and suggest kid-friendly variations whenever we can.

We also built free DIY tools — a paint calculator, chalk paint mixer, budget planner, palette generator and project quiz — because we were tired of doing the math on the back of a receipt.

Have an idea or a question? Email us at info@ohmtoherminediy.com. We'd love to hear what you're making.

What we believe

Our DIY promises

Doable over perfect

Projects you can finish with normal tools, a normal budget and a normal amount of chaos at home.

Budget first

We look for the thrifted, upcycled and dollar-store route before the expensive one.

Safety matters

Lead-paint checks, low-temp glue guns, ventilation and kid-safe supplies — we always say when it matters.

Honest & tested

We share what works and what didn't, so you skip the mistakes we already made.
