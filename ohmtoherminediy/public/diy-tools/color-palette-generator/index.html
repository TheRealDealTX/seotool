    Which color harmony should I choose?

    • Analogous: neighbors on the color wheel — calm and cohesive, great for bedrooms.
    • Complementary: opposites — lively contrast, best used with lots of neutral.
    • Triadic: three evenly spaced hues — playful, perfect for kids' rooms.
    • Split complementary: a softer take on complementary.
    • Monochrome: tints and shades of one color — elegant and easy.

    The 60-30-10 rule

    Use your lightest shade on about 60% of the room (walls), a mid-tone on 30% (sofa, rug, curtains) and your boldest color on 10% (pillows, art, a painted accent piece). Screen colors differ from paint, so always test a sample on your wall. Read more in how to choose paint colors, then use the paint calculator to buy the right amount.

    FAQ

    How do I choose a color palette for my home?

    Start with one color you love — from a rug, a painting or a favorite mug — then build a palette around it and repeat the colors from room to room for flow.

    Can I match these hex codes to real paint?

    Most paint stores can color-match a hex code or a printed swatch. Always test a sample on your wall in morning and evening light.

    What is the 60-30-10 rule?

    A decorating guideline: 60% dominant color, 30% secondary color and 10% accent color for a balanced room.
