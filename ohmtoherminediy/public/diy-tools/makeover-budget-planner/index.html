    How to budget a DIY room makeover

    1. Set your number first. Decide what you're comfortable spending before you shop — then build the plan to fit it.
    2. Spend on what you touch. Put money into things you use daily (a comfy rug, good lighting) and DIY the decor.
    3. Add a buffer. Extra sandpaper, a second can of paint, the hardware that didn't fit — a 10% buffer covers the surprises.
    4. Shop secondhand first. Thrift stores and marketplaces are full of solid-wood furniture waiting for a chalk paint makeover.

    FAQ

    How much does a DIY living room makeover cost?

    A paint-and-accessories refresh can be done for a few hundred dollars, especially if you upcycle furniture instead of buying new.

    Where do the store-bought prices come from?

    They're typical planning numbers for comparable new items. Edit any row to match the prices you see locally.

    What should I DIY versus buy?

    DIY decor, paint, art and furniture makeovers; consider buying items where safety or comfort matters most, like mattresses and electrical fixtures.
