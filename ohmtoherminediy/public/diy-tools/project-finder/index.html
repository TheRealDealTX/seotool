FAQ

What's a good first DIY project for beginners?

A painted side table, a terracotta pot makeover or a simple wreath are all great first projects — low cost, quick to finish and hard to mess up.

Can I do these projects with kids?

Many of them, yes. Pick “With the kids” in the quiz to see projects that are safe and fun for little helpers.

Do I need special tools?

Most of our projects use basic supplies: paintbrushes, sandpaper, a glue gun and scissors. Each tutorial lists exactly what you need.
