Covered isn’t the same as paid

Why owners call a New York Insurance Appraiser instead of arguing alone

Most short claims aren’t denials. They’re estimates with a slope missing, a ceiling priced as patch-and-paint, a co-op wall assigned to the wrong policy, or an ice-dam leak written up as “maintenance.” New York Insurance Appraiser works the space between “we’ll cover it” and a check that actually rebuilds.

We start from our own inspection, never an edited copy of the carrier’s file, and we price what we can defend in front of an umpire. If your problem is a denial or a deadline, we’ll say so on the first call and point you to the right professional.

Before you demand appraisal

Three questions a New York Insurance Appraiser asks first

  1. Has the carrier accepted coverage?Appraisal values a covered loss. A denial is a coverage fight, and that usually means a New York attorney.
  2. Is the gap worth the cost?Each side pays its own appraiser and shares the umpire. Run the numbers in the box.
  3. Can you document it?Photos, measurements and a credible line-item estimate move awards. Opinions don’t.

Questions

Ask a New York Insurance Appraiser

Plain answers about the process, the cost and when appraisal is the wrong move.

Every FAQ Claim vocabulary
What does a New York Insurance Appraiser do?

A New York Insurance Appraiser values a property loss when you and your insurer agree the damage is covered but disagree on what it costs. Once appraisal is invoked we act as your appointed appraiser: inspect, build an independent estimate, work with the carrier’s appraiser and, if needed, present open items to the umpire.

Is appraisal the same as hiring a public adjuster?

No. A public adjuster, licensed by the Department of Financial Services, manages and negotiates the whole claim. An appraiser works inside the policy’s appraisal clause on one question: the amount of loss. We don’t handle coverage disputes or give legal advice.

When is it worth calling a New York Insurance Appraiser?

When coverage is accepted, you have the carrier’s estimate, and the gap to a credible repair estimate is meaningful. Small gaps are often closed faster with a documented supplement request.

Is the appraisal award binding?

On the amount of loss, generally yes, once two of the three panel members sign. That’s why documentation matters before you invoke appraisal.

What does appraisal cost?

Each side usually pays its own appraiser and splits the umpire. Our fee is agreed in writing before any work starts, and the first conversation is free.

Does New York Insurance Appraiser cover upstate as well as the city?

Yes. We work across all 62 counties, from the five boroughs and Long Island to the Capital Region, Central New York, the Finger Lakes, Western New York and the Southern Tier.
