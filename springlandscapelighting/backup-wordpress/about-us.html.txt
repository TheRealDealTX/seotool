About Spring Landscape Lighting
At
Spring Landscape Lighting
, we believe a home should feel just as welcoming, comfortable, and beautifully designed after sunset as it does during the day.
We provide custom landscape lighting design, installation, repair, and system upgrades for homeowners in Spring, Texas, and surrounding North Houston communities. Every lighting plan is created around the property itself—not pulled from a standard package or based on a fixed number of fixtures.
That distinction matters.
The best outdoor lighting does more than brighten a yard. It reveals architectural details, makes walkways easier to navigate, brings mature trees and landscaping to life, and creates outdoor spaces where people genuinely want to spend time.
Our goal is to achieve all of that without making the property feel overlit, harsh, or artificial.
Lighting Designed Around Your Home
No two properties in Spring are exactly alike.
Some homes feature deep front yards, large oak or pine trees, layered garden beds, and brick or stone elevations. Others center around pools, patios, outdoor kitchens, pergolas, or long driveways. Each property presents different opportunities, viewing angles, and practical lighting needs.
That is why our process begins with listening.
We take time to understand:
Which areas you use most often
What architectural or landscape features you want to highlight
Where additional visibility would be helpful
How you want the property to feel after dark
Whether you prefer subtle accents, a dramatic presentation, or a balanced combination of both
How lighting should be controlled throughout the evening
From there, we develop a site-specific lighting plan using appropriate fixture locations, beam spreads, brightness levels, color temperatures, zones, and controls.
The result is a system that looks like it belongs on your property.
A Thoughtful Approach to Outdoor Lighting
Installing more fixtures does not automatically create a better result.
In fact, excessive lighting can flatten architectural details, produce uncomfortable glare, distract from the landscaping, and make an otherwise attractive home feel commercial. Good lighting requires restraint.
We use a layered approach that places light where it adds purpose and atmosphere.
Architectural uplighting can reveal brick, stone, columns, and entry features. Path lighting can improve movement without creating rows of visible glare. Tree lighting can add height and depth. Soft illumination around patios and pools can make outdoor areas more inviting while preserving a relaxed evening environment.
Just as importantly, we consider where darkness should remain.
Those quieter areas create contrast, protect views of the night sky, and allow the highlighted parts of the property to stand out naturally.
Built for Spring, Texas Conditions
Outdoor lighting systems in Southeast Texas must perform through more than an occasional rainstorm.
Heat, humidity, heavy rainfall, irrigation, insects, soil movement, fast-growing plants, and severe weather can all affect fixtures, wiring, connections, and transformers. A system that looks good on installation day is not enough. It also needs to be installed with long-term performance in mind.
Our lighting plans account for local conditions from the beginning.
We use professional-grade low-voltage components, weather-conscious installation practices, secure electrical connections, carefully concealed wiring, and fixture placements that remain practical as the landscape grows and changes.
We also consider drainage, mowing patterns, sprinkler coverage, planting beds, tree roots, and other property conditions that can affect the system over time.
Our Services
Spring Landscape Lighting provides complete outdoor lighting solutions for both new and existing systems.
Custom Landscape Lighting Design
We create lighting plans tailored to your home’s architecture, landscaping, outdoor spaces, and nighttime goals. Each plan is developed to balance beauty, visibility, comfort, and energy efficiency.
Architectural Lighting
Carefully positioned uplights and wash lights can bring depth to brick walls, stonework, columns, rooflines, entryways, and other exterior features.
Our aim is to reveal the architecture—not overpower it.
Pathway and Driveway Lighting
Low-glare lighting along walks, steps, driveways, and transitions can make nighttime movement safer while creating a more polished arrival experience.
Fixture placement is planned to provide useful illumination without producing distracting rows of visible light sources.
Tree and Garden Lighting
Mature trees, ornamental plants, textured garden beds, and landscape structures can become some of the most memorable parts of a lighting design.
Depending on the property, we may use uplighting, downlighting, moonlighting, silhouetting, or soft accent lighting to create depth and visual interest.
Patio, Pool, and Outdoor Living Lighting
Outdoor kitchens, seating areas, pergolas, fire features, patios, and pool environments require lighting that is both functional and comfortable.
We design these areas so homeowners can cook, entertain, relax, and move safely without the space feeling excessively bright.
Smart Lighting Controls
Timers, photocells, astronomical controls, dimming systems, and app-based options can make a landscape lighting system easier to manage.
We can organize fixtures into separate zones so different parts of the property operate according to your schedule and preferences.
Repairs and System Upgrades
Existing landscape lighting systems often develop issues gradually.
Fixtures may become dim, wiring can be damaged, transformers may be undersized, lenses can become dirty, lamps may no longer match, and landscaping can grow around poorly placed fixtures.
We can evaluate the system, identify performance problems, and recommend practical repairs or upgrades. In some cases, a few targeted improvements are enough. In others, a redesign may provide a cleaner and more dependable result.
Clean, Careful Installation
A well-designed lighting plan can still fall short if the installation is rushed.
We approach each property carefully. Wiring is concealed whenever practical. Fixtures are placed with mowing, irrigation, landscaping, and future maintenance in mind. Planting beds and lawn areas are treated with respect, and work areas are left clean.
Fixture aiming is also a critical part of the installation.
A light positioned only a few inches in the wrong direction can create glare, miss an architectural feature, or illuminate a window. We aim and adjust each fixture based on the intended effect, viewing angle, and surrounding environment.
When appropriate, final adjustments are made after dark, when the true balance of the system can be evaluated.
