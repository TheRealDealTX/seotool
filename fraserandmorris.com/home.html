The studio

A garden designer who starts with the planting

Fraser & Morris designs gardens around plants. Layout, paving and structure matter, and we draw them carefully, but what makes a garden memorable is how it is planted: the layering, the colour, the way it changes through the year.

Whether it is a shaded courtyard in south London, a family garden in Highgate or a country garden in Oxfordshire, we design for the site, the soil and the people who will use it.

  • Planting plans drawn and set out by the designer
  • Gardens that look good in winter as well as June
  • Practical layouts for families, entertaining and working from home

Services

From first sketch to first flowering

We can take a garden from survey to planting, or help with one part of it. Every project starts with a conversation in the garden.

01

Garden design

A complete design: measured survey, concept, detailed layout, construction drawings and a planting plan, ready to build.

02

Planting design

Planting plans for new borders or a whole garden, with plants sourced, set out and placed by us on the day.

03

We tender the work to trusted landscapers, oversee the build and make sure the details are right.

Aftercare

Seasonal visits in the first years to edit, replant and guide the garden as it matures.

How it works

A clear process, start to finish

Designing a garden is a collaboration. Here is what happens between the first visit and the first summer.

1

Consultation

We visit, walk the garden with you and talk about how you want to use it.

2

Survey

A measured survey records levels, boundaries, trees, soil and aspect.

3

Concept

Sketch layouts and mood images explore ideas before anything is fixed.

4

Design

A detailed plan, drawings and planting plan, costed and ready to build.

5

Build & plant

We oversee the landscapers and set out every plant ourselves.

Planting through the year

Gardens designed for all four seasons

Choose a season to see how a Fraser & Morris planting plan keeps the garden interesting all year round.

Bulbs push through, blossom opens and fresh foliage unfurls. We plan for a long spring: snowdrops and hellebores, then tulips, alliums, blossom and the first perennials.

  • Tulips
  • Alliums
  • Crab apple blossom
  • Hellebores
  • Epimedium
  • Camassia

Borders reach their height. Roses, salvias, geraniums and grasses carry the garden from June into a late summer flush of dahlias and asters.

  • Roses
  • Salvias
  • Dahlias
  • Geranium 'Rozanne'
  • Verbena bonariensis
  • Agapanthus

Grasses turn to straw and copper, seedheads form and leaves colour. Autumn planting is often the most atmospheric of the year.

  • Miscanthus
  • Sanguisorba
  • Asters
  • Japanese maple
  • Persicaria
  • Malus fruit

Structure takes over. Clipped evergreens, bark, seedheads and winter-flowering shrubs keep a well-designed garden alive in the coldest months.

  • Yew & clipped shapes
  • Betula bark
  • Sarcococca
  • Hellebores
  • Grasses
  • Snowdrops

Questions

Frequently asked

What areas do you cover?

We design gardens across London, north and south, and further afield in Oxfordshire, the Cotswolds, Suffolk, Sussex and the Home Counties.

How does the garden design process work?

It starts with a consultation visit. We then produce a measured survey, a concept design for discussion, a final layout and a detailed planting plan. We can also manage the build with trusted landscapers and set out the planting ourselves.

How long does it take to design a garden?

A small garden can be designed in a few weeks. Larger gardens usually take two to three months from survey to final planting plan, depending on how quickly decisions are made.

Do you build the gardens you design?

We work with a small group of trusted landscape contractors who build our designs, and we oversee the work on site. We set out and place every plant ourselves.

Can you just help with planting?

Yes. Many of our projects are planting-only: new borders, replanting a tired garden or a seasonal planting plan for an existing layout.
