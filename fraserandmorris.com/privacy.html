Privacy notice

This notice explains how Fraser & Morris uses personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we receive the details you enter in the form: your name, email address, phone number if given, the location of your garden and your message. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to manage your project. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within twelve months. Project records are kept for as long as needed for our legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@fraserandmorris.com.
