What to send

Send what you have now. You can always upload more later under the same homeowner.

Most helpful

Photos & videos

Roof, elevations, interior damage, collateral (gutters, screens, AC fins, soft metals). Wide shots and close-ups both help. Date-stamped if possible.

Most helpful

Your estimate & measurements

Your scope or Xactimate estimate, EagleView or Hover report, and any invoices for emergency repairs or tarping.

Most helpful

Policy & declarations page

The dec page tells us coverage, deductible and endorsements. If the homeowner only has the policy number and carrier, that works to start.

If you have it

Carrier letters & estimates

The insurer's estimate, payment or denial letters, and any adjuster emails. These shape the supplement or dispute.

If you have it

Signed contract / LOR

A signed public adjuster contract or letter of representation if the homeowner has already signed with us.

If you have it

Notes

Deadlines, supplements you need, what the carrier said on site, or links to large videos. Anything you'd tell us on a call.

Can I upload more files for the same homeowner later?

Yes. Use the same homeowner name and loss address and the new files join the existing claim file.

What file types and sizes work?

Photos, videos, PDFs, spreadsheets and documents, up to 25 MB per file. For large videos, paste a share link in the notes.

Do I need an account?

No. Your contact details are remembered on your device for next time, but there is no login.

How do I know you got it?

The tool confirms on screen when the upload finishes and we are notified right away. If you don't hear back within one business day, text Joseph.
