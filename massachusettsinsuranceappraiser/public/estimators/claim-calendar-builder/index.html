Insurance Claim Deadline Calendar Builder

Claims are lost on dates as often as on dollars. Build a dated checklist from your date of loss and drop it straight into your phone’s calendar.

    Milestones worth tracking

    1. Notice — policies ask for prompt notice. Report as soon as you can; the target here is your own.
    2. Temporary repairs — tarps, water extraction and heat to prevent freezing protect the property and the claim.
    3. Proof of loss — if the carrier requests a sworn proof of loss, the clock often runs from that request.
    4. Depreciation recovery — replacement cost policies commonly require repairs within a set time to release holdback.
    5. Suit limitation — many Massachusetts policies limit lawsuits to two years. Confirm your wording and talk to an attorney well before it arrives.
    Not legal adviceDeadlines depend on your policy, your letters and the law. Appraisal does not automatically pause a suit-limitation clock.

    How the export works

    The download is a standard .ics file built in your browser. Open it on a phone or computer and each milestone becomes an all-day event. Nothing is sent to us. Next, check whether your file is ready with the appraisal file checkup.

    About this planner

    Is my date of loss saved anywhere?

    No. The calendar is created entirely in your browser and nothing is transmitted.

    Which date should I use after a multi-day nor’easter?

    Use the date the damage occurred if you know it; otherwise use the date in the carrier’s claim file and note the storm dates.

    Can I change the windows later?

    Yes. Edit any number and the list and download update immediately.
