Where to start

Most files start with an estimate review

Before anyone invokes appraisal, it helps to know exactly where the carrier’s estimate falls short and what that gap is worth. A review answers that in days, and many disputes close at that stage.

About estimate reviews
Can I hire you for just an estimate review?

Yes. Many owners start with a review and decide afterward whether to invoke appraisal.

Do you handle condo associations and commercial buildings?

Yes — single-family homes, condos and master policies, multifamily, retail, office, mixed-use, churches and other commercial property.

Do you only handle storm claims?

No. We value ice dam, wind, hurricane, water, fire, tree and other covered losses.

Is the carrier’s number short?

Send us the estimate and a few photos. We’ll review the gap and tell you what we see.
