### What Is Arrive Outdoors?

Arrive Outdoors (found at [ArriveOutdoors.com](https://arriveoutdoors.com)) is an online outdoor gear rental platform that partners with premium outdoor brands — think Marmot, Helinox, Therm-a-Rest, and Nemo — to make outdoor adventures more accessible.

Instead of buying expensive gear that you’ll use a few times a year (and then have to store), you can rent everything you need: tents, sleeping bags, pads, stoves, cookware, lanterns, [backpacks](https://arriveoutdoors.com/group/camphike/packs/), and even outerwear.

They deliver it right to your home, hotel, or even a FedEx location near your campground. When you’re done, you just pack everything back into the same box, slap on the prepaid label, and send it back.

It’s [camping](https://arriveoutdoors.com/edge-of-the-world-arizona/) made simple — and sustainable.

### How Arrive Outdoors Works

Arrive Outdoors works like a mix between REI Rentals and Airbnb for gear. The process is intuitive, even for someone renting for the first time.

Here’s how it went for me:

- I chose my gear online.

I selected the Camping Set for Two, which includes all the essentials: a Marmot tent, two Nemo sleeping bags, sleeping pads, headlamps, a lantern, a compact camp stove, and a cooler. You can also add optional extras like [chairs](https://arriveoutdoors.com/group/camphike/chairs/) or cooking kits.

- I picked my rental dates.

Arrive Outdoors makes scheduling easy — you just select your start and end dates and they handle the shipping to ensure it arrives before your trip begins.

- The gear arrived right at my doorstep.

My boxes showed up two days before my departure, perfectly on time. Everything was neatly packed, clean, and labeled. They even included instructions for setup and return.

- After the trip, I shipped it all back.

Once I got home, I packed everything into the same boxes, attached the pre-paid return labels, and dropped them off at FedEx. Done.

There’s no waiting in line at a gear shop, no worrying about storage space, and no clutter in my apartment when I get home.

When my Arrive Outdoors boxes arrived, I was impressed right away. The gear was spotless and organized — each piece was packed with care and labeled by brand and use.

I took the gear on a weekend trip to a lakeside campground about two hours from home. Setting up was surprisingly easy. The Marmot tent came with color-coded poles and clear instructions, and even though I hadn’t used that model before, it took me less than 10 minutes to pitch.

The Nemo sleeping bags were cozy and clean, and the Therm-a-Rest pads made a world of difference — I actually slept through the night. The lantern included in the set doubled as a power bank for my phone, which was a thoughtful touch I didn’t expect.

This alone makes the brand stand out among outdoor retailers.
