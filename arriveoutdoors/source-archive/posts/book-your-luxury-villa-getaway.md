## Choosing the Right Location on the Island

St. John may be small, but choosing the right area matters more than people expect.

Cruz Bay is ideal if you like walking to restaurants and grabbing a quick ferry without planning your whole day around it.

Coral Bay feels quieter and more residential, perfect for travelers who want mornings without traffic or crowds.

I stayed near the North Shore once, and every beach day felt effortless because we were minutes from Trunk Bay and Cinnamon Bay.

Think about how you want your days to flow before locking in a location.

## Practical Tips From Someone Who Learned the Hard Way

Not all villas are created equal, and I learned that lesson early.

Always confirm road access because some hillside homes require four-wheel drive.

Ask about backup power since island outages can happen during storms.

Check whether air conditioning is throughout the home or only in bedrooms, especially if you’re sensitive to humidity.

These small details make a big difference once you arrive.
