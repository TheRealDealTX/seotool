Frequently asked questions

What national park is best to visit in August?

On weather alone, Redwood National and State Parks scores highest in August (highs near 70°F, lows 56°F, mostly dry). Check each park's guide for road closures, crowds and permits.

Where is it warm in August?

Warmest highs this month: Death Valley (~108°F), Arches (~95°F), White Sands (~92°F).

Is August a good time to go camping?

Depends where. 26 of 34 parks we track have good or excellent camping weather in August. Watch overnight lows: Cloud Peak Wilderness averages 43°F, Rocky Mountain averages 46°F at night.
