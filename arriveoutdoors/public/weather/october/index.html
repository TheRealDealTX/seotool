Frequently asked questions

What national park is best to visit in October?

On weather alone, Joshua Tree scores highest in October (highs near 73°F, lows 58°F, mostly dry). Check each park's guide for road closures, crowds and permits.

Where is it warm in October?

Warmest highs this month: Death Valley (~87°F), Everglades (~85°F), White Sands (~76°F).

Is October a good time to go camping?

Depends where. 26 of 34 parks we track have good or excellent camping weather in October. Watch overnight lows: Cloud Peak Wilderness averages 24°F, Grand Mesa averages 26°F at night.
