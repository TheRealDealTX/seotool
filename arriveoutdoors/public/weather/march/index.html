Frequently asked questions

What national park is best to visit in March?

On weather alone, Big Bend scores highest in March (highs near 70°F, lows 48°F, mostly dry). Check each park's guide for road closures, crowds and permits.

Where is it warm in March?

Warmest highs this month: Death Valley (~77°F), Big Bend (~70°F), Great Smoky Mountains (~62°F).

Is March a good time to go camping?

Depends where. 6 of 20 parks we track have good or excellent camping weather in March. Watch overnight lows: Cloud Peak Wilderness averages 13°F, Rocky Mountain averages 15°F at night.
