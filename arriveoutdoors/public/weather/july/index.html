Frequently asked questions

What national park is best to visit in July?

On weather alone, Sequoia scores highest in July (highs near 78°F, lows 56°F, mostly dry). Check each park's guide for road closures, crowds and permits.

Where is it warm in July?

Warmest highs this month: Death Valley (~111°F), Arches (~98°F), Zion (~94°F).

Is July a good time to go camping?

Depends where. 15 of 20 parks we track have good or excellent camping weather in July. Watch overnight lows: Cloud Peak Wilderness averages 44°F, Mount Rainier averages 45°F at night.
