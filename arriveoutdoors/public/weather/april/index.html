Frequently asked questions

What national park is best to visit in April?

On weather alone, Joshua Tree scores highest in April (highs near 69°F, lows 51°F, mostly dry). Check each park's guide for road closures, crowds and permits.

Where is it warm in April?

Warmest highs this month: Death Valley (~86°F), Big Bend (~78°F), Arches (~70°F).

Is April a good time to go camping?

Depends where. 9 of 20 parks we track have good or excellent camping weather in April. Watch overnight lows: Cloud Peak Wilderness averages 18°F, Rocky Mountain averages 20°F at night.
