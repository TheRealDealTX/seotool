Frequently asked questions

What national park is best to visit in February?

On weather alone, Death Valley scores highest in February (highs near 70°F, lows 53°F, mostly dry). Check each park's guide for road closures, crowds and permits.

Where is it warm in February?

Warmest highs this month: Death Valley (~70°F), Big Bend (~63°F), Joshua Tree (~56°F).

Is February a good time to go camping?

Depends where. 4 of 20 parks we track have good or excellent camping weather in February. Watch overnight lows: Cloud Peak Wilderness averages 7°F, Grand Mesa averages 10°F at night.
