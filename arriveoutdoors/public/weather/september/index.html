Frequently asked questions

What national park is best to visit in September?

On weather alone, Sequoia scores highest in September (highs near 70°F, lows 49°F, mostly dry). Check each park's guide for road closures, crowds and permits.

Where is it warm in September?

Warmest highs this month: Death Valley (~100°F), Arches (~87°F), Zion (~84°F).

Is September a good time to go camping?

Depends where. 19 of 20 parks we track have good or excellent camping weather in September. Watch overnight lows: Cloud Peak Wilderness averages 37°F, Grand Mesa averages 40°F at night.
