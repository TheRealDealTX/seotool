Connected Renter FAQ

What is Connected Renter?

Connected Renter is a free resource hub for renters. We bring together guides, checklists and calculators for every stage of renting — finding an apartment, moving, setting up utilities, insurance, credit and more.

Is Connected Renter free to use?

Yes. Every Connected Renter guide and tool is free, with no sign-up. Our Moving Made Easy courses are optional and cost $20 each.

Who is Connected Renter for?

Anyone who rents: first-time renters, families relocating, students, roommates and anyone rebuilding credit or planning a move.

Does Connected Renter sell my information?

No. The tools run in your browser and checklists save only on your device. See our privacy policy for details.
