KEEPING IT SIMPLE

Your work stays yours.

Browser tools

Code, Markdown, JSON and text are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded or stored by this website. Reloading or leaving a tool clears its input. Download a copy to keep your work.

Website checks

The favicon and meta tag checkers send your submitted public URL to this website’s server. The server requests that page and, for favicon checks, its declared icon and manifest resources. Results are returned to you and are not saved by the application. A temporary hash of your network address is used to limit requests for up to two minutes.

Hosting and external sites

The hosting provider may retain standard access logs, including network addresses and requested URLs. Links to portfolio projects and social profiles lead to external websites with their own privacy policies. HTML previews can request external assets if your code references them.

Contact

Email links open your email provider. For privacy questions, contact jraymondautida@gmail.com.
