Good to know

How to use Contrast checker

Choose a text color and background color to calculate their contrast ratio. Compare the result with WCAG 2.x AA and AAA thresholds for normal and large text.

  1. Enter six-digit hex colors or use the text and background color pickers.
  2. Read the contrast ratio and the four AA/AAA results.
  3. Adjust or swap the colors until the pair meets the text-size threshold you need.

Common questions

What contrast ratio do I need?

For WCAG AA, normal text requires at least 4.5:1 and large text at least 3:1. AAA requires 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.

What counts as large text?

WCAG defines large text as at least 18pt (24px), or at least 14pt (about 18.7px) when bold.

Does this test a complete page for accessibility?

No. It tests a pair of opaque colors on a solid background. Page structure, interaction, focus behavior and other accessibility requirements need separate checks.
