FAQ

Common questions

Does this check JavaScript-generated metadata?

No. The checker reads the server-delivered HTML, the same first view most crawlers and social networks get. Tags injected only after JavaScript runs will not appear — which is worth fixing for reliable sharing previews.

How long should a title and meta description be?

Google truncates by pixel width rather than characters. Aim for titles under roughly 580 pixels (about 50–60 characters) and descriptions under roughly 920 pixels (about 140–160 characters). The previews show where your text would be cut.

Does a high score guarantee rankings?

No. The score covers on-page metadata and structure that you control. Rankings also depend on content quality, links, intent and many other signals. Use the report as a technical checklist.

Why might a page fail to load?

Some sites block automated requests, require login, or respond slowly. The checker only fetches public pages on standard ports and limits page size to keep checks fast and safe.
