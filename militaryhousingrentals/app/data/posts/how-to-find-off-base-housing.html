Not every family lives on base. Waitlists, family size, pets, or simply wanting more space can lead you to the off-base rental market. Finding the right home from hundreds or thousands of miles away, often on a tight PCS timeline, takes a smart approach.

Start With the Housing Service Office

Every installation has a housing office that helps with both on-base and off-base housing. They can tell you about waitlist times, neighborhoods that are popular with military families, typical commute times, and landlords with complaints on file. Contact them as soon as you have orders. Many installations also list rentals on the DoD’s official Automated Housing Referral Network (AHRN.com).

Know Your Budget

Look up your BAH rate first, then set a total monthly budget that includes utilities, internet, and renters insurance. Our guide BAH Explained breaks down how the allowance works.

Choose the Right Neighborhood

  • Commute: check drive times to the gate you will actually use, at rush hour.
  • Schools: if you have children, research schools by address, since assignment boundaries can split neighborhoods.
  • Safety: look at local crime maps and talk to people who live there.
  • Amenities: grocery stores, parks, gyms, and medical care.

Avoid Rental Scams

Military families are frequent scam targets because they often rent sight unseen. Watch out for:

  • Rent that is far below market for the area
  • A “landlord” who is out of the country and cannot show the home
  • Requests to pay by wire transfer, gift cards, or payment apps before you have a lease
  • Pressure to sign or pay immediately

Verify ownership through county property records, ask for a live video tour, and never send money before seeing a lease. The housing office can help you check a listing.

Ask the Right Questions

  • Does the lease include a military clause?
  • What utilities are included?
  • What is the pet policy and pet deposit?
  • Who handles maintenance, and how fast?
  • Is renters insurance required?

Remember that the SCRA lets you terminate a lease if you receive PCS or qualifying deployment orders. Read our SCRA lease guide before you sign.

Get Renters Insurance

Renters insurance is affordable and covers your belongings if there is a fire, theft, or water damage. Keep an inventory of your belongings on your phone or in a home inventory notebook. It also makes household goods claims easier after a PCS.

Make Moving In Easier

Off-base rentals do not always come with the extras that on-base homes do. Depending on the home, you may want window AC units, a washer and dryer if hookups are available but appliances are not, or a mesh Wi-Fi system for a bigger house. Ask the landlord what is included before you buy.
