At Military Housing Rentals, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website or submit a property for listing.

We may collect the following personal information when you interact with our site:

  • Full Name

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for purposes directly related to your property listing or general site use. This includes:

  • Confirming your property submission

We do not use your information for advertising, promotional outreach, or share it with third parties for unrelated purposes.

3. Payment Processing with Stripe

All payments made through Military Housing Rentals are processed securely via Stripe, a trusted third-party payment gateway.

When making a payment, your sensitive billing information is handled exclusively by Stripe and is subject to Stripe’s Privacy Policy.
We do not store or have access to your full payment details (e.g., card number, CVV).

We use appropriate security measures (including SSL encryption and secure form handling) to safeguard your data. However, please note that no method of online transmission or storage is 100% secure.
