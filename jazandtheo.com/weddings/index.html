Questions, answered

How far in advance should we book a wedding photographer?

Popular photographers often book 9 to 15 months ahead for spring and fall Saturdays. If your date is flexible, a Friday, Sunday or off-season date widens your options.

What's the best time of day for wedding photos in the desert?

The hour before sunset. Midday desert sun is harsh and hot; plan portraits for golden hour and use open shade or a first look for anything earlier.

Do we need a permit for photos at a park or national recreation area?

Often, yes — especially for weddings and commercial photography on public land. Check with the park, city or land manager well before your date.
