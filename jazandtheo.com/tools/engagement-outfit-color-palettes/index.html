Engagement Photo Outfit Color Palettes

Coordinated outfit colors for your setting.

The easiest way to look good together on camera is to coordinate, not match — and to choose colors that suit the place. Pick your setting and get a palette to build both outfits from.

Engagement outfit palette picker

Coordinated outfit colors for your setting.

Start with the landscape. Earth tones sing in the desert, jewel tones glow under winter lights, and soft neutrals look natural near water. One partner can take the lightest color in the palette and the other a deeper one, with the middle tones as accents.

Solid colors and subtle textures photograph better than busy prints and logos. Bring a second outfit if your session allows — one dressier, one relaxed — and make sure your shoes work for the terrain.

How it works

  • Choose a setting to see a five-color palette.
  • Hover or tap a swatch to see it larger and get a pairing suggestion.
  • Each palette comes with a styling tip for that setting.

Questions, answered

What colors look best in engagement photos?

Mid-tone and earthy colors, soft neutrals and jewel tones photograph well. Very bright neon colors and pure white in harsh sun are harder to expose and can reflect color onto skin.

Should couples match for engagement photos?

Coordinate instead: pick two or three colors from one palette and share them between you, so you look like you belong together without wearing the same thing.

What should we wear for desert engagement photos?

Warm neutrals — cream, sand, terracotta, rust and sage — with one flowing piece that catches the wind. Closed shoes help on rocky trails.
