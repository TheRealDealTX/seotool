Welcome

Real places, honest advice, and the light that makes it all glow

Jaz & Theo started as a photographers’ blog about weddings in the desert Southwest and the house projects in between. The journal carries that on.

You’ll find guides to venues and photo locations across West Texas, Arizona and Austin, plain-spoken planning help — what photography really costs, how to build a wedding-day timeline, when golden hour starts — and home projects like the peg rail shelf readers still ask us about.
