Questions, answered

What's the easiest DIY project for a beginner?

A peg rail. It's a single board, a row of drilled holes and a few screws into studs — and it solves the pile of coats by the door.

How do I find studs before hanging a shelf?

Use a stud finder, then confirm with a small nail or a magnet that finds the drywall screws. In most US homes studs sit 16 inches apart on center.

Is shiplap still in style?

Painted horizontal boards in an entry or mudroom remain a durable, practical choice. Narrow boards, a matte finish and restrained use keep it from feeling dated.
