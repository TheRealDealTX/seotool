Family formals shot list

Family photos go fastest with a written list and one helper who knows everyone. Tick the groups you want (your choices stay in this browser), delete the rest, then print it or screenshot it for your photographer. Plan roughly three minutes per group.

  • Couple with both sets of parents
  • Couple with Partner 1's parents
  • Couple with Partner 1's parents and siblings
  • Couple with Partner 1's grandparents
  • Couple with Partner 1's full immediate family
  • Couple with Partner 2's parents
  • Couple with Partner 2's parents and siblings
  • Couple with Partner 2's grandparents
  • Couple with Partner 2's full immediate family
  • Couple with all grandparents
  • Couple with both immediate families together
  • Extended family (aunts, uncles, cousins) — one big group each side
  • Couple with godparents or special mentors
  • Couple with the officiant
  • Couple with any children in the family
