How claims differ across the state

The same policy language plays out very differently in a mountain cabin, a Charlotte high-rise and a Nags Head cottage on pilings.

Asheville · Boone · Sylva

Mountains

Ice dams, heavy snow loads, falling hardwoods and, after Helene, wind-versus-flood questions on mountain homes and cabins.

Hickory · Morganton · Lenoir

Foothills

Strong thunderstorms, hail and ice storms where the Piedmont meets the Blue Ridge escarpment.

Charlotte · Triad · Triangle

Piedmont

The state’s hail and ice-storm belt, with dense tree canopy over older neighborhoods and fast-growing HOA suburbs.

Fayetteville · Southern Pines

Sandhills

Tornadoes, tropical remnants and long-leaf pines that snap in high wind, plus large rental and military housing stocks.

Greenville · Kinston · Lumberton

Coastal Plain

River flooding from slow hurricanes, farm structures and older homes on crawlspaces.

Wilmington · New Bern · Nags Head

Coast & Outer Banks

Named-storm deductibles, coastal pool policies, wind-driven rain and homes on pilings.
