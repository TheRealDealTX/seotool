504 loans finance owner-occupied buildings and long-life equipment. A bank lends about 50%, a Certified Development Company (CDC) lends about 40% backed by the SBA, and you put in about 10%.

Your real rate is a blend

Only the CDC portion carries the 504 rate. On a $2 million building with a bank first mortgage at, say, 7.5% on 50% and the 25-year 504 rate on 40%, your blended rate on the borrowed money is about 7.26%. Ask the bank and the CDC for both rates before comparing a 504 loan with a 7(a) or conventional loan.

Questions

What is the SBA 504 rate today?

The October 2026 pricing set effective fixed rates of 6.59% for 10-year, 6.97% for 20-year and 6.97% for 25-year debentures. Manufacturing, food supply chain and rural projects priced slightly lower, at 6.74% for 25 years.

Is the 504 rate fixed?

Yes. The CDC/SBA portion is fixed for the whole term once the debenture sells. The bank's first mortgage is priced separately and may be fixed or variable.

When are 504 rates set?

Once a month, at the SBA's debenture sale. The next pricing date is November 5, 2026. Your rate locks at the sale your loan is included in, not when you're approved.
