Why owners call us

Covered isn’t the same as paid in full

Most short claims aren’t denials. They’re missing parapet flashing, a stucco wall priced as a patch, a roof measured two squares small or a cooler nobody looked at. New Mexico Insurance Appraiser exists for the gap between what the insurer agreed to cover and what the repair actually costs.

As a New Mexico Insurance Appraiser, we work for the policyholder inside the appraisal process. We don’t argue coverage and we don’t inflate numbers — we document the loss well enough that the panel can see it.

  • An independent scopeOur own inspection and measurements before we read anyone else’s estimate.
  • Built for the umpirePricing and photos that hold up when a neutral reviews them, not just on day one.
  • Straight triageIf the claim needs an attorney or a supplement instead of appraisal, you’ll hear it on the first call.

How it works

From first call to signed award

The appraisal clause turns an open-ended argument into a defined process. Here is how New Mexico Insurance Appraiser moves a file through it.

  1. 01

    Free review

    We read the policy, the insurer’s estimate and your photos, then tell you honestly whether appraisal fits.

  2. 02

    Inspect & measure

    Our own site visit, measurements and photos — never an edited copy of the carrier’s scope.

  3. 03

    Price it locally

    A line-item estimate priced for your part of New Mexico, with code items and overhead handled the way your policy reads.

  4. 04

    Work the panel

    We sit down with the insurer’s appraiser line by line. Many differences close at this stage.

  5. 05

    Award

    Anything left goes to the umpire. Two signatures set the amount of loss.

FAQ

Questions for a New Mexico Insurance Appraiser

Straight answers about how appraisal works, what it costs and when it’s the wrong move.

All questions Claim terms A–Z
What does a New Mexico Insurance Appraiser do?

We value property damage when you and your insurer agree the loss is covered but disagree about the cost. As your appointed appraiser we inspect, build an independent line-item estimate, work with the insurer’s appraiser and, if needed, present unresolved items to an umpire.

Is appraisal the same as hiring a public adjuster?

No. A public adjuster handles the whole claim. An appraiser works inside the policy’s appraisal clause on one question: the amount of loss. We don’t negotiate outside appraisal and we don’t give legal advice.

When does it make sense to call?

When coverage has been accepted, you have the insurer’s itemized estimate, and the gap to a contractor’s number is meaningful — often more than about ten percent or several thousand dollars.

Is the award binding?

On the amount of loss, generally yes once two of the three panel members sign. Coverage questions are separate and may need a New Mexico attorney.

What does appraisal cost?

Each side usually pays its own appraiser and splits the umpire. Our fee is put in writing before any work starts, and the first conversation is free.

Do you work outside Albuquerque?

Yes. We work statewide in all 33 counties, including Las Cruces, Santa Fe, the eastern plains, the Four Corners and the mountain communities.
