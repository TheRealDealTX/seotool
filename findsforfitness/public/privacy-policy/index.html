Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

This policy explains what information findsforfitness.com (“we”) collects and how we use it.

Information you give us

If you use our contact form or newsletter sign-up, we receive your name (if given), email address and message. We use it only to reply to you or send the newsletter you asked for, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Calculators and tools

Our tools run in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us. The water tracker saves your glasses count in your browser's local storage on your device only.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and performance. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only when required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing info@findsforfitness.com.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
