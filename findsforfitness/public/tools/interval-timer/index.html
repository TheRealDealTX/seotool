Interval Timer

Free online interval timer for HIIT, Tabata and circuit workouts. Set work, rest and rounds, get audio beeps, and follow a big progress ring from any phone.

How to use the interval timer

Choose a preset or set your own work time, rest time and number of rounds, then press Start. The ring turns lime during work and blue during rest, and you'll hear a short beep at each change plus a countdown in the last three seconds. Keep your phone screen on during the workout.

  • HIIT vs steady-state cardio — when intervals make sense.
Tip: Classic Tabata (20 seconds hard, 10 seconds rest, eight rounds) only works at a genuinely high effort. If you're new to exercise, start with 30/30 and build up.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Tabata workout?

Eight rounds of 20 seconds of very hard work followed by 10 seconds of rest, four minutes in total.

How long should a HIIT workout be?

For most people 10 to 25 minutes of intervals, plus a warm-up and cooldown, is plenty. Two or three sessions a week is a good limit.

Does the timer work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in your mobile browser. Tap Start once so the browser allows sound, and keep the screen awake.
