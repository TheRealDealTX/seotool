# Bexar County storm facts (NOAA NCEI Storm Events Database, downloaded 2026-10-19)

Source: https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/stormevents/ — county + forecast-zone entries for Bexar County, TX.
Full list with event links: `storm-facts-raw.txt` (and `site/data/storm-history.json`).

* 1,525 tracked events 1953–2026: 618 hail, 420 flash flood, 45 flood, 309 thunderstorm wind, 71 tornado, winter storms/ice.
* Hail: 618 reports since 1957; 203 were 1.75" (golf ball) or larger. Largest: **5.5" on May 3, 2021** near Sayers / China Grove, east San Antonio (event 961040); 4.5" same day near I-410 & Paula Dr (961017).
* Hail by month (count of reports): Jan 3, Feb 44, Mar 117, **Apr 225, May 160**, Jun 30, Jul 6, Aug 4, Sep 8, Oct 9, Nov 5, Dec 7 → peak season March–May, peak April.
* Hail reports per year: 2016: 63, 2021: 45, 2023: 45, 2025: 34, 2020: 30, 2024: 30.
* **April 12, 2016**: giant hail across northwest San Antonio — 4.5" near Tezel Rd & Bandera Rd (623870, "tied for the largest hailstone ever reported in Bexar County" at the time), 4" near Bandera Rd & Loop 1604 (623868), 4" Kirby (623883), 3.5" Leon Valley (623878), 3" Olmos Park (623885).
* **May 6, 2001**: "one of the most devastating hail-and-wind storms in the history of Bexar County", hail up to 4" (5243267).
* April 13, 2019: 3" hail Leon Springs / Grey Forest (815178, 815169). May 26, 2025: 3" hail Helotes (1264523).
* Wind: 309 thunderstorm-wind reports. **May 27, 1997** downburst estimated >100 mph across southwest San Antonio near Kelly AFB; roofs, windows, power lines; >100,000 lost power (5599060).
* Tornadoes: 71 reports since 1953, mostly weak (F0/F1/EF0/EF1). March 19, 2002: six tornadoes near Von Ormy/Somerset, strongest F2 (5285553).
* Flooding: San Antonio is in "Flash Flood Alley" (Balcones Escarpment). 420 flash-flood reports. October 1998 and other major floods — see raw file "Flood events" section for event links/text.
* Winter: **Winter Storm Uri, Feb 13–16, 2021** — wind chills to -5 °F (943791), ice (943615, 943758). Ice storms Feb 3, 2022 (1007523), Jan 20, 2025 (1232249), Jan 24, 2026 (1314172, wind chill 9 °F at SAT 1314436).
* Tropical: Hurricane Harvey remnants, Aug 26, 2017 (720275).
