Projects

How a project comes together.

From a coffee bar's seasonal drinks to a full coloring book — the kinds of projects we take on, and exactly how each one runs.

Projects we love to take on

Scoped to fit independent businesses and big brands alike.

Seasonal drink launch

A family of drink illustrations for a café's new seasonal menu: board art, cup sleeve, postcards and social posts, all delivered for launch day.

Beauty product series

A set of product portraits for a cosmetics launch, each paired with a short personality line for social and packaging inserts.

Illustrated menu

Cover, section headers and item icons for a bakery or restaurant, supplied in layers so prices and items can change without redrawing.

Custom coloring book

A 12–24 page book for a hotel, restaurant or brand, printed as a kids' menu, welcome gift or merch item.

Campaign key art

One hero illustration adapted for print, out-of-home, web banners and social, with a palette built from the product.

Styled shoot

Concept, props and on-set direction for a catalog or editorial shoot with illustrated set pieces.

Anatomy of a seasonal latte launch

A typical café commission, start to finish, in about three weeks.

  1. Taste & brief

    We visit, taste the new drinks, photograph references and agree where the art will appear.

  2. Roughs

    Pencil roughs of each drink in two layouts: a hero board and a set of matching spots.

  3. Color

    A palette pulled from the ingredients — spice, citrus, matcha — applied across the set.

  4. Production

    Board, sleeve, postcard and social files delivered, plus a short guide for staff posting them.

Working together

What to expect

  • A written quote with usage rights spelled out before any drawing starts.
  • Two rounds of revisions at the rough stage and one at final.
  • Layered source files and print-ready exports for every agreed placement.
  • Clear timelines — and we tell you early if anything moves.
