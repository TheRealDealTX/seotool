Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Hutto Roofers respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting https://huttoroofs.com/. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Submit a contact or estimate request form
  • Call or email us
  • Request information about roofing services
  • Communicate with us regarding a roofing project or service inquiry

The information you provide may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address or service location
  • Details about your roofing needs
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We ask that you avoid submitting sensitive personal information through our website unless it is necessary for your service request.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically through cookies, analytics tools, server logs, and similar technologies.

This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the website
  • Referring website
  • General geographic location
  • Date and time of visits

This information helps us understand how visitors use our website and identify opportunities to improve its performance and usability.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to roofing inquiries
  • Provide estimates or requested information
  • Schedule inspections or consultations
  • Communicate about roofing services
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Understand website traffic and visitor behavior
  • Maintain website security
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Maintain business and service records

We do not sell your personal information for payment.

Contact Forms and Service Requests

When you submit information through a contact form or request an estimate from Hutto Roofers, the information may be used to respond to your request and communicate with you regarding roofing services.

Submitting a form does not obligate you to purchase any service.

We may contact you using the phone number or email address you provide in connection with your request.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Hutto Roofers may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality and better understand how visitors interact with the site.

Cookies are small files stored on your device that may help a website remember certain information.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Website functionality
  • Traffic measurement
  • Performance monitoring
  • Visitor analytics
  • Security
  • Remembering user preferences

You can usually control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some website features function.

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services to better understand website traffic, visitor behavior, and website performance.

These services may collect information such as your device type, browser, approximate location, pages viewed, and interaction with the website.

Analytics information is generally used to improve the website and understand which content or services are most useful to visitors.

How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted third parties when reasonably necessary to operate our website or provide services.

These parties may include:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Email service providers
  • Website maintenance providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Customer management or communication platforms
  • Contractors or service providers assisting with business operations

We may also disclose information when required to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or legal processes
  • Respond to lawful government requests
  • Protect our rights or property
  • Investigate suspected fraud or misuse
  • Protect the safety of our customers, visitors, or others

We do not authorize service providers to use personal information for purposes unrelated to the services they provide to us.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, or services.

Hutto Roofers does not control the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. If you follow a link to another website, that site’s privacy policy will govern your use of that website.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to help protect information submitted through our website.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted through our website.

Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Provide requested services
  • Maintain business records
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce agreements
  • Meet legal or regulatory requirements

Information that is no longer reasonably needed may be deleted, anonymized, or securely stored in accordance with our business practices.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we:

  • Update inaccurate contact information
  • Correct information you previously provided
  • Delete certain personal information, where applicable
  • Stop sending non-essential communications

Some information may need to be retained when required for legitimate business, legal, accounting, or recordkeeping purposes.

Email Communications

If you communicate with us by email, we may retain your email and related correspondence as necessary to respond to your request and maintain business records.

If we send optional marketing communications, you may request that we stop sending them at any time.

Service-related communications regarding an active inquiry, estimate, appointment, or project may still be sent when necessary.

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we may take reasonable steps to remove it.

Do Not Track Signals

Some web browsers provide a “Do Not Track” setting.

Because there is not currently a universally accepted standard for responding to these signals, our website may not respond to all browser-based Do Not Track requests.

Visitors may still control cookies and similar technologies through their browser settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the revised Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
