About Christina Dittman Creations

Every celebration deserves something personal. Something that feels like it was made just for that moment. That’s exactly where Christina Dittman Creations began.

Christina DittmanArtist · Belton, Texas

How it started

What started as a love for art, lettering and meaningful details grew into a passion for creating custom hand-painted banners that turn ordinary events into unforgettable experiences. Whether it’s a wedding, birthday, baby shower, engagement, graduation, church service or a once-in-a-lifetime celebration, each banner is designed to reflect your story.

No templates. No shortcuts. Just thoughtful, handcrafted work.

Today the studio is better known around Bell County simply as Belton Banners - the place in Belton, Texas where a birthday banner, a church verse or a wedding welcome sign gets painted by hand.

The heart behind the craft

The small details matter

A banner isn’t just decoration. It’s the backdrop to your photos. The focal point of your event. The piece people remember long after the celebration ends. That’s why every banner is:

✦

Hand-painted with care

Every letter and illustration is painted by hand. Visible brushwork is part of the charm.

✦

Designed for your occasion

No templates. The banner is drawn around your story, your colors and your theme.

✦

Attention to color, style and theme

Palettes are matched to invitations, decorations or the room where the banner will hang.

✦

Personal, not mass-produced

Each order is treated like it’s for a close friend or family member - because that level of care shows.

Custom made, start to finish

Every banner begins with your vision. From the wording and colors to the overall style, each detail is carefully planned and brought to life by hand. Whether you want something elegant and minimal or bold and eye-catching, the goal is always the same: to create a piece that feels uniquely yours.

You’re not just ordering a banner - you’re collaborating on something meaningful.
