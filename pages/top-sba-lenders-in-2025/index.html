Top SBA lenders by loan volume

The biggest SBA lenders fall into two groups: a few that make very large loans nationwide, and high-volume banks that make many smaller ones.

Largest by dollars: Live Oak Bank

Live Oak Bank in Wilmington, North Carolina, was the largest SBA 7(a) lender by dollar volume in fiscal year 2025, with 2,280 approvals totaling more than $2.8 billion. It lends nationwide in a set of specialty industries, so its average loan is over $1 million. Live Oak profile

Largest by number of loans: high-volume banks

Huntington National Bank has long been one of the top SBA lenders by number of loans, mostly under $500,000 and concentrated in its Midwest footprint. Branchless lenders such as Newtek Bank also make thousands of SBA loans a year nationwide. Huntington profile · Newtek profile

Why lender volume matters

High-volume lenders are almost always SBA Preferred Lenders, so they approve loans without sending them to the SBA first. They also know which deals the SBA will accept. A community bank can still win on rate or service, so get a quote from both kinds.
