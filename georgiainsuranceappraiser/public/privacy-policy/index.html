Privacy Policy

How Georgia Insurance Appraiser handles privacy matters.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

What we collect

When you submit a form, call or email us, we collect the information you choose to provide — such as your name, phone number, email address, property location and details about your claim. Our web server also records standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and performance.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and evaluate your claim.
  • To provide services you request.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it only with people who need it to help with your claim (with your permission), with service providers who host our site and email, or when required by law.

Claim tools

Our calculators and checklists run in your browser. The numbers you enter aren’t sent to us or stored on our servers.

Cookies

This site doesn’t use advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log standard request data under Google’s privacy policy.

Text messages

If you agree to be contacted by text, message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you’ve given us by emailing info@georgiainsuranceappraiser.com.
