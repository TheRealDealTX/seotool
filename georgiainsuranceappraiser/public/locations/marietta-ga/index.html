Cobb County’s hail reputation

Ask a roofer about Marietta and you’ll hear about hail. Spring storms building along the Kennesaw Mountain ridge often drop large stones across Marietta, East Cobb, Kennesaw and Smyrna, and many roofs in these neighborhoods have been through more than one claim. That history gives carriers room to argue about prior damage, matching and repairability — the kinds of disputes appraisal is built to settle.

East Cobb’s 1980s and 1990s subdivisions present a specific matching problem: original architectural shingles from that era are long discontinued, and patching a few slopes with modern products leaves an obvious mismatch. Near the Marietta Square, historic homes on Church Street and Whitlock Avenue bring their own pricing challenges.

Common Marietta disputes

  • Partial roof approvals on discontinued shingle lines.
  • Siding damage with no matching profile available.
  • Prior-claim and “wear and tear” arguments on repeat hail roofs.
  • Water losses in finished basements common across Cobb County.
  • HOA restrictions on materials and colors that affect the repair scope.

Neighborhoods we serve

Questions from Marietta property owners

My HOA requires a specific shingle color. Does insurance pay for that?

HOA rules aren’t building codes, but they can support a matching argument when a patch would violate the association’s standards. We include that in the documentation.

We had a hail claim three years ago. Can we still file?

Yes, for new damage from a new storm. Clear documentation of the new date of loss — and of the prior repairs — is essential.
