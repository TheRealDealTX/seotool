What we do

Insurance Appraisal Services for Georgia Property Claims

From a single estimate review to full panel representation and neutral umpire work, every Georgia Insurance Appraiser service is built around an accurate, defensible valuation of your loss.

Call (844) 537-1427Free claim review
Service 01

Appraisal Clause Representation

We serve as your appointed appraiser once either side invokes the policy’s appraisal provision.

Service 02

Homeowner Claim Appraisal

Single-family, townhome and condo unit claims where the carrier’s estimate leaves out real repair costs.

Service 03

Commercial Claim Appraisal

Retail, office, multifamily, church, warehouse and HOA losses with larger scopes and higher stakes.

Service 04

Hail & Roof Claim Appraisal

Hail-bruised shingles, metal panels and soft metals — repair versus full replacement disputes.

Service 05

Wind & Tornado Damage Appraisal

Straight-line winds, tornado paths and severe thunderstorm losses, from lifted shingles to structural damage.

Service 06

Hurricane & Tropical Storm Appraisal

Named-storm losses on the coast and inland — wind-driven rain, roof failure and tree impacts.

Service 07

Water & Leak Damage Appraisal

Sudden pipe bursts, appliance failures and storm water intrusion — drying, tear-out and rebuild.

Service 08

Fire & Smoke Claim Appraisal

Kitchen fires to total losses — structure, smoke and soot cleaning, odor, and contents.

Service 09

Fallen Tree Damage Appraisal

Pines and hardwoods on roofs, decks and vehicles — removal, structural repair and debris limits.

Service 10

Insurance Estimate Review

A line-by-line audit of the carrier’s Xactimate or Symbility estimate before you decide on appraisal.

Service 11

Umpire & Neutral Services

Neutral umpire service for appraisal panels that reach an impasse, plus guidance on umpire selection.

Not sure where to start?

Most claims begin with an estimate review

Before anyone invokes appraisal, it helps to know exactly where the carrier’s estimate falls short and what that gap is worth. A review gives you that answer in days, and many disputes close at that stage.

About estimate reviews
Can I hire you for just one part of the process?

Yes. Many clients start with an estimate review and decide later whether to invoke appraisal.

Do you work on both residential and commercial claims?

Yes — houses, condos, multifamily, retail, office, industrial, churches and HOA properties.

Do you only work on hail claims?

No. We handle wind, hurricane, water, fire, tree and other covered perils.

Have a claim that doesn’t add up?

Send us the carrier’s estimate. We’ll review it and tell you what we see.
