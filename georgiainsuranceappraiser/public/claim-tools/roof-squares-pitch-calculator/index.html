Roof Squares and Pitch Calculator for Insurance Claims

Measurement errors are one of the most common reasons roof claims come up short. Use your home’s footprint and pitch to estimate true roof area, squares and bundles.

How the math works

A roof’s surface is larger than the footprint beneath it. The steeper the pitch, the bigger the difference. The pitch multiplier is √(1 + (rise ÷ 12)²): a 6/12 roof is about 11.8% larger than its footprint; a 12/12 roof is about 41.4% larger.

Checking your estimate

  • Find the roof section of the carrier’s estimate and note total squares and the waste percentage.
  • Compare those figures with the result here. Differences over 5–10% deserve a closer look.
  • Check for steep charges (often 7/12 and up) and high-roof charges for two-story sections.
  • Confirm ridge cap, starter, drip edge and valley materials are listed separately.

Limits of a footprint calculation

Complex roofs with multiple pitches, dormers and valleys need measuring by plane. Aerial measurement reports or a field measurement will always beat a footprint estimate — use this as a sanity check.

Questions about this tool

What’s a ‘square’?

100 square feet of roof surface. A typical Georgia two-story home might have 25 to 40 squares.

Why does waste change by roof type?

Hips, valleys and dormers require more cutting, so more material ends up as scrap.
