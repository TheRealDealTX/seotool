# Dallas County storm facts (NOAA NCEI Storm Events Database, downloaded 2026-10-20)

Source: https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/stormevents/ — county + forecast-zone entries for Dallas County, TX.
Full list with event links: `site/data/storm-history.json` (event URL = https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=<number>).
Note: NCEI "locations" are reference points (e.g. "Gribble", "Sowers", "Bouchard"); use the city named in the event text.

* 2,142 tracked events 1952–2026: **841 hail**, **708 thunderstorm wind**, **305 flash flood**, 22 flood, **109 tornado**, 42 lightning, plus winter storm / ice / snow / extreme cold entries.
* Hail: 841 reports since 1955; **250 were 1.75" (golf ball) or larger**, 46 were 2.75" (baseball) or larger.
* Largest hail: 4.5" on April 12, 1986 (county-wide entry, id 10144200); **4.25" grapefruit-size hail in Irving on May 24, 2011** — windows broken, damage at the TPC Four Seasons Las Colinas during the Byron Nelson (306259); same day 2.75" baseball hail at Skillman St & Southwestern Blvd (306783) and golf-ball hail at Hwy 183 & Mockingbird that damaged 8 Southwest planes at Love Field (306271). **4" hail near Cedar Hill on June 12, 2023** (1105878; 2.75" near Cedar Hill State Park 1105875). 3.75" hail in Garland, Feb 25, 2000 (5129560). 3" hail at Carrollton & Coppell, June 6, 2018 (766971, 766939). 3" hail near Dallas Baptist University, April 25, 2026 — car windows blown out (1326034).
* Hail by month (reports): Jan 10, Feb 15, Mar 105, **Apr 241, May 197, Jun 123**, Jul 24, Aug 16, Sep 31, Oct 61, Nov 15, Dec 3 → main season March–June, peak April–May, with a secondary bump in October.
* Busiest hail years: 2012 (53), 2011 (52), 2023 (51), 1994 (41), 1996 (39), 2021 (29), 2026 (28 so far), 2024 (28).
* **April 3, 2012** outbreak: 17 Dallas County reports — tornadoes in Mesquite, Coppell, west Irving, DeSoto (EF2 crossing from Ellis County, 377596), hail up to 3" in Coppell (368108).
* Wind: 708 thunderstorm-wind reports. Highest measured: **115 mph gust at Addison Airport, June 14, 1996** (5556565). **May 28, 2024** derecho-type event: NWS survey found winds up to 95 mph in Garland (1186742) and up to 90 mph in Richardson (1186739); 75 mph gust at Dallas Love Field (1186842); roof torn from a commercial building in Carrollton (1186866); partial roof collapse in Pleasant Grove (1187353); 71 mph Irving (1186771).
* Tornadoes: 109 reports since 1952. **Dec 26, 2015 — EF4 Garland/Rowlett tornado** (606471), ~13 mi long, nearly 10 mi in Dallas County, crossed I-30 at the President George Bush Turnpike; EF3 Glenn Heights/DeSoto tornado the same night (606463). **Oct 20, 2019 — EF3 tornado in northwest/north Dallas** from SH-348 & Luna Rd across I-35E and Harry Hines Blvd (856409), plus EF2 in Garland (856477) and EF1 in Rowlett (856502). April 25, 1994 — F4 Lancaster tornado, 223 homes destroyed (10351936). May 16, 2021 — EF0 Highland Park / EF1 University Park-area tornadoes (961151, 961160). March 16, 2023 — EF1 tornadoes in Irving and Grand Prairie (1087760, 1087759). March 4, 2025 — EF1 in Irving (1248200).
* Flooding: 305 flash-flood reports. **Aug 21–22, 2022 flash flood**: upwards of 15" of rain in parts of Dallas (1053557); water rescues in Balch Springs (1053541, 1053520), 10"+ in a few hours in Mesquite with I-635 at Scyene closed (1053519), 2 ft of water entered an east Dallas apartment complex (1053496), 42+ roads with high water reported by Dallas police (1053524). Other creeks: White Rock Creek, Turtle Creek, Five Mile/Ten Mile creeks, Rowlett Creek; Trinity River levee system downtown.
* Winter: **Winter Storm Uri, Feb 13–19, 2021** — 2–5" of snow Feb 14–15 then a second round of snow/ice/sleet (944869); lows in the single digits Feb 15–16, wind chills 0 to −15 °F Feb 14–16 (942630). Ice storm Feb 1, 2023 (1083186); winter storms Feb 2022 (1008262, 1009200), Jan 9, 2025 (1231214), Jan 24, 2026 (1313571, extreme cold 1314937). Snow March 4–5, 2015; ice storm Feb 1, 2011 (283045).
* No tropical-storm entries in the Dallas County record; tropical remnants mainly bring heavy rain.
