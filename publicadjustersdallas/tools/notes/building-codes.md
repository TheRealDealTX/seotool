## IRC reroofing amendments (Ch. 57, 23-0471)
* R908.1 amended: materials/methods for recovering or replacing a roof covering must comply with Chapter 9; individual replacement shingles must comply with R902.1. Exceptions: reroofing need not meet the 1/4:12 minimum slope (R905) where roofs provide positive drainage; secondary (emergency overflow) drains/scuppers not required on reroof with positive drainage.
* R908.3.1 roof recover conditions amended.

## Permits (Chapter 52, as of 3-27-24)
Source: https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/buildinginspection/DCH%20documents/Chapter%2052%20as%20of%203-27-24.pdf
* 301.2.1 No permit required for (building), among others: finish work (painting, papering, paneling, floor coverings, cabinets, moldings, countertops); storm windows, screens, shutters, rain gutters, insulation; trim or siding on single-family/duplex; interior non-load-bearing remodeling of single-family/duplex that adds no floor area; **"Reroofing if the work does not exceed the equivalent of two roofing squares or less."** (roofing square = 100 sq ft).
* 301.4.6.3 Reroofing permits: "New roof coverings must not be applied without first obtaining a permit." Application must list disposal sites for reroofing debris; final inspection and approval required when complete; no final until proof of debris disposal at a City of Dallas landfill or transfer station is submitted. Building official may require special inspections (301.4.6.3.1).
* Subchapter 11: contractors (incl. "Roof contractor", residential general contractor) must hold a valid City registration (1102.1); homeowner exemption/homeowner registration for work on own homestead (1102.1.1).
