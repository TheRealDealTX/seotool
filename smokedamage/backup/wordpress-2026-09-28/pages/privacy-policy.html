Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

SmokeDamage.com is operated by Rise Public Adjusting LLC (“Smoke Damage Public Adjuster,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit SmokeDamage.com, contact us, submit information through our website, call us, email us, or otherwise interact with our services.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request information, submit a website form, schedule a consultation, or communicate with our team.

Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Property address
  • City, ZIP code, or general location
  • Property type
  • Date of loss
  • Insurance company
  • Insurance claim status
  • Claim number, if voluntarily provided
  • Description of smoke, fire, soot, odor, or related property damage
  • Information about your insurance claim
  • Documents or photographs you choose to provide
  • Communications you have with our staff
  • Any other information you voluntarily submit

Please do not provide sensitive information that is not reasonably necessary for us to evaluate or respond to your inquiry.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit SmokeDamage.com, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our website, hosting provider, analytics services, or similar technologies.

This information may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Referring website or source
  • Approximate geographic location
  • Date and time of visits
  • Links or buttons clicked
  • Website performance information

This information can help us understand how visitors use the website, identify technical issues, improve website performance, and evaluate our marketing efforts.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through SmokeDamage.com to:

  • Respond to questions and inquiries
  • Contact you regarding a potential insurance claim
  • Evaluate whether our public adjusting services may be appropriate for your situation
  • Schedule consultations
  • Communicate about smoke damage, fire damage, and property insurance claims
  • Provide requested information
  • Maintain records of communications
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising and marketing campaigns
  • Detect or prevent fraudulent, abusive, or unlawful activity
  • Protect our legal rights and business interests
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

Submitting information through our website does not automatically create a client relationship with Rise Public Adjusting LLC.

4. Calls, Emails, and Communications

If you provide your telephone number or email address, we may use that information to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you regarding the services you requested or expressed interest in.

You may contact us at any time if you no longer wish to receive non-essential communications.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SmokeDamage.com may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, understand website activity, improve performance, and measure marketing effectiveness.

Cookies are small data files that websites may store on your browser or device.

Depending on the tools implemented on the website, cookies may be used for:

  • Website functionality
  • Traffic measurement
  • Analytics
  • Advertising attribution
  • Performance monitoring
  • Security
  • User preferences

You can usually adjust your browser settings to block, restrict, or delete cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect how some website features function.

6. Analytics

We may use website analytics tools to better understand how visitors interact with SmokeDamage.com.

Analytics providers may collect information such as your device type, browser, approximate location, pages viewed, referral source, and how you navigate the website.

The analytics platforms used on this website may change over time. If additional analytics or tracking technologies are implemented, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Advertising and Marketing Technologies

We may use online advertising, call tracking, conversion tracking, or similar marketing technologies to understand how prospective clients find SmokeDamage.com and how effectively our advertising campaigns perform.

These technologies may use cookies, device identifiers, browser information, referral information, or similar data.

We do not guarantee that advertising or tracking technologies will always be used on the website. The technologies implemented may change as our marketing programs evolve.

8. How We May Share Information

We do not sell your personal information simply because you submit an inquiry requesting assistance with an insurance claim.

However, we may share information with third parties when reasonably necessary to operate our business, provide services, respond to your request, or comply with legal requirements.

These parties may include:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Email service providers
  • Customer relationship management platforms
  • Website developers and technical service providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Communication providers
  • Call-tracking or telephone service providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Contractors assisting with business operations
  • Government agencies or regulatory authorities when legally required

Third-party service providers may receive access to information only to the extent reasonably necessary for the services they perform on our behalf.

We may also disclose information when we believe disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect our rights, investigate fraud, protect individuals or property, respond to lawful requests, or comply with applicable law.

9. Insurance Claim Information

Because our services relate to property insurance claims, you may voluntarily provide information relating to an existing or potential claim.

This could include information about:

  • Your insurance carrier
  • Policy or claim details
  • Property damage
  • Smoke or soot contamination
  • Fire damage
  • Personal property losses
  • Restoration work
  • Contractor estimates
  • Insurance company estimates
  • Claim payments
  • Photographs or supporting documentation

We use this information primarily to evaluate your inquiry, communicate with you, and, when applicable, provide public adjusting services after an appropriate professional relationship has been established.

We encourage you not to send unnecessary sensitive personal information through an unsecured website form.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information in our possession from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure.

However, no website, email transmission, internet connection, electronic storage system, or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use appropriate caution when submitting personal or confidential information online.

11. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Provide requested services
  • Maintain business and client records
  • Meet professional or regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce agreements
  • Prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the reason it was collected, whether you become a client, and applicable legal requirements.

  • Correct inaccurate information
