# SmokeDamage.com keyword research

Source: SEMrush keyword export, US database, 2026-09-28 (`smoke-damage-public-adjuster_all-keywords_us_2026-09-28.csv`, 20,699 keyword rows).

Every Volume / KD / CPC below is copied from its CSV row. A blank KD or intent means the cell is blank in the export. "Not in export" means the phrase is our proposed target but has no row in the CSV, so its volume is left blank.

**What this export is and isn't.** It was seeded from "smoke damage public adjuster", so most of its volume sits in *public adjuster* head terms, adjuster salary/career terms, and Florida/California/Georgia/Tennessee locations, none of which we target. Smoke-specific demand is small (the largest smoke row is 480/mo). HVAC, odor, electronics, Texas wildfire/event and "underpaid/delayed" terms are almost absent. Pages flagged **GAP** need a separate keyword pull before they are optimized.

Excluded as irrelevant: water, flood, hail, storm, roof, hurricane, vandalism, theft, mold and auto terms; adjuster salary/jobs/licensing/exam terms (except where noted on the rules page); non-Texas locations (Anaheim, Malibu, Miami, Nashville, Atlanta, etc.); company-name navigational terms.
