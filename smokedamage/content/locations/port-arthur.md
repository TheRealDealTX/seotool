Port Arthur sits at the southern point of the Golden Triangle, in Jefferson County along Sabine Lake. It is a refinery city first: the Port Arthur Refinery, now operated by Motiva, began operating in the early 1900s and is described as the largest oil refinery in the United States. That industrial backdrop shapes how many residents think about smoke, but most smoke claims here still start the ordinary way, with a kitchen fire, an electrical fire, or a fire in an attached unit.

As a Smoke Damage Public Adjuster, our role is narrow and specific. Smoke Damage Public Adjuster is the smoke and fire claim practice of Rise Public Adjusting LLC (TDI License #3356839). We represent the policyholder. We do not have an office in Port Arthur; we work with property owners across Texas, and Port Arthur claims are handled the same way as any other.

## What Port Arthur properties look like in a smoke claim

Port Arthur's housing is weighted toward older single-family homes, many built during the refinery and shipping boom years, along with duplexes, small rental houses, and apartment complexes. Newer subdivisions sit mostly toward the north and west edges of town and in neighboring Groves, Port Neches and Nederland.

Those differences matter in a claim:

- **Older frame homes** often have pier-and-beam foundations, plaster or older drywall, wood trim and attic spaces where smoke and soot settle out of sight.
- **Rental houses and duplexes** raise questions about whose policy responds to what: the owner's dwelling coverage, the tenant's contents coverage, or both.
- **Apartments and attached units** can show smoke and odor in units that never saw flame, through shared walls, attics and ventilation.
- **Small commercial buildings** along Procter Street, Highway 69 and the industrial corridors often hold inventory, equipment and records that need separate documentation.

For a closer look at how building materials and contents are handled, see our pages on [residential smoke damage claims](/residential-smoke-damage-claims/) and [smoke-damaged contents](/smoke-damaged-contents/).

## Industrial smoke and the Golden Triangle

Port Arthur shares the Sabine-Neches area with a cluster of refineries and petrochemical plants. In November 2019, explosions at the TPC Group plant in neighboring Port Neches led to a mandatory evacuation within a four-mile radius that reached parts of Port Arthur, Groves and Nederland.

When a smoke event starts somewhere other than your property, the claim questions change. Was there visible soot or residue? Did odor enter the home or building? Did the HVAC system draw smoke inside? What does your policy say about smoke from an outside source? Some policies address this directly; others do not. Coverage depends on the policy wording and the facts, and the claim is stronger when the damage is documented early and specifically. Our page on [smoke odor claims](/smoke-odor-claims/) explains why odor alone can be hard to prove without organized documentation.

A separate note: we do not handle claims against a plant or company that caused a loss. Those are liability matters. Our work is on first-party claims under your own property insurance policy.

## Weather and coastal considerations

Port Arthur is low-lying and has been hit repeatedly by hurricanes and major flooding. That history can complicate a later smoke or fire claim. Carriers may ask whether damage existed before the fire, whether earlier repairs were completed, or whether a prior claim already paid for a particular area. Keeping repair records, photos, and prior claim paperwork makes it easier to separate new smoke damage from older issues.

## How we help Port Arthur policyholders

Our work covers the claim itself, not cleanup or rebuilding. We are not a restoration contractor. In a Port Arthur smoke claim, we typically:

- Review the policy so the claim follows its actual language
- Document smoke, soot and odor throughout the property, not only near the origin
- Look at [HVAC and ductwork](/hvac-smoke-damage/) as a pathway for residue
- Prepare an estimate and a contents inventory with supporting information
- Present the claim to the carrier and compare its estimate against the documented scope

If your claim has already been [underpaid](/underpaid-smoke-damage-claims/) or [denied](/denied-smoke-damage-claims/), we can review the file and explain where the documentation may be incomplete. No result is guaranteed.

### Does my homeowners policy cover smoke from a refinery or plant fire?
It depends on the policy and the facts. Some policies cover sudden smoke damage regardless of where it started, and some contain exclusions or conditions that matter. We review the actual policy language before giving you our view.

### Do you have an office in Port Arthur?
No. Smoke Damage Public Adjuster works with policyholders throughout Texas and does not maintain local offices.

### Can you help a landlord with smoke damage in a rental house?
Yes, we can represent property owners on their own policies. Tenants' belongings are usually addressed under the tenant's own renters policy, if they have one.

### Will you clean or repair my home?
No. We are public adjusters, not contractors. We document, present and negotiate the insurance claim.
