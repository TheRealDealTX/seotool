Irving is in Dallas County, between DFW International Airport and Dallas Love Field, and a large portion of DFW Airport lies inside the city limits. The Las Colinas area has become one of the region's biggest concentrations of corporate headquarters, and the Irving chamber of commerce promotes the city as the "headquarters of headquarters."

We represent policyholders in smoke and fire claims across Texas, including Irving. Smoke Damage Public Adjuster is operated by Rise Public Adjusting LLC (TDI License #3356839). We do not have an office or staff in Irving; we work with Irving owners and businesses on their claims.

## Corporate offices and commercial property

Las Colinas includes high-rise and mid-rise office towers, corporate campuses, hotels, restaurants and the Urban Center around Lake Carolyn. Irving also has warehouse and logistics space near the airport and along SH 114, SH 183 and I-635, plus retail and restaurants across the city.

Commercial smoke claims here tend to have several moving parts:

- **Multi-tenant buildings.** Smoke from one suite, or from a restaurant on the ground floor, can reach other tenants through corridors, shafts and shared HVAC.
- **Electronics and equipment.** Office equipment, servers and specialized tools can be exposed to soot even when the fire was elsewhere in the building.
- **Inventory in warehouses.** Stored goods can pick up odor and residue that affects their value.
- **Business interruption.** Closures or reduced operations after a smoke event may be claimable if the policy includes that coverage and its conditions are met.
- **Leases.** Who insures tenant improvements and building systems depends on the lease and each party's policy.

Our [commercial smoke damage claims](/commercial-smoke-damage-claims/) page covers these in more depth, and [smoke-damaged contents](/smoke-damaged-contents/) explains how equipment and inventory are usually documented.

## Apartments and multifamily

Irving has a very large apartment market, from older garden-style complexes to newer mid-rise buildings in Las Colinas. When a fire starts in one unit, smoke can spread through breezeways, shared attics and adjacent units. That can mean:

- The owner's commercial property policy covers the building
- Each tenant's renters policy (if any) covers their belongings and possibly additional living expenses
- Loss of rents may be part of the owner's claim, depending on the policy

For property owners and managers, organizing documentation across many units is a large part of the work.

## Established neighborhoods

Outside the corporate corridors, Irving has many established single-family neighborhoods, some several decades old. Older homes may have original ductwork, older wiring and finishes that have been updated piecemeal. After a fire, questions about matching and the scope of repairs are common, and the answers depend on policy language. See [residential smoke damage claims](/residential-smoke-damage-claims/).

## Smoke risk in a dense urban setting

Irving is almost fully developed, so wildland fire is not a main concern. Most smoke claims start with structure fires: cooking, electrical, dryer, and fires spreading from a neighboring unit or business. In dense buildings, one fire can affect many policyholders at once. Coverage for any smoke loss depends on the policy and the facts.
