Frisco straddles Collin and Denton counties north of Plano. According to the city's downtown organization, it was a farming community of about 6,500 people in 1990 and has grown to nearly 250,000. Along the way it became home to the Dallas Cowboys' headquarters at The Star and the PGA of America's national headquarters at PGA Frisco, plus a large retail, office and hotel base along the Dallas North Tollway and SH 121.

As a Smoke Damage Public Adjuster, we represent policyholders in smoke and fire claims across Texas, including Frisco. We are Rise Public Adjusting LLC (TDI License #3356839). We have no Frisco office and no local staff. We work with Frisco owners on their claims.

## Newer homes, different smoke questions

Because so much of Frisco has been built in recent decades, many smoke claims here involve newer, often larger homes. That brings some specific issues:

- **Multiple HVAC systems and zones.** Two-story homes commonly have separate upstairs and downstairs systems. Smoke can travel through one or both, depending on what was running. See [HVAC smoke damage](/hvac-smoke-damage/).
- **Open floor plans and tall ceilings.** Smoke moves easily through open living areas, stairwells and lofts, reaching rooms well away from the source.
- **Attached garages.** Many Frisco homes have large attached garages where vehicles, tools, freezers and storage live. Garage fires are a common starting point for smoke that ends up in the house.
- **Builder-grade and upgraded finishes side by side.** Custom upgrades may not match what an estimate assumes. Documenting what was actually installed helps the claim reflect the home.

Master-planned communities and HOAs are common, so there may also be association rules and shared amenities to consider.

## Apartments, mixed-use and commercial

Frisco has added large apartment communities and mixed-use districts alongside its office and retail corridors. For multifamily properties, a single fire can bring in the building owner's policy and several renters' policies at once.

For businesses, including restaurants, retail stores, medical and dental offices and corporate tenants, smoke claims often involve equipment, inventory, tenant improvements and possible business income. See [commercial smoke damage claims](/commercial-smoke-damage-claims/).

## The edge of the Metroplex

Frisco's northern and western edges still meet farmland and open land as development moves toward Prosper, Celina and Little Elm. Grass fires on undeveloped tracts and construction sites can send smoke into nearby neighborhoods. The Texas A&M Forest Service describes this kind of boundary, where development sits next to undeveloped land, as the wildland-urban interface.

Still, most Frisco smoke claims start inside a structure: a kitchen fire, dryer fire, electrical issue, garage fire or a fire in a neighboring unit. Whatever the source, coverage depends on the policy and the facts.

### My home is only a few years old. Doesn't that make the claim simple?
Not always. Newer homes with open layouts and multiple HVAC systems can spread smoke widely. The scope depends on where smoke actually went, which is why documentation matters.

### The fire started in my garage. What about my car and tools?
Vehicles are usually handled under auto coverage, while tools and stored items may fall under your homeowners contents coverage. Each policy's wording decides.

Our client is the policyholder. If the association's policy is involved, we can help you understand how your claim and the association's responsibilities relate, based on the documents.
