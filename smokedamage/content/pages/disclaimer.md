**Effective date: September 28, 2026**

Information on this website is provided for general educational purposes and is not legal advice, an insurance coverage determination, a restoration recommendation, or a guarantee of any claim result. Insurance policies, losses, and claim circumstances vary.

Whether a particular loss is covered, and how it is valued, depends on the language of the specific policy and the facts of the loss. Articles, FAQs, and guides on this site describe how claims commonly work. They cannot account for the details of your policy or your property. For questions about your own claim, request a review or consult an appropriate professional.

## Not a restoration contractor

Smoke Damage Public Adjuster and Rise Public Adjusting LLC are not restoration contractors. We do not clean, remediate, demolish, repair, or rebuild property, and we do not perform repairs on claims we adjust.

Nothing on this site is a recommendation about how to clean, restore, or repair property, or about health or safety. For those matters, rely on qualified restoration, construction, HVAC, and health professionals, and follow the instructions of fire officials and other authorities.

## Not a law firm

We are licensed public adjusters, not attorneys. Nothing on this site is legal advice, and no attorney-client relationship is created by using the site or contacting us. If you have legal questions about your claim, your rights, or a dispute with your insurer, consult a licensed Texas attorney.

## Calculators and tools

The [smoke damage scope calculator](/tools/smoke-damage-scope-calculator/), the [contents RCV/ACV calculator](/tools/contents-rcv-acv-calculator/), and any other tools on this site are for estimating and organizational purposes only. Their results are approximations based on the information you enter and on general assumptions. They are not appraisals, claim valuations, or coverage determinations, and actual claim figures depend on the policy, the documented damage, and the insurer's evaluation.

## Event pages

Pages about Texas fires, smoke events, and fire history, including [Texas fire and smoke events](/texas-fire-smoke-events/) and [Texas fire and smoke history](/texas-fire-smoke-history/), report factual information drawn from cited public sources. Details about active or recent events can change, and we update pages as information becomes available.

Factual incident reporting is not a statement about any individual property's damage or coverage. Being near an event does not mean a property was damaged or that a claim would be covered, and being farther away does not mean it was not.

## No guarantee

We do not guarantee any claim result, payment amount, or timeline. No public adjuster can. Past or general descriptions of how claims work are not predictions about your claim. Representation begins only under a signed written contract.
