Church Banner Ideas: Hand-Painted Scripture Banners for Children’s Church, Easter and Christmas

By Christina Dittman, Christina Dittman Creations · Belton, TX

Ask a child what the lesson was about three weeks later and they will describe the picture on the wall. That is why hand-painted church banners are such a large part of the work at Christina Dittman Creations. A verse in big, clear lettering with a scene painted around it does what a printed poster cannot: it holds attention, and it holds up to being taped, re-hung and loved for years. Here are the banner ideas churches in Belton and across Bell County ask for most, with notes on lettering, size and timing.

Children's church lesson banners

The classic children's church banner has three parts: the words "Children's Church" across the top, the lesson's key phrase in the middle and the verse reference underneath, with an illustration that tells the story.

  • "Rejoice, for He is risen" - Matthew 28:6 - the empty tomb, a draped cross, butterflies and spring wildflowers. See the Easter banner.
  • "God's love letters to you!" - a heart garland, a red mailbox and painted envelopes, each carrying a verse about love (Romans 12:9, 1 John 4:7, 1 Peter 4:8). A Valentine's lesson that kids actually read. See the love letters banner.
  • "With all my heart I will thank the Lord" - Psalm 111:1 - a praying cowboy and his horse at a cross, a cornucopia and a wagon wheel for Thanksgiving. See the Thanksgiving banner.

Portrait banners (36" x 60") fit classroom walls and doors. If the banner will hang on a stage, go wide instead.

Easter banner ideas

  • "He is not here, for He is risen" with the stone rolled away
  • "Rejoice!" in gold with a cross and lilies
  • "Because He lives" with sunrise colors and butterflies
  • Palm branches and "Hosanna" for Palm Sunday

Easter banners photograph well in natural light, so plan the colors against the wall where they will hang: whites and golds on kraft paper glow; pastels need a little contrast.

Christmas banner ideas

  • Luke 2:11 over a nativity silhouette with the star shining down, framed by pine and red berries. See the Luke 2:11 banner.
  • Isaiah 9:6 as an acrostic: the letters of CHRISTMAS picked out in gold running down the verse, with holly around the edge. See the Isaiah 9:6 banner.
  • "Joy to the world" with bells and greenery
  • "O come let us adore Him" over a manger

Fall and Thanksgiving banner ideas

  • "Seasons change but Jesus Christ remains the same" - Hebrews 13:8 - a rope frame, sunflowers, a pumpkin, a boot, a horseshoe and cocoa. See the Hebrews 13:8 banner.
  • "Give thanks" with a cornucopia
  • "Happy Fall!" with a pumpkin, an acorn and a small cross for a church porch or festival table

Verse banners for any week

  • Mark 12:30 in red script with pink and purple hearts. See the Mark 12:30 banner.
  • Psalm 23, Philippians 4:13, Joshua 1:9, Proverbs 3:5-6 and Jeremiah 29:11 are the most-requested verses for classrooms, baptisms and graduation parties.
  • A sermon-series banner carries the series title with a blank panel for the week's verse, written in dry-erase marker on a laminated strip.

Lettering that kids can read

Three rules keep a scripture banner readable from the back row:

  1. One script, one block. Use a script style for the key phrase and a bold block style for the rest. More than two lettering styles gets busy.
  2. Contrast first. White, cream, gold and black all read on kraft paper. Pale pastels need an outline.
  3. Big words, small pictures. The verse should take up at least half the banner. Illustrations frame it; they do not compete with it.

FAQs

1. What size should a church banner be?

Classroom walls suit portrait banners around 36" x 60". Stages and fellowship halls suit wide banners at 60" x 30" or larger on request.

2. Can you paint a banner for our VBS theme?

Yes. Send the VBS artwork and theme verse and the banner is painted to match the published look.

3. How long does a scripture banner take?

A few days to a couple of weeks depending on the amount of illustration. Easter and Christmas fill up early, so order ahead.

4. Do you deliver to churches in Temple or Killeen?

Yes. Christina Dittman Creations serves churches across Belton, Temple, Killeen, Harker Heights, Salado and the rest of Bell County.
