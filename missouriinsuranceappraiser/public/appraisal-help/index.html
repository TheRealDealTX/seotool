Not sure where to start?

Most files begin with an estimate review

Before anyone invokes appraisal, it helps to know exactly where the insurer’s estimate falls short and what that’s worth. A review answers that quickly, and some disputes close right there with a documented supplement.

About estimate reviews
Can I hire you for just one stage?

Yes. Many owners start with an estimate review and decide later whether to invoke appraisal.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes: houses, condos, apartment complexes, HOA communities, offices, retail, warehouses and churches.

Do you handle more than hail?

Yes. Tornado and straight-line wind, ice and freeze, water, fire and smoke, and sinkhole or collapse claims are all part of our work.

Claim covered, check too small?

Send the insurer’s estimate and a few photos. We’ll tell you what we see, at no cost.
