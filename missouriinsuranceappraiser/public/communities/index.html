Regions

How claims differ across Missouri

The same policy language plays out differently on a St. Louis brick flat, a Joplin rebuild and an Ozarks home on karst ground.

Kansas City · Independence · Lee’s Summit

Kansas City Metro

Spring hail tracks across Jackson, Clay and Cass counties, landing on brick bungalows in Brookside and new HOA subdivisions alike.

St. Louis · Chesterfield · St. Charles

St. Louis Metro

Old brick flats and slate roofs in the city, ranch homes in the county, and river-bottom flood history in Chesterfield Valley.

Columbia · Jefferson City

Mid-Missouri

Tornadoes, Missouri River flooding and hail over university housing and older capital-city neighborhoods.

Springfield · Branson · Rolla

Ozarks

Karst ground with thousands of mapped sinkholes, ice storms like January 2007, and wooded lots where limbs meet roofs.

Joplin · Carthage · Neosho

Southwest Missouri

Tornado Alley’s eastern edge. Rebuilding after 2011 shaped how this corner of the state thinks about code upgrades.

St. Joseph · Cape Girardeau

Northwest & Bootheel

Missouri and Mississippi river towns with farm buildings, historic brick downtowns and New Madrid earthquake questions.
