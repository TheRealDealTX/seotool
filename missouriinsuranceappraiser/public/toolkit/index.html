Free Claim Tools for Missouri Property Owners

Seven estimators, calculators and planners drawn from appraisal work. Use them to understand your claim, check the insurer’s math and plan your next step. No sign-up, nothing stored.

Estimator

Award & Net Payout Estimator

Model where an appraisal award might land and what reaches you after the deductible, prior checks and fees.Calculator

Depreciation & Recovery Calculator

Split each line item into actual cash value and recoverable depreciation, then see your first check and the holdback.Calculator

Roof Measurement Calculator

Turn a footprint and pitch into roof squares, waste, bundles and tear-off layers to check the insurer’s numbers.Calculator

Wind & Hail Deductible Calculator

See what a percentage wind/hail deductible really costs on your dwelling limit, next to a flat deductible.Planner

Claim Calendar Builder

Turn your date of loss and policy time limits into a dated milestone list, and download it to your calendar.Checklist

Appraisal Readiness Score

Nine yes-or-no questions that score how ready your claim is for appraisal, plus what to fix first.Estimator

Roof Repair-or-Replace Splitter

Enter damaged squares slope by slope and compare a repair-only scope with a full roof replacement.Prefer a person?Have an appraiser check the numbersSend the insurer’s estimate and we’ll review it line by line, at no cost for the first look.

Suggested order

Using the tools together

  1. Start with the claim calendar builder so no date slips.
  2. Check roof figures with the roof measurement calculator and the repair-or-replace splitter.
  3. See what you’re owed with the wind & hail deductible and depreciation & recovery calculators.
  4. Test the dispute in the award & net payout estimator, then score your file with the readiness score.
