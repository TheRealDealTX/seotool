Belton Banners: hand-painted in Belton, Texas

Belton Banners is the local name for the custom hand-painted banners made by Christina Dittman Creations. Every one is lettered and illustrated by hand in a Belton studio and carried to parties, churches, schools and porches across Bell County and Central Texas.

Why Belton families choose hand-painted

A printed banner from a big-box store is identical to a thousand others. Belton Banners are painted one at a time - the name in script, the age painted like a road for a construction party, the family dog in the corner, a verse for Sunday school. They photograph like art because they are art, and they come home after the event instead of going in the trash.

Because the studio is local, the process is personal: a quick text to talk through the idea, a sketch to approve, pickup in Belton or delivery to Temple, Killeen, Harker Heights or Salado when the banner is ready.

What we paint for Belton

  • Birthday banners for kids, milestones and 21sts
  • Wedding welcome signs and sweetheart-table banners
  • Baby shower and gender reveal banners
  • Children’s church and scripture banners
  • Graduation and senior-night banners in school colors
  • Fall, Christmas and Easter banners
