Privacy Policy

Who we are

Dukora Idea (dukoraidea.com) publishes DIY lighting ideas, light guides and free calculators. You can contact us at info@dukoraidea.com.

Information we collect

We don't run accounts, shops or comment sections, so we collect very little. If you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address and message. Our web host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested, time) for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

We use your message only to reply to you. We don't sell, rent or share your information for marketing. Server logs are used to keep the site secure and working.

Cookies

The site itself does not set tracking cookies. The calculators run entirely in your browser and don't send your inputs anywhere. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering them.

Retention

We keep contact emails only as long as needed to answer and follow up, then delete them. Server logs are rotated by our host.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete any message you've sent us by emailing us. Residents of places with specific privacy rights (such as California or the EU/UK) can exercise those rights the same way.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date below shows the latest revision.
