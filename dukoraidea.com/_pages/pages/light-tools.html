Battery life

How long will battery fairy lights last?

Pick your battery, LED count, mode and timer. The estimate updates as you go. Twinkle and fade modes use less power than steady-on, and rechargeable NiMH cells hold slightly less charge than alkaline but save money over a season. Related: battery-operated fairy lights and fairy light battery guide.

LED strips

How big a power supply does my LED strip need?

Enter the length and the watts per meter printed on the reel. We add 20% headroom (power supplies run cooler and last longer that way), round up to a common size and flag runs long enough to need power injection. Related: smart LED strip lights and how to cut and connect strips.

Color & mood

Which light color fits the mood?

Color temperature is measured in kelvin: lower numbers are warm and candle-like, higher numbers are cool and daylight-like. Slide through the range or tap a mood to see how a room changes. Related: smart LED light bulbs and LED colors and moods.
