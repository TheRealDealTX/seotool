Free Insurance Claim Tools for Nevada Property Owners

Seven calculators, planners and checklists built from real appraisal work. Use them to understand your claim, check the insurer’s math and decide on next steps. No sign-up, nothing stored.

Calculator

Claim Gap & Fee Estimator

Measure the disputed amount, project an award and find the break-even point after appraisal fees.

Calculator

Depreciation Recovery Calculator

Estimate your first ACV check, the depreciation held back and the total once repairs are finished.

Calculator

Roof Squares & Tile Calculator

Turn footprint and pitch into roof squares, waste, tile counts and the share of broken tile.

Calculator

Percentage Deductible Calculator

See a wind, hail or earthquake percentage deductible in dollars and compare it to a flat deductible.

Planner

Claim Calendar Builder

Turn your date of loss into dated milestones and download them to your calendar.

Checklist

Appraisal File Scorecard

Twelve yes-or-no questions that score how ready your file is for an appraisal panel.

Estimator

Smoke & Ash Cleaning Estimator

Build an illustrative smoke and ash cleaning scope after a wildfire or house fire.

Suggested order

Using the tools together

  1. Start with the claim calendar builder so nothing lapses.
  2. Check roof measurements with the roof squares & tile calculator.
  3. See what you are actually owed with the percentage deductible and depreciation recovery calculators.
  4. For wildfire or house-fire smoke, build a scope with the smoke & ash cleaning estimator.
  5. Test the dispute with the claim gap & fee estimator.
  6. Finish with the appraisal file scorecard.
