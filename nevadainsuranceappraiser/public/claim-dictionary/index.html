Reference

Property Insurance Claim Dictionary

43 terms you will see in policies, estimates and appraisal awards, defined in plain English, with a few that are specific to Nevada.

43 terms

ABCDEFILMNOPRSUW
Actual cash value (ACV)
The value of damaged property at the time of loss, usually replacement cost minus depreciation.
Additional living expense (ALE)
Coverage for extra costs of living elsewhere while your home is uninhabitable after a covered loss.
Amount of loss
The dollar value of a covered loss. Appraisal resolves disagreements about this figure.
Appraisal award
The written decision signed by two of the three panel members setting the amount of loss.
Appraisal clause
The policy provision that allows either party, or both, to resolve a disagreement over the amount of loss through appraisal.
Bad faith
A legal claim that an insurer unreasonably denied, delayed or underpaid a claim. Decided by courts, not appraisal panels.
Caliche
A hard, cemented soil layer common in Southern Nevada that can complicate excavation and foundation work.
Concrete tile
A roof covering of molded concrete tiles, very common in Southern Nevada, laid over underlayment on battens or deck.
Contents
Personal property such as furniture, clothing and electronics, usually Coverage C on a homeowners policy.
Coverage A
Dwelling coverage on a homeowners policy: the house and attached structures.
Coverage B
Other structures coverage: detached garages, block walls, fences, sheds and similar.
Declarations page
The summary page of your policy listing coverages, limits, deductibles and the policy period.
Deductible
The portion of a covered loss you pay before the insurer pays.
Depreciation
A reduction in value for age, wear and condition.
Detach and reset
Removing an item, such as solar panels or a satellite dish, to allow repairs and reinstalling it afterward.
Earth movement exclusion
A common policy exclusion for earthquake, landslide and similar ground movement.
Ember intrusion
Wildfire embers entering a building through vents, gaps or openings and igniting material inside.
Estimate
An itemized list of repair tasks, quantities and prices used to value a loss.
Flood
Surface water from outside rising onto normally dry land. Usually excluded from homeowners policies and covered by separate flood insurance.
Ice dam
A ridge of ice at the eaves that traps meltwater, which can back up under roofing and leak inside.
Impartial appraiser
An appraiser without a financial interest in the outcome. Many policies require appraisers to be competent and impartial.
Line item
A single task or material in an estimate, with quantity, unit and price.
Matching
The question of whether repaired areas must reasonably match undamaged areas in color, texture and appearance.
Microburst
A sudden, localized thunderstorm downdraft producing damaging straight-line winds.
Mitigation
Reasonable steps to prevent further damage after a loss, such as tarping or drying.
Monsoon
Southern Nevada’s summer thunderstorm season, usually July through September.
Nevada Division of Insurance
The state agency that regulates insurers, licenses adjusters and handles consumer complaints.
Ordinance or law coverage
Coverage for added costs of meeting current building codes during repairs.
Overhead and profit (O&P)
A general contractor’s charge for coordinating multiple trades, often 10% and 10%.
Percentage deductible
A deductible calculated as a percentage of the dwelling limit rather than a fixed dollar amount.
Proof of loss
A sworn statement of the claimed loss, often required within a set time after the insurer requests it.
Recoverable depreciation
Depreciation held back from the first payment and paid after repairs are completed.
Replacement cost value (RCV)
The cost to repair or replace damaged property with like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.
Scope of loss
The full list of damage and the repairs needed to restore the property.
Slab leak
A leak in water lines beneath or within a concrete slab foundation.
Square
One hundred square feet of roof surface.
Stucco
A cement-based exterior wall finish common in Nevada, applied over lath and building paper.
Suit limitation
The policy’s deadline for filing a lawsuit against the insurer.
Supplement
A request to add missed items or revise an estimate after the first inspection.
Surplus lines insurer
An insurer not licensed as an admitted carrier in the state, allowed to write coverage that admitted carriers won’t.
Umpire
The neutral third member of an appraisal panel who decides items the appraisers can’t agree on.
Underlayment
The waterproof layer beneath roof tiles or shingles.
Washoe Zephyr
A strong afternoon westerly wind common in the Reno area.
