## 2. TWIA (Texas Windstorm Insurance Association) and TDI windstorm program

- **Designated catastrophe area:** the 14 first-tier coastal counties plus parts of Harris County east of Highway 146.
  - Counties: Aransas, Brazoria, Calhoun, Cameron, Chambers, Galveston, Jefferson, Kenedy, Kleberg, Matagorda, Nueces, Refugio, San Patricio and Willacy.
  - **Nueces, San Patricio, Aransas and Kleberg are all included (confirmed).**
  - Sources: https://www.twia.org/about-us/overview/ and https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/generalquestio.html
- **TWIA covers wind and hail only:** "TWIA policies provide coverage for wind and hail losses only… No other perils are covered."
  - It is not flood insurance.
  - TWIA was established by the Texas Legislature in 1971.
  - Source: https://www.twia.org/about-us/overview/
- **Flood insurance proof for some V-zone properties:** properties in V, VE or V1-30 flood zones that were built, altered, remodeled or enlarged on or after Sept. 1, 2009 must show proof of NFIP flood coverage (when available) to get TWIA coverage.
  - Source: https://www.twia.org/coverage-eligibility/
- **Certificate of compliance (WPI-8 basics):**
  - Structures built, altered, remodeled, enlarged or repaired, or with additions, on or after Jan. 1, 1988 need a certificate of compliance to be TWIA-eligible.
  - TDI issues WPI-8 and WPI-8-E. TWIA issues WPI-8-C.
  - TDI took over issuing certificates for completed improvements on June 1, 2020.
  - Sources: https://www.twia.org/about-us/overview/ and Tex. Ins. Code §2210.251, https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/IN/htm/IN.2210.htm
- **TDI Windstorm Inspection Program:**
  - Created by HB 2012 (1987); TDI inspections began Jan. 1, 1988.
  - Applies in the catastrophe areas. Covered work includes "re-roofs or roof repairs."
  - Inspections are done by appointed Texas-licensed professional engineers or TDI inspectors.
  - Source: https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/generalquestio.html
- **Current TDI windstorm building code:** starting April 1, 2026, Windstorm Certificate of Compliance applications (WPI-1) must be certified to the **2024 IRC or 2024 IBC**.
  - "Construction must be certified to be eligible for windstorm insurance coverage through TWIA."
  - Source: https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/adopted-codes.html
  - Earlier code editions and date ranges were not on that page. Treat them as UNVERIFIED.

### TWIA claim deadlines: Tex. Ins. Code Ch. 2210, Subchapter L-1

Statute text source: https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/IN/htm/IN.2210.htm. This is the official Texas Legislature statutes file that statutes.capitol.texas.gov loads; that site is now a JavaScript app and does not serve text directly.

- **Time to file a claim:** no later than **one year** (the first anniversary) after the date the damage occurs (§2210.573(a), §2210.205(a)(1)).
  - **Extension:** the Commissioner may extend this for good cause shown by the insured, by up to **180 days** (§2210.205(b)).
- **TWIA request for information:** TWIA may ask in writing for missing information within **30 days** after the claim is filed (§2210.573(b)).
- **TWIA accept/deny decision:** TWIA must give written notice accepting in full, accepting in part, or denying within **60 days**. The clock runs from receiving the claim or the requested information, whichever is later (§2210.573(d)).
  - The Commissioner may extend this deadline (§2210.581). Extensions for TWIA-only deadlines on claims from one event are capped at **120 days** total.
- **Payment:** TWIA must pay an accepted claim, or the accepted part, within **10 days** after the notice. If payment depends on an act by the claimant, the 10 days run from that act (§2210.5731).
- **Disputes over the amount (appraisal):** the claimant may demand appraisal within **60 days** after receiving the accept or partial-accept notice (§2210.574(b)).
  - TWIA may grant **30 more days** if the claimant asks in writing, with good cause, within 15 days after the 60-day period ends (§2210.574(c)).
  - The claimant and TWIA split appraisal costs equally (§2210.574(d)(2)).
  - If the two appraisers cannot agree on an umpire, the Commissioner picks one from a TDI roster (§2210.574(e)).
- **Disputes over denied coverage (notice of intent, ADR, suit):**
  - Notice of intent: the claimant must notify TWIA of intent to sue before the limitations period runs out. Missing this waives the right to contest the denial (§2210.575(a)-(b)).
  - ADR request: TWIA may require mediation or a moderated settlement conference, and must ask for it within **60 days** of receiving the notice (§2210.575(c)).
  - ADR deadline: ADR must be completed within **60 days** of the request, unless the Commissioner or both parties extend it (§2210.575(d)).
  - Where to sue: if the claimant is not satisfied, or ADR is not finished in time, the claimant may sue in district court in the county of the loss (§2210.575(e)).
- **Deadline to sue:** no later than **two years** (the second anniversary) after receiving the partial or full denial notice. This is a statute of repose (§2210.577).
- **Ch. 542A pre-suit notice does not apply to TWIA:** Chapter 542A "does not apply to an action against the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association" (§542A.002(b)).
  - Source: https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/IN/htm/IN.542A.htm
- **Subchapter L-1 controls** over any other law that conflicts with it (§2210.579(a)).
- **Ombudsman:** TDI runs an ombudsman program to help TWIA policyholders with the claims process (§2210.582).

### Texas FAIR Plan (brief)

- **What it is:** the Texas FAIR Plan Association (TFPA) gives property coverage to eligible owners who have been turned down by other insurers. Applicants generally need two declinations.
  - Source: https://www.texasfairplan.org/
- **No wind or hail where TWIA is available:** the FAIR Plan "may not provide windstorm and hail insurance coverage for a risk eligible for that coverage under Chapter 2210" (Tex. Ins. Code §2211.156).
  - Coastal owners use TWIA for wind and hail.
  - Source: https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/IN/htm/IN.2211.htm

## 3. Hurricane and storm history: Corpus Christi and Coastal Bend

**Sources and caveats for this section:**
- Primary sources are the NHC Tropical Cyclone Reports (TCRs) and the NOAA HURDAT list of U.S. landfalling hurricanes: https://www.aoml.noaa.gov/hrd/hurdat/All_U.S._Hurricanes.html. HURDAT says it was revised Sept. 2026.
- Category means the Saffir-Simpson category at U.S. or Texas landfall.
- Times are UTC as published, with CDT conversions.

**Storm list:**
- **Hurricane Beulah: Sept. 20, 1967.** Category 3 conditions in South Texas.
  - Landfall was near the mouth of the Rio Grande / Brownsville area. HURDAT marks Beulah "#": landfall over Mexico, but with sustained hurricane-force winds in Texas.
  - Sources: HURDAT list above; NWS CRP https://www.weather.gov/crp/Beulah
- **Hurricane Celia: Aug. 3, 1970.** HURDAT gives Category 4 (120 kt, 944 mb) in South Texas.
  - The HURDAT2 landfall point is 27.8N 97.1W at 2100 UTC (about 4 p.m. CDT), in the Port Aransas / Aransas Pass area.
  - The current NWS Corpus Christi page says Category 4 near Aransas Pass, with 140 mph sustained winds measured at Corpus Christi International Airport.
  - **Caution:** an older NWS 40th-anniversary page (https://www.weather.gov/crp/celia1970) says Category 3. Use Category 4, citing HURDAT and https://www.weather.gov/crp/hurricanecelia
- **Hurricane Allen: Aug. 10, 1980.** Category 3 in South Texas (HURDAT).
  - The HURDAT2 track crosses the lower Texas coast near 26.1N 97.2W around 0600 UTC Aug. 10 (South Padre Island area, north of Brownsville).
  - NWS pages disagree on the exact point: Brownsville (NWS Lake Charles) vs. Port Mansfield (NWS Brownsville). Say "the lower Texas coast."
- **Hurricane Bret: Aug. 22-23, 1999.** Category 3 (100 kt, 951 mb).
  - The center crossed central Padre Island, "midway between Brownsville and Corpus Christi," at 0000 UTC Aug. 23 (7 p.m. CDT Aug. 22). Kenedy County took most of the hurricane-force winds.
  - Source: https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/AL031999_Bret.pdf
- **Hurricane Harvey: Aug. 25-26, 2017.** Category 4 (115 kt, 937 mb).
  - Landfall on the northern end of San José Island, about 5 n mi east of Rockport, at 0300 UTC Aug. 26 (10 p.m. CDT Aug. 25).
  - Second landfall 3 hours later, at Category 3 (105 kt), on the northeast shore of Copano Bay west of Holiday Beach.
  - Source: https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/AL092017_Harvey.pdf
- **Hurricane Hanna: July 25, 2020.** Category 1 (80 kt).
  - Landfall on Padre Island around 2200 UTC (5 p.m. CDT).
  - Second landfall in eastern Kenedy County about 10 n mi NNW of Port Mansfield around 2315 UTC.
  - It was the first July hurricane landfall in Texas since Dolly (2008).
  - Source: https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/AL082020_Hanna.pdf
- **Hurricane Beryl: July 8, 2024.** Category 1 (80 kt).
  - Landfall near Matagorda, TX at 0840 UTC (3:40 a.m. CDT).
  - Source: https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/AL022024_Beryl.pdf
- **2025 season:** none of the 13 NHC 2025 Atlantic TCRs describe a Texas landfall.
  - Barry made landfall in Veracruz, Mexico. NHC says Barry and its remnants "were not the primary cause" of the July 4-5, 2025 Hill Country floods.
  - Source: https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/AL022025_Barry.pdf; index at https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/index.php?season=2025&basin=atl
- **2026 season, Tropical Storm Arthur (June 16-17, 2026):**
  - It began as Potential Tropical Cyclone One, centered about 65 mi SW of Corpus Christi (inland) at 10 a.m. CDT June 16.
  - It moved northeast up the coast as Tropical Storm Arthur (45 mph, about 20 mi NNW of Matagorda on June 17). It became post-tropical near Galveston late June 17.
  - Tropical storm watches and warnings ran from **Sargent, TX to Morgan City, LA**, which is not the Coastal Bend. The main hazard was heavy rain and flash flooding.
  - Source: https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2026/ARTHUR.shtml (advisories al012026.public.001-008)
  - No final TCR yet. Damage and rainfall in Corpus Christi are UNVERIFIED.

## 5. Texas Insurance Code basics

Sources: https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/IN/htm/IN.4102.htm, https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/IN/htm/IN.542.htm and https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/IN/htm/IN.542A.htm

**Public adjusters (Chapter 4102):**
- **§4102.051:** a license is required to act as, or hold oneself out as, a public adjuster in Texas.
- **§4102.103:** a written contract on a TDI-approved form is required. It must let the client **rescind by written notice within 72 hours of signature**, and must display "WE REPRESENT THE INSURED ONLY" in 12-point bold.
- **§4102.104(a):** the total commission may not exceed **10% of the insurance settlement** on the claim.
- **§4102.104(b):** no percentage fee is allowed if the insurer pays, or commits in writing to pay, policy limits within 72 hours after the loss is reported. Reasonable time-based compensation is allowed instead.
- **§4102.104(c)-(e):** the insured must be named as a payee on claim checks and must endorse them. A public adjuster may not sign or endorse claim checks for the insured.

**Prompt Payment of Claims Act (Chapter 542, Subchapter B):**
- **§542.055 (acknowledge):** within **15 days** of notice of a claim (30 business days for eligible surplus lines insurers), the insurer must acknowledge the claim, start its investigation and request the items it needs.
- **§542.056 (accept or reject):** within **15 business days** after receiving all required items. This is 30 days if arson is suspected.
  - If the insurer needs more time, it must say why within that period, then decide within **45 days** of that notice.
- **§542.057 (pay):** within **5 business days** after notice of acceptance (20 business days for surplus lines).
- **§542.058 (delay damages):** apply when payment is delayed beyond the statutory period, or more than 60 days if no other statute sets a period.
- **§542.059(b):** after a weather-related catastrophe or major natural disaster, as defined by the Commissioner, these deadlines are extended **15 days**.

**Pre-suit notice (Chapter 542A):**
- **§542A.003:** written notice is required at least **61 days** before filing suit. It must state the acts or omissions, the specific amount owed, and attorney's fees.
- The notice is excused if limitations is about to expire.
- Ch. 542A does **not** apply to suits against TWIA (§542A.002(b)).

### RainViewer weather-maps.json

- URL: https://api.rainviewer.com/public/weather-maps.json. Returns HTTP 200, `application/json`, no key needed.
- Top-level keys: `version, generated, host, radar, satellite`.
- `host` = `https://tilecache.rainviewer.com`.
