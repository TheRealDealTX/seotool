Understanding Hail Damage: What Really Happens to Your Roof

Hail damage is rarely dramatic. That’s the trap. A roof can look fine from the ground while quietly deteriorating underneath. Impact from hail compresses materials, weakens protective layers, and creates stress points that worsen over time.

FAQs

How do I know if I have hail damage on my roof?

Look for dents, granule loss, or soft spots, but a professional inspection is the most reliable way to confirm damage.

How long do I have to file a hail damage roof insurance claim?

Most policies allow up to one year, but filing sooner improves accuracy and claim strength.

Will my insurance premium increase after a hail claim?

Hail is typically considered a natural event, so increases aren’t guaranteed, but it depends on your insurer.

Can I file a claim if there are no leaks yet?

Yes, many claims are approved based on structural damage before leaks appear.

What is the difference between cosmetic and functional damage?

Cosmetic affects appearance, while functional damage impacts your roof’s performance and protection.

Should I get more than one roof inspection?

Yes, multiple inspections can reveal missed damage and provide stronger claim support.

What happens if my hail damage claim is denied?

You can request a re-inspection, submit additional evidence, or escalate the claim if necessary.

How long does a hail damage roof insurance claim take?

It can take a few weeks to several months depending on claim complexity and follow-ups.

Do I need a public adjuster for my claim?

Not always, but they can help maximize payouts, especially for complex or underpaid claims.

Can hail damage affect my home’s resale value?

Yes, unresolved or poorly repaired damage can reduce property value and buyer confidence.
