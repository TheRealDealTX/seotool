About Us

Insurance claims are supposed to bring relief after property damage. In reality, they often create frustration, delays, and confusion—especially for Corpus Christi property owners facing complex policies and aggressive insurance tactics. That’s where Corpus Christi Public Adjuster comes in.

We are a team of licensed public adjusters dedicated to representing policyholders, not insurance companies. Our role is simple but powerful: we help homeowners and business owners throughout Corpus Christi document their losses accurately, challenge unfair claim decisions, and pursue the full settlement their insurance policies allow.

When disaster strikes, insurance companies already have experts working for them. We believe you deserve the same level of representation.

Who We Serve

Corpus Christi is a diverse city with equally diverse property risks. From sudden storms to long-term water damage, no two claims are ever the same. We work with a wide range of clients, including:

  • Homeowners dealing with storm, fire, water, or structural damage
  • Business owners facing interrupted operations and property losses
  • Property managers handling multi-unit or commercial claims
  • Landlords navigating complex coverage and tenant-related issues

Whether your claim is residential or commercial, small or large, we approach every case with the same focus: accuracy, persistence, and accountability.

What Makes Public Adjusters Different

Most people only interact with insurance adjusters after a loss—but those adjusters work for the insurance company, not for you. A public adjuster changes the balance.

Our Role in the Claims Process

We step in to manage the technical, time-consuming, and often overwhelming parts of the claim so you don’t have to.

Our Approach

At Corpus Christi Public Adjuster, we don’t believe in shortcuts or cookie-cutter claims. Every property, policy, and loss scenario deserves a tailored strategy.

Our process typically includes:

  • Comprehensive damage inspection to identify visible and hidden losses
  • Detailed documentation including photos, measurements, and reports
  • Policy analysis to uncover all applicable coverages
  • Claim preparation and submission with accurate valuations
  • Direct negotiation with the insurance company on your behalf
  • Ongoing follow-up until the claim is resolved

We stay involved from start to finish. No handoffs. No guesswork. And no disappearing once paperwork is filed.

When Should You Call a Public Adjuster?

Many clients reach out too late—after mistakes have already limited their claim. Others call early and avoid costly missteps altogether.

You should consider contacting us if:

  • Your claim was denied or underpaid
  • The insurance process feels overwhelming
  • You’re asked to provide extensive documentation
  • The insurer keeps delaying or changing adjusters
  • You want an expert review before accepting a settlement

Early involvement often leads to stronger outcomes—but help is available at nearly any stage of the claim.
